Multiscale modelling of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V alloy: Fatigue performance evaluation from material to structural level

Weiqian Chi, Wenjing Wang, Hongchang Zhou, Rui‐Guo Yan, Yoshiki Mikami International Journal of Fatigue 2025-07-25 10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2025.109181
相关性: 该论文直接针对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的疲劳性能进行多尺度建模，完全符合L1层方向要求。
边界: Mainly computational modeling without experimental validation data.
动机· 为增材制造Ti-6Al-4V合金开发多尺度疲劳预测模型
方法· 从微观组织到结构层次的多尺度有限元建模方法
结果· 实现了从材料到结构层次的疲劳性能准确评估
验证· 通过数值模拟案例验证模型有效性
English summary & terms
Motivation· Develop a multiscale fatigue prediction model for additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· A multiscale finite element modeling approach spanning from microstructure to structural level.
Result· The model accurately evaluates fatigue performance across scales.
Validation· Validation through numerical simulation case studies.

The Influence of Heat and Surface Treatment on the Functional Properties of Ti6Al4V Alloy Samples Obtained by Additive Technology for Applications in Personalized Implantology

Anita Kajzer, Gabriela Wielgus, Krystian Drobina, Aleksandra Żurawska, Wojciech Kajzer Applied Sciences 2025-07-25 10.3390/app15158311
单位: Department of Biomaterials and Medical Devices Engineering, Faculty of Biomedical Engineering, Silesian University of Technology, Roosevelta 40 Street, 41-800 Zabrze, Poland
通讯: Gabriela Wielgus @ Department of Biomaterials and Medical Devices Engineering, Faculty of Biomedical Engineering, Silesian University of Te
相关性: 研究了Ti6Al4V的热处理和表面处理，但缺乏疲劳和拉伸性能数据，仅涉及硬度和腐蚀，与用户关注的疲劳/力学性能方向关联较弱。
边界: The paper does not report fatigue or tensile mechanical properties; it focuses on hardness, corrosion resistance, and wettability, missing the core mechanical performance metrics (fatigue, tensile) required by the direction.
动机· 评估热处理和表面处理对增材制造Ti6Al4V样品理化性能的影响
方法· 对喷砂和抛光样品进行不同温度热处理，测试硬度、润湿性、表面形貌和耐腐蚀性
结果· 在1020℃热处理获得最佳耐点蚀性能，所有样品呈亲水性
验证· 通过金相观察、硬度测试和电化学腐蚀实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study evaluates the influence of heat and surface treatments on the physicochemical properties of Ti6Al4V samples produced by direct metal sintering, aiming to inform implant material selection.
Method· Sandblasted and polished samples were subjected to heat treatment at 800, 910, and 1020°C, then characterized by microscopy, hardness, wettability, and pitting corrosion tests in Ringer's solution.
Result· Heat treatment at 1020°C yielded the highest pitting corrosion resistance, and all samples exhibited hydrophilic behavior.
Validation· Experimental validation through metallographic observation, hardness measurements, and electrochemical corrosion testing.

Effects of Ultrasonic Nanocrystal Surface Modification on the Formation of a Nitride Layer in Ti-6Al-4V Alloy

Бауыржан Рахадилов, Нуртолеу Магазов, Zarina Aringozhina, Gulzhaz Uazyrkhanova, Zhuldyz Uazyrkhanova et al. Materials 2025-07-25 10.3390/ma18153487
单位: D. Serikbayev East Kazakhstan Technical University, 69 Protozanov Street, Ust-Kamenogorsk 070000, Kazakhstan
通讯: Gulzhaz Uazyrkhanova @ D. Serikbayev East Kazakhstan Technical University, 69 Protozanov Street, Ust-Kamenogorsk 070000, Kazakhstan  |  Auezhan Amanov @ Faculty of Engineering and Natural Sciences, Tampere University, FIN-33014 Tampere, Finland
相关性: 该论文主要研究表面后处理工艺，而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学或疲劳性能。
边界: The paper focuses on a post-processing surface modification (UNSM+nitriding) for Ti-6Al-4V, not on the as-built or process-state mechanical/fatigue performance of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it lacks any additive manufacturing context or fatigue data.
动机· 探究超声波纳米晶表面改性对Ti-6Al-4V合金氮化物层形成的影响。
方法· 系统改变UNSM参数，通过SEM/XRD/硬度/磨损测试进行表征。
结果· 优化UNSM预处理显著增强氮扩散，形成致密TiN/Ti2N层，提升硬度和耐磨性。
验证· 通过实验表征（SEM、XRD、硬度、摩擦系数）验证效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates how ultrasonic nanocrystalline surface modification (UNSM) influences the formation of nitride layers on Ti-6Al-4V alloy during ion-plasma nitriding.
Method· UNSM parameters (vibration amplitude, static load, temperature) were varied systematically, and the resulting microstructure, hardness, elastic modulus, and wear behavior were characterized using SEM, XRD, and tribological tests.
Result· Optimal UNSM pre-treatment significantly enhances nitrogen diffusion, producing dense TiN/Ti2N layers that increase surface hardness by up to 25% and elastic modulus by 18%, with reduced and stabilized friction coefficient (~0.55).
Validation· The findings are validated through experimental measurements including SEM, XRD, hardness testing, and wear resistance evaluation.
