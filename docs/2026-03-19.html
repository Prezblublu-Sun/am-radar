Fatigue Life Prediction of TC4 Titanium Alloy Bolted Structures in Thermal Environments Below 400 °C Using an Enhanced DFR Method

Hang Peng, Bintuan Wang, Jianbo Qin, Shiyu Li, Yan Zhou et al. Materials 2026-03-19 10.3390/ma19061210
单位: AVIC The First Aircraft Institute, Xi’an 710089, China
通讯: Hang Peng @ AVIC The First Aircraft Institute, Xi’an 710089, China
相关性: 该论文直接针对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的疲劳性能，提出了增强型DFR方法用于螺栓结构在热环境下的寿命预测，与L1方向高度相关。
边界: It is not primarily about additive manufacturing; the Ti-6Al-4V material is conventionally produced, not additively manufactured.
动机· 解决400°C以下热环境中TC4螺栓结构疲劳寿命预测不准确的问题
方法· 基于基础材料疲劳实验数据，建立考虑温度影响的增强型DFR方法
结果· 增强DFR方法在20~400°C范围内将平均疲劳寿命预测精度提高9.29%
验证· 通过TC4螺栓双剪结构疲劳实验验证预测精度
English summary & terms
Motivation· To address the lack of accurate fatigue life prediction methods for TC4 bolted structures operating at moderate elevated temperatures up to 400°C.
Method· An enhanced detail fatigue rating (DFR) method was developed by incorporating temperature-dependent thermal influences based on fatigue test data of TC4 base material at 20°C, 200°C, and 400°C.
Result· The enhanced DFR method improved the average fatigue life prediction accuracy by 9.29% compared to the conventional DFR method within the 20–400°C range.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed using fatigue tests on double-shear TC4 bolted structures at different temperatures.

Motivation· The study aims to understand how additive manufacturing process variables affect the surface integrity of Ti-6Al-4V components.
Method· Samples were fabricated with different process parameters and subjected to tensile and fatigue tests.
Result· Process variables were found to significantly influence surface integrity and the resulting mechanical behaviors.
Validation· Experimental mechanical testing was performed, but no specific validation of methods is described.

Preparation and properties of CuNiIn coating on Ti-6Al-4V alloy surface by supersonic plasma spraying

Longlong Zhou, Weiling Guo, Gengchao He, Haidou Wang, Jun Zhou et al. Materials Science and Technology 2026-03-19 10.1177/02670836261433602
单位: Army Academy of Armored Forces
通讯: Zhenbing Cai @ Southwest Jiaotong University  |  Zhiguo Xing @ Army Academy of Armored Forces
相关性: 该论文研究的是钛合金表面涂层制备而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学性能，与用户方向无关。
边界: Paper is about a coating deposition technique (supersonic plasma spraying) on Ti-6Al-4V substrate, not additive manufacturing of Ti-6Al-4V; it contains no AM process, no mechanical/fatigue characterization of AM Ti-6Al-4V, and no relevance to L1 focus.
动机· 提高钛合金表面耐磨性，延长使用寿命。
方法· 采用超音速等离子喷涂制备CuNiIn涂层，研究粉末粒径和摩擦参数对涂层的影响。
结果· 中等粒径粉末涂层致密性最好，硬度163.1 HV，弹性模量122.8 GPa；高载荷下磨损机制转变。
验证· 通过SEM、XRD、硬度测试和磨损实验验证涂层性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the wear resistance of Ti-6Al-4V alloy and extend its service life by depositing a CuNiIn coating.
Method· CuNiIn coatings were prepared by supersonic plasma spraying, and the effects of powder particle size and friction parameters on coating microstructure and wear mechanism were investigated.
Result· Medium-sized particles yielded the densest coating with the lowest porosity (2.62%), highest hardness (163.1 HV), and elastic modulus (122.8 GPa); the dominant wear mechanism shifted from abrasive/oxidative to abrasive/adhesive/oxidative under 20 N load.
Validation· Microstructure and mechanical properties were characterized by SEM, XRD, Vickers hardness testing, and wear morphology analysis.
