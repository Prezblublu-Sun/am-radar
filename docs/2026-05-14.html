3D printed Ti-6Al-4V titanium matrix composites with synergistic enhancement of tensile strength and wear resistance through incorporating TiB2 nanoceramics

Hui Li, Dasheng Wei, Guodong Li, Maowen Liu, Y Liu et al. Transactions of Materials Research 2026-05-14 10.1016/j.tramat.2026.100298
单位: School of Materials Science and Engineering, Beihang University, Beijing 100191, China
通讯: Ruixiao Zheng @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, Beihang University, Beijing 100191, China
相关性: 该论文通过TiB2纳米陶瓷增强Ti-6Al-4V基复合材料，显著提升了拉伸强度和耐磨性，直接涉及增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学性能，符合L1层方向。
边界: 论文主要关注复合材料的强度与耐磨性协同增强，而非纯Ti-6Al-4V的疲劳性能或缺陷影响，缺乏疲劳数据。
动机· 提升Ti-6Al-4V的强度与耐磨性，以满足工程应用需求。
方法· 采用激光粉末床熔融技术添加TiB2纳米陶瓷制备Ti-6Al-4V基复合材料。
结果· 添加1 wt.% TiB2后拉伸强度达约1350 MPa，耐磨性显著提高。
验证· 通过拉伸试验和磨损试验验证力学性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To simultaneously enhance the tensile strength and wear resistance of Ti-6Al-4V for critical engineering applications.
Method· Laser powder bed fusion was employed to fabricate crack-free Ti-6Al-4V matrix composites reinforced with TiB2 nanoceramics.
Result· Addition of 1 wt.% TiB2 yielded a tensile strength of approximately 1350 MPa and improved wear resistance.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated via tensile tests and wear resistance measurements.

Optimization of Mechanical Properties and Corrosion Resistance of β-Ti-18Mo-7Cr Alloy through Heat Treatment for Orthopedic Implant Applications

Rosita Utari, Bunga Rani Elvira, Adi Noer Syahid, Alfirano Alfirano, Galih Senopati et al. Journal of biomimetics, biomaterials and biomedical engineering 2026-05-14 10.4028/p-2xmddv
单位: Sultan Ageng Tirtayasa University
通讯: Rosita Utari @ Sultan Ageng Tirtayasa University
相关性: 该论文研究的是β-Ti-18Mo-7Cr合金，而非Ti-6Al-4V，且不涉及股骨柄设计或增材制造。
边界: This paper does not study Ti-6Al-4V, does not involve femoral stem design, and lacks additive manufacturing or structural biomechanical analysis.
动机· 寻找具有高耐腐蚀性和低弹性模量的β钛合金用于骨科植入物。
方法· 通过电弧熔炼制备Ti-18Mo-7Cr合金，然后进行固溶和时效热处理，并测试弹性模量、硬度和耐腐蚀性。
结果· 固溶处理可获得较低的弹性模量（91 GPa）和较高硬度（471.42 HV），时效处理可提高耐腐蚀性。
验证· 实验测量弹性模量、硬度和电化学腐蚀速率。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to develop β-Ti-18Mo-7Cr alloy with high corrosion resistance and low elastic modulus for orthopedic implants.
Method· The alloy was prepared by arc melting and subjected to solution and aging heat treatments, followed by characterization of elastic modulus, hardness, and corrosion resistance.
Result· Solution treatment yielded the lowest elastic modulus (91 GPa) and highest hardness (471.42 HV), while aging treatment improved corrosion resistance.
Validation· Validation was performed through experimental measurements of elastic modulus, hardness, and electrochemical corrosion tests.
