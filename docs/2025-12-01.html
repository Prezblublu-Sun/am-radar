Alejandro Javier Hernandez scholarworks - UTEP (The University of Texas at El Paso) 2025-12-01 link
相关性: 该论文研究不同供应商粉末对Ti-6Al-4V DED制件力学性能的影响，直接相关于L1层力学与疲劳性能评估。
边界: No fatigue testing or S-N data, but covers tensile strength, hardness, and strain for as-built DED Ti-6Al-4V specimens.

English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates whether different Ti-6Al-4V powder feedstocks from various manufacturers affect the mechanical properties of directed energy deposition (DED) fabricated bulk specimens under identical processing parameters.

Validation· Experimental mechanical testing combined with statistical analysis validated that powder quality contributes to variance in as-built mechanical properties.

Validation· Experimental measurements of erosion depth and volume were combined with microstructural analysis to explain the anisotropy.

边界: The paper focuses on the microstructure and wear properties of a TiFeNiCuV HEA coating on a Ti-6Al-4V substrate, not on the additive manufacturing or mechanical/fatigue characterization of Ti-6Al-4V itself.
动机· 提高钛合金表面性能，制备新型高熵合金涂层。
方法· 在不同激光功率下激光熔覆制备TiFeNiCuV高熵合金涂层。
结果· 涂层硬度最高HV 692.2，体积磨损量仅为TC4基体的14.47%。
验证· 通过显微硬度测试和摩擦磨损实验验证涂层性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the surface properties of titanium alloys by developing a novel high-entropy alloy coating.
Method· A TiFeNiCuV high-entropy alloy coating was fabricated on a Ti-6Al-4V substrate via laser cladding at varying laser powers.
Result· The coating achieved an average hardness of HV 692.2 and a volume loss of 4.22×10^{-3} mm^3, only 14.47% that of the TC4 substrate.
Validation· Microhardness testing and friction-wear experiments were conducted to characterize the coating's mechanical and tribological properties.

SiCNO Ceramic Coatings on Surface‐Treated Ti‐6Al‐4V for Fretting Corrosion Protection

Detang Lu, Cheng‐Ta Wu, Lung‐Hao Hu Advanced Engineering Materials 2025-12-01 10.1002/adem.202502194
单位: Department of Mechanical and Electro‐Mechanical Engineering National Sun Yat‐sen University Kaohsiung 804 Taiwan
通讯: Cheng‐Ta Wu @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery Kaohsiung Chang Gung Memorial Hospital Kaohsiung 833 Taiwan  |  Lung‐Hao Hu @ Department of Mechanical and Electro‐Mechanical Engineering National Sun Yat‐sen University Kaohsiung 804 Taiwan
相关性: 该论文主要研究Ti-6Al-4V基体上的SiCNO涂层，不涉及股骨柄结构设计或增材制造，与本方向无关。
边界: The paper focuses on ceramic coating for fretting corrosion protection, not on structural design, additive manufacturing, or mechanical performance of Ti-6Al-4V femoral stems.
动机· 为提升髋关节假体柄颈界面耐磨损腐蚀性能，研究SiCNO涂层对Ti-6Al-4V的保护效果
方法· 在不同预处理Ti-6Al-4V表面涂覆不同浓度聚硅氮烷前驱体，经700-900°C热解形成SiCNO薄膜
结果· 20 wt% PSZ在800°C热解制备的涂层在耐腐蚀和力学性能间取得最佳平衡
验证· 通过电化学测试、硬度与模量测量评估涂层性能
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve fretting corrosion resistance at the femoral stem-neck and head interfaces, this study investigates SiCNO coatings on Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Polysilazane precursors with different concentrations were coated on acid-alkali and plasma pretreated Ti-6Al-4V disks and pyrolyzed at 700-900°C to form SiCNO films.
Result· The coating with 20 wt% PSZ pyrolyzed at 800°C achieved the best balance between corrosion resistance and mechanical properties.
Validation· Electrochemical corrosion tests and nanoindentation hardness/modulus measurements were performed to evaluate coating performance.
