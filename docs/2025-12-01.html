Comparison Of Ti-6al-4v Powder And Bulk Specimen Properties Across Manufacturers In Laser Powder-Blown Ded

Alejandro Javier Hernandez scholarworks - UTEP (The University of Texas at El Paso) 2025-12-01 link
相关性: 该论文直接研究了增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学性能，但未涉及疲劳性能。
边界: No fatigue testing or data (S-N, crack initiation/growth) included; only focus on tensile strength, hardness, and strain.
动机· 比较不同供应商Ti64粉末对DED制件力学性能的影响
方法· 保持工艺参数不变，使用四种粉末打印不同角度试样并测试力学性能
结果· 粉末差异导致UTS、应变和硬度显著变化
验证· 通过力学测试和统计分析验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how the quality of Ti-6Al-4V powder feedstocks from different manufacturers affects the mechanical properties of DED-fabricated bulk specimens under identical processing parameters.
Method· Four powder variants from three manufacturers were used to print coupons at build angles of 0-30°, followed by tensile, hardness, and strain measurements, with stress relief applied to preserve as-built microstructure.
Result· Despite consistent printing parameters, significant variations in ultimate tensile strength, strain, and hardness were observed among powder variants, validated by statistical analysis.
Validation· Experimental mechanical testing (tensile, hardness, strain) combined with statistical analysis.

Enhancement of grinding titanium alloy fatigue performance via synergistic effect of low surface roughness and gradient microstructure

Zhenyang Liu, Kangkang Song, Yingxin Wang, Kun Zhou, Ruisong Jiang et al. Chinese Journal of Aeronautics 2025-12-01 10.1016/j.cja.2025.104008
单位: College of Mechanical and Vehicle Engineering, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400030, China
通讯: Kun Zhou @ College of Mechanical and Vehicle Engineering, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400030, China
相关性: 该论文研究了Ti-6Al-4V合金的磨削抛光后处理工艺对疲劳性能的提升，虽非增材制造材料，但其表面完整性和梯度微结构的优化机制对增材制造后处理具有借鉴意义。
边界: This paper does not study additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it focuses on conventional wrought material, thus lacking direct evidence for AM post-processing effects.
动机· 提高钛合金零件加工效率和疲劳性能。
方法· 提出大切削深度缓进给砂带磨削-抛光混合工艺，优化表面/亚表面完整性。
结果· 疲劳寿命提升约5倍，归因于低粗糙度与梯度微结构的协同效应。
验证· 通过疲劳测试和微观表征验证了表面完整性的改善。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance both machining efficiency and fatigue performance of titanium alloy components.
Method· A large-depth-of-cut creep-feed belt grinding-polishing hybrid process was proposed to optimize surface/subsurface integrity.
Result· The fatigue life of Ti-6Al-4V was improved nearly fivefold, attributed to the synergistic effect of ultralow surface roughness and a gradient microstructure with refined grains and compressive residual stress.
Validation· Fatigue testing and microstructural characterization confirmed the improved surface integrity and the anti-fatigue mechanism.

Surface enhancement by micro-arc oxidation induced TiO2 ceramic coating on additive manufacturing Ti-6Al-4V

单位: Advanced Materials Additive Manufacturing Innovation Research Center, Hangzhou City University, Hangzhou 310015, China
通讯: Huan Qi @ Advanced Materials Additive Manufacturing Innovation Research Center, Hangzhou City University, Hangzhou 310015, China  |  Huiping Tang @ Advanced Materials Additive Manufacturing Innovation Research Center, Hangzhou City University, Hangzhou 310015, China
相关性: 该论文关注微弧氧化后处理对Ti-6Al-4V表面性能的影响，但未涉及疲劳或力学性能。
边界: No mechanical performance data (fatigue, tensile) was reported; only surface roughness and corrosion resistance were evaluated.
动机· 提高SLM/SEBM制备Ti-6Al-4V表面性能。
方法· 微弧氧化制备TiO2陶瓷涂层。
结果· 表面粗糙度和耐腐蚀性改善。
验证· 实验测量表面粗糙度和腐蚀电流密度。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the surface quality of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V via micro-arc oxidation.
Method· MAO treatment was applied to pre-polished SLM and SEBM samples.
Result· MAO reduced surface roughness for SEBM but increased for SLM, and improved corrosion resistance for both.
Validation· Surface profilometry and electrochemical corrosion tests.

Analysis of the Properties of Spark Plasma Sintered Ti6al4v Matrix Composites Enhanced with Nitride Nanoparticles

J. O. Abe, O. M. Popoola, A. P. I. Popoola Arid zone journal of engineering technology and environment/Arid Zone Journal of Engineering Technology and Environment 2025-12-01 10.63958/azojete/2025/21/04/009
单位: Centre for Energy and Electric Power, Department of Electrical Engineering, Tshwane University of Technology, P.M.B. X680, Pretoria, South Africa
通讯: J. O. Abe @ Centre for Energy and Electric Power, Department of Electrical Engineering, Tshwane University of Technology, P.M.B. X6
相关性: 该论文使用火花等离子烧结（SPS）而非增材制造技术，且研究的是氮化物纳米颗粒增强复合材料，并非LPBF/SLM/EBM/DED工艺下的纯Ti-6Al-4V力学与疲劳性能。
边界: This paper uses spark plasma sintering (SPS), not additive manufacturing processes (LPBF/SLM/EBM/DED), and focuses on Ti-6Al-4V matrix composites with nitride nanoparticles, reporting only microhardness and corrosion, not tensile/fatigue properties.
动机· 提高Ti6Al4V合金的硬度和耐磨性以拓展其在航空航天中的应用。
方法· 采用火花等离子烧结制备掺入h-BN、TiN和AlN纳米颗粒的Ti6Al4V基复合材料。
结果· h-BN增强复合材料硬度达740 HV，腐蚀性能改善；3 wt.% h-BN性能最优。
验证· 通过SEM、XRD、密度测量、显微硬度和电化学测试进行表征。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the hardness and wear resistance of Ti6Al4V alloy for broader aerospace applications.
Method· Ti6Al4V matrix composites with h-BN, TiN, and AlN nanoparticles (1,3,5 wt.%) were fabricated via spark plasma sintering.
Result· h-BN-reinforced composites achieved hardness up to 740 HV and improved corrosion resistance; 3 wt.% h-BN showed optimal overall performance.
Validation· Characterization by SEM, XRD, density, microindentation, and potentiodynamic polarization tests.

Biocompatibility evaluation of titanium nitride coating for canine patellar groove implant

Yangwon Chae, Euisin Yang, Taehwan Kim, Hyunmin Jo, Dong-Hyun Kim et al. Journal of Biomedical Translational Research 2025-12-01 10.12729/jbtr.2025.26.4.107
相关性: 该论文不涉及增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学或疲劳性能，而是评估涂层生物相容性，与用户方向无关。
边界: No investigation of mechanical or fatigue properties; focus is on biocompatibility and coating evaluation; material is not additively manufactured; no tensile, fatigue, or fracture data.
动机· 评估TiN涂层犬髌骨沟植入物的生物相容性与力学性能，但非AM力学/疲劳研究
方法· 注射成型结合电弧离子镀制备TiN涂层Ti-6Al-4V植入物，进行体外和体内评估
结果· TiN涂层与ADLC涂层性能相当，但制造成本更低
验证· 大鼠皮下模型和犬股骨模型的一年随访评估
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to evaluate the biocompatibility and mechanical properties of TiN-coated Ti-6Al-4V patellar groove implants for veterinary use.
Method· TiN coatings were applied via arc ion plating on injection-molded Ti-6Al-4V substrates; in vitro MTT, cell adhesion, corrosion, hardness, and in vivo rat and canine models were used.
Result· TiN-coated implants showed equivalent biocompatibility and slightly higher hardness compared to ADLC-coated implants, with lower manufacturing cost.
Validation· Validation involved rat subcutaneous implantation and one-year bilateral canine femoral implantation with imaging and histological assessments.
