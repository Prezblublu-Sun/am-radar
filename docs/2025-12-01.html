Performance of a cementless medial collared, triple-tapered femoral stem used with the posterior approach in total hip arthroplasty: a comparative analysis using medicare data.

James A Germano, Anshu Gupta, Biju Varughese, Jill Ruppenkamp, Rodrigo Diaz Expert review of medical devices 2025-12-01 10.1080/17434440.2025.2587302
单位: Orthopedic Surgery, Norwell Health Garden City, NY, USA.
通讯: Rodrigo Diaz @ Johnson & Johnson, 1 Johnson & Johnson Plaza New Brunswick, NJ, USA. [推断]
相关性: 该论文仅关注临床结果，未涉及生物力学分析或增材制造设计，与用户研究方向关联度低。
边界: No biomechanical modeling, no implant design optimization, no additive manufacturing, and no quantification of stress shielding or micromotion.
动机· 比较带内侧领的三锥度股骨柄与其它柄在后入路THA中的临床和经济效果
方法· 回顾性队列研究，利用Medicare数据，倾向评分分层平衡组别
结果· TTS柄3个月再手术率非劣效，24个月脱位率较低，但假体周围骨折率略高
验证· 基于美国Medicare数据库的大样本临床数据分析
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study compares clinical and economic outcomes of a cementless medial collared triple-tapered femoral stem versus other stems in posterior approach THA.
Method· A retrospective cohort study using US Medicare SAF data from 2016-2023, with propensity score fine stratification to balance cohorts.
Result· The TTS stem showed non-inferior 3-month reoperation rate, lower 24-month dislocation rate, but higher periprosthetic fracture rate compared to non-TTS stems.
Validation· Clinical validation using large-scale Medicare claims data with propensity score matching.

单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Inje University Haeundae Paik Hospital, Busan, Korea
通讯: Jae‐Young Lim @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Inje University Haeundae Paik Hospital, Busan, Korea
相关性: 该研究分析了影响无骨水泥股骨柄术后早期下沉的临床因素，但未涉及增材制造或有限元分析。
边界: No additive manufacturing, no finite element analysis, no implant design optimization, no surrogate modeling.

Motivation· To identify patient-, morphology-, and surgery-related determinants of early subsidence of cementless femoral stems.
Method· Retrospective review of 86 patients with standardized radiographic analysis and multivariate regression.
Result· Varus alignment and metaphyseal fill ratio were the strongest modifiable factors influencing early subsidence.
Validation· Clinical retrospective study with radiographic measurements at 3 and 6 months.

Design of Surgical Impaction Instruments Matters.

Peter J Schlieker, Frank Lampe, Johann Zwirner, Benjamin Ondruschka, Michael M Morlock et al. Arthroplasty today 2025-12-01 10.1016/j.artd.2025.101898
单位: Institute of Biomechanics, Hamburg University of Technology, Hamburg, Germany.
通讯: Gerd Huber @ Institute of Biomechanics, Hamburg University of Technology, Hamburg, Germany. [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究髋关节置换术中撞击器的力传递，与用户关注的假体柄设计或骨-假体界面力学无直接关系。
边界: Does not quantify stress shielding, micromotion, or implant-bone interface mechanics; focuses on surgical instrument design rather than implant or bone mechanics.
动机· 探究不同撞击器设计对力传递的影响及其与患者因素的关系
方法· 测量尸体实验中不同撞击器设计的力传递，建立现象学模型
结果· 撞击器设计主导力传递，传递率仅24%-47%，与患者和手术入路无关
验证· 4具尸体标本、2种手术入路验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how different impactor designs affect force transmission during femoral stem impaction and whether patient-specific factors interact.
Method· Measured mallet and impactor forces in cadaveric experiments with two surgical approaches and developed a phenomenological model for force transmission.
Result· Force transmission primarily depends on impactor design, with only 24%-47% of mallet peak force reaching the implant, independent of patient and surgical approach.
Validation· Validated with 4 cadavers and 2 different surgical approaches.

单位: Orthopaedic Surgery, Kawasaki Hospital, Kobe, JPN.; Orthopaedic Surgery, Nishinomiya Kaisei Hospital, Nishinomiya, JPN.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the accuracy of intraoperative femoral stem anteversion measurements using a mechanical alignment guide versus a CT-based navigation system.

Result· CT-based navigation provided significantly lower absolute discrepancy from postoperative values compared to the mechanical alignment guide.
Validation· Clinical validation with 83 primary THA and bipolar hemiarthroplasty patients.

通讯: Mackenzie C. Kelly @ Department of Orthopaedics and Rehabilitation, Oregon Health & Science University, Portland, Oregon
相关性: 该论文为临床注册研究，与用户的研究方向无直接关联。
边界: No biomechanical modeling, FEA, implant design optimization, or AM characterization; purely clinical registry analysis.
动机· 探讨股骨柄设计是否影响全髋置换术后翻修风险
方法· 分析美国关节置换注册中心（AJRR）数据
结果· 在65岁以上患者中未发现设计差异与翻修风险显著关联

English summary & terms
Motivation· To determine whether cemented femoral stem design influences the risk of revision after total hip arthroplasty in elderly patients.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 156,458 primary THA cases from the American Joint Replacement Registry (AJRR), stratifying stems into 18 design categories.
Result· No meaningful association between stem design and revision risk was found at 5 years for patients ≥65 years old.
Validation· Large-scale clinical registry analysis with multivariable adjustment and sensitivity analyses.

Can different stem design impact hospital healthcare resource utilization following total hip arthroplasty: a comparative analysis using US hospital billing database.

单位: Intermountain Health St George Regional Hospital, Saint George, UT, USA.
通讯: Rodrigo Diaz @ Johnson & Johnson, New Brunswick, NJ, USA. [推断]
相关性: 该论文仅涉及临床结局比较，与用户的研究方向（AI生物打印、髋关节植入物应力遮挡、FEA代理模型、生物医学增材制造）无关。
边界: This paper is a retrospective clinical database study comparing stem designs based on healthcare utilization and costs, with no biomechanical modeling, FEA, stress shielding analysis, AM, or AI/ML methods.
动机· 比较两种不同股骨柄设计在全髋置换术中的医疗资源利用和成本。
方法· 基于美国医院数据库的回顾性观察研究，使用倾向性评分平衡队列并比较结局。
结果· 三锥形柄组住院时间更短、再入院率更低、费用更低。
验证· 利用大型真实世界数据库进行统计比较。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate differences in healthcare resource utilization and hospital costs between two cementless femoral stem designs in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective observational analysis of the Premier Healthcare Database compared a medial collared triple tapered stem (TTS) to morphometric wedge (MMW) stems, using propensity score balancing and generalized linear models.
Result· The TTS cohort showed significantly shorter length of stay, lower 90- and 365-day readmission rates, fewer discharges to skilled nursing facilities, and lower total costs at 365 days.
Validation· The study relied on a large administrative database with propensity score matching to reduce selection bias, but no biomechanical or experimental validation was performed.
