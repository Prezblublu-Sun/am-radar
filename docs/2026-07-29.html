Selective laser melting of CoCrMo-Ti6Al4V bimetallic composites: fabrication, characterization, and cytocompatibility

SiJing Lin, Tiantian Gao, Sung‐Min Chung, In-Seop Lee, Cen Chen Biomedical Materials 2026-07-29 10.1088/1748-605x/ae9232
单位: Zhejiang Sci-Tech University
通讯: Cen Chen @ Zhejiang Sci-Tech University
相关性: 该论文研究SLM制备CoCrMo-Ti6Al4V双金属复合材料的工艺与力学性能，虽涉及Ti6Al4V，但核心是异种金属结合而非纯Ti6Al4V的机械/疲劳性能。
边界: This paper does not report tensile, fatigue, or S-N behavior of bulk Ti-6Al-4V; its focus is on bimetallic composite fabrication, hardness/wear, and cytocompatibility, with no fatigue or monotonic mechanical characterization relevant to the Ti-6Al-4V AM fatigue direction.
动机· CoCrMo与Ti6Al4V异种金属直接SLM成形困难，需解决界面开裂和残余应力问题。
方法· 采用松散CoCrMo过渡层和外部支撑结构实现SLM双金属复合材料的无缺陷成形。
结果· 三层结构消除了宏观缺陷，硬度和耐磨性接近纯CoCrMo，界面结合良好且细胞相容性优异。
验证· 通过硬度、滑动磨损、断口分析及骨髓间充质干细胞培养进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study aims to overcome metallurgical incompatibility and residual stress issues that prevent direct selective laser melting of CoCrMo-Ti6Al4V bimetallic structures for orthopedic implants.
Method· A loose CoCrMo transition layer and optimized external supports were used as mechanical buffers to accommodate residual stresses and suppress geometric distortion during SLM fabrication.
Result· The three-layer architecture eliminated macro-defects, achieved hardness and wear resistance comparable to monolithic CoCrMo, and showed cohesive interfacial failure with excellent in vitro cytocompatibility.
Validation· Fabricated samples were validated through hardness testing, sliding wear tests, fracture analysis, and BMSC cell culture assessments.

Additive manufacturing of functionally graded thickness in TPMS lattice structures with optimised mechanical strength

Kaitlyn Hartman, Sy Nguyen-Van, Jacklyn Griffis, Avez Shaikh, Lan-Hsuan Huang et al. Virtual and Physical Prototyping 2026-07-29 10.1080/17452759.2026.2686063
单位: Pennsylvania State University
相关性: 该论文虽未直接研究股骨柄，但其Ti-6Al-4V梯度TPMS点阵的力学优化方法可迁移至多孔股骨柄的结构设计。
边界: The paper does not study a femoral/hip stem or implant structure; it focuses on generic TPMS lattice beams, lacking stem-specific geometry, stress shielding, or ISO fatigue assessment.
动机· 建立梯度优化方法，设计兼具强度与质量效率的TPMS点阵结构。
方法· 采用基于梯度的优化结合增材制造约束，通过L-PBF制备Ti-6Al-4V点阵梁，并开展弯曲测试、DIC与FEA分析。
结果· 梯度梁应力分布更均匀，平均承载能力比均匀厚度梁提高35%。
验证· 通过弯曲实验、数字图像相关和有限元分析进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To establish a gradient-based optimisation method for designing TPMS lattice structures with functionally graded thickness, aiming to improve mechanical strength while managing mass constraints.
Method· Gradient-based optimisation combined with AM constraints was used to design Ti-6Al-4V TPMS lattice beams, which were fabricated via laser powder bed fusion and evaluated through flexural testing with digital image correlation and finite element analysis.
Result· The graded beams redistributed high-stress regions to achieve a more uniform strain distribution and withstood on average 35% higher loads compared to uniform-thickness beams.
Validation· The findings were validated experimentally via flexural testing with digital image correlation, supported by finite element analysis.

Comparative study of the influence of tool diameter on axial tool wear, cavity formation, and surface integrity in electro discharge drilling of Ti–6Al–4V

Saurabh Jain, Sudhanshu Kumar, M. K. Pradhan Proceedings of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers Part E Journal of Process Mechanical Engineering 2026-07-29 10.1177/09544089261472574
单位: MANIT)
通讯: Saurabh Jain @ MANIT)
相关性: 该论文研究电火花钻孔对Ti-6Al-4V的加工性能，但未涉及增材制造或后处理对疲劳/力学性能的影响，与该方向不直接相关。
边界: This paper concerns electro discharge drilling of conventional Ti-6Al-4V, not additive manufacturing, and it does not evaluate post-processing effects on fatigue or mechanical performance.
动机· 研究电火花钻孔中电极直径对加工效率和表面质量的影响。
方法· 采用0.3、0.5、0.7mm铜电极，通过单因素实验评估放电参数对MRR、磨损和表面粗糙度的影响。
结果· 0.7mm电极产生更深孔腔、更小磨损和更好表面，而0.3mm电极在部分参数下MRR最高。
验证· 使用光学轮廓仪和扫描电镜观察表面缺陷与显微裂纹。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the influence of tool diameter on machining efficiency and surface quality in electro discharge drilling of Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Tubular copper electrodes of 0.3, 0.5, and 0.7 mm diameter were used with a single-factor method to evaluate the effects of discharge parameters on MRR, tool wear, and surface roughness.
Result· The 0.7 mm electrode produced deeper cavities (8.5–9.0 mm), lower axial tool wear, and superior surface integrity, while the 0.3 mm electrode achieved higher MRR under certain current and pulse-off time conditions.
Validation· Surface integrity and defect formation were characterized using optical profilometry and scanning electron microscopy.
