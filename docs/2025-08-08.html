Evaluation of a Method for Determining Material Strength Based on Hardness Measurements: A Case Study of the Ti6Al4V Alloy

Karolina Karolewska, Mateusz Wirwicki, Bogdan Ligaj Materials 2025-08-08 10.3390/ma18163726
单位: Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, al. PBS Bydgoszcz University of Science and Technology, Kaliskiego 7, 85-796 Bydgoszcz, Poland
通讯: Karolina Karolewska @ Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, al. PBS Bydgoszcz University of Science and Technology, Kaliskiego 7, 85-796 Bydgoszc
相关性: 该论文研究了热处理对Ti6Al4V合金拉伸强度与硬度关系的影响，为后处理控制提供了间接评估方法。
边界: 论文主要关注硬度估算拉伸强度的方法，而非直接揭示后处理对疲劳或力学性能的调控机理。
动机· 评估通过硬度测量间接估算Ti6Al4V合金拉伸强度的可行性。
方法· 对增材制造和传统方法制备的Ti6Al4V样品进行拉伸试验和HV硬度测量。
结果· 硬度与拉伸强度总体相关，但热处理后样品呈现非线性关系，增材制造样品强度最高。
验证· 通过对比不同工艺状态下硬度与拉伸数据的实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study evaluates the feasibility of indirectly estimating the tensile strength of Ti6Al4V alloy from hardness measurements across different manufacturing routes and post-processing conditions.
Method· Static tensile tests and Vickers hardness measurements were performed on Ti6Al4V samples fabricated by additive manufacturing and conventional methods, including as-built and heat-treated conditions.
Result· Hardness generally correlated with tensile strength, but a nonlinear relationship was observed in heat-treated specimens; as-built samples exhibited the highest tensile strength.
Validation· Experimental validation was achieved by comparing hardness data with tensile test results from multiple material conditions.

Retrofitting of a High-Performance Aerospace Component via Topology Optimization and Additive Manufacturing

Jorge Crespo-Sánchez, Claudia Solek, Sergio Fuentes del Toro, Ana María Camacho, Álvaro Rodríguez-Prieto Machines 2025-08-08 10.3390/machines13080700
单位: Department of Manufacturing Engineering, Universidad Nacional de Educación a Distancia (UNED), C. Juan del Rosal, 12, 28040 Madrid, Spain
通讯: Jorge Crespo-Sánchez @ Department of Manufacturing Engineering, Universidad Nacional de Educación a Distancia (UNED), C. Juan del Rosal, 12, 28
相关性: 该论文聚焦于拓扑优化与增材制造结合的航空部件轻量化设计，虽然使用了Ti6Al4V，但并未深入探讨其力学性能或疲劳特性，因此与用户关注的Ti-6Al-4V基础力学与疲劳研究方向关联较弱。
边界: The paper focuses on a design methodology (topology optimization) and component-level simulation rather than on mechanical property characterization or fatigue performance of Ti-6Al-4V; no experimental mechanical testing or fatigue data are provided.
动机· 通过拓扑优化和增材制造实现航空部件轻量化和降成本。
方法· 先设计最优几何再评估材料的'设计优先'策略，结合SLM和FE仿真。
结果· 在Ti6Al4V和Al6061中实现71%减重，结构完整性保持。
验证· 有限元仿真模拟鸟撞等工况。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Propose a design-first, material-second methodology combining topology optimization and additive manufacturing to lightweight and reduce cost of high-performance aerospace components.
Method· The optimal geometry is determined using an artificially stiff material, then real materials (Al 6061, Ti6Al4V, PEEK) are evaluated via FE simulations for structural and manufacturing feasibility; SLM is chosen for metallic options.
Result· Weight reduction of up to 71% is achieved for Ti6Al4V and Al 6061 while preserving critical functional regions and maintaining structural integrity under flight loads including bird-strike.
Validation· Finite element simulations of the optimized geometries under diverse loading conditions, including bird-strike.
