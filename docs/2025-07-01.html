Result· Fatigue limit increased by 15.2% due to a ~100 μm deep compressive residual stress layer and microstructural refinement.
Validation· Experimental fatigue tests and microstructural analysis (EBSD, TEM) confirmed the improvements.

Defect-organized microstructure-stress synergy in laser in-situ forging additive manufacturing (LIF-AM): A pathway to fatigue-resistant Ti-6Al-4V

单位: Institute of Aeronautics Engine, School of Mechanical Engineering, Xi’an Jiaotong University, Xi’an, Shaanxi 710049, China
通讯: Liucheng Zhou @ Institute of Aeronautics Engine, School of Mechanical Engineering, Xi’an Jiaotong University, Xi’an, Shaanxi 710049, Chi  |  Yinghong Li @ National Key Laboratory of Aerospace Power System and Plasma Technology, Air Force Engineering University, Xi’an 710038,
相关性: 该论文提出了一种原位激光冲击锻造增材制造技术，属于制造过程改进而非后处理，因此与后处理对疲劳性能的研究方向关联较弱。
边界: The paper proposes an in-situ laser shock peening during LPBF, not a post-processing treatment applied after the build; thus it does not address post-processing control as defined in Layer 2.
动机· 提高LPBF成形Ti-6Al-4V的疲劳性能，解决组织应力和缺陷问题。
方法· 提出激光原位冲击锻造(LIF-AM)技术，在LPBF过程中逐层施加飞秒激光冲击。
结果· LIF-AM使缺陷尺寸从65μm降至27μm，β柱状晶转变为等轴晶，引入800μm深残余压应力场，疲劳极限提高19.3%。
验证· 通过疲劳测试、显微组织和残余应力表征验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the fatigue endurance of LPBF-processed Ti-6Al-4V by mitigating heterogeneous tensile stresses, coarse grains, and fusion defects.
Method· A laser in-situ forging additive manufacturing (LIF-AM) technique is developed, applying layer-by-layer femtosecond laser shock during the LPBF process.
Result· LIF-AM reduces maximum defect size from 65 µm to 27 µm, transforms β columnar grains into equiaxed grains, introduces a compressive residual stress field ~800 µm deep, and increases the fatigue limit by 19.3%.
Validation· Fatigue testing, microstructural analysis, and residual stress measurements confirm the improvements.
