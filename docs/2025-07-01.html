单位: George W. Woodruff School of Mechanical Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology, 801 Ferst Drive, Atlanta, 30332, GA, USA
通讯: Wei Huang @ George W. Woodruff School of Mechanical Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology, 801 Ferst Drive, Atlanta, 30332, G
相关性: 该论文针对Ti-6Al-4V合金建立了晶粒尺寸分析模型，与力学性能间接相关，可用于工艺-结构-性能预测。
边界: The paper focuses on grain size prediction modeling rather than direct mechanical or fatigue characterization; no tensile or fatigue data are reported.
动机· 增材制造Ti-6Al-4V晶粒尺寸的精确预测模型缺乏，尤其是考虑热-力耦合及织构的物理模型。

验证· 与文献中Ti-6Al-4V实验晶粒尺寸数据对比验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Accurate physics-based grain size prediction models for additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V are lacking, especially those accounting for thermal-mechanical coupling and texture.
Method· An analytical grain size model integrating thermal analysis, JMAK kinetics, columnar-to-equiaxed transition, and visco-plastic self-consistent (VPSC) homogenization is developed and applied to Ti-6Al-4V.
Result· The predicted grain sizes agree with experimental data with a mean accuracy of 93.03%, reaching 97.01% at 600 mm/s scanning speed, and outperform prior analytical models by up to 34.6%.
Validation· The model is validated against experimental grain size measurements reported in the literature for Ti-6Al-4V fabricated by additive manufacturing.
