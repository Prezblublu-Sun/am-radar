Topological Optimization of the Femoral Stem

José L. Serna-Landivar, Madelaine Violeta Risco Sernaqué, Daniela Milagros Anticona-Valderrama, Marleni Mendoza Damas, Maria Ysabel Garcia-Alvarez et al. International Journal of Online and Biomedical Engineering (iJOE) 2026-01-22 10.3991/ijoe.v22i01.58385
单位: Universidad Científica del Sur, Lima, Peru
通讯: José L. Serna-Landivar @ Universidad Científica del Sur, Lima, Peru
相关性: 该论文直接针对Ti-6Al-4V股骨柄进行拓扑优化设计，符合方向需求。
边界: No experimental validation (mechanical testing) was performed; only finite element simulations were used.
动机· 通过拓扑优化减轻股骨柄重量，提高力学兼容性。
方法· 在SolidWorks建模，ANSYS有限元分析，按ASTM F2996-20评估不同质量保留率的设计。
结果· Ti-6Al-4V 25%质量保留率设计减重27.9%，安全系数高。
验证· 仅通过有限元模拟验证，无实验测试。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To reduce weight and improve mechanical compatibility of femoral stems through topological optimization.
Method· 3D models were built in SolidWorks and analyzed via finite element simulations in ANSYS following ASTM F2996-20 for six configurations varying material (Ti-6Al-4V vs CoCr) and retained mass percentage (25%, 35%, 45%).
Result· The Ti-6Al-4V configuration with 25% retained mass achieved a 27.9% weight reduction while maintaining high safety factors.
Validation· The study relied solely on finite element simulations without experimental mechanical testing.

Design and validation of Offset Split-P porous titanium implants for mandibular defect repair

Yi Lu, Zhe Chen, Qiulong Chen, Bin Wu, Bin Yan et al. Materials & Design 2026-01-22 10.1016/j.matdes.2026.115533
单位: College of Mechanical and Electronic Engineering, Nanjing Forestry University, Nanjing 210037, China
通讯: Bin Wu @ College of Mechanical and Electronic Engineering, Nanjing Forestry University, Nanjing 210037, China  |  Bin Yan @ Department of Orthodontics, The Affiliated Stomatological Hospital of Nanjing Medical University, Nanjing 210029, China
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V增材制造多孔结构，但应用于下颌骨修复而非股骨柄，因此与用户关注的髋关节假体结构设计方向相关性较弱。
边界: The paper focuses on mandibular implant design and validation, not on femoral/hip stem structural design, stress shielding, or stem fatigue testing as required by the direction.
动机· 为修复下颌骨缺损开发力学与生物学性能匹配的钛合金多孔植入体。
方法· 采用偏移优化和梯度设计的Split-P三周期极小曲面晶格结构，结合仿真与实验评估。
结果· 小尺度Offset Split-P结构在60-90%孔隙率下弹性模量与下颌骨匹配，并具有良好的生物相容性和耐腐蚀性。
验证· 通过压缩力学测试、细胞培养和腐蚀浸泡实验验证了结构性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a porous titanium implant with tailored mechanical and biological properties for mandibular defect repair.
Method· An offset-optimized and gradient-designed Split-P triply periodic minimal surface lattice was evaluated via mechanical simulations, fluid dynamics, compression testing, in vitro cell culture, and corrosion immersion.
Result· The small-scale Offset Split-P structures (porosity 60-90%) achieved elastic modulus matching mandibular bone (3.83-18.89 GPa), yield strength of 28.51-171.90 MPa, and demonstrated good biocompatibility and corrosion resistance.
Validation· Validation included compression mechanical testing, in vitro cell proliferation and differentiation assays, and corrosion immersion experiments.

Effect of V Content on Microstructure and Properties of TiNbZrVx Medium-Entropy Alloy Coatings on TC4 Substrate by Laser Cladding

Wen Zhang, Ying Wu, Chuan Yang, Yongsheng Zhao, Zhenhong Wang et al. Coatings 2026-01-22 10.3390/coatings16010141
单位: Key Laboratory of Advanced Technologies of Materials, Ministry of Education, School of Materials Science and Engineering, Southwest Jiaotong University, Chengdu 610031, China
通讯: Wen Zhang @ Key Laboratory of Advanced Technologies of Materials, Ministry of Education, School of Materials Science and Engineering
相关性: 论文研究的是激光熔覆TiNbZrVx涂层在TC4基体上的性能，而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V本身的力学与疲劳性能。
边界: The paper does not study the mechanical/fatigue performance of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; instead it focuses on a laser-cladded TiNbZrVx coating on a TC4 substrate, which is a different alloy and process.
动机· 为了提高钛合金的耐磨性，应用于高速列车制动盘。
方法· 在TC4基体上激光熔覆制备不同V含量的TiNbZrVx涂层。
结果· V含量增加提高了硬度和耐磨性，但降低了延展性，断裂模式由韧性变为脆性。
验证· 通过微观结构、硬度、拉伸和磨损测试验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the wear resistance of titanium alloys for application in high-speed train brake discs.
Method· Laser cladding of TiNbZrVx medium-entropy alloy coatings with varying V content on TC4 substrates.
Result· Increasing V content enhanced hardness and wear resistance but reduced ductility, changing fracture from ductile to brittle.
Validation· Validated through microstructural analysis, hardness, tensile, and wear tests.

Enhanced Bioactivity and Antibacterial Properties of Ti-6Al-4V Alloy Surfaces Modified by Electrical Discharge Machining

Bárbara Alyne Barbaro Dos Santos, Rafael E. G. Leal, Ana P. G. Gomes, Liszt Y. C. Madruga, Ketul C. Popat et al. Colloids and Interfaces 2026-01-22 10.3390/colloids10010012
单位: Department of Materials Engineering, Federal Center for Technological Education of Minas Gerais (CEFET-MG), Belo Horizonte 30421-169, Brazil
通讯: Roberta M. Sabino @ Department of Chemical and Biomedical Engineering, University of Wyoming (UW), Laramie, WY 82071, USA
相关性: 该论文专注于Ti-6Al-4V表面的生物活性和抗菌性能，而非力学性能，与用户的后处理对疲劳/力学性能的影响方向无关。
边界: No mechanical performance (fatigue/tensile) data; focuses on bioactivity and antibacterial properties, not on post-processing effects on mechanical properties.
动机· 提高Ti-6Al-4V植入物的生物活性和抗菌性能
方法· 使用电火花加工（EDM）在不同介电液中处理Ti-6Al-4V表面
结果· 含钙磷介电液处理表面促进矿化并显著减少细菌附着
验证· 在模拟体液中浸泡和金黄色葡萄球菌培养验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To simultaneously improve bioactivity and antibacterial properties of Ti-6Al-4V implant surfaces.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V ELI surfaces were modified by electrical discharge machining using two dielectric fluids with different ion compositions and polarities.
Result· The Ca/P-containing dielectric under positive polarity produced surfaces with favorable Ca/P ratios and over 80–90% reduction in Staphylococcus aureus attachment.
Validation· Bioactivity was evaluated by immersion in simulated body fluid for 21 days, and antibacterial performance was assessed against Staphylococcus aureus.
