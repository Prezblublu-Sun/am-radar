Additively Produced Ti-6Al-4V Osteosynthesis Devices Meet the Requirements for Tensile Strength and Fatigue

Alisdair MacLeod, Matthew Bishop, Antonio Longo, Alborz Shokrani, Chris Bowen et al. Journal of Manufacturing and Materials Processing 2025-07-03 10.3390/jmmp9070227
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Bath, Bath BA2 7AY, UK
通讯: H.S. Gill @ CBio, University of Bath, Bath BA2 7AY, UK
相关性: 该论文直接研究了热处理和抛光后处理对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V疲劳性能的影响，与您的后处理控制方向高度相关。
边界: 论文仅涉及热处理和抛光两种后处理，未涵盖其他如HIP或喷丸等后处理工艺。
动机· 评估增材制造Ti-6Al-4V接骨板是否满足生理载荷下的力学要求。
方法· 对28例患者个性化接骨板进行有限元分析，并对热处理和抛光后的试样进行拉伸和疲劳测试。
结果· 热处理试样疲劳极限227 MPa，抛光后提升至286 MPa，均高于预测的生理应力。
验证· 通过ISO标准拉伸和疲劳测试验证力学性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To determine whether additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V osteosynthesis plates can withstand physiological loading after post-processing.
Method· Finite element models of patient-specific plates were used to predict peak stresses, and tensile and fatigue tests were conducted on heat-treated and polished specimens.
Result· The fatigue limit increased from 227 MPa (heat-treated only) to 286 MPa (heat-treated and polished), both exceeding the predicted physiological stress of 166 MPa.
Validation· Mechanical validation was performed using ISO-compliant tensile and fatigue testing.

Development of tribo-efficient novel nanostructured silica coatings on additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V via PMMA degradation

Babra Abbas, Hui-Ci Weng, Syed Junaid Nawaz, Nitya Nand Gosvami, E‐Wen Huang et al. Tribology International 2025-07-03 10.1016/j.triboint.2025.110962
单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi, 110016, India
通讯: Jayant Jain @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi, 110016, India
相关性: 该论文关注增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的涂层，而非其基体力学或疲劳性能，与方向焦点无关。
边界: Paper focuses on tribological coating development and wear performance, not on mechanical or fatigue properties of base Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 开发适用于增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的耐磨纳米结构二氧化硅涂层
方法· 通过PMMA降解在AM Ti-6Al-4V上制备二氧化硅涂层
结果· 涂层显著提高了表面的摩擦学性能
验证· 通过摩擦磨损实验进行验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a tribo-efficient novel nanostructured silica coating for additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V substrates.
Method· Silica coatings were synthesized on LPBF Ti-6Al-4V via thermal degradation of PMMA templates.
Result· The coated surfaces exhibited improved wear resistance and reduced friction coefficients compared to uncoated samples.
Validation· Tribological performance was validated through pin-on-disc wear tests and surface characterization.

Effect of 3 wt% Cu on the Microstructure and Hardness of a Ti-10Ta-1.6Zr Alloy

Nobom Gretta Hashe, Lee Fowler, Susanne Norgren, Lesley Cornish, Lesley H. Chown et al. Materials 2025-07-03 10.3390/ma18133163
单位: Department of Physics, Nelson Mandela University (NMU), Port Elizabeth 6031, South Africa
通讯: Nobom Gretta Hashe @ Department of Physics, Nelson Mandela University (NMU), Port Elizabeth 6031, South Africa  |  Nomsombuluko Hadebe @ Mintek, Advanced Materials Division, Randburg 2194, South Africa
相关性: 该论文研究的Ti-10Ta-1.6Zr合金并非Ti-6Al-4V，且采用电弧熔炼非增材制造，与用户关注的增材制造Ti-6Al-4V力学疲劳性能无关。
边界: This paper does not study Ti-6Al-4V, does not use additive manufacturing, and only evaluates hardness (not tensile/fatigue properties) so it is outside the L1 scope.
动机· 开发替代Ti-6Al-4V的生物相容合金
方法· 电弧熔炼制备Ti-10Ta-1.6Zr加Cu，热处理后表征
结果· 含Cu合金硬度更高但标准差大
验证· 实验表征（SEM, XRD, 硬度测试）
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a biocompatible alloy to replace Ti-6Al-4V
Method· Ti-10Ta-1.6Zr alloys with and without Cu were arc-melted, heat-treated, and characterized by SEM, TKD, XRD
Result· The Cu-containing alloy exhibited higher hardness with large standard deviation due to martensitic transformation
Validation· Experimental characterization including hardness testing
