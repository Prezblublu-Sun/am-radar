Microstructural evolution and mechanical properties of laser-powder bed fusion-fabricated Ti-10Ta-2Nb-2Zr alloy as a potential orthopedic implant material

Igor Polozov, Victoria Nefyodova, А. И. Золотарев, Anatoly Popovich Materials Science in Additive Manufacturing 2025-08-12 10.36922/msam025220044
相关性: 该论文研究的是Ti-10Ta-2Nb-2Zr合金而非Ti-6Al-4V，且未涉及股骨柄设计或应力屏蔽分析，与本方向要求不符。
边界: The paper does not address Ti-6Al-4V femoral stem design, stress shielding, stem fatigue, or any stem-specific structural analysis; it is a material study on an alternative alloy with TPMS porous structures.
动机· 开发低弹性模量的新型钛合金以替代Ti-6Al-4V用于骨科植入物
方法· 通过L-PBF制备Ti-10Ta-2Nb-2Zr合金，进行热处理并设计TPMS多孔结构
结果· 致密样品抗拉强度641.2 MPa，弹性模量89 GPa；多孔结构弹性模量降至9.2-9.7 GPa
验证· 采用拉伸试验和压缩试验验证力学性能
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a new titanium alloy with lower elastic modulus as an alternative to Ti-6Al-4V for orthopedic implants.
Method· Ti-10Ta-2Nb-2Zr alloy was fabricated by L-PBF with varying parameters, followed by heat treatment and fabrication of TPMS porous structures.
Result· Solid samples achieved yield strength of 551.8 MPa, ultimate tensile strength of 641.2 MPa, elongation of 19.0%, and elastic modulus of 89 GPa; TPMS porous structures exhibited modulus as low as 9.2 GPa.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through tensile and compression testing of solid and porous specimens.

Effect of Graphene Oxide-Based Nanodielectric on ElectricalDischarge Machining Performance of Ti-6Al‑4 V

Zheng Yang (264955), Zihan Wang (3129810), Jinjin Li (849002), Songlin Ding (19018799), Shuang Yi (8698824) Figshare 2025-08-12 10.1021/acs.langmuir.5c01811.s001
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的电火花加工，但未研究任何后处理工艺（如热处理或热等静压）对机械性能的影响。
边界: The paper focuses on electrical discharge machining (EDM) process modification with graphene oxide, not on any post-processing treatment (e.g., heat treatment, HIP, shot peening) that modifies fatigue or mechanical performance of AM Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 探究氧化石墨烯纳米电介质对Ti-6Al-4V电火花加工性能的影响
方法· 在电火花加工介质中添加氧化石墨烯纳米片，使用铜电极加工Ti-6Al-4V合金
结果· 添加0.2 wt% GO使材料去除率提高79.3%，电极损耗降低41.3%，表面粗糙度改善38.5%
验证· 通过SEM表面形貌、振动分析和波形评估验证加工效果
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of graphene oxide nanodielectric on the electrical discharge machining performance of Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· Graphene oxide nanosheets were dispersed into the dielectric fluid during EDM of Ti-6Al-4V using copper electrodes.
Result· Adding 0.2 wt% GO increased material removal rate by 79.3%, reduced electrode wear ratio by 41.3%, and improved surface roughness by 38.5%.
Validation· Validation through SEM surface morphology, vibration analysis, and waveform evaluation.
