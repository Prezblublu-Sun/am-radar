Accelerating the Design of Resorbable Magnesium Alloys: A Machine Learning Approach to Property Prediction

Vickey Nandal, Vít Beneš, Pavel Baláž, Jiří Ryjáček, Karel Tesař arXiv preprint 2026-04-21 10.1016/j.matdes.2026.116060
相关性: 该研究聚焦于可降解镁合金的力学性能预测，与用户关注的生物打印、髋植入物应力遮挡或FEA替代模型方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address additive manufacturing process-property relationships, hip implant stress shielding or micromotion, nor FEA surrogate modeling for biomechanics.
动机· 为加速可降解镁合金植入物的设计，需要高效预测其力学性能。
方法· 基于410个样本数据，训练六种ML模型（CatBoost最优）预测屈服强度、抗拉强度和延伸率。
结果· CatBoost模型R²达0.9以上，SHAP分析揭示加工条件和合金元素（Zn, Mn, Gd）是关键因素。
验证· 通过实验数据集验证模型的鲁棒性和泛化能力。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To accelerate the design of resorbable magnesium alloys for medical implants, a data-driven framework is developed to predict their mechanical properties.
Method· Six machine learning models, including ensemble methods, are trained on a dataset of 410 samples to predict yield strength, ultimate tensile strength, and elongation, with CatBoost achieving the highest accuracy.
Result· The CatBoost model attains R² values above 0.9, and SHAP analysis identifies thermomechanical processing and alloying elements (Zn, Mn, Gd) as primary influencers.
Validation· The models are validated on an experimental dataset, confirming robustness and generalization in capturing process-property relationships.

Initial stability and bone response at 6 months post-operation in the Avenir complete stem: a comparative study by femoral canal shape.

Musashi Ima, Tamon Kabata, Daisuke Inoue, Yu Yanagi, Takahiro Iyobe et al. Hip international : the journal of clinical and experimental research on hip pathology and therapy 2026-04-21 10.1177/11207000251410505
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Graduate School of Medical Science, Kanazawa University, Kanazawa, Ishikawa, Japan.
通讯: Satoru Demura @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Graduate School of Medical Science, Kanazawa University, Kanazawa, Ishikawa, Japan. [推断]
相关性: 该论文为髋关节假体临床追踪研究，未涉及力学建模或增材制造，与用户研究方向关联度低。
边界: No biomechanical modeling (FEA/surrogate), no stress shielding/micromotion quantification, no additive manufacturing or AI/ML.
动机· 比较不同股骨管形态下Avenir完全涂层柄的术后初始固定和6个月骨反应。
方法· 回顾性分析95例THA患者，按Dorr分型分组，测量放射学参数。
结果· Dorr C型髓腔占位率最高，骨接触率高；Dorr A型有远端固定风险。
验证· 基于术后X线片的临床观察验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare initial postoperative fixation and 6-month bone response of a fully coated femoral stem across different femoral canal morphologies.
Method· A retrospective study of 95 hips classified by Dorr types A/B/C from radiographs, measuring canal axis alignment, medullary occupancy, and bone contact rates.
Result· Dorr type C showed highest medullary occupancy and bone contact in specific zones; Dorr type A lacked spot welds in proximal zones, indicating potential distal fixation.
Validation· Clinical validation via postoperative and 6-month radiographic follow-up.

Organosilane-functionalized hydrothermal-derived coatings on titanium alloys for hydrophobization and corrosion protection

S. Rahimipour, B. Rafiei, E. Salahinejad arXiv preprint 2026-04-21 10.1016/j.porgcoat.2020.105594
相关性: 该论文关注钛合金表面疏水处理，与用户的后处理及热等静压研究方向不直接相关。
边界: This paper focuses on surface wettability and corrosion protection via chemical coatings, not on hot isostatic pressing or structural post-processing of Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 改善钛合金表面疏水性和耐腐蚀性
方法· 采用碱热处理和有机硅烷修饰
结果· 接触角达147°，耐蚀性提高
验证· 接触角测量和电化学测试
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve hydrophobicity and corrosion resistance of Ti-6Al-4V alloy surfaces.
Method· Alkaline hydrothermal roughening followed by hexadecyltrimethoxysilane coating.
Result· Water contact angle up to 147° and enhanced corrosion protection.
Validation· Contact angle measurements, potentiodynamic polarization, and electrochemical impedance spectroscopy.
