Effect of shape on void growth: A coupled Extended Finite Element Method (XFEM) and Discrete Dislocation Plasticity (DDP) study

Muhammad Usman, Sana Waheed, Aamir Mubashar arXiv preprint 2025-11-10 10.1016/j.euromechsol.2021.104471
相关性: 该论文研究微观孔洞生长机制，与用户关注的增材制造植入体力学性能无直接关联。
边界: No additive manufacturing context, no biomedical implant application, no experimental mechanical testing on AM materials.
动机· 研究孔洞形状对孔洞生长机制的影响
方法· 采用扩展有限元法(XFEM)与离散位错塑性(DDP)耦合模拟
结果· 椭圆孔洞表面面积越大生长越快，圆孔洞生长速率最小但硬化效应最大
验证· 数值模拟与理论对比
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of void shape on the micro-mechanism of void growth in ductile fracture.
Method· A coupled Extended Finite Element Method (XFEM) and Discrete Dislocation Plasticity (DDP) approach is used to simulate void growth under different loading conditions.
Result· Voids with larger surface area exhibit faster growth rates, while circular voids show minimum growth but maximum strain hardening.
Validation· Numerical simulation results are compared with theoretical expectations and DDP benchmark.

Radiographic Evaluation of Femoral Offset Reconstruction in Hip Arthroplasty: A Retrospective Analysis.

Weixuan Zheng, Chengsheng Wu, Hongmei Xu Annali italiani di chirurgia 2025-11-10 10.62713/aic.4334
单位: Department of Radiology, Wuyi County Traditional Chinese Medicine Hospital, 321200 Jinhua, Zhejiang, China.
通讯: Hongmei Xu @ B-ultrasound Room, Wuyi County Traditional Chinese Medicine Hospital, 321200 Jinhua, Zhejiang, China. [推断]
相关性: 该论文为临床回顾性研究，未涉及用户关注的生物力学建模、有限元分析、增材制造或人工智能方法。
边界: No computational modeling, no FEA, no implant design, no AM, no AI; purely clinical retrospective analysis.
动机· 探讨股骨偏距重建对THA术后功能及并发症的影响。
方法· 回顾性分析146例THA患者，按术后FO差异分组比较。
结果· 重建组术后功能更好，并发症更低。
验证· 临床评分和影像学参数对比验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the impact of femoral offset reconstruction on functional recovery and complication rates after THA.
Method· Retrospective analysis of 146 patients grouped by postoperative FO difference (≤4mm vs >4mm).
Result· Better functional outcomes and lower complication rates in the reconstruction group.
Validation· Clinical scores (HHS, VAS) and radiographic parameters compared between groups.
