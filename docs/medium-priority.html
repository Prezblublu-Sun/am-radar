English summary & terms

English summary & terms
Motivation· To report medium-term clinical outcomes of a modular, cementless, hydroxyapatite-coated anatomical femoral stem in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A prospective study of 185 hips with a median follow-up of 7.8 years, assessing pain, range of motion, and walking ability using Merle d'Aubigne and Postel scores.
Result· The stem achieved excellent clinical outcomes with low rates of aseptic loosening and revision.
Validation· Multi-patient clinical follow-up with no patients lost to follow-up.
