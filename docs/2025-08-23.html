Effects of laser beam shaping on Ti-6Al-4V single tracks in PBF-LB/M: A study with Aconity MIDI+

Abid Ullah, Ahmad Reshad Bakhtarı, Alexander E. Medvedev, Andrey Molotnikov, Dirk Herzog et al. Optics & Laser Technology 2025-08-23 10.1016/j.optlastec.2025.113762
单位: Centre for Additive Manufacturing, School of Engineering, RMIT University, Melbourne, VIC 3000, Australia
通讯: Abid Ullah @ Centre for Additive Manufacturing, School of Engineering, RMIT University, Melbourne, VIC 3000, Australia  |  Milan Brandt @ Centre for Additive Manufacturing, School of Engineering, RMIT University, Melbourne, VIC 3000, Australia
相关性: 该论文研究Ti-6Al-4V的激光粉末床熔融单道成形工艺，虽不直接提供力学或疲劳数据，但工艺优化结果可间接影响后续力学性能。
边界: The paper does not report any mechanical or fatigue properties; it is solely a process study on single-track morphology and defect control.
动机· 研究激光束形状对Ti-6Al-4V单道成形的影响，为工艺优化提供依据。
方法· 使用Aconity MIDI+系统，对比高斯、环形光束在三种层厚和能量密度下的单道形貌与熔池行为。
结果· 环形光束产生更宽更平滑的熔道，减少飞溅和球化；高斯光束熔池更深，易形成锁孔。
验证· 通过实验和有限元模拟验证，熔池尺寸预测误差小于5%。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the effects of laser beam shape on single-track formation in PBF-LB/M of Ti-6Al-4V to improve process control.
Method· Gaussian, ring-core, and ring beam profiles were compared at layer thicknesses of 30, 60, and 90 μm and linear energy inputs up to 1.0 J/mm using an Aconity MIDI+ system.
Result· Ring-shaped beams produced wider and smoother tracks with reduced spattering and balling, while Gaussian beams caused deeper melt pools and keyholing at high energies.
Validation· Experimental measurements and FEM simulations confirmed the trends with melt pool geometry errors below 5%.

Energy- and material-efficient Ti-6Al-4V sheet part fabrication by the novel TISTRAQ-process, including resistance heating and tool-based quenching: Insights into test stand design and material potential

Nina Pfeffer, M. Kaiser, Werner Feix, Nils Kälble, Mathias Merten et al. Materials Science and Engineering A 2025-08-23 10.1016/j.msea.2025.149015
单位: Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU), Department of Materials Science & Engineering, Institute I: General Materials Properties, Erlangen, Germany
通讯: Nina Pfeffer @ Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU), Department of Materials Science & Engineering, Institute I: Gen
相关性: 不涉及增材制造，因此与AM Ti-6Al-4V后处理无关
边界: This paper does not concern additive manufacturing Ti-6Al-4V; it focuses on sheet forming of wrought Ti-6Al-4V, not post-processing of AM material.
动机· 开发节能高效的钛合金板材成形工艺
方法· 设计实验室级TISTRAQ工艺试验台，结合电阻加热和模具淬火
结果· 证实该工艺可提升Ti-6Al-4V抗拉强度，溶液温度和延迟时间是关键参数
验证· 通过拉伸测试和有限元模拟验证工艺效果
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop an energy- and material-efficient process for forming Ti-6Al-4V sheet parts.
Method· A laboratory-scale test stand was designed integrating direct electric resistance heating and tool-based quenching, combined with finite element simulations.
Result· The TISTRAQ process enhanced tensile strength of Ti-6Al-4V, with solution heat treatment temperature and delay time identified as critical parameters.
Validation· Experimental tensile tests and FE simulations validated the process design and material property improvements.
