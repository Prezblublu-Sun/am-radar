单位: Institute of Lightweight Engineering and Polymer Technology, Dresden University of Technology, Dresden, 01307, Germany
通讯: Paul Schulz @ Institute of Lightweight Engineering and Polymer Technology, Dresden University of Technology, Dresden, 01307, Germany
相关性: 该论文研究了不同热处理工艺对激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V超高周疲劳性能的影响，直接相关于后处理控制方向。
边界: This paper focuses on heat treatment and defect analysis but does not cover other post-processing methods such as HIP, machining, shot peening, or surface coatings.

方法· 制备不同热处理工艺的疲劳试样，进行xCT扫描和超高周疲劳试验，结合SEM分析裂纹源。
结果· 熔合缺陷主导疲劳失效，El-Haddad模型可行但分散性大，xCT可成像但低对比小缺陷定量困难。
验证· 通过超高周疲劳试验和断口SEM验证缺陷预测模型。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To predict crack-initiating defects and estimate the fatigue limit of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V using Murakami's area-parameter model, with different heat treatments.
Method· Fatigue specimens were fabricated by laser powder bed fusion with various process parameters and heat treatments, scanned by xCT, cycled in the very high cycle fatigue regime, and analyzed post-mortem by SEM.
Result· Lack-of-fusion defects are the primary crack initiators; El-Haddad fatigue limit estimates are feasible but exhibit high scatter due to defect size variability, and xCT can image defects but low contrast hinders quantitative analysis.
Validation· Experimental fatigue testing combined with post-mortem SEM and xCT imaging validates defect-based fatigue life predictions.

单位: School of Materials and Chemistry, University of Shanghai for Science and Technology, Shanghai 200093, China
通讯: Yi Yang @ School of Materials and Chemistry, University of Shanghai for Science and Technology, Shanghai 200093, China  |  Ruifeng Zhang @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, Central South University, Changsha 410083, China  |  Kai Zhang @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Monash University, Clayton, VIC 3800, Australia
相关性: 该论文直接研究LPBF Ti-6Al-4V的工艺参数对微观结构和力学性能（显微硬度、残余应力）的影响，符合L1层的关注点。

方法· 设计56种工艺条件，采用OM/SEM、EBSD、显微硬度和钻孔法残余应力测试表征。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the distinct roles of laser power, scanning speed, and hatch distance on microstructural evolution and mechanical response of LPBF Ti-6Al-4V beyond the volumetric energy density (VED) metric.

Result· Prior-β grain morphology, α' martensite thickness, texture, microhardness, and residual stress show different sensitivities to process parameters; microhardness follows a Hall-Petch relationship with α' lath thickness.
Validation· Experimental validation through microhardness and residual stress measurements.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the tribocorrosion resistance of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V by forming an oxygen diffusion layer via thermal oxidation.
Method· An oxygen diffusion layer was formed on SLM Ti-6Al-4V by thermal oxidation at 850°C for 6 h, and tribocorrosion behavior was evaluated using a reciprocating ball-on-flat tribometer.
