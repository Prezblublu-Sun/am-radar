The influence of stem length on perioperative complications following cemented hip arthroplasty in metastatic femoral disease: A systematic review.

Joseph D Giacalone, Jared Garfinkle, Surabhi Panda, Jason Abraham, Kassandra Parrales et al. Journal of orthopaedics 2025-10-01 10.1016/j.jor.2025.07.029
单位: Rowan-Virtua School of Osteopathic Medicine, Stratford, NJ, USA.
通讯: Christopher Haydel @ Virtua Reconstructive Orthopedics, Medford, NJ, USA. [推断]
相关性: 该论文是关于转移性股骨疾病中骨水泥型髋关节置换术的临床结果系统综述，未涉及生物力学建模或植入物设计，与研究者的方向无关。
边界: This paper is a clinical systematic review of perioperative complications in cemented hip arthroplasty for metastatic disease, with no biomechanics modeling, finite element analysis, implant design, or additive manufacturing content.
动机· 比较长短骨水泥柄在转移性股骨疾病中的围术期并发症率。
方法· 按照PRISMA指南系统检索四个数据库，纳入7项研究共379个股骨。
结果· 长柄组总围术期心肺并发症率（26.0%）和总并发症率（28.8%）显著高于短柄组（3.1%和10.6%）。
验证· 系统回顾现有临床研究，无新的实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This systematic review aims to compare perioperative complication rates between long and short cemented femoral stems in patients undergoing hip arthroplasty for proximal femoral metastases.
Method· A systematic search of PubMed, EMBASE, Web of Science, and Cochrane Library was conducted following PRISMA guidelines, including seven studies with 379 femurs.
Result· Long-stem constructs were associated with significantly higher rates of total perioperative cardiopulmonary complications (26.0% vs. 3.1%) and overall complications (28.8% vs. 10.6%) compared to short/standard stems.
Validation· The study is a systematic review of existing clinical literature, without original experimental validation.

A Comparison Between Two Bearing Surfaces for Total Hip Arthroplasty-Ceramic-on-Ceramic and Metal-Polycarbonate-Urethane-A Pseudo-Randomized Study.

单位: Servicio Cirugía Ortopédica y Traumatología, Hospital Universitario Poniente, 04700 El Ejido, Spain.
通讯: Jorge Albareda Albareda @ Servicio de Cirugía Ortopédica y Traumatología, Hospital Clínico Universitario Lozano Blesa, 50009 Zaragoza, Spain. [推断]
相关性: 这篇论文仅关注髋关节置换术中轴承面的临床结果，不涉及任何生物力学建模或应力遮挡分析。
边界: The paper does not include any biomechanics modeling, FEA, stress shielding analysis, implant design, or additive manufacturing aspects.
动机· 比较新型PCU轴承面与陶瓷轴承面的临床效果。
方法· 采用倾向评分匹配的前瞻性数据库分析，比较两组患者临床评分和并发症。
结果· 两组在临床结果、并发症和假体存活率上无显著差异，但PCU组吱吱声发生率更高。
验证· 105例患者的临床对照研究。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study aims to compare the clinical outcomes of polycarbonate-urethane (PCU) bearing surfaces against ceramic-on-ceramic (CoC) in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A propensity score matched analysis of a prospective database was performed, comparing Harris Hip Score, WOMAC, and complication rates between PCU and CoC groups.
Result· No significant differences were found in clinical scores, survival, or satisfaction, but the PCU group had a higher incidence of squeaking.
Validation· Clinical validation with 105 patients per group in a pseudo-randomized study.

Femoral Stem Subsidence Rate with Cementless Stems in Patients with Sub-capital Femoral Neck Fractures: Relationship to Proximal Femoral Geometry.

Aviram Albagly, Anton Maliarov, Oren Ben-Lulu Journal of orthopaedic case reports 2025-10-01 10.13107/jocr.2025.v15.i10.6254
单位: Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Bnai Zion Medical Center, Haifa, Israel.
通讯: Oren Ben-Lulu @ Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Bnai Zion Medical Center, Haifa, Israel. [推断]
相关性: 该论文仅关注临床股骨柄下沉率与骨形态的关系，未涉及增材制造或传统股骨柄设计、有限元分析或生物力学建模。
边界: This paper is a purely clinical observational study; it does not include any additive manufacturing, implant design, FEA, or biomechanical mechanics.
动机· 探讨股骨近端几何形态对无水泥股骨柄下沉率的影响
方法· 回顾性分析74例患者，按Dorr分型分组比较下沉率
结果· Dorr A型下沉率显著低于B型和C型
验证· 多变量逻辑回归确认Dorr A型与低下沉率相关
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the relationship between proximal femoral geometry and subsidence rate of cementless femoral stems.
Method· A retrospective cohort study of 74 patients categorized by Dorr classification compared low and high subsidence groups.
Result· Dorr type A stems had significantly lower subsidence rates than types B and C.
Validation· Multivariate logistic regression confirmed Dorr type A was associated with lower subsidence (OR 3.4, p=0.022).

Accuracy of AI-Assisted Methods in Preoperative Templating Compared to the Standard X-Ray-Based Surgeon-Performed Templating in Total Hip Replacement Surgery: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.

Osamah Al-Zubaidi, Aiman Alzuabidi, Ban Alkurdi, Dina Alkurdi, Nayef Aslam-Pervez Cureus 2025-10-01 10.7759/cureus.95328
单位: Plastic Surgery, Hull University Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust, Hull, GBR.
通讯: Nayef Aslam-Pervez @ Trauma and Orthopaedics, Hull University Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust, Hull, GBR. [推断]
相关性: 该论文主要评估AI在术前模板规划中的准确性，与用户关注的植入物生物力学和增材制造方向不直接相关。
边界: This paper focuses on preoperative implant size prediction accuracy, does not include biomechanical analysis, stress shielding quantification, FEA, or additive manufacturing of implants.
动机· 比较AI辅助与标准X线模板在髋关节置换术中预测植入物尺寸的准确性。
方法· 对9项临床研究共1049例患者进行系统评价和Meta分析。
结果· AI方法在预测髋臼杯和股骨柄尺寸时准确性显著高于手工方法，并缩短手术时间。
验证· 基于已发表临床研究的Meta分析，统计检验验证差异显著性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare the accuracy of AI-assisted versus standard manual X-ray templating for implant size prediction in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A systematic review and meta-analysis of nine clinical studies totaling 1049 patients, using a random effects model.
Result· AI-assisted templating significantly improved the odds of exact size match for both acetabular cup and femoral stem components and reduced operative time.
Validation· Validation was based on statistical aggregation of clinical studies with 95% confidence intervals and meta-analytic tests.

Exploring Chalcogen Influence on Sc2BeX4 (X = S, Se) for Green Energy Applications Using DFT

Ahmad Ali, Haris Haider, Sikander Azam, Muhammad Talha, Muhammad Jawad et al. arXiv preprint 2025-10-01 link
相关性: 该论文完全聚焦于能源应用的硫族化合物DFT计算，与您的AM机械性能研究方向无关。
边界: This paper does not involve additive manufacturing, mechanical testing, or any biomedical context; it is a purely computational materials science study for energy applications.
动机· 探索Sc2BeX4硫族化物在绿色能源中的潜力
方法· 使用第一性原理DFT计算其结构、电子、光学和热电性质
结果· 两种化合物稳定，带隙1.2-1.8 eV，ZT达0.80，适合光伏和热电应用
验证· 通过声子谱和形成能验证稳定性，与TB-mBJ近似对比
English summary & terms
Motivation· This work aims to evaluate the potential of Sc2BeX4 chalcogenides for green energy applications via first-principles calculations.
Method· Density functional theory (DFT) with TB-mBJ approximation was used to compute structural, electronic, optical, and thermoelectric properties of Sc2BeS4 and Sc2BeSe4.
Result· Both compounds exhibit thermodynamic and dynamic stability, direct band gaps (1.8 eV for S, 1.2 eV for Se), and a maximum ZT of 0.80 at 800 K, indicating promise for photovoltaics and thermoelectrics.
Validation· Stability was confirmed by negative formation energies and phonon dispersion; electronic properties were benchmarked against TB-mBJ.

边界: This paper focuses on rare-earth-doped phosphors for optical and thermoelectric applications, with no relation to additive manufacturing, mechanical properties, or biomedical implants.
动机· 探索Eu掺杂CaAlSiN3在照明和光电应用中的潜力
方法· 使用GGA+U的DFT计算电子结构、光学和热电性质

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the potential of Eu-doped CaAlSiN3 as a stable red-emitting phosphor for white LEDs and optoelectronic applications.
Method· First-principles DFT calculations with GGA+U were performed to study electronic structure, optical spectra, and thermoelectric transport properties.
Result· Eu3+ doping introduces localized 4f states, narrowing the band gap and enabling red photoluminescence, while also improving the power factor and reducing lattice thermal conductivity.
Validation· Thermodynamic feasibility, mechanical stability, and consistency with experimental optical spectra partially validate the computational predictions.
