Gleeble-Simulated Ultra-Fast Cooling Unlocks Strength–Ductility Synergy in Fully Martensitic Ti-6Al-4V

Yaohong Xiao, Hongling Zhou, Pengwei Liu, Lei Chen Materials 2025-10-01 10.3390/ma18194572
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Mississippi State University, Starkville, MS 39762, USA
通讯: Yaohong Xiao @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Mississippi State University, Starkville, MS 39762, USA  |  Hongling Zhou @ College of Materials Science and Engineering, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400044, China
相关性: 该论文直接研究增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的冷却速率对力学性能的影响，揭示了完全马氏体组织的强度-塑性协同提升机制。
边界: It does not include fatigue testing or cyclic loading data; only quasi-static compression is reported.
动机· 为了隔离冷却速率对Ti-6Al-4V马氏体力学性能的影响，澄清α′的脆性/韧性争议。
方法· 使用Gleeble热模拟器实现高达7000 °C/s的冷却速率，获得α/β、αm/α′和全α′三种组织，进行压缩测试和EVP-FFT晶体塑性模拟。
结果· 超快冷却的全马氏体Ti-6Al-4V同时获得更高强度和更大断裂应变，延性韧窝分布均匀。
验证· 通过压缩实验和三维微观结构重建的晶体塑性模型验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study isolates the intrinsic effect of cooling rate on the mechanical behavior of martensitic Ti-6Al-4V to resolve the debated role of α′ in brittleness versus toughness.
Method· A Gleeble thermal simulator was used to apply precisely controlled cooling rates up to ~7000 °C/s, producing α/β, αm/α′, and fully α′ microstructures, which were then characterized by compression tests and EVP-FFT crystal plasticity modeling.
Result· Ultra-fast-cooled fully martensitic Ti-6Al-4V achieved simultaneous improvement in both strength and fracture strain, with densely distributed elongated dimples indicating ductile failure.
Validation· Experimental compression tests and three-dimensional microstructure-based crystal plasticity simulations provided validation.

Validation· Surface roughness and channel dimensions were measured using touch probe and tomography.
