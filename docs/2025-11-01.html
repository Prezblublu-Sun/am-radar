Heterogeneous globularization in lamellar-structured Ti-6Al-4V alloy by heavy warm rolling and annealing treatment

单位: School of Materials Science and Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Shanghai 200240, China
通讯: Li-ming FU @ Collaborative Innovation Center for Advanced Ship and Deep-Sea Exploration, Shanghai 200240, China  |  Ai-dang SHAN @ Collaborative Innovation Center for Advanced Ship and Deep-Sea Exploration, Shanghai 200240, China
相关性: 该论文研究传统轧制Ti-6Al-4V的球化行为，未涉及增材制造及其后处理，对AM Ti-6Al-4V后处理控制方向无直接参考价值。
边界: The paper does not involve additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it studies conventionally processed material with no post-processing relevant to AM fatigue/mechanical performance.
动机· 研究轧制温度和α团取向对Ti-6Al-4V合金层状组织球化行为的影响。
方法· 在600-900°C对层状组织进行重轧，随后退火处理，分析球化分数和微观组织。
结果· 升高轧制温度降低球化率，硬取向α团球化率显著低于软取向，导致不均匀球化和强微织构。
验证· 通过微观组织观察和滑移变形分析验证球化行为差异。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the influence of rolling temperature and crystallographic orientation of α-colonies on the globularization behavior of lamellar α+β microstructure in Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· Heavy warm rolling was performed at 600, 700, 800, and 900 °C on lamellar structures, followed by annealing to spheroidize the α+β lamellae into equiaxed grains.
Result· Higher rolling temperatures decreased the globularization fraction, and α-colonies with hard orientations showed significantly lower globularization compared to softer orientations, leading to heterogeneous globularization and sharp micro-texture.
Validation· Microstructural observations and analysis of slip deformation during rolling were used to validate the globularization behavior.
