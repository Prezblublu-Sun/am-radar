Preclinical analysis of a novel short hip-stem design: A finite element model-based investigation.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To design a novel short hip stem that reduces strain shielding and bone resorption while improving osseointegration.
Method· CT-based 3D finite element models of intact and reconstructed femurs were developed and solved under walking and stair climbing loads.
Result· The porous short stem reduced strain shielding by approximately 44% and bone resorption by approximately 42% compared to its solid counterpart.
Validation· Validation was performed through comparative finite element simulations against a solid stem design.
