Effect of Ultra-Rapid Heating/Cooling on the Microstructure and Properties of TC4-B-Si Titanium Matrix Composites

Xiaonan Lu, Jianchao Li, Cheng Liu, Likun Wang, Sainan Ma et al. Materials 2025-09-09 10.3390/ma18184223
单位: Institute for Composites Science Innovation (InCSI), School of Materials Science and Engineering, Zhejiang University, Hangzhou 300027, China
通讯: Xiang Gao @ Institute for Composites Science Innovation (InCSI), School of Materials Science and Engineering, Zhejiang University, H  |  Hua‐Xin Peng @ Institute for Composites Science Innovation (InCSI), School of Materials Science and Engineering, Zhejiang University, H
相关性: 该论文研究了TC4钛合金基复合材料的SLM制备工艺与微观组织及硬度关系，但未涉及拉伸或疲劳力学性能，对L1层机械疲劳方向的直接贡献有限。
边界: The paper reports only microhardness and microstructure, lacking tensile/yield/strength, ductility, fatigue life, or S-N data, which are the core mechanical properties for L1 direction.
动机· 通过超快速加热/冷却获得超细多尺度网状结构以增强钛基复合材料
方法· 采用SLM原位合成TC4-B-Si复合材料，调控激光能量密度
结果· 适当能量密度下获得高致密度，TiB网状结构细化至纳米级，硬度最高达454 HV
验证· 通过显微硬度测试和微观组织表征验证工艺-结构-性能关系
English summary & terms
Motivation· To achieve an ultrafine multi-scale network structure in titanium matrix composites for enhanced properties via ultra-rapid heating/cooling.
Method· In situ synthesized TC4-B-Si composites were prepared by selective laser melting with varying laser energy density.
Result· Appropriate energy density produced high-density composites with refined sub-micron to nano-scale TiB network and maximum microhardness of 454 HV.
Validation· Microhardness testing and microstructural characterization (SEM) were used to validate the process-structure-property relationships.

Vacuum Performance of Metal Additive Manufacturing Ti-6Al-4V UHV Chamber

J. Kamiya, Yuko Morohashi, Kenichiro Takeishi, Toru Yanagibashi Vacuum and Surface Science 2025-09-09 10.1380/vss.68.504
单位: Japan Atomic Energy Agency, J-PARC Center
通讯: J. Kamiya @ Japan Atomic Energy Agency, J-PARC Center
相关性: 涉及Ti-6Al-4V后处理（真空退火），但仅评估真空性能，未讨论疲劳或力学性能。
边界: Paper evaluates vacuum performance of AM Ti-6Al-4V chamber with post-processing (vacuum firing), but provides no data on mechanical properties (fatigue, tensile) or microstructure modification relevant to fatigue performance.
动机· 评估增材制造Ti-6Al-4V真空室的真空性能，探索后处理对出气率的影响。
方法· 通过真空退火（高温烘烤）处理AM Ti-6Al-4V样品，测量真空度和残余气体成分。
结果· 经真空退火后，AM室达到10⁻⁹ Pa超高真空，NEG涂层后无需泵可实现10⁻⁸ Pa。
验证· 实验测量真空度及残余气体谱图，与常规不锈钢室对比。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study evaluates the vacuum performance of Ti-6Al-4V chambers produced by additive manufacturing and the effect of post-processing on outgassing.
Method· Vacuum firing (high-temperature heat treatment in vacuum furnace) was applied to AM Ti-6Al-4V samples, and vacuum levels and residual gas composition were measured.
Result· After vacuum firing, ultra-high vacuum of 10⁻⁹ Pa was achieved; with NEG coating, 10⁻⁸ Pa was reached without pumping.
Validation· Vacuum gauging and residual gas analysis (RGA) were performed and compared with conventional stainless steel chambers.

Laser-Driven Surface Alloying of Ti6Al4V: Coupled Microstructural Evolution, Phase Behavior, and Mechanical Performance

Hana Beyene Mamo, Klaudiusz Gołombek, Gilmar Ferreira Batalha, Marcin Adamiak Materials 2025-09-09 10.3390/ma18184237
单位: Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, Silesian University of Technology, Konarskiego Street 18A, 44-100 Gliwice, Poland
通讯: Hana Beyene Mamo @ Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, Silesian University of Technology, Konarskiego Street 18A, 44-100 Gliwice, Poland  |  Marcin Adamiak @ Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, Silesian University of Technology, Konarskiego Street 18A, 44-100 Gliwice, Poland
相关性: 该论文使用激光表面合金化处理锻造Ti6Al4V，而非增材制造工艺，且未关注疲劳或拉伸性能。
边界: This paper does not study additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it uses laser surface alloying on wrought alloy, which is a post-processing surface modification, not an AM process. It focuses on hardness and microstructure, not tensile or fatigue properties.
动机· 通过激光表面合金化提高Ti6Al4V的表面硬度和抗菌性能。
方法· 使用1000W脉冲激光在Ti6Al4V表面合金化银和铜元素，分析微观结构和相变。
结果· 铜合金化形成Ti2Cu相，硬度提升显著高于银合金化。
验证· 通过EBSD、EDS和XRD表征微观结构，硬度测试验证力学性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the surface hardness and antimicrobial properties of Ti6Al4V via laser surface alloying with silver and copper.
Method· A 1000 W pulsed laser was used to alloy Ag and Cu into the Ti6Al4V surface, and the resulting microstructural evolution and phase formation were analyzed.
Result· Cu alloying produced Ti2Cu intermetallics and dendritic structures, leading to greater hardness improvement than Ag alloying.
Validation· Microstructural characterization by EBSD, EDS, and XRD, along with hardness testing, validated the mechanical changes.

Features and Latest Trends of Electron Beam Additive Manufacturing System

Ayumu MIYAKITA, Takashi Sato Vacuum and Surface Science 2025-09-09 10.1380/vss.68.510
单位: JEOL Ltd
通讯: Ayumu MIYAKITA @ JEOL Ltd
相关性: 该论文是关于电子束粉末床熔融技术的综述，未涉及Ti-6Al-4V的后处理工艺对力学性能的影响。
边界: The paper does not address any post-processing treatment for Ti-6Al-4V; it is a general overview of EB-PBF technology without mechanical performance data or post-processing methods.
动机· 综述电子束粉末床熔融技术的特征与最新趋势。
方法· 归纳EB-PBF系统特点及近年发展，包括高质量电子束和缺陷监测。
结果· 指出EB-PBF适用于钛合金等多种材料，可实现无裂纹和微观组织控制。
验证· 未提供实验验证，为综述性质。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This paper reviews the features and latest trends of electron beam powder bed fusion (EB-PBF) systems.
Method· It summarizes the general characteristics of EB-PBF and recent developments such as high-quality electron beams and backscattered electron image monitoring for defect detection.
Result· The paper states that EB-PBF is suitable for Ti-6Al-4V and other alloys, meeting AMS standards and enabling crack-free fabrication with microstructure control.
Validation· No experimental validation is provided; this is a review article.
