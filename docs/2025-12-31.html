Microstructure evolution and corrosion performance of LPBF-fabricated Ti-6Al-4V with nano-Mo reinforcement

Peipei Lu, Xiu Ye, Weiwei Song, Enhui Lu, Fei Ding et al. Journal of Laser Applications 2025-12-31 10.2351/7.0001958
单位: College of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, Huangshan University 1 , Huangshan 245041,
通讯: Peipei Lu @ College of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, Huangshan University 1 , Huangshan 245041,
相关性: 该论文研究LPBF制造的Ti-6Al-4V添加纳米Mo后的微观结构和腐蚀性能，但未涉及力学或疲劳性能，与用户关注的力学与疲劳方向关联较弱。
边界: No tensile or fatigue data; core contribution is corrosion performance and microstructure, not mechanical/fatigue characterization.
动机· 探究纳米Mo添加和线能量密度对LPBF制备Ti-6Al-4V微观结构与性能的影响
方法· 实验表征与电化学分析相结合，研究不同纳米Mo含量和线能量密度下的组织演变
结果· 7 wt.%纳米Mo与0.21 J/mm线能量密度协同优化，使晶粒细化50.4%，显微硬度达413.5 HV0.2，耐腐蚀性提升78.4%
验证· 通过扫描电镜、X射线衍射和电化学测试进行实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· This work aims to understand how nano-Mo addition and linear energy density affect the microstructure and properties of LPBF-fabricated Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Microstructural characterization and electrochemical analysis were performed on specimens with varying nano-Mo content and linear energy density.
Result· Adding 7 wt.% nano-Mo at 0.21 J/mm linear energy density reduced grain size by 50.4%, achieved a peak microhardness of 413.5 HV0.2, and increased charge transfer resistance by 78.4% compared to unmodified Ti-6Al-4V.
Validation· Experimental validation was conducted using SEM, XRD, and electrochemical impedance spectroscopy.

Influence of Tool Clearance Angle and Cutting Conditions on Tool Life When Turning Ti-6Al-4V—Design of Experiments Approach

单位: Department of Machining Technology, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, University of West Bohemia in Pilsen, 30100 Pilsen, Czech Republic
通讯: Adam Lukáš @ Department of Machining Technology, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, University of West Bohemia in Pilsen, 30100 Pilse
相关性: 该论文研究的是钛合金车削过程中的刀具寿命，与增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的后处理无关。
边界: This paper investigates tool wear during conventional turning of Ti-6Al-4V, not post-processing of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V. It does not address any post-processing treatment (heat treatment, HIP, surface treatments) for AM components.
动机· 研究刀具后角对车削Ti-6Al-4V时刀具磨损的影响
方法· 采用实验设计方法，变化后角、切削速度和进给量，分析刀具磨损
结果· 后角对刀具寿命有显著影响，适中后角可减少刀具磨损并改善表面完整性
验证· 通过3D形貌测量和回归分析验证模型准确性
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the influence of tool clearance angle on tool wear during turning of Ti-6Al-4V under wet cutting conditions.
Method· A Design of Experiments approach was used varying clearance angle, cutting speed, and feed rate to determine their effects on tool wear, with analysis via 3D topography and regression.
Result· The clearance angle significantly impacts crater wear and tool life; an optimized moderate clearance angle reduces tool degradation and improves surface integrity.
Validation· Experimental data was assessed using coefficient of determination (R2) and adjusted R2 from regression analysis.

Optimised Magnetic Field-Assisted Finishing Improves Surface and Mechanical Performance of CAD/CAM Titanium Abutments

Liujun Lin, Hongli Yu, Guanwen Yang, Hengyue Wang, Qitong Chen et al. International Dental Journal 2025-12-31 10.1016/j.identj.2025.109370
单位: Fujian Key Laboratory of Oral Diseases & Fujian Provincial Engineering Research Center of Oral Biomaterial & Stomatological Key Laboratory of Fujian College and University, School and Hospital of Stom
通讯: Liujun Lin @ Fujian Key Laboratory of Oral Diseases & Fujian Provincial Engineering Research Center of Oral Biomaterial & Stomatologi
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的后处理（磁辅助精加工），但未涉及增材制造，且未测试疲劳性能。
边界: The paper does not concern additive manufacturing; it uses CAD/CAM subtractive manufacturing for dental abutments and does not report fatigue testing.
动机· 优化磁辅助精加工参数以改善CAD/CAM钛基台的表面和机械性能。
方法· 在不同转速和时间下对Ti-6Al-4V圆盘进行磁辅助精加工，并表征表面形貌、粗糙度、硬度及残余应力。
结果· 磁辅助精加工获得无划痕表面，提高压应力，硬度不变，最佳参数为1200 rpm/40分钟。
验证· 通过表面轮廓仪、SEM、AFM、XRD和显微硬度测试验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study optimizes magnetic field-assisted finishing (MAF) parameters to enhance surface and mechanical properties of CAD/CAM Ti-6Al-4V dental abutments.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V discs were polished with MAF at varying rotational speeds (1000-1500 rpm) and durations (20-60 min); surface morphology, roughness, composition, microhardness, and residual stress were characterized.
Result· MAF produced scratch-free surfaces with beneficial compressive residual stress, maintained hydrophilicity, and did not alter microhardness; optimal parameters were 1200 rpm for 40 min.
Validation· Validation included 2D/3D profilometry, SEM, AFM, EDS, contact angle, Vickers microhardness, and XRD residual stress measurements, plus clinical benchmarking against prefabricated abutments.
