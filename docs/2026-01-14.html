Influence of ultrasound on evolution of the Ti-6Al-4V alloy microstructure during wire-feed electron-beam additive manufacturing

K. S. Osipovich, A. O. Panfilov, A. V. Chumaevskii, V. M. Semenchuk, Yu. V. Kushnarev et al. Russian Physics Journal 2026-01-14 10.1007/s11182-026-03654-w
单位: Institute of Strength Physics and Materials Science of the Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Tomsk, Russian Federation
通讯: K. S. Osipovich @ Institute of Strength Physics and Materials Science of the Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Tomsk, Ru
相关性: 该论文研究超声对Ti-6Al-4V线材电子束增材制造中微观结构的影响，与力学性能间接相关。
边界: No mechanical or fatigue testing reported; focuses solely on microstructure evolution during processing.
动机· 研究超声对Ti-6Al-4V合金增材制造中微观结构演变的影响。
方法· 采用线材电子束增材制造并施加超声进行实验。
结果· 超声改变了合金的微观结构特征。
验证· 通过微观结构表征验证了超声的作用。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the influence of ultrasound on microstructure evolution of Ti-6Al-4V during wire-feed electron-beam additive manufacturing.
Method· Ultrasound was applied during the additive manufacturing process to study its effect on microstructure.
Result· Ultrasound altered the microstructure of the Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Validation· Microstructural characterization was performed to validate the influence of ultrasound.

Investigation of the microstructure-property behaviour of powder bed fusion Ti-6Al-4V parts after heat treatment via an integrated high-fidelity phase-field and crystal plasticity framework

Yuhui Tu, Jianxin Zhang, Yaoyi Geng, S.B. Leen, Noel M. Harrison Journal of Alloys and Compounds 2026-01-14 10.1016/j.jallcom.2026.186152
单位: I-Form Advanced Manufacturing Research Centre, Ireland
通讯: Yuhui Tu @ I-Form Advanced Manufacturing Research Centre, Ireland  |  Yaoyi Geng @ Mechanical Engineering, University of Galway, Ireland  |  S.B. Leen @ I-Form Advanced Manufacturing Research Centre, Ireland  |  Noel M. Harrison @ I-Form Advanced Manufacturing Research Centre, Ireland
相关性: 本文直接研究热处理对Ti-6Al-4V粉末床熔融件微观结构-性能关系的影响，符合后处理控制方向。
边界: The paper is purely computational (phase-field + crystal plasticity) without experimental validation of the predicted mechanical properties. No fatigue data or porosity closure results are reported explicitly.
动机· 揭示热处理对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V微观结构与力学性能的影响机制
方法· 采用相场与晶体塑性耦合的高保真框架模拟热处理后的微观组织演变与力学响应
结果· 预测了不同热处理路径下的微观组织特征与强度变化趋势
验证· 通过与文献中数值基准比较验证模型准确性
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand how heat treatment alters the microstructure and mechanical behavior of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· An integrated phase-field and crystal plasticity framework is employed to simulate microstructural evolution and mechanical response after heat treatment.
Result· The model predicts changes in phase fractions, grain morphology, and resulting strength for different heat treatment conditions.
Validation· Model predictions are validated against numerical benchmarks from the literature.

单位: School of Intelligent Manufacturing, Guangzhou Maritime University, Guangzhou 510725, China
通讯: Wanghui Xu @ School of Intelligent Manufacturing, Guangzhou Maritime University, Guangzhou 510725, China
相关性: 该论文研究的是Ti6Al4V的TIG焊接工艺，与增材制造无关。
边界: This paper studies TIG welding of Ti6Al4V, not additive manufacturing; no LPBF/SLM/EBM/DED process is involved.
动机· 提高Ti6Al4V钛合金TIG焊接接头性能

English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the performance of Ti6Al4V titanium alloy TIG welded joints.
Method· Ultrasonic frequency pulsed TIG welding was employed.
Result· Grain refinement and enhanced mechanical properties were achieved.
Validation· Microstructural characterization and mechanical testing were performed.
