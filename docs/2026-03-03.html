Research Progress on the Microstructure, Mechanical Properties, and Corrosion Behavior of TC4 Alloy Fabricated by Selective Laser Melting

Huiling Zhou, J. Li, Shugang Zhang, Bin Yang, Yuanbin Gui et al. Metals 2026-03-03 10.3390/met16030284
单位: School of Materials Science and Engineering, Jiangsu University of Science and Technology, Zhenjiang 212100, China
通讯: Sheng Lu @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, Jiangsu University of Science and Technology, Zhenjiang 212100, China  |  Yanxin Qiao @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, Jiangsu University of Science and Technology, Zhenjiang 212100, China
相关性: 该综述系统总结了热处理和热等静压等后处理对选区激光熔化TC4合金微观组织及疲劳性能的影响，直接符合用户关注的后处理调控方向。
边界: This is a review paper without novel experimental or modeling data; it synthesizes existing literature rather than contributing new findings on a specific post-processing route.
动机· 全面更新SLM成型TC4合金的研究进展，填补先前综述在机理分析和多维比较上的空白。
方法· 综合评述了SLM工艺参数、后热处理及热等静压对组织、力学和腐蚀性能的影响。
结果· 后处理可显著改善塑性（延伸率达17.4%）和疲劳性能（HIP降低孔隙率至0.01%）。
验证· 综述引用了多篇已发表论文中的实验数据，未提出新实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To comprehensively update the state of the art on SLM-fabricated Ti-6Al-4V, addressing gaps in previous reviews by integrating novel progress and mechanistic analysis.
Method· The review synthesizes findings on how SLM process parameters, post-heat treatment, and hot isostatic pressing influence microstructure, tensile properties, fatigue, and corrosion behavior.
Result· Post-treatment strategies such as annealing at 800°C for 2 h yield 1099 MPa tensile strength and 17.4% elongation, while HIP reduces porosity to 0.01% and weakens fatigue anisotropy.
Validation· No new experimental validation is provided; the review compiles and compares existing experimental results from the literature.

Strength prediction and microstructure quantification of Ti-6Al-4V alloy by selective laser melting based on machine learning

M.Y. Zuo, Y.H. Zeng, K. Sun, J. F. Sun, M. Wang Materials & Design 2026-03-03 10.1016/j.matdes.2026.115775
单位: School of Materials Science and Engineering, Liaoning Technical University, 47 Zhonghua Road, Fuxin, Liaoning 123000, China
通讯: M. Wang @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, Liaoning Technical University, 47 Zhonghua Road, Fuxin, Liaoning 123000, Ch
相关性: 该论文虽未专门研究后处理对疲劳性能的影响，但通过机器学习建立了退火等后处理参数与力学性能的关系，为后处理效果预测提供了数据驱动方法。
边界: The paper does not experimentally isolate the effect of a specific post-processing treatment on fatigue or mechanical performance; it is a predictive model using existing data that includes annealing parameters.
动机· 建立SLM钛合金工艺-结构-性能关系
方法· 集成XGBoost和深度学习的跨尺度框架
结果· 预测抗拉强度R²=0.98，识别退火温度和激光功率为关键因素
验证· 使用750组工艺参数和1225幅显微图像验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To establish quantitative process-structure-property relationships for SLM Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· A cross-scale framework integrating Bayesian-optimized XGBoost for strength prediction and deep learning for microstructure analysis.
Result· Achieved tensile strength prediction with R²=0.98 and identified annealing temperature and laser power as dominant factors.
Validation· Validated on 750 process parameter sets and 1,225 metallographic images, with deep learning models achieving 98% classification accuracy.

The role of martensite decomposition and β-stabilizer segregation to interfaces in Ti-6Al-4V with an initial dual-phase α+α' microstructure

Nina Pfeffer, A. Bezold, Jan Vollhüter, O. Nagel, Martin Weiser et al. Materials Science and Engineering A 2026-03-03 10.1016/j.msea.2026.150024
单位: Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU), Department of Materials Science & Engineering, Institute I: General Materials Properties, Martensstraße 5, 91058 Erlangen, Germany
通讯: Nina Pfeffer @ Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU), Department of Materials Science & Engineering, Institute I: Gen
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的退火后处理，但研究对象为传统片材而非增材制造，且未涉及疲劳性能。
边界: Paper does not involve additive manufacturing (uses conventional sheet material) and does not report fatigue data, only tensile properties.
动机· 探究短时退火对双相α+α' Ti-6Al-4V显微组织演变的影响及其强化机制。
方法· 结合高分辨STEM、原子探针层析、高能X射线衍射与拉伸测试，分析退火过程中的相变和元素偏聚。
结果· 短时退火触发α'→α+β转变，V和Fe向界面偏聚，形成团簇，抑制马氏体再取向塑性，提高强度。
验证· 通过拉伸试验验证不同退火时间下的力学性能变化。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To reveal the microstructural mechanisms underlying the strength enhancement of dual-phase α+α' Ti-6Al-4V by short-time annealing.
Method· High-resolution STEM, atom probe tomography, and high-energy X-ray diffraction combined with tensile testing were used to characterize the microstructural evolution during annealing.
Result· Short-time annealing at 570°C for 180 s triggers α'→α+β transformation with V and Fe segregation to interfaces, forming clusters that suppress reorientation-induced plasticity and increase strength.
Validation· Mechanical assessment via tensile testing on differently annealed samples.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of welding current on the properties of Ti-HA composite coatings deposited on Ti-6Al-4V by TIG welding.
Method· Ti-HA coatings were fabricated at 90A, 100A, and 110A using TIG welding, and characterized for microstructure, mechanical, electrochemical, and biological performance.
Result· Welding current significantly influenced coating adhesion, corrosion resistance, and apatite formation; the optimal current varied by property.
Validation· Mechanical integrity was assessed via ASTM E190 bending tests, electrochemical behavior via potentiodynamic polarization, and bioactivity via SBF immersion.
