Comparative Biomechanical Analysis of Shape-Closed and Force-Closed Femoral Stems in Total Hip Arthroplasty

Umme Nashitun Haque, Samia Jahan Emu, Ishrat Sayeeda Tashfia, Saira Binte Awal, Faria Tasnim et al. 2025-10-06 10.1109/khpiweek61436.2025.11288605
单位: Military Institute of Science and Technology,Dept of Biomedical Engineering,Dhaka,Bangladesh
通讯: Umme Nashitun Haque @ Military Institute of Science and Technology,Dept of Biomedical Engineering,Dhaka,Bangladesh
相关性: 该论文直接比较两种骨水泥型股骨柄在植入后的应力与疲劳性能，与用户髋关节植入物方向高度相关
边界: 该研究仅为有限元仿真对比，缺少实验验证、增材制造工艺或AI代理模型相关内容
动机· 比较形状闭合与力闭合型骨水泥股骨柄的生物力学性能
方法· 对两种股骨柄进行三维有限元静力学和疲劳分析
结果· 形状闭合型股骨柄的von Mises应力、应变和变形更小，安全系数更高
验证· 通过有限元模拟验证，无实验数据
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study compares the biomechanical performance of shape-closed and force-closed cemented femoral stems in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Three-dimensional finite element analysis was performed for static structural and fatigue simulations under physiologically relevant loading.
Result· The shape-closed design exhibited significantly lower von Mises stress, strain, and deformation, along with a higher safety factor under both static and dynamic conditions.
Validation· Validation was limited to simulation results; no experimental or clinical validation was conducted.

Predisposition of Hip Prosthesis Component Positioning on Dislocation Risk: Biomechanical Considerations Based on Finite Element Method Analysis.

Maciej Kostewicz, Marcin Zaczyk, Grzegorz Szczęsny Journal of clinical medicine 2025-10-06 10.3390/jcm14197056
单位: Department of Orthopedics and Traumatology of the Musculoskeletal System, Medical University of Warsaw, 02-091 Warsaw, Poland.
通讯: Grzegorz Szczęsny @ Department of Orthopedics and Traumatology of the Musculoskeletal System, Medical University of Warsaw, 02-091 Warsaw, P [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究了髋关节假体位置对脱位风险的影响，与用户关注的人工髋关节应力遮挡和稳定性有一定关联，但未涉及应力遮挡或微动分析。
边界: This paper focuses on dislocation risk due to implant positioning, not on stress shielding, micromotion, or bone-implant interface mechanics which are the core of the user's hip implant direction.
动机· 评估髋关节假体位置变化对脱位风险的生物力学影响
方法· 基于CT建立骨盆-髋关节三维有限元模型，改变髋臼杯倾角、前倾角、股骨柄深度和股骨偏距
结果· 最佳位置组合安全系数高，不良位置显著降低稳定性
验证· 单一解剖模型下的有限元模拟分析
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the biomechanical consequences of hip prosthesis component positioning on dislocation risk under single-leg stance loading.
Method· A 3D finite element model of the pelvis and hip joint was developed from CT data, simulating variations in cup inclination, anteversion, stem depth, and femoral offset.
Result· Optimal implant positioning achieved safety factors of 9-12, while malposition reduced values to 2-5, indicating increased dislocation risk.
Validation· The study is a computational simulation case using a single anatomical model without experimental or clinical validation.

The roles of elasticity and dimension in liquid-gel phase separation

Shichen Wang, Peter D. Olmsted arXiv preprint 2025-10-06 link
相关性: 本文讨论凝胶中液-固相分离的弹性模型，与用户关注的增材制造或生物医学植入物方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address additive manufacturing, biomedical implants, or mechanical property characterization of AM parts; it focuses on theoretical elasticity models of polymer network phase separation.
动机· 研究凝胶相分离中不同弹性模型的影响
方法· 比较六种弹性模型，提出结合Arruda-Boyce和滑移管模型的新模型

English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand how different elastic models affect phase separation behavior in polymer gels.
Method· Six elastic models are compared, including a new model combining the Arruda-Boyce and slip tube models, under various deformations.
Result· The proposed model captures both strain softening and hardening; the neo-Hookean model fails in 3D phase coexistence.
Validation· No experimental validation; purely theoretical analysis.

Role of chromium oxides and carbides in strengthening CoCrFeNi multi-principle element alloys

Artur Olejarz, Wenyi Huo, Anna Kosinska, Maciej Zielinski, Tomasz Stasiak et al. arXiv preprint 2025-10-06 10.1016/j.msea.2025.149058
相关性: 与用户研究方向无关，不涉及生物医学植入物或增材制造。
边界: This paper does not involve additive manufacturing (LPBF or other) for biomedical implants, nor does it address osseointegration, fatigue, or implant-specific mechanical testing; it focuses on non-biomedical MPEAs processed by arc melting, gas atomization, and mechanical alloying for high-temperature applications.
动机· 研究铬化合物对多主元合金力学性能的影响。
方法· 通过电弧熔炼、气雾化和机械合金化制备不同铬化合物的样品，进行力学测试。
结果· 氧化物增强高温性能，碳化物提高强度但降低延性。
验证· 室温和575°C力学测试。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the role of chromium oxides and carbides in strengthening CoCrFeNi multi-principal element alloys for high-temperature applications.
Method· Samples were prepared via arc melting, gas atomization, and mechanical alloying, and mechanical properties were tested at room temperature and 575°C.
Result· Oxide dispersoids enhanced high-temperature mechanical properties through precipitation strengthening and twinning-induced plasticity, while carbides increased strength but reduced ductility.
Validation· Mechanical testing at two temperatures.

Investigating into mechanisms of high temperature strength of refractory high-entropy alloys

Sai Anandhi Seetharaman, Soumyadipta Maiti, Ambesh Gupta, Beena Rai arXiv preprint 2025-10-06 link
相关性: 该论文研究难熔高熵合金的高温强度机制，与用户的研究方向（生物打印、髋关节植入物、FEA代理模型、增材制造生物医学）无直接关联。
边界: This paper investigates high-temperature strength mechanisms of refractory high-entropy alloys using atomistic simulations, with no experimental validation, no biomedical application, no implant mechanics, no additive manufacturing context, and no FEA surrogate modeling.
动机· 理解BCC难熔高熵合金在中温下的屈服强度平台机制
方法· 采用混合蒙特卡洛和分子动力学模拟分析位错核心附近的原子扩散和应力
结果· 发现屈服强度平台源于动态应变时效和热应力的共同作用
验证· 无实验验证，纯数值模拟
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand the mechanisms behind the yield strength plateau in BCC refractory high-entropy alloys at intermediate temperatures.
Method· Hybrid Monte Carlo and molecular dynamics simulations were used to analyze atomic diffusion and stress around edge dislocation cores.
Result· The yield strength plateau arises from the interplay between dynamic strain ageing and athermal stress from lattice distortions.
Validation· No experimental validation was performed; the study relies solely on numerical simulations.
