Torsional and axial tension/compression fatigue behavior of Ti-6Al-4V diamond lattices produced by Laser Powder Bed Fusion (LPBF)

Suresh D. Meshram, Elina Vanderhenst, Antonio Cutolo, Brecht Van Hooreweder International Journal of Fatigue 2025-10-06 10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2025.109320
单位: KU Leuven, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Celestijnenlaan 300, Leuven, 3001, Belgium
通讯: Suresh D. Meshram @ KU Leuven, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Celestijnenlaan 300, Leuven, 3001, Belgium
相关性: 本文研究了LPBF制造的Ti-6Al-4V金刚石点阵结构的扭转和轴向疲劳行为，属于L1层核心内容。
边界: Focuses on lattice structures rather than bulk Ti-6Al-4V mechanical properties; no parametric study of process variables or anisotropy.
动机· 表征LPBF Ti-6Al-4V点阵结构的扭转疲劳性能
方法· 设计独特试样进行扭转和轴向疲劳试验，并提出局部应力法
结果· 拉伸模式对疲劳寿命最不利，局部应力法可统一不同载荷模式数据
验证· 通过扭转和轴向疲劳试验及断口分析验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study aims to characterize the torsional fatigue behavior of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V diamond lattice structures for load-bearing applications.
Method· A unique sample design minimizes stress concentration and enables consistent torsional fatigue testing; axial tension/compression tests and an analytical local stress method (LSM) are also employed.
Result· Tensile loading is most detrimental to fatigue life, and the local stress method collapses axial and torsional fatigue data onto a single curve.
Validation· Experimental fatigue tests under various loading modes combined with fractographic analysis validate the findings.

Effect of Cutting Parameters and Cooling Conditions on Surface Roughness and Tool Wear in Turning Ti-6Al-4V Titanium Alloy on an All-Gear Lathe: A Full Factorial Experimental Study

D. Kumar, A. Murugarajan, M. Prabhu, K. Pratheesh Journal of Materials Engineering and Performance 2025-10-06 10.1007/s11665-025-12293-0
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, JCT College of Engineering and Technology, Coimbatore, India
通讯: D. Kumar @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, JCT College of Engineering and Technology, Coimbatore, India
相关性: 该论文研究的是Ti-6Al-4V的车削加工，不涉及增材制造，因此与用户关注的增材制造Ti-6Al-4V力学与疲劳性能无关。
边界: The paper investigates turning (subtractive machining) of Ti-6Al-4V, not additive manufacturing (LPBF/EBM/DED), and thus does not address AM process-structure-property relationships or fatigue behavior of AM Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 研究车削参数和冷却条件对Ti-6Al-4V表面粗糙度和刀具磨损的影响
方法· 采用全因子实验设计在普通车床上进行Ti-6Al-4V车削实验
结果· 切削速度和冷却条件对表面粗糙度和刀具磨损有显著影响
验证· 通过实测表面粗糙度和刀具磨损数据进行验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effects of cutting parameters and cooling conditions on surface roughness and tool wear during turning of Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· A full factorial experimental design was employed on an all-gear lathe to conduct turning experiments on Ti-6Al-4V.
Result· Cutting speed and cooling condition significantly influence surface roughness and tool wear.
Validation· Experimental measurements of surface roughness and tool wear were used for validation.

An Experimental Investigation of the Shape Deviation of SLM Printed Parts

Thai‐Son Nguyen, Ngoc-Hien Tran Engineering Technology & Applied Science Research 2025-10-06 10.48084/etasr.12777
单位: Center for Transport Science and Technology, University of Transport and Communications, Hanoi, Vietnam | National Research Institute of Mechanical Engineering, Hanoi, Vietnam
通讯: Thai‐Son Nguyen @ Center for Transport Science and Technology, University of Transport and Communications, Hanoi, Vietnam | National Resea
相关性: 该论文研究SLM打印参数对Ti6Al4V零件形状偏差的影响，不涉及任何后处理工艺，因此与用户关注的后处理对疲劳/力学性能的影响无关。
边界: No post-processing treatment is studied; the paper focuses on as-built shape deviation from printing parameters, which belongs to Layer 1 process optimization.
动机· 探究SLM打印参数对Ti6Al4V零件形状偏差的影响
方法· 通过实验建立回归模型，分析激光功率、速度、层厚对形状偏差的关系
结果· 确定了最优打印参数组合以最小化形状偏差
验证· 实验测量形状偏差，回归模型验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the influence of SLM printing parameters on the shape deviation of Ti6Al4V parts.
Method· Experimental regression modeling relating laser power, scanning speed, and layer thickness to shape deviation.
Result· An optimal set of printing parameters was determined to minimize shape deviation.
Validation· Experimental measurement of shape deviation validated the regression model.
