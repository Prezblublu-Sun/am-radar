Study on Fatigue Behavior and Life Prediction of Laser Powder Bed Fused Ti6Al4V Alloy at 400 °C

Liangliang Wu, Ruida Xu, Jiaming Zhang, Huichen Yu, Zehui Jiao Materials 2025-12-18 10.3390/ma18245678
单位: Beijing Key Laboratory of Aeronautical Materials Testing and Evaluation, Aero Engine Corporation of China Key Laboratory of Materials Testing and Evaluation, AECC Beijing Institute of Aeronautical Mat
通讯: Liangliang Wu @ Beijing Key Laboratory of Aeronautical Materials Testing and Evaluation, Aero Engine Corporation of China Key Laboratory  |  Zehui Jiao @ Beijing Key Laboratory of Aeronautical Materials Testing and Evaluation, Aero Engine Corporation of China Key Laboratory
相关性: 该论文直接研究了激光粉末床熔融Ti6Al4V合金在400°C下的高周疲劳行为，完全符合L1方向的力学与疲劳性能要求。
边界: The paper is fully in scope; no missing elements relative to the direction focus.
动机· 研究SLM Ti6Al4V在400°C下不同应力比的高周疲劳行为及寿命预测。
方法· 开展高周疲劳试验并观察断口，结合疲劳与蠕变损伤建立复合模型。
结果· 表面/亚表面缺陷成为裂纹源，高应力比下疲劳分散性降低，复合模型预测精度最佳。
验证· 将模型预测寿命与实际试验结果对比验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the high-cycle fatigue behavior of selective laser melted Ti6Al4V alloy at 400°C under various stress ratios for aerospace applications.
Method· High-cycle fatigue tests were conducted at stress ratios of -1 to 0.8, fracture surfaces were observed, and combined fatigue-creep damage models were developed using linear damage law.
Result· Surface/subsurface defects acted as crack initiation sites; fatigue life dispersion decreased with increasing stress ratio, and the composite model provided the best life prediction.
Validation· Predicted fatigue lives from single and composite models were compared against experimental data to identify the most accurate model.

Influence of heat treatments and hot isostatic processes (HIP) on microstructure and mechanical behavior of Ti-6Al-4V alloy produced by Powder Bed Fusion Using Electron Beam (PBF-EB)

Mutlu Karasoglu, Evren Yasa, Evren Tan, Sina Kavak, Emrecan Söylemez et al. Kovove Materialy-Metallic Materials 2025-12-18 10.31577/km.2025.5-6.331
相关性: 该论文直接研究了Ti-6Al-4V合金经热处理和热等静压后的微观结构和力学性能，完全符合后处理控制方向。
边界: No fatigue testing results; only tensile and microstructural characterization.
动机· 研究热处理和热等静压对PBF-EB制备Ti-6Al-4V微观结构和力学性能的影响。
方法· 进行固溶时效处理和不同HIP循环，分析孔隙率、显微组织和拉伸性能。
结果· HIP显著降低孔隙率，800°C/200 MPa HIP实现最佳强度与延展性平衡。
验证· 通过密度测量、金相分析和拉伸试验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how post-processing heat treatments and hot isostatic pressing modify the microstructure and mechanical behavior of Ti-6Al-4V fabricated by electron beam powder bed fusion.
Method· Solution treatment and aging, as well as HIP cycles at various temperatures and pressures, were applied, followed by porosity analysis, microstructural characterization, and tensile testing.
Result· HIP effectively reduced porosity; the 800°C/200 MPa cycle achieved the best combination of strength (yield 948 MPa, UTS 1013 MPa) and ductility, while super-transus HIP caused strength loss due to lath coarsening.
Validation· Experimental validation via relative density measurements, optical microscopy/SEM, and uniaxial tensile tests.

Microstructural evolution and globularization in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V via cyclic heat treatment

Mahsa Khorshidi, Akbar Heidarzadeh, Faseeulla Khan Mohammad, Quadir Moinuddin Syed Journal of Adhesion Science and Technology 2025-12-18 10.1080/01694243.2025.2601285
单位: Department of Materials Engineering, Azarbaijan Shahid Madani University
通讯: Akbar Heidarzadeh @ Department of Materials and Metallurgy, Faculty of Mechanical and Energy Engineering, Shahid Beheshti University  |  Faseeulla Khan Mohammad @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, College of Engineering, King Faisal University
相关性: 该论文研究了循环热处理对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V微观组织演变和球化行为的影响，属于典型的后处理调控研究。
边界: No direct measurement of fatigue or mechanical properties after post-processing; focuses solely on microstructural evolution.
动机· 探究循环热处理对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V微观组织演变和球化行为的影响。
方法· 对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V进行循环热处理，利用SEM和EBSD表征微观组织。
结果· 观察到循环热处理后组织发生球化，微观结构逐渐演变。
验证· 通过微观组织表征验证循环热处理效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the microstructural evolution and globularization behavior of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V subjected to cyclic heat treatment.
Method· Cyclic heat treatment was applied to additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V samples, and microstructural characterization was performed using SEM and EBSD.
Result· Cyclic heat treatment induced globularization and significant microstructural evolution in the material.
Validation· The post-processing effect was validated through microstructural characterization without mechanical property testing.

Laser Remelting of Biocompatible Ti-Based Glass-Forming Alloys: Microstructure, Mechanical Properties, and Cytotoxicity

Aleksandra Małachowska, Wiktoria Drej, Agnieszka Rusak, Tomasz Kozieł, Denis A. Pikulski et al. Materials 2025-12-18 10.3390/ma18245687
单位: Center for Materials Engineering and Metal Forming, Wroclaw University of Science and Technology, Wybrzez Wyspianskiego 27, 50-370 Wroclaw, Poland
通讯: Aleksandra Małachowska @ Center for Materials Engineering and Metal Forming, Wroclaw University of Science and Technology, Wybrzez Wyspianskiego
相关性: 该论文研究的是非Ti-6Al-4V的钛基非晶合金，与用户方向不匹配。
边界: The paper does not study Ti-6Al-4V alloy; it investigates novel Ti-based glass-forming alloys without Cu/Ni/Be, thus falling outside the required material scope.
动机· 研究不含有毒元素的钛基非晶合金，用于增材制造。
方法· 电弧熔炼加吸铸获得合金，再用SLM系统进行单道激光重熔。
结果· 未形成非晶，获得α/β-Ti晶体基体，裂纹密度随激光能量增加而减少。
验证· 力学性能测试和ISO 10993-5细胞毒性实验。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop biocompatible Ti-based glass-forming alloys without toxic elements for additive manufacturing.
Method· Arc melting and suction casting followed by single-track laser remelting using an SLM system.
Result· Fully crystalline α/β-Ti matrices with silicides, no amorphous structure, and crack density reduced at higher laser energy.
Validation· Mechanical property characterization and ISO 10993-5 cytotoxicity tests.
