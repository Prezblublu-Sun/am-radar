Passive Behavior of Ti-6Al-4V Alloy Fabricated Using Pulsed Arc Additive Manufacturing

Longfei Song, Yang Chao, Bokai Liao, Yipeng Wang, Benli Luan Journal of Materials Engineering and Performance 2025-11-13 10.1007/s11665-025-12747-5
单位: Eastern Institute for Advanced Study, Eastern Institute of Technology, Ningbo, 315200, China
通讯: Yipeng Wang @ College of Materials Science and Engineering, Beijing University of Technology, Beijing, 100124, China  |  Benli Luan @ Department of Chemistry, Western University, London, ON, N6A 5B7, Canada
相关性: 该论文涉及增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的钝化行为，而非力学或疲劳性能，与本方向聚焦点不一致。
边界: The paper focuses on corrosion/passive behavior rather than mechanical or fatigue properties, and contains no tensile, fatigue, or other mechanical characterization data.
动机· 研究脉冲电弧增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的钝化行为
方法· 通过电化学测试分析不同工艺条件下的钝化膜特性
结果· 发现增材制造试样的钝化行为与锻造态存在差异
验证· 电化学实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the passive behavior of Ti-6Al-4V alloy fabricated by pulsed arc additive manufacturing to understand its corrosion resistance.
Method· Electrochemical tests were conducted to characterize the passive film properties under different processing conditions.
Result· The passive behavior of the additively manufactured specimens differed from that of wrought counterparts.
Validation· Experimental electrochemical validation.

MATAI: A Generalist Machine Learning Framework for Property Prediction and Inverse Design of Advanced Alloys

Yanchen Deng, Chendong Zhao, Yixuan Li, Bijun Tang, Xinrun Wang et al. arXiv preprint 2025-11-13 link
相关性: 该论文并非专门针对Ti-6Al-4V的力学或疲劳性能研究，而是提出通用机器学习框架，仅以钛基合金为例验证，不满足方向聚焦要求。
边界: The paper does not focus on Ti-6Al-4V; it presents a general ML framework for alloy design with a Ti-based alloy case study, not a dedicated mechanical/fatigue characterization of Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 利用机器学习加速先进合金的发现
方法· 集成数据库、深度神经网络预测器和约束优化框架
结果· 在钛基合金中快速设计出性能优于TC4的候选合金
验证· 实验验证设计合金优于商业参考材料
English summary & terms
Motivation· To accelerate discovery of advanced metallic alloys by overcoming vast composition spaces and competing objectives.
Method· A machine learning framework integrating curated database, multi-task neural network predictors, constraint-aware optimization, and iterative feedback loop.
Result· Identified Ti-based alloy candidates with lower density, higher strength, and appreciable ductility within seven iterations.
Validation· Experimental confirmation that designed alloys outperform commercial TC4 reference.
