Exploring the Potential of Superplastic Forming of Ti-6Al-4V Tailored Machined Blanks for Lightweight Design

Marco Bucconi, Elangovan Parameswaran, Diego González Key engineering materials 2025-11-18 10.4028/p-lar54a
单位: University of Strathclyde
通讯: Marco Bucconi @ University of Strathclyde
相关性: 该论文主要研究超塑性成形工艺，未涉及Ti-6Al-4V后处理对疲劳性能的影响。
边界: No investigation of post-processing effect on fatigue or mechanical performance; no mechanical testing (fatigue/tensile), no microstructure analysis, no comparison of as-built vs. post-processed properties.
动机· 开发成本效益高的轻量化Ti-6Al-4V部件成形方法
方法· 通过数控加工制备变厚度板材，然后进行超塑性成形，并用有限元模拟指导设计
结果· 成功成形三种设计案例，有限元与实验吻合良好，验证了可行性
验证· 超塑性成形实验与有限元模拟对比验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a cost-effective method for manufacturing lightweight Ti-6Al-4V components via superplastic forming of tailored machined blanks.
Method· CNC machining was used to create variable thickness sheets, which were then superplastically formed; finite element simulations guided the blank design.
Result· Three industrial design examples were successfully formed, showing good agreement between FE predictions and experiments, demonstrating feasibility for weight reduction.
Validation· The approach was validated by comparing experimental forming trials with finite element simulations.

Development and Characterization of Biogenic Hydroxyapatite Coatings Derived from Crab Shell Waste on Ti6Al4V Substrates

单位: Materials Engineering Program, Federal Institute of Education, Science and Technology of Pará—IFPA, Avenida Almirante Barroso, 1155, Marco, Belém CEP 66093-020, PA, Brazil
通讯: Douglas Santos Silva @ Military Institute of Engineering—IME, Department of Materials Science, Praça General Tibúrcio, 80, Praia Vermelha, Urca
相关性: 该论文研究蟹壳废料制备的羟基磷灰石涂层在Ti6Al4V基体上的开发与表征，与增材制造Ti6Al4V的机械或疲劳性能无关。
边界: This paper does not address mechanical or fatigue performance of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it focuses on coating synthesis from waste shells and coating characterization on a non-AM Ti6Al4V substrate, lacking any AM process, fatigue testing, or mechanical property evaluation.
动机· 开发基于海洋生物废弃物的生物活性涂层用于钛基植入体
方法· 从蟹壳制备羟基磷灰石并通过水相合成涂覆在Ti6Al4V基体上
结果· 获得连续多孔的纳米晶涂层，Ca/P比约1.85-1.88，硬度约35 HRC
验证· 通过SEM、EDS、XRF、XRD等表征涂层形貌、成分和物相
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a bioactive coating from marine biowaste for titanium implant applications.
Method· Biogenic hydroxyapatite was synthesized from crab shell waste and deposited onto Ti6Al4V substrates with/without thermochemical pretreatment.
Result· Continuous nanocrystalline coatings ~95-98 µm thick with Ca/P ratio 1.85-1.88 and Rockwell C hardness ~35 HRC were obtained.
Validation· Coating morphology, composition, and phase were characterized by SEM, EDS, XRF, and XRD.

Application of Machine Learning for Predicting Surface Roughness (Ra) in Abrasive Water Jet Machined Ti-6Al-4V MMCs

L Torrealba M, M. Balasubramanian, S Priyadarsini, P.K.Devan, PUsha Rani et al. 2025-11-18 10.1109/icaaid68975.2025.11358319
单位: Saveetha Institute of Medical and Technical Sciences,Saveetha School of Engineering,Chennai,Tamil Nadu,India
通讯: L Torrealba M @ Saveetha Institute of Medical and Technical Sciences,Saveetha School of Engineering,Chennai,Tamil Nadu,India
相关性: 本文涉及Ti-6Al-4V金属基复合材料的后处理（磨料水射流加工），但非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V，且未研究疲劳或力学性能。
边界: The paper does not address additive-manufactured Ti-6Al-4V, focuses on MMCs and surface roughness prediction, with no evaluation of fatigue or mechanical performance.
动机· 预测磨料水射流加工Ti-6Al-4V金属基复合材料的表面粗糙度。
方法· 利用随机森林、决策树和XGBoost等机器学习模型预测表面粗糙度。
结果· 决策树模型预测精度最高，MAE为0.0260，R²为0.9626。
验证· 通过实验测量的表面粗糙度数据验证模型预测。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The motivation is to develop a predictive model for surface roughness (Ra) of Ti-6Al-4V metal matrix composites machined by abrasive water jet machining (AWJM).
Method· Machine learning algorithms (Random Forest, Decision Tree, XGBoost) are applied to predict Ra based on an L18 orthogonal array of machining parameters.
Result· Decision Tree achieves the highest prediction accuracy with the lowest MAE (0.0260) and highest R² (0.9626).
Validation· Experimental Ra measurements from AWJM holes are used to validate the machine learning predictions.
