Investigation of chessboard scanning strategy in selective laser melting of ti6al4v

Aqlima Putri, Sugeng Supriadi, Bambang Suharno Proceeding SNTTM BKS-TM Indonesia 2026-03-09 10.71452/j8jemj90
单位: Universitas Indonesia
通讯: Aqlima Putri @ Universitas Indonesia
相关性: 研究Ti6Al4V的棋盘扫描策略，但未涉及力学性能或疲劳行为，与用户方向关联较弱。
边界: No mechanical or fatigue testing data is reported; the paper focuses solely on surface morphology and porosity characterization without any tensile, yield, fatigue, or other mechanical property evaluation.
动机· 研究棋盘扫描策略对SLM成形Ti6Al4V的影响。
方法· 使用恒定参数制备圆柱试样，进行表面形貌和孔隙率表征。
结果· 试样几何精度好，表面粗糙度Ra=4.275µm，存在熔合不良孔隙。

English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the effect of the chessboard scanning strategy on the quality of SLM-fabricated Ti6Al4V parts.
Method· Cylindrical specimens were fabricated under constant process parameters, and surface morphology and porosity were characterized using profilometry and SEM.
Result· The fabricated geometry showed good dimensional accuracy, with an average surface roughness Ra of 4.275 ± 0.655 µm and lack-of-fusion porosity detected.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed via surface roughness measurements and scanning electron microscopy.

The property of a root-extended Ti-6Al-4V endocrown utilizing 3D printing for a four-wall defect in endodontically treated molars: An in vitro study

Yu Chen, Tingting Song, Kexin Chen, Ying Xu, Lei Lei et al. BMC Oral Health 2026-03-09 10.1186/s12903-026-08012-3
单位: State Key Laboratory of Oral Diseases & National Center for Stomatology & National Clinical Research Center for Oral Diseases & Frontier Innovation Center for Dental Medicine Plus, Department of Preve
通讯: R. Holland Cheng @ State Key Laboratory of Oral Diseases & National Center for Stomatology & National Clinical Research Center for Oral Dis
相关性: 该论文涉及3D打印Ti-6Al-4V在牙科修复中的应用，而非Ti-6Al-4V的基础力学与疲劳性能研究。
边界: This paper focuses on clinical dental restoration application of Ti-6Al-4V endocrowns, not on base mechanical or fatigue characterization of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V material.
动机· 修复严重缺损磨牙的挑战
方法· 使用3D打印Ti-6Al-4V根延伸端冠与树脂修复比较
结果· 3D打印Ti-6Al-4V端冠具有更高的断裂强度和更低的微渗漏
验证· 体外力学测试和微渗漏评估
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare mechanical and marginal properties of 3D-printed Ti-6Al-4V root-extended endocrowns with direct resin composites for molars with severe defects.
Method· Forty-two extracted molars were restored with either 3D-printed Ti-6Al-4V endocrowns or resin composites, then subjected to thermocycling and mechanical loading.
Result· The Ti-6Al-4V endocrowns exhibited significantly higher fracture resistance and superior marginal integrity compared to resin composites.
Validation· Fracture resistance testing, microleakage staining, SEM evaluation, and micro-CT analysis.

Pengembangan gear dengan proses additive manufacturing berbasis lattice structure dengan dimensi variasional

Vynna Alviolina Indriyana, Gandjar Kiswanto, Ridho Irwansyah Proceeding SNTTM BKS-TM Indonesia 2026-03-09 10.71452/rpb15d50
单位: University of Indonesia
通讯: Vynna Alviolina Indriyana @ University of Indonesia
相关性: 该论文研究的是齿轮的轻量化设计，与股骨柄植入物的结构设计无关。
边界: This paper does not address femoral stem design, implant biomechanics, stress shielding, or any orthopedic application; it focuses on gear weight reduction.
动机· 减轻齿轮重量而不降低强度是机械设计中的关键挑战。
方法· 采用K-means算法优化变径金刚石线立方晶格，结合有限元应力分布数据设计多材料齿轮。
结果· 与实体模型相比，齿轮质量减少34.8%，应力低于材料极限强度。
验证· 通过ANSYS显式动力学和疲劳仿真进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Reducing gear weight without compromising strength is a key engineering challenge in mechanical systems.
Method· A multi-material gear with a diamond wire cube lattice of variable strut diameters was designed, optimized using stress distribution from FEA and K-means clustering.
Result· The lattice gear achieved a 34.8% mass reduction compared to a solid model while keeping stresses below ultimate tensile strength.
Validation· Structural and fatigue simulations in ANSYS Explicit Dynamics confirmed the design's feasibility and service life.
