A design framework for additively manufactured Ti–6Al–4V cervical corpectomy cage combining standardized topology-optimized core and customized lattice interfaces

Shih-Chieh Shen, S. H. Huang, Chiao-Min Chang, Hao Wang, CH Lin Journal of Materials Research and Technology 2026-03-09 10.1016/j.jmrt.2026.03.079
单位: Department of Biomedical Engineering, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, Taipei, Taiwan
通讯: CH Lin @ Department of Biomedical Engineering, Innovation & Translation Center of Medical Device, National Yang Ming Chaio Tung U
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V增材制造颈椎融合器的力学与疲劳性能测试，提供了相关实验数据，但主要聚焦于植入物设计而非基础材料性能。
边界: The paper focuses on implant-specific design and testing (cervical corpectomy cage) rather than fundamental as-built or process-state mechanical/fatigue characterization of Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 结合拓扑优化与点阵结构设计钛合金颈椎融合器，提升力学与骨整合性能。
方法· 有限元分析与拓扑优化设计核心，患者CT数据定制点阵接触面，按ASTM标准进行力学测试。
结果· 融合器轴向刚度7397 N/mm，屈服载荷8250 N，疲劳寿命超500万次，抗下沉刚度476 N/mm。
验证· 静态压缩、疲劳、下沉实验及细胞活性测试验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a Ti-6Al-4V cervical corpectomy cage combining topology-optimized load-bearing core and patient-specific lattice interfaces for enhanced mechanical performance and osseointegration.
Method· Finite element analysis, topology optimization under triaxial loading, and CT-based lattice design were employed, followed by mechanical testing per ASTM F2077 and F2267 standards.
Result· The cage showed axial stiffness of 7397.93 N/mm, yield load of 8250.79 N, survived 5 million cycles in combined axial-torsional fatigue, and exhibited subsidence stiffness 1.85 times higher than ISO benchmark.
Validation· Experimental validation through static compression, fatigue, subsidence tests, and in vitro cell viability assays.

Investigation of chessboard scanning strategy in selective laser melting of ti6al4v

Aqlima Putri, Sugeng Supriadi, Bambang Suharno Proceeding SNTTM BKS-TM Indonesia 2026-03-09 10.71452/j8jemj90
单位: Universitas Indonesia
通讯: Aqlima Putri @ Universitas Indonesia
相关性: 该论文研究了Ti6Al4V的SLM棋盘扫描策略对表面粗糙度和孔隙的影响，但未涉及任何力学或疲劳性能，与L1方向的核心关注点关联较弱。
边界: This paper does not report any mechanical or fatigue properties (tensile, fatigue, hardness) or modeling; it only focuses on scanning strategy effects on surface roughness and porosity without linking to mechanical performance.
动机· 研究SLM中棋盘扫描策略对Ti6Al4V成形质量的影响。
方法· 采用棋盘扫描策略制造圆柱试样，进行表面粗糙度和SEM分析。
结果· 表面粗糙度Ra=4.275±0.655µm，观察到熔合不良孔隙。
验证· 通过表面形貌和SEM实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of chessboard scanning strategy on the quality of SLM-fabricated Ti6Al4V parts.
Method· Cylindrical Ti6Al4V specimens were fabricated using the chessboard scanning strategy and characterized by surface roughness measurements and SEM.
Result· The average surface roughness was 4.275 ± 0.655 µm, and lack-of-fusion porosity was observed.
Validation· The findings were validated through experimental surface characterization and SEM analysis.

The property of a root-extended Ti-6Al-4V endocrown utilizing 3D printing for a four-wall defect in endodontically treated molars: An in vitro study

Yu Chen, Tingting Song, Kexin Chen, Ying Xu, Lei Lei et al. BMC Oral Health 2026-03-09 10.1186/s12903-026-08012-3
单位: State Key Laboratory of Oral Diseases & National Center for Stomatology & National Clinical Research Center for Oral Diseases & Frontier Innovation Center for Dental Medicine Plus, Department of Preve
通讯: R. Holland Cheng @ State Key Laboratory of Oral Diseases & National Center for Stomatology & National Clinical Research Center for Oral Dis
相关性: 本文研究了3D打印Ti-6Al-4V牙冠的力学性能，但侧重于牙科修复应用而非基础力学/疲劳性能。
边界: Does not report fundamental mechanical or fatigue properties (e.g., tensile, S-N curves) of AM Ti-6Al-4V; focus is dental restorative performance rather than material characterization.
动机· 比较3D打印Ti-6Al-4V根延伸牙冠与树脂修复体在严重缺损磨牙中的力学性能。
方法· 体外实验，对42颗离体第三磨牙进行垂直加载断裂测试、微渗漏评估和SEM/Micro-CT分析。
结果· Ti-6Al-4V牙冠断裂强度显著高于树脂组（4682 vs 1334 N），微渗漏更低，边缘完整性更好。
验证· 通过断裂力学测试、染料渗入和显微成像验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare the mechanical properties and marginal integrity of 3D-printed Ti-6Al-4V root-extended endocrowns versus direct resin composite restorations in molars with severe defects.
Method· An in vitro study on 42 extracted third molars, assessing fracture resistance via vertical loading, microleakage by methylene blue staining, and marginal integrity by SEM and micro-CT.
Result· The Ti-6Al-4V endocrowns exhibited significantly higher fracture resistance (4682 vs 1334 N), lower microleakage, and better marginal integrity than resin restorations.
Validation· Mechanical testing, dye penetration, and microscopic imaging.

Pengembangan gear dengan proses additive manufacturing berbasis lattice structure dengan dimensi variasional

Vynna Alviolina Indriyana, Gandjar Kiswanto, Ridho Irwansyah Proceeding SNTTM BKS-TM Indonesia 2026-03-09 10.71452/rpb15d50
单位: University of Indonesia
通讯: Vynna Alviolina Indriyana @ University of Indonesia
相关性: 该论文研究的是齿轮而非股骨柄假体，不涉及骨科植入物结构设计或力学性能。
边界: The paper studies a multi-material gear, not a femoral/hip stem or orthopaedic implant; there is no biomechanical analysis, stress shielding, or implant-related performance evaluation.
动机· 为减轻齿轮重量同时保持强度，提出多材料点阵结构设计。
方法· 采用Ti-6Al-4V外齿轮和316L内齿轮，结合点阵结构和K-means聚类优化。
结果· 齿轮减重34.8%，应力和温度均在安全范围内。
验证· 通过有限元分析验证结构强度和疲劳寿命，无实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To achieve weight reduction in gears without sacrificing strength by employing additive manufacturing and lattice structures.
Method· A multi-material gear design with an outer Ti-6Al-4V ring and inner 316L support, incorporating a diamond wire cube lattice with variable strut diameters optimized via finite element analysis and K-means clustering.
Result· The optimized gear achieves a 34.8% mass reduction while maintaining stresses below the ultimate tensile strength of both materials and a maximum operating temperature of 64.1 °C.
Validation· Validation is performed through finite element analysis (static, fatigue, and thermal CFD) without experimental testing.
