Properties of Ti-6Al-4V Lattice Structures with In-Situ Alloying and Functional Gradient

Svitlana Kyrylakha Mechanics and Advanced Technologies 2025-12-29 10.20535/2521-1943.2025.9.4(107).339736
单位: Zaporizhzhya National Technical University
通讯: Svitlana Kyrylakha @ Zaporizhzhya National Technical University
相关性: 该论文直接研究Ti-6Al-4V增材制造晶格结构的力学性能，符合L1层对机械性能的关注。
边界: 论文未涉及疲劳性能，且主要聚焦晶格结构而非致密材料，对纯疲劳研究参考有限。
动机· 研究原位合金化和功能梯度对Ti-6Al-4V晶格结构力学性能的影响
方法· 采用SLM/LPBF制备试样，结合XRD、SEM、EBSD显微分析及FEM模拟
结果· 原位合金化提高强度（UTS达1050 MPa），功能梯度晶格改善应力传递和能量吸收
验证· 通过实验拉伸测试和显微组织分析验证模拟结果
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of in-situ alloying and functional gradient design on the mechanical properties of Ti-6Al-4V lattice structures fabricated by additive manufacturing.
Method· Specimens were fabricated by SLM/LPBF with controlled energy density and scanning parameters, characterized by XRD, SEM, EBSD, EDS, and simulated using finite element modeling.
Result· In-situ alloying with Cr stabilized β-phase and increased strength (UTS up to 1050 MPa, elongation 8–10%), while functionally graded lattices improved stress transfer and energy absorption.
Validation· Experimental mechanical testing and microstructural analysis validated the finite element predictions.

Stiffness and Damping Properties of LPBF-Fabricated TC4 Alloy Beams Incorporating Self-Sealing Powder and Lattice Structures

Yong Xu, Zheng Xiang, Chenyu Zhang, Jingang Tang, Xiaohui Ge et al. Metals 2025-12-29 10.3390/met16010040
单位: Institute of Systems Engineering, CAEP, Mianyang 621999, China
通讯: Jie Chen @ Institute of Machinery Manufacturing Technology, CAEP, Mianyang 621000, China  |  Shuke Huang @ Institute of Machinery Manufacturing Technology, CAEP, Mianyang 621000, China
相关性: 该论文直接研究了LPBF制造的TC4（Ti-6Al-4V）合金梁的刚度和阻尼性能，属于L1方向的力学表征。
边界: 论文未涉及疲劳寿命、S-N曲线或拉伸强度等典型力学/疲劳指标，而是聚焦于刚度和阻尼性能。
动机· 为实现LPBF自封粉末阻尼组件刚度和阻尼的协同提升。
方法· 设计制备含自封粉末和点阵结构的TC4梁，表征制造质量、力学和阻尼性能，建立静动态模型。
结果· 增大点阵支柱直径提高刚度，降低放大因子和结构阻尼比；含点阵梁在显著提升刚度的同时保持较大阻尼比。
验证· 通过振动测试和力学实验验证模型与性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To achieve synergistic improvement in stiffness and damping of LPBF-fabricated self-sealing powder damping components by incorporating lattice structures.
Method· Designed and fabricated TC4 alloy beams with self-sealing powder and lattice structures, characterized their manufacturing quality, mechanical properties, and damping performance, and developed static and dynamic models.
Result· Increasing lattice strut diameter increased stiffness while decreasing amplification factor and structural damping ratio; beams with lattice structures (0.6 mm and 0.9 mm strut diameters) showed significant stiffness improvement and maintained large damping ratios under 1g and 2g vibration inputs.
Validation· Experimental vibration and mechanical testing validated the models and performance improvements.

Microstructural evolution and performance optimization of Ti-6Al-4V coatings reinforced with in situ-synthesized TiC/TiBx by laser directed energy deposition

Wenlong Wang, Kai Zhang, Weijun Liu, Wenchao Xi, Zhuangzhuang Hou et al. Ceramics International 2025-12-29 10.1016/j.ceramint.2025.12.456
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Shenyang University of Technology, Shenyang, Liaoning 110870, China
通讯: Kai Zhang @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Shenyang University of Technology, Shenyang, Liaoning 110870, China  |  Weijun Liu @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Shenyang University of Technology, Shenyang, Liaoning 110870, China
相关性: 该论文涉及激光定向能量沉积制备Ti-6Al-4V涂层，与Ti-6Al-4V增材制造的机械和疲劳性能方向有相关性，但侧重于涂层和原位增强，而非基体本身的力学行为。
边界: This paper focuses on coatings with in-situ ceramic reinforcement, not on the base mechanical/fatigue properties of bulk Ti-6Al-4V produced by additive manufacturing.
动机· 通过原位合成TiC/TiBx增强相优化激光定向能量沉积Ti-6Al-4V涂层的性能。
方法· 采用激光定向能量沉积技术，在Ti-6Al-4V粉末中混合前驱体原位形成增强相。
结果· 分析了微观结构演变，并评估了涂层的力学性能（如硬度、耐磨性）得到优化。
验证· 通过微观表征和力学测试验证涂层性能提升。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To optimize the performance of Ti-6Al-4V coatings fabricated by laser directed energy deposition through in situ synthesis of TiC/TiBx reinforcements.
Method· Laser directed energy deposition was used with precursor powders to form TiC/TiBx phases in the Ti-6Al-4V matrix during deposition.
Result· Microstructural evolution was analyzed, and mechanical properties such as hardness and wear resistance were improved compared to unreinforced coatings.
Validation· Validation was performed through microstructural characterization and mechanical testing (e.g., hardness, wear tests).
