Influence of the Manufacturing Process and Dynamic Testing on the Residual Stress State of Additively Manufactured Ti-6Al-4V Lattice Structure Specimens

Lena Burger, Heinz Palkowski, S. Ulrich, Hadi Mozaffari-Jovein JOM 2025-07-08 10.1007/s11837-025-07508-7
单位: Institute for Applied Materials – Applied Materials Physics (IAM-AWP), Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, Hermann-von-Helmholtz-Platz 1, 76021, Karlsruhe, Germany
通讯: Lena Burger @ Institute for Applied Materials – Applied Materials Physics (IAM-AWP), Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, Hermann-von-He
相关性: 该论文研究Ti-6Al-4V点阵结构的残余应力，但不涉及股骨柄的具体设计或力学性能评估。
边界: No femoral stem design or implant-specific structural analysis; focuses on residual stress in generic lattice specimens.
动机· 研究增材制造Ti-6Al-4V点阵结构中残余应力的影响因素。
方法· 通过实验分析热处理和动态加载对残余应力的影响。
结果· 制造和动态测试显著影响残余应力状态。
验证· 实验测量残余应力。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To analyze the influence of manufacturing and dynamic testing on residual stress in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V lattice structures.
Method· Experimental investigation of heat treatment and dynamic loading effects on residual stress.
Result· Manufacturing and dynamic testing significantly affect the residual stress state.
Validation· Experimental measurement of residual stress.

Performance Evaluation of F1 Car Chassis Frame using Finite Element Analysis

Atif Ali Hashmi International Journal for Research in Applied Science and Engineering Technology 2025-07-08 10.22214/ijraset.2025.73032
相关性: 该论文未涉及增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的机械或疲劳性能，仅将其作为普通材料进行通用底盘仿真。
边界: This paper does not focus on additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it uses Ti-6Al-4V as one of several materials in a general FEA chassis study with no fatigue or AM-specific mechanical analysis.
动机· 评估F1赛车底盘结构性能
方法· CATIA建模与ANSYS静力分析，比较四种材料
结果· 碳纤维性能最佳，钛合金强度好但重量大
验证· 有限元仿真验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the structural performance of an F1 car chassis frame under frontal load.
Method· 3D modeling in CATIA and static structural FEA in ANSYS comparing Carbon Fiber, Aluminum 7075-T6, Titanium Alloy Ti-6Al-4V, and AISI 4130.
Result· Carbon fiber offers the best balance of stiffness and low deformation; titanium alloy exhibits good strength and ductility but higher weight.
Validation· Finite element simulation with a 15,000 N frontal load replicating crash conditions.
