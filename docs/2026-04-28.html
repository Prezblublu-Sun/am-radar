Microstructure, Defects, and Fatigue Response of High‐Strength Tool Steels

Katerina Chantziara, Jens Bergström, Dimitrios Nikas, Mikael Thomas Grehk steel research international 2026-04-28 10.1002/srin.202500730
单位: Department of Mechanical and Materials Engineering Karlstad University Karlstad Sweden
通讯: Katerina Chantziara @ Department of Mechanical and Materials Engineering Karlstad University Karlstad Sweden
相关性: 该论文研究工具钢的疲劳性能，未涉及生物医学植入物或骨科相关材料，与用户研究方向关联度低。
边界: This paper studies fatigue of tool steels (including AM) but does not address biomedical alloys, implant design, osseointegration, or any biomedical context.
动机· 探究高强工具钢中微观结构和缺陷对疲劳性能的影响。
方法· 对6种工具钢进行疲劳测试，并运用改进的Murakami模型分析缺陷分布。
结果· 细马氏体板条和最小缺陷尺寸分布带来最高疲劳强度（610 MPa）。
验证· 通过实验疲劳测试与模型预测对比验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Investigate the combined effect of microstructure and defects on fatigue performance of high-strength tool steels.
Method· Fatigue tests at R=-1 and 0.1 on six tool steels (including AM grade) with microstructure and defect analysis via modified Murakami model.
Result· The cold-work grade with finest martensitic lath size (0.71 μm) and smallest defect size (11 μm) achieved the highest fatigue strength (610 MPa).
Validation· Experimental fatigue data and model predictions are compared to validate findings.

First-Principles Study of Structural, Electronic, Thermal, and Optical Properties of Quasi-2D C2 N2 O Using GGA and HSE06

Hemn. G. H, Nzar. R. Abdullah, Vidar Gudmundsson arXiv preprint 2026-04-28 link
相关性: 该论文研究的是准二维材料C2N2O的电子、光学和热学性质，与增材制造力学性能无关。
边界: This paper does not involve additive manufacturing, mechanical testing, implant materials, or any biomedical context relevant to AM mechanical properties.
动机· 探索准二维C2N2O的结构稳定性及其电子、光学和热学性质
方法· 采用密度泛函理论(DFT)和从头算分子动力学(AIMD)进行计算分析
结果· 材料具有适中的间接带隙、显著的光吸收和极低的晶格热导率
验证· 基于理论计算，无实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the structural stability, electronic, optical, and thermal properties of the quasi-2D C2N2O material.
Method· First-principles density functional theory (DFT) and ab initio molecular dynamics (AIMD) simulations were employed.
Result· C2N2O exhibits semiconducting behavior with a moderate indirect band gap, strong visible-UV absorption, and extremely low lattice thermal conductivity of ~0.017 W/m·K at 300 K.
Validation· Validation is entirely computational with no experimental confirmation.

Tuning magnitude and direction of lattice thermal conductivity in transition metal dichalcogenide heterobilayers

Elliot Perviz, Antonio Cammarata arXiv preprint 2026-04-28 link
相关性: 该论文研究二维材料热导率，与增材制造机械性能或生物医学植入物无关。
边界: This paper does not address additive manufacturing, mechanical properties of implants, or any biomedical application.
动机· 研究二维材料异质双层中的晶格热导率调控机制。
方法· 采用第一性原理和线性化玻尔兹曼输运方程精确求解。
结果· 发现掺杂可调控热导率的各向异性和方向性。
验证· 基于理论计算的数值验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand nanoscale mechanisms governing lattice thermal conductivity in transition metal dichalcogenide heterobilayers.
Method· First-principles calculations with exact solution of linearized Boltzmann transport equation in phonon and relaxon bases.
Result· Doping reduces and anisotropizes thermal conductivity, enabling directional tuning of thermal transport.
Validation· First-principles theoretical validation.
