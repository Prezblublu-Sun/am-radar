Probabilistic Framework for Effective Wall Width Estimation in Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing

Tasbirul Zihan 2026-03-28 10.31224/6710
单位: University of Oklahoma
通讯: Tasbirul Zihan @ University of Oklahoma
相关性: 该论文关注Ti-6Al-4V线弧增材制造中的有效壁厚概率估计，但未涉及力学或疲劳性能，与用户对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V力学/疲劳性能的研究方向关联较弱。
边界: No mechanical or fatigue data reported; focuses on geometric estimation rather than mechanical characterization.
动机· 准确估计线弧增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的有效壁厚对尺寸一致性和结构完整性至关重要。
方法· 基于文献热物理和几何关系，结合蒙特卡洛模拟和重要性抽样，构建了概率不确定性量化框架。
结果· 通过量化不确定性，估计了有效壁厚的分布，并利用分位数阈值计算了失效概率。
验证· 仅采用数值模拟，未进行实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Accurate estimation of Effective Wall Width (EWW) in Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing (WAAM) is crucial for dimensional consistency and structural integrity.
Method· A probabilistic framework combining literature-based thermophysical and geometric relations with Monte Carlo simulation and importance sampling was developed to estimate EWW under uncertain process inputs.
Result· The framework provides uncertainty-aware EWW estimates and failure probability via quantile-based thresholding of simulated values.
Validation· No experimental validation was performed; the study is purely computational.
