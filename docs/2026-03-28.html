Cooling and Lubrication Strategies for Internal Grinding of Ti-6Al-4V: A Review with Emphasis on MQL-Cold CO2 Hybrid Cooling

Nguyen Thi Thu Dung Zenodo (CERN European Organization for Nuclear Research) 2026-03-28 10.5281/zenodo.19328563
单位: Thai Nguyen University of Technology
通讯: Nguyen Thi Thu Dung @ Thai Nguyen University of Technology
相关性: 综述了Ti-6Al-4V内圆磨削的冷却润滑策略，但未涉及后处理对疲劳或力学性能的影响。
边界: Does not investigate fatigue or mechanical performance of Ti-6Al-4V after grinding; focuses on machining process parameters and grindability.
动机· Ti-6Al-4V内圆磨削散热困难，需优化冷却润滑策略。
方法· 综述了MQL、低温冷却及混合冷却润滑方案。
结果· MQL与冷CO2混合方案最具潜力，但尚无最优方法。
验证· 基于已发表文献的数据总结，无实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The poor thermal conductivity and high chemical reactivity of Ti-6Al-4V cause severe grinding challenges, especially in confined internal grinding, necessitating a review of cooling and lubrication strategies.
Method· This review systematically analyzes multiple cooling/lubrication approaches for internal grinding of Ti-6Al-4V, including MQL/SQL, cryogenic cooling (LN2, CO2), and hybrid configurations.
Result· No single optimal method is established, but MQL combined with chilled CO2 shows promise for balancing lubrication and heat dissipation.
Validation· The review aggregates experimental findings from previous studies without presenting new experimental data.

Synergistic effects of TC4 and heat treatment on the microstructure, mechanical properties, and high-temperature oxidation properties of laser-deposited GH4169 alloys

Yeqing Wang, Jialong Liu, Xin Jiao, Rui Tan, Chunlong Cheng et al. Journal of Materials Research and Technology 2026-03-28 10.1016/j.jmrt.2026.03.248
相关性: 该论文研究了TC4增强GH4169合金的热处理效果，而非纯Ti-6Al-4V的后处理，与用户方向无关。
边界: The paper focuses on GH4169 Ni-based superalloy, not on Ti-6Al-4V; the post-processing is applied to the composite, not to pure Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 探索TC4添加和热处理对GH4169合金微观结构及性能的协同影响。
方法· 激光熔化沉积制备不同TC4含量的GH4169复合材料，并进行均匀化+双时效热处理。
结果· 热处理提升了5TC4/GH4169的屈服强度45%和延伸率28%，高温氧化增重降低9%。
验证· 通过微观结构表征、拉伸测试和800°C氧化实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the synergistic effect of TC4 addition and heat treatment on the microstructure and properties of GH4169 alloy.
Method· GH4169 composites with varying TC4 content were fabricated by laser melting deposition and subjected to homogenization plus dual aging heat treatment.
Result· The heat treatment increased yield strength by 45% and elongation by 28% in the 5TC4/GH4169 composite, and reduced high-temperature oxidation mass gain by 9%.
Validation· Microstructural analysis, tensile testing, and oxidation experiments at 800°C were conducted.
