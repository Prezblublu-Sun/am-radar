Influence of Supertransus Heat Treatment on the Microstructure and Compressive Performance of Additively Manufactured Ti6Al4V (ELI)

Mahendra Sukre, Anil Meena Engineering headway 2025-06-06 10.4028/p-hthw87
单位: Indian Institute of Technology Madras
通讯: Mahendra Sukre @ Indian Institute of Technology Madras
相关性: 该论文直接研究了超转态热处理对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V(ELI)微观结构和压缩性能的影响，与用户关注的后处理对力学性能的影响高度相关。
边界: No fatigue or tensile testing was performed; only compressive properties were evaluated.
动机· 探究超转态热处理对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V(ELI)压缩性能的影响。
方法· 进行超转态固溶时效处理，用OM和SEM表征微观结构，并实施压缩测试。
结果· 热处理降低了压缩强度但提高了延伸率，微观结构变化与性能相关。
验证· 通过压缩力学测试和微观结构观察进行实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of supertransus heat treatment on the compressive behavior of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V (ELI).
Method· Specimens were subjected to solution treatment and aging above the β-transus, with microstructure characterized by OM and SEM and compressive tests conducted.
Result· Heat treatment reduced compressive strength but improved elongation, with microstructural changes closely correlating to compressive properties.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed through compressive mechanical testing and microstructural analysis.

Achieving the strength-ductility balance through the control of the prior β-grain size in laser beam powder bed fusion of Ti-6Al-4V

Dina Fouad, Chinnapat Panwisawas, Honghao Ge, Yu‐Lung Chiu, Moataz M. Attallah Additive manufacturing 2025-06-06 10.1016/j.addma.2025.104842
相关性: 该论文研究了应力消除热处理后β晶粒尺寸对Ti-6Al-4V力学性能的影响，但并未对不同后处理路线进行比较。
边界: The paper focuses on controlling as-built prior-β grain size through process parameters, not on varying post-processing treatments to modify mechanical performance.
动机· 探索通过控制激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V的β晶粒尺寸来优化强度-延展性平衡。
方法· 改变热输入参数与扫描策略，经650°C/2小时应力消除处理后，使用EBSD和热-微观组织建模表征。
结果· 细小β晶粒在应力消除后提升延展性，能量密度不能单独预测微观组织。
验证· 通过力学测试和微观组织表征验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to control prior-β grain size in laser beam powder bed fusion of Ti-6Al-4V to improve the strength-ductility balance after stress relief.
Method· Heat input parameters and scanning strategies were varied, followed by a 650°C/2hrs stress-relief treatment, with microstructural characterization via EBSD and coupled thermal-microstructural modeling.
Result· Finer prior-β grain sizes led to higher ductility after stress relief, while energy density alone was insufficient to predict microstructure.
Validation· The findings are validated by mechanical testing (tensile) and microstructural analysis.

Further Studies into the Growth of Small Naturally Occurring Three-Dimensional Cracks in Additively Manufactured and Conventionally Built Materials

Shareen S. L. Chan, Daren Peng, Andrew Siao Ming Ang, Michael Nicholas, Victor K. Champagne et al. Crystals 2025-06-06 10.3390/cryst15060544
单位: ARC Industrial Transformation Training Centre on Surface Engineering for Advanced Materials, Faculty of Science, Engineering and Technology, Swinburne University of Technology, John Street, Hawthorn,
通讯: R. Jones @ ARC Industrial Transformation Training Centre on Surface Engineering for Advanced Materials, Faculty of Science, Enginee
相关性: 该论文主要涉及其他材料（CP-Ti、Inconel 718、M300钢）的裂纹扩展，未以Ti-6Al-4V为研究核心，对用户关注的方向帮助有限。
边界: The paper does not focus on Ti-6Al-4V; it extends crack growth predictions to other materials (CP-Ti, Inconel 718, M300 steel) without new experimental or computational results specific to Ti-6Al-4V mechanical/fatigue performance.
动机· 扩展预测增材制造和传统制造材料中自然三维裂纹生长的方法。
方法· 使用Hartman-Schijve裂纹扩展方程，基于长裂纹标准测试常数进行分析。
结果· 成功预测了WAAM CP-Ti、Inconel 718和M300钢的裂纹生长。
验证· 未提供新的实验验证，依赖前人长裂纹数据。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study aims to extend the capability of predicting naturally occurring three-dimensional crack growth to additively manufactured and conventionally built materials beyond previously studied Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· The Hartman-Schijve crack growth equation is employed using constants derived from ASTM E647 long-crack tests and a fixed threshold term of 0.1 MPa√m.
Result· Crack growth predictions are demonstrated for WAAM CP-Ti, WAAM Inconel 718, and conventionally built M300 steel.
Validation· No new experimental validation is provided; predictions rely on previously established long-crack data from other studies.

A Study on the Mechanism of the Effect of Low‐Frequency High‐Density Pulse Current on the Strength and Ductility of TC4 Titanium Alloy

Ping Li, Baisong Liu, Shaofeng Liu, Siliang Yan, Kemin Xue Advanced Engineering Materials 2025-06-06 10.1002/adem.202500552
单位: School of Materials Science and Engineering Hefei University of Technology Hefei 230009 China
通讯: Siliang Yan @ School of Materials Science and Engineering Hefei University of Technology Hefei 230009 China
相关性: 该论文研究的是传统TC4钛合金的电辅助成形，而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学性能，与本方向无关。
边界: Not about additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; paper studies electrically assisted forming of wrought TC4, with no AM content.
动机· 研究低频高密度脉冲电流对TC4合金强度和塑性的影响机制。
方法· 在不同应变率下进行高密度脉冲电流拉伸实验，结合第一性原理计算。
结果· 脉冲电流可降低流变应力，提高延伸率，改变位错结构并降低空位形成能。
验证· 实验与第一性原理计算相结合进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the mechanism by which low-frequency high-density pulse current affects the strength and ductility of TC4 titanium alloy during tensile deformation.
Method· Tensile tests were conducted under high-density pulsed current at various strain rates, combined with first-principles calculations.
Result· Pulsed current reduced flow stress by 15-44%, increased elongation, altered dislocation structures, and lowered vacancy formation energy from 11.5 eV to 8.2 eV.
Validation· Experimental results were corroborated by first-principles calculations and characterization of fracture surfaces and dislocation configurations.
