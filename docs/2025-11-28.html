Adiabatic shear bands in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V under high strain rate and elevated temperature conditions

Hanna Czarise Regidor, Jubert Pasco, Kudakwashe Nyamuchiwa, Candy C. Mercado, Clodualdo Aranas Materials Today Communications 2025-11-28 10.1016/j.mtcomm.2025.114412
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of New Brunswick, Fredericton, New Brunswick, Canada
通讯: Hanna Czarise Regidor @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of New Brunswick, Fredericton, New Brunswick, Canada
相关性: 该论文研究了激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V在高应变率和高温下的动态力学行为，与层1的力学性能表征直接相关，但未涉及疲劳性能。
边界: The paper does not address fatigue life, S-N curves, or cyclic crack initiation/growth, which are central to the fatigue performance focus of this direction.
动机· 探究激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V在高应变率和高温下的动态力学行为。
方法· 使用分离式霍普金森压杆进行动态压缩实验，并用修正Johnson-Cook、Hensel-Spittel等模型进行本构建模。
结果· 高应变率下绝热剪切带导致模型预测偏差，修正Hensel-Spittel模型误差最小。
验证· 通过实验数据与微观结构表征验证本构模型精度。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study investigates the dynamic mechanical behavior of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V under high strain rates and elevated temperatures relevant to aerospace applications.
Method· High-temperature compressive Split-Hopkinson Pressure Bar tests were conducted at strain rates of 1000 and 2000 s⁻¹ across temperatures from 298 to 773 K, followed by constitutive modeling using modified Johnson-Cook, Hensel-Spittel, and modified Hensel-Spittel models.
Result· Adiabatic shear bands formed at high strain rates caused deviations in model predictions; the modified Hensel-Spittel model exhibited the lowest average absolute relative errors (5.51%) and highest correlation coefficient (0.95).
Validation· Experimental SHPB stress-strain data and microstructural characterization validated the constitutive model predictions.

The mode I fracture behavior and the fracture toughness model of Ti6Al4V ELI titanium alloy with stable microstructure

Zhu Shi, Zuhan Cao, Xiaohui Shi Journal of Alloys and Compounds 2025-11-28 10.1016/j.jallcom.2025.185330
单位: College of Materials Science and Engineering, Taiyuan University of Technology, Taiyuan, 030024, China
通讯: Xiaohui Shi @ College of Materials Science and Engineering, Taiyuan University of Technology, Taiyuan, 030024, China
相关性: 该论文直接研究了Ti-6Al-4V ELI合金的断裂韧性，属于L1方向的力学性能核心内容。
边界: 论文未涵盖疲劳寿命或S-N曲线，但断裂韧性是力学性能的重要组成部分。
动机· 研究Ti6Al4V ELI合金稳定微观组织下的I型断裂行为，建立断裂韧性模型。
方法· 通过实验测试断裂韧性并基于微观结构建立模型。
结果· 获得了断裂韧性数据并验证了模型准确性。
验证· 通过实验数据验证模型。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the mode I fracture behavior of Ti6Al4V ELI alloy with stable microstructure and develop a fracture toughness model.
Method· Experimental fracture toughness tests were combined with microstructure analysis to construct a theoretical model.
Result· The model accurately predicted fracture toughness values validated by experimental data.
Validation· Experimental mechanical testing of fracture toughness for model validation.

Unveiling the Effect of Scanning Speed on the Corrosion and Tribological Performance of Electron Beam Melted (EBM) Ti-6Al-4V-ELI Alloy

Eurico Felix Pieretti, Davide Piaggio, Renato Altobelli Antunes, Mara Cristina Lopes de Oliveira, Luís Carlos Elias da Silva et al. Materials 2025-11-28 10.3390/ma18235367
单位: Center for Engineering, Modeling and Applied Social Sciences (CECS), Federal University of ABC (UFABC), Santo André 09210-580, Brazil
通讯: Eurico Felix Pieretti @ Center for Engineering, Modeling and Applied Social Sciences (CECS), Federal University of ABC (UFABC), Santo André 0921
相关性: 该论文研究了EBM Ti-6Al-4V-ELI的腐蚀和摩擦学性能，但未涉及机械强度或疲劳性能。
边界: No mechanical properties (tensile, fatigue, hardness) or fatigue data; focus is on corrosion and tribology.
动机· 探索EBM扫描速度对Ti-6Al-4V-ELI合金腐蚀和摩擦学性能的影响。
方法· 以五种扫描速度制备试样，进行干磨损、动电位极化及成纤维细胞粘附测试。
结果· 扫描速度影响钝化电流密度和耐磨性；中速试样磨损最浅但轨迹最宽。
验证· 通过实验测试验证扫描速度对性能的影响。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the influence of electron beam melting scan speed on the corrosion and tribological performance of Ti-6Al-4V-ELI for biomedical applications.
Method· Specimens fabricated at five scanning speeds were subjected to reciprocating dry wear tests, open circuit potential and potentiodynamic polarization in Ringer's solution, and human fibroblast adhesion assays.
Result· Scan speed affected passive current density and wear resistance; an intermediate speed yielded minimal wear depth but widest track, while faster speeds increased corrosion susceptibility.
Validation· Experimental characterization including wear, electrochemical, and cell adhesion tests.

Effect of electroshock treatment on microstructure evolution of Ti-6Al-4V/Cu-Cr-Zr interface fabricated by laser melting deposition

Shiqiang Xie, Changlin Huang, Jiawei Gong, Pei Wang, Wen Yan et al. Engineering Science in Additive Manufacturing 2025-11-28 10.36922/esam025430030
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V与Cu-Cr-Zr的界面，而非纯Ti-6Al-4V的力学与疲劳性能。
边界: The paper focuses on a dissimilar metal interface (Ti-6Al-4V/Cu-Cr-Zr) and shear strength, not on Ti-6Al-4V base mechanical/fatigue characterization or modeling.
动机· 研究电冲击处理对Ti-6Al-4V/Cu-Cr-Zr界面组织演化的影响
方法· 对激光熔沉积样品进行电冲击处理，观察界面组织与剪切强度
结果· 电冲击后剪切强度提高65%，归因于晶间纳米析出和润湿性改善
验证· 通过剪切试验和显微组织表征验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of electroshock treatment on the microstructure evolution of Ti-6Al-4V/Cu-Cr-Zr interface fabricated by laser melting deposition.
Method· Electroshock treatment was applied to laser-melted-deposited Ti-6Al-4V/Cu-Cr-Zr samples, and interface microstructure and shear strength were characterized.
Result· Electroshock treatment increased shear strength by 65%, attributed to intergranular nano-precipitation and improved wettability.
Validation· Validation through shear mechanical testing and microstructural analysis.
