单位: Indian Institute of Technology , ,
通讯: Vipparla Srikanth @ Indian Institute of Technology , ,
相关性: 该论文只关注激光冲击强化对Ti-6Al-4V ELI润湿性的影响，未涉及力学性能（如疲劳、拉伸），与用户关注的后处理对力学性能的影响方向关联弱。
边界: The paper does not evaluate any mechanical property (fatigue, tensile, hardness) nor microstructure evolution affecting mechanical performance; it only reports wettability changes.
动机· 研究激光冲击强化对Ti Grade 23合金润湿性的影响以改善生物相容性。
方法· 改变激光冲击的扫描次数和水层高度，测量接触角变化。
结果· 接触角从65°增至90°，表面由亲水转变为疏水。
验证· 通过接触角实验测量验证润湿性转变。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how Laser Shock Peening (LSP) alters the wettability of Ti Grade 23 alloy for enhanced biocompatibility in biomedical applications.
Method· LSP was applied with varying number of passes and water levels, and wettability was characterized by contact angle measurements.
Result· The contact angle increased from 65° to 90°, indicating a transition from hydrophilic to hydrophobic surface behavior.
Validation· Validation was performed through direct experimental measurements of contact angle under different LSP parameters.

通讯: Rajesh Babu Tere @ Indian Institute of Technology , ,
相关性: 该论文研究微车削过程而非增材制造，未涉及增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学或疲劳性能。
边界: Paper studies micro-turning (subtractive) of Ti-6Al-4V, not additive manufacturing; no mechanical or fatigue characterization of AM parts.
动机· 开发微车削过程中Ti-6Al-4V表面粗糙度的实时检测方法。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a real-time, non-contact surface roughness measurement method for micro-turning of Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Machine vision with GLCM texture features and machine learning models (RFR, RBFNN, SVR, XGBoost) are used to predict surface roughness.
Result· SVR achieved best prediction with MSE 0.032 and R2 0.84.
Validation· Predicted roughness values are compared with contact profilometer measurements.

单位: Central Manufacturing Technology Institute , ,
通讯: Sarmistha Dhan @ Central Manufacturing Technology Institute , ,
相关性: 论文研究Ti6Al4V基体上DLC涂层的附着力，不涉及增材制造或力学/疲劳性能，与用户方向无关。
边界: Paper does not concern additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it uses conventional substrates and focuses on coating adhesion, not mechanical/fatigue characterization.
动机· 提高Ti6Al4V基体上类金刚石碳涂层的附着力
方法· 通过表面粗糙度分析和钛中间层沉积

English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the adhesion strength of diamond-like carbon coatings on Ti6Al4V substrates by tailoring surface roughness and introducing a titanium interlayer.
Method· Two Ti6Al4V substrates with distinct roughness (50 nm vs. 3.5 μm) were used, and a Ti interlayer was deposited via CVD before DLC coating by PECVD; adhesion was assessed via nanoindentation and wear tests.
Result· Optimal adhesion was achieved at a surface roughness of 3.5 μm, and the Ti interlayer further enhanced coating adhesion.
Validation· Mechanical testing (nanoindentation and wear testing) and Raman spectroscopy were used to characterize the coated samples.

Evaluation of Grain Size and Improvement of Residual Stresses Through the Heat Treatment in Ti-6Al-4V Alloy Using Electron Beam Welding

Anupam Kundu, Mrinmay Pal, Dilip Kumar Pratihar, Debalay Chakrabarti 2025-09-10 10.1115/imece-india2025-160236
单位: Indian Institute of Technology , ,
通讯: Anupam Kundu @ Indian Institute of Technology , ,
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V合金的焊后热处理，但研究对象是焊接而非增材制造，不符合L2方向要求的增材制造Ti-6Al-4V后处理控制。
边界: The paper uses electron beam welding to join Ti-6Al-4V, not additive manufacturing; therefore, it does not address post-processing of AM Ti-6Al-4V as required by Layer 2.
动机· 研究电子束焊接Ti-6Al-4V合金后的焊后热处理对晶粒尺寸和残余应力的改善效果。
方法· 通过电子束焊接制备Ti-6Al-4V对接接头，并进行焊后热处理，利用EBSD和残余应力分析表征。
结果· 焊后热处理显著降低了残余应力，且焊缝区晶粒尺寸细化为1.30-1.33 μm。
验证· 实验验证：EBSD和残余应力测量对比了焊态和热处理态。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the effect of post-weld heat treatment on grain size and residual stress in electron beam welded Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V butt joints were fabricated by electron beam welding and subjected to post-weld heat treatment, characterized by EBSD and residual stress analysis.
Result· Post-weld heat treatment significantly mitigated residual stresses, and the fusion zone exhibited fine grain sizes averaging 1.30-1.33 μm.
Validation· Experimental validation through EBSD and residual stress measurements comparing as-welded and heat-treated conditions.

Fabrication of Bio-Inspired Surface Texturing of Ti-6Al-4V ELI by Laser Induced Technique to Improve Wear Resistance and Controlled Wettability for Biomedical Application

Chitaranjan Kumar Yadav, G.L. Samuel 2025-09-10 10.1115/imece-india2025-160864
单位: Indian Institute of Technology , ,
通讯: Chitaranjan Kumar Yadav @ Indian Institute of Technology , ,
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的后处理（激光表面织构），但未关注疲劳或力学性能，与研究方向不符。
边界: This paper does not evaluate fatigue or tensile mechanical performance; it focuses on surface wettability and wear resistance, not on the effect of post-processing on mechanical properties of AM Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 通过激光表面织构控制Ti-6Al-4V的润湿性和耐磨性
方法· 使用超短脉冲激光在不同扫描角度下制备表面织构
结果· 织构使表面从亲水变为疏水，润湿性可调
验证· 接触角测量验证润湿性变化
English summary & terms
Motivation· To achieve controlled wettability and improved wear resistance on Ti-6Al-4V ELI surfaces via bio-inspired laser-induced texturing.
Method· Ultra-short pulse laser was used to create oblique textures at varying scanning angles (0°, 0°/90°, 0°/30°, 0°/45°) on Ti-6Al-4V ELI.
Result· The textured surface transitioned from hydrophilic to hydrophobic depending on texture type and laser parameters, indicating tunable wettability.
Validation· Wettability was validated through contact angle measurements; no mechanical testing was performed.
