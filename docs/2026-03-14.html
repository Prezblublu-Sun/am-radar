Lightweight phase-field surrogate for modelling ductile-to-brittle transition through phenomenological elastoplastic coupling

P G Kubendran Amos arXiv preprint 2026-03-14 link
相关性: 该论文研究了基于代理模型的相场断裂方法，但未涉及生物医学植入物或增材制造，与用户方向关联较弱。
边界: This paper does not include biomedical implants, additive manufacturing, or experimental validation; it is a computational study on a generic ductile-to-brittle transition problem.
动机· 降低韧性-脆性转变参数研究的计算成本。
方法· 提出轻量级相场代理模型，通过温度相关现象学机制模拟DBT。
结果· 模型在77-293K范围内复现了从脆性到韧性的系统转变趋势。
验证· 对单边缺口试样进行有限元模拟验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To reduce the computational cost of parametric studies on ductile-to-brittle transition in body-centred cubic systems.
Method· A lightweight phase-field surrogate is proposed, incorporating temperature-dependent degradation exponent, yield stress, elastic modulus, and fracture toughness to capture DBT-like trends within a standard isothermal two-field setting.
Result· Simulations of a single-edge-notched specimen from 77 to 293 K demonstrate systematic progression from brittle-like to ductile-like response, with robust qualitative trends across interpolation schemes.
Validation· The model is validated through numerical simulations of a single-edge-notched tensile specimen, comparing different temperature-dependent interpolation schemes.

Digital Hydrogen Platform (DigHyd): A Rigorously Curated Database for Hydrogen Storage Materials Empowered by AI-Assisted Literature Mining

Seong-Hoon Jang, Di Zhang, Xue Jia, Hung Ba Tran, Linda Zhang et al. arXiv preprint 2026-03-14 link
相关性: 该论文是关于氢存储材料数据库的，与增材制造力学性能无关。
边界: This paper focuses on hydrogen storage materials and thermodynamic properties, with no connection to additive manufacturing, mechanical properties, or biomedical implants.
动机· 氢存储材料缺乏大规模结构化数据集
方法· AI辅助文献挖掘结合人工验证构建数据库
结果· 数据库含超过3万条记录，并展示了组成-性能关系
验证· 使用符号回归和XGBoost模型验证数据一致性
English summary & terms
Motivation· The lack of large-scale, well-curated datasets hinders data-driven discovery in solid-state hydrogen storage materials.
Method· AI-assisted literature mining with human-in-the-loop validation was used to construct a database of over 30,000 entries from more than 4,000 literature sources.
Result· The database provides thermodynamic parameters and enables statistical analysis across material classes, with machine learning models showing predictive performance.
Validation· Symbolic regression and XGBoost models were used to verify internal consistency and learnable composition-property relationships.

Effects of uniaxial strain on monolayer transition-metal dichalcogenides revisited

Igor Evangelista, Abdul Saboor, Muhammad Zubair, Intuon Chatratin, Ruiqi Hu et al. arXiv preprint 2026-03-14 link
相关性: 该论文研究与增材制造、生物医学植入物或力学性能无关的二维材料电子结构。
边界: No additive manufacturing, no mechanical testing, no implant design, no biomedical context.
动机· 研究单层过渡金属二硫属化物在单轴应变下的能带结构变化。
方法· 使用含自旋轨道耦合的杂化密度泛函计算。
结果· 拉伸应变导致带隙减小，谷漂移，且间接带隙增大。
验证· 与实验光致发光强度下降趋势定性一致。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To accurately compute the strain evolution of the electronic band structure of monolayer transition-metal dichalcogenides for optoelectronic applications.
Method· Hybrid density functional calculations including spin-orbit coupling are employed to compute strain-dependent band gaps and valley shifts.
Result· Tensile uniaxial strain reduces the fundamental gap, induces valley drift from the K point, and increases indirectness, explaining photoluminescence decrease.
Validation· Theoretical results are compared qualitatively with experimental photoluminescence trends under uniaxial deformation.
