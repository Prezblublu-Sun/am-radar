Fabrication of robust high-aspect-ratio Ti-6Al-4V ultra-thin-walled structures using powder bed fusion with in-situ focused infrared heating

Arif Hussain, Junghoon Lee, Rae Eon Kim, Hyoung Seop Kim, Jeonghong Ha et al. Additive manufacturing 2025-06-24 10.1016/j.addma.2025.104867
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH), Pohang 37673, Republic of Korea
通讯: Dongsik Kim @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH), Pohang 37673, Republic of K
相关性: 此论文直接针对Ti-6Al-4V增材制造的力学性能，包括拉伸强度和微观结构，对用户方向有直接参考价值。
边界: No fatigue testing or S-N data, only static mechanical properties.
动机· 为解决LPBF制造薄壁Ti-6Al-4V结构中的裂纹和缺陷问题。
方法· 采用粉末床熔融结合原位聚焦红外加热及工艺优化。
结果· 实现了近全密度、无裂纹、等轴晶组织，并提升了拉伸强度与塑性。
验证· 通过密度测量、微观结构分析和拉伸测试进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To fabricate robust high-aspect-ratio Ti-6Al-4V thin-walled structures by mitigating cracks and defects common in laser powder bed fusion.
Method· Implemented powder bed fusion with in-situ focused infrared heating and optimized process parameters to enhance densification and microstructure control.
Result· Achieved near-full density, crack-free samples with equiaxed recrystallized grains, and improved tensile strength and ductility compared to conventional processing.
Validation· Validated by density measurements, microstructural characterization (EBSD, XRD), and tensile mechanical testing.

Hybrid Modeling of Material Imperfection Indications in X-ray Computed Tomography Datasets

Erik Lindgren, Fabian Hanning, Linda Squillaci e-Journal of Nondestructive Testing 2025-06-24 10.58286/31443
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V材料，但仅用于生成CT合成训练数据，不包含任何力学或疲劳性能研究，与用户关注的力学性能方向不直接相关。
边界: No mechanical or fatigue data; focuses on synthetic CT training data generation for defect detection, not on Ti-6Al-4V mechanical characterization.
动机· 为X射线CT缺陷检测生成合成训练数据，减少对昂贵实验数据的依赖。
方法· 使用混合方法将2D/3D二值缺陷表示直接变换为实验CT重建图像。
结果· 生成了逼真的训练数据，计算资源消耗低，适用于深度学习训练。
验证· 通过视觉检查和现有成像模型验证数据真实性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To generate synthetic training data for X-ray CT defect detection, reducing reliance on expensive experimental data.
Method· A hybrid approach transforms 2D/3D binary defect representations directly into experimental-like CT reconstructions using gVXR models and FDK algorithm.
Result· Realistic-looking training data is produced with low computational cost, suitable for deep learning and conventional segmentation.
Validation· Validation is based on visual inspection and existing imaging models, without quantitative experimental mechanical testing.

Effect of Process Parameters on Surface Roughness in Wire EDM of Titanium Alloys

Kamal Kumar INTERANTIONAL JOURNAL OF SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH IN ENGINEERING AND MANAGEMENT 2025-06-24 10.55041/ijsrem50989
相关性: 该论文研究了线切割加工Ti-6Al-4V的表面粗糙度，但未涉及力学性能（疲劳/拉伸），与后处理对力学性能影响的方向关联较弱。
边界: This paper studies surface roughness of Ti-6Al-4V after wire EDM but does not link to mechanical fatigue or tensile performance, and does not involve post-processing treatments like heat treatment or HIP.
动机· 研究线切割参数对钛合金表面粗糙度的影响
方法· 使用全因子实验设计并建立回归模型
结果· 高电流和长脉冲时间增加粗糙度，优化参数可得到光滑表面
验证· 通过触针轮廓仪测量和ANOVA验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of wire EDM parameters on surface roughness of Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· A full-factorial experimental design and ANOVA were used to identify significant parameters and develop a predictive regression model.
Result· Spark current and pulse-on time increase roughness, while optimized pulse-off and flushing pressure reduce it, with model R²=0.92.
Validation· Surface roughness was measured with a contact profilometer and statistically analyzed.

Method for Determining Contact Temperature of Tool Rake Face During Orthogonal Turning of Ti-6Al-4V Alloy

Łukasz Ślusarczyk, A. Twardowska Materials 2025-06-24 10.3390/ma18132980
单位: Department of Production Engineering, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, Cracow University of Technology, Al. Jana Pawła II 37, 31-864 Krakow, Poland
通讯: Łukasz Ślusarczyk @ Department of Production Engineering, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, Cracow University of Technology, Al. Jana Pawła
相关性: 这篇论文研究的是Ti-6Al-4V合金正交车削过程中的接触温度，而非增材制造后的后处理对疲劳/力学性能的影响。
边界: The paper focuses on machining (turning) temperature measurement and modeling, not on any post-processing treatment for AM Ti-6Al-4V such as heat treatment, HIP, or surface treatments.
动机· 提出正交车削Ti-6Al-4V时刀具前刀面接触温度的确定方法
方法· 实验测力、热像、高速摄像结合双区热力学模型和Shaw方法确定热分区系数和接触温度
结果· 建立了工艺参数与摩擦系数、接触长度的关系，并可视化刀具前刀面温度分布
验证· 基于正交车削实验数据，模型与文献中的双区模型一致
English summary & terms
Motivation· This paper proposes a method to determine the contact temperature on the rake face during orthogonal turning of Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· Experimental cutting force, thermovision, and high-speed camera data are combined with a dual-zone thermomechanical model and the Shaw approach to identify heat partition coefficients and contact temperature.
Result· Relationships among process parameters, friction coefficients, and chip-tool contact length are established, and the temperature distribution on the rake face is visualized using MATLAB PDE.
Validation· The model is validated against orthogonal turning experimental data and is consistent with existing dual-zone models in the literature.
