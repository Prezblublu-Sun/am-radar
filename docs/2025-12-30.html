TEMPERATURE DEPENDENCE OF CRACK INITIATION MECHANISMS IN FRETTING FATIGUE OF THE TI-6AL-4V TITANIUM ALLOY

Yurii Ryzhkov Bulletin of Kharkov National Automobile and Highway University 2025-12-30 10.30977/bul.2219-5548.2025.111.0.95
单位: Харківський національний автомобільно- дорожній університет, 61002, Україна, м. Харків, вул. Ярослава Мудрого, 25
通讯: Yurii Ryzhkov @ Харківський національний автомобільно- дорожній університет, 61002, Україна, м. Харків, вул. Ярослава Мудрого, 25
相关性: 该论文直接研究了增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的疲劳裂纹萌生机制，与用户关注的力学与疲劳性能高度相关。
边界: 该论文专注于传统的锻造/加工状态下的Ti-6Al-4V，而非增材制造态试样，因此未涉及AM特有的缺陷、孔隙或各向异性。
动机· 温度对Ti-6Al-4V微动疲劳裂纹萌生机制的影响尚未量化，特别是在200–500°C范围内。
方法· 进行圆筒-平面微动疲劳实验（20,250,450°C），结合红外热成像和3D热力耦合有限元模型分析。
结果· 250°C时氧化膜/TTS降低平均摩擦但降低损伤阈值，450°C时损伤热点移至次表面，累计能量阈值较室温下降45–65%。
验证· 通过实测温度场与有限元结果对比验证，峰值偏差≤8%。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The temperature dependence of fretting fatigue crack initiation mechanisms in Ti-6Al-4V remains insufficiently quantified, especially the transition from mechanically-driven to oxide-assisted mechanisms at elevated temperatures.
Method· Cylinder-on-flat fretting tests were conducted at 20°C, 250°C, and 450°C, with synchronous infrared and micro-thermocouple thermometry, and supplemented by a validated 3D thermomechanically coupled finite element model.
Result· Crack initiation at 250°C is dominated by oxide film and tribologically transformed structures, reducing the cumulative energy threshold by 25–35%, while at 450°C intergranular mechanisms shift damage subsurface, further lowering the threshold by 20–30%.
Validation· The coupled FEM was validated by comparing predicted temperature maps with experimental measurements, achieving a peak mismatch ≤8%.

Andrii Kononenko, Vyacheslav Lanin, J. I. Kononenko, Andrii Skrebtsov, Антон Фасоляк Bulletin of Kharkov National Automobile and Highway University 2025-12-30 10.30977/bul.2219-5548.2025.111.0.75
单位: Національний університет «Запорізька політехніка»
通讯: Andrii Kononenko @ Національний університет «Запорізька політехніка»
相关性: 该论文研究钛粉流动性，不涉及Ti-6Al-4V增材制造件的力学或疲劳性能。
边界: The paper focuses solely on powder flowability enhancement using SiO₂ additives, with no measurement, modeling, or discussion of mechanical or fatigue properties of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 改善非球形钛粉流动性，拓展其在增材制造中的应用。
方法· 使用Hall流量计测量添加两种SiO₂的钛粉流动性。
结果· SiO₂改善非球形HDH钛粉流动性，但降低球形Ti-6Al-4V粉流动性。
验证· 实验测量流动时间，重复五次取平均值。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the flowability of non-spherical titanium powders for additive manufacturing by adding silicon dioxide additives.
Method· Flowability of HDH Ti and spherical Ti-6Al-4V powders mixed with pyrogenic or precipitated SiO₂ was measured using a Hall flowmeter.
Result· SiO₂ additives enhanced flowability of non-spherical HDH Ti powder significantly but decreased that of spherical Ti-6Al-4V powder by up to 45%.
Validation· Five repeated flow time measurements per condition were averaged to quantify the effect.
