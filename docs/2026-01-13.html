Proximal femoral periprosthetic fractures: A review of current concepts

Dylan Green, Humza Osmani, James Li, Vishal Senthilkumar, Andreas Fontalis et al. Journal of Joint Surgery and Research 2026-01-13 10.1016/j.jjoisr.2025.12.004
单位: University College Hospital London NHS Foundation Trust, 250 Euston Road, London NW1 2PB, UK
通讯: Dylan Green @ University College Hospital London NHS Foundation Trust, 250 Euston Road, London NW1 2PB, UK
相关性: 该综述直接讨论了股骨柄设计（包括骨水泥与非骨水泥）以及生物力学因素对假体周围骨折风险的影响，与您的研究方向（股骨柄设计与生物力学）直接相关。
边界: This paper is a narrative review combining clinical, registry, and biomechanical studies without presenting any original biomechanical modeling, implant design optimization, or AM fabrication; it lacks experimental or computational validation of stem performance.
动机· 总结近端股骨假体周围骨折的现有证据，以支持合理的预防和治疗。
方法· 对临床、注册研究和生物力学研究进行叙述性综述。
结果· 强调设计特异性、界面导向的分类和围手术期优化对改善骨折预后的重要性。
验证· 未进行独立验证，基于文献综述。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To consolidate current clinical, registry, and biomechanical evidence on proximal femoral periprosthetic fractures to guide prevention and management.
Method· A narrative review of contemporary studies focusing on epidemiology, stem design biomechanics, classification, and treatment.
Result· Design-specific stem risks, an interface-based classification for polished taper slip fractures, and selective ORIF as an alternative to revision are highlighted.
Validation· No original validation; evidence synthesis from existing literature.

Early loosening of the femoral stem in cemented segmental prostheses with distal femur replacement (DFR): a multicenter observational study.

Clara Chimeno-Pigrau, Marta Sabater-Martos, Lluís Font-Vizcarra, María Surroca-Espelt, José Baeza-Oliete et al. Journal of orthopaedic surgery and research 2026-01-13 10.1186/s13018-025-06607-0
单位: Knee Unit, Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Hospital Clínic de Barcelona, Barcelona, Spain. chimeno@clinic.cat.
通讯: Clara Chimeno-Pigrau @ Knee Unit, Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Hospital Clínic de Barcelona, Barcelona, Spain. chimeno@clinic.cat.
相关性: 该论文涉及股骨柄假体松动，但没有进行生物力学建模或应力遮挡分析，与用户的研究方向关联较弱。
边界: This paper is a clinical observational study with no biomechanical modeling, FEA, implant design analysis, or stress shielding quantification.
动机· 探讨远端股骨置换假体中骨水泥型股骨柄早期松动的发生率及影响因素。
方法· 回顾性多中心研究，纳入70例患者，分析临床资料并进行生存分析。
结果· 两年内股骨柄松动率为15.7%，女性风险较低，再手术率为24.3%。
验证· 基于多中心临床数据的回顾性统计分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the incidence and risk factors of early loosening of cemented femoral stems in distal femur replacement prostheses.
Method· A retrospective multicenter study including 70 patients with cemented DFR prostheses, analyzing clinical variables and performing survival analysis.
Result· The early loosening rate was 15.7% within two years, with female gender associated with lower risk; reoperation rate was 24.3% mainly for mechanical causes.
Validation· Validation was based on retrospective clinical data from multiple centers with statistical analysis.

Effect of Interatomic Potential Choice on Fracture Modes of Graphene with Parallel Cracks

Suyeong Jin, Jung-Wuk Hong, Alexandre F. Fonseca arXiv preprint 2026-01-13 link
相关性: 该论文研究石墨烯的原子尺度断裂，与用户研究方向（AI驱动的生物打印、髋关节植入物力学、有限元代理模型、增材制造生物医学植入物）无直接或间接关联。
边界: This paper studies atomistic fracture of graphene using MD, with no biomedical implant mechanics, no AM, no FEA surrogate, no bioprinting, and no experimental validation.
动机· 研究石墨烯平行裂纹的断裂行为对势函数的敏感性。
方法· 使用AIREBO和ReaxFF势函数进行分子动力学模拟对比。

验证· 仅对两种势函数结果进行对比，无实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To quantify how interatomic potential choice affects fracture modes in graphene with parallel cracks.
Method· Molecular dynamics simulations using AIREBO and ReaxFF potentials under identical geometry and loading conditions.
Result· AIREBO predicts lower peak stress and reduced energy absorption compared to ReaxFF, indicating strong potential dependence.
Validation· Only computational comparison between two potentials, no experimental validation.

Pressure-Induced Martensitic Phase Transformation and Microstructure Evolution in nanograined $\text{Fe}\text{-}7\%\text{Mn}$ Alloy

Mrinmay Sahu, Sorb Yesudhas, Valery I. Levitas, Dean Smith arXiv preprint 2026-01-13 link
相关性: 该论文研究Fe-Mn合金在高压下的相变行为，与增材制造力学性能无关。
边界: No additive manufacturing context, no mechanical property characterization for AM, and no build process or post-processing effects are considered.
动机· 研究纳米晶Fe-Mn合金在高压下的相变机制。
方法· 利用原位同步辐射X射线衍射分析高压下结构演变。
结果· 在11.4 GPa时发生BCC到HCP相变，并遵循Burger取向关系。
验证· 通过高压XRD实验验证相变路径和微观结构变化。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the pressure-induced martensitic phase transformation and microstructural evolution in nanograined Fe-7%Mn alloy.
Method· In situ high-pressure synchrotron X-ray diffraction was performed to track structural and microstructural changes up to 30.3 GPa.
Result· A BCC-to-HCP phase transformation via the Burger's martensitic pathway was observed at 11.4 GPa, with the two phases coexisting until 15.9 GPa.
Validation· Experimental XRD data confirmed the phase transformation sequence and the orientational relationship between the BCC and HCP phases.
