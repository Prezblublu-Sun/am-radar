Achieving superior tensile and fatigue properties than conventional wrought state via hybrid additive-forging manufacturing

Wei Wang, Zinong Tan, Yaping Wang, Ruiqiang Zhang, Jianglin Huang et al. Materials & Design 2026-01-13 10.1016/j.matdes.2026.115485
相关性: 该论文直接研究了Ti-6Al-4V的后处理（热锻）对力学性能的改善，与用户关注的后处理控制高度相关。
边界: Paper focuses on a hybrid additive-forging process rather than conventional post-processing treatments like HIP, annealing, or shot peening.
动机· 解决增材制造材料缺陷和微观结构问题，提升力学性能。
方法· 采用增材制造结合热锻的混合工艺，并系统研究工艺参数。
结果· 获得优于纯增材制造和传统锻态的拉伸和疲劳性能。
验证· 通过实验表征、力学测试和有限元建模验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To overcome the detrimental defects and microstructures inherent in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V, this study explores a hybrid additive-forging strategy.
Method· A series of combined forging and heat treatment processes were designed and applied to additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V, with systematic experimental characterization, finite element modeling, and mechanical testing to reveal microstructure-property correlations.
Result· The hybrid processed Ti-6Al-4V exhibits superior tensile and fatigue properties compared to both as-built and conventional wrought states, with a demonstration on an aero-engine blade component.
Validation· The results are validated through integrated experimental characterization (microscopy, XRD), finite element simulations, and mechanical tests (tensile and fatigue).

单位: Department of Small Animal Surgery and Anesthesiology, Institute of Veterinary Medicine, Warsaw University of Life Sciences-SGGW, Nowoursynowska 159 C, 02-776 Warsaw, Poland
通讯: Piotr Trębacz @ Department of Small Animal Surgery and Anesthesiology, Institute of Veterinary Medicine, Warsaw University of Life Scien

Validation· Cadaver procedures assessed surgical feasibility and timing, while finite element analysis validated mechanical behavior under loading.
