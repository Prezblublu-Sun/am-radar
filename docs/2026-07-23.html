Influence of minor print parameter variations on the mechanical properties of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V parts using laser powder bed fusion

Joshua Hall, Marybeth Potter Radtke, Jay Waterman, Zahed Siddique, Yingtao Liu The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology 2026-07-23 10.1007/s00170-026-18735-w
单位: School of Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering, The University of Oklahoma, Norman, OK, USA
通讯: Joshua Hall @ School of Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering, The University of Oklahoma, Norman, OK, USA
相关性: 该论文直接针对激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V的打印参数变化对力学性能的影响，完全符合方向L1的评估焦点。
边界: This paper does not cover fatigue life or S-N curves explicitly; it focuses on static mechanical properties (tensile, hardness) under minor parameter variations.
动机· 研究微小打印参数变化对LPBF Ti-6Al-4V力学性能的影响
方法· 通过系统改变激光功率、扫描速度等参数，制备试样并进行拉伸与硬度测试
结果· 发现参数微小变化会导致强度与延展性的显著差异，归因于孔隙率和微观组织变化
验证· 实验力学测试与微观结构表征相结合
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the influence of minor variations in laser powder bed fusion parameters on the mechanical properties of Ti-6Al-4V parts.
Method· A systematic variation of key print parameters (laser power, scan speed, hatch spacing) was performed, and tensile and hardness tests were conducted on as-built specimens.
Result· Small parameter changes were found to cause significant differences in tensile strength and ductility, correlated with porosity and microstructural alterations.
Validation· Experimental mechanical testing (tensile, hardness) combined with microstructural analysis (SEM, EBSD) validates the findings.

Physics-driven digital twin based architecture of wire arc additive manufacturing enabled by fast surrogate calculations

Petro Pavlenko, Xuezhi Shi, Yi-Xiang Wang, Oleh Makhnenko, O.S. Milenin et al. The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology 2026-07-23 10.1007/s00170-026-18661-x
单位: School of Marine Engineering Equipment, Zhejiang Ocean University, Zhoushan, 316022, China
通讯: O.S. Milenin @ E.O. Paton Electric Welding Institute of the NAS of Ukraine, Kyiv, 03150, Ukraine
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V WAAM增材制造，但主要贡献是数字孪生框架而非力学或疲劳性能研究。
边界: This paper does not provide mechanical or fatigue characterization of Ti-6Al-4V; it focuses on a digital twin framework for WAAM process simulation rather than material mechanical performance.
动机· 开发用于WAAM的物理驱动数字孪生架构以支持过程监控与控制
方法· 集成高保真热-力有限元模型与数据驱动代理模型，构建多层框架
结果· 代理模型以>95%精度复现有限元结果，计算时间减少85-90%
验证· 通过Ti-6Al-4V WAAM演示件的代理模型与有限元对比验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· Develop a physics-driven digital twin architecture for wire arc additive manufacturing to enable monitoring, prediction, and closed-loop control.
Method· Integrate high-fidelity thermo-mechanical finite element models with neural-network-based surrogate models in a multilayer framework with standardized data exchange.
Result· Surrogate models reproduce finite element responses with >95% accuracy while reducing computational time by 85-90%.
Validation· Validated quantitatively on a Ti-6Al-4V WAAM demonstrator by comparing surrogate and FE model outputs.

Laser-assisted cold spray additive manufacturing of Ti6Al4V: Synergistic evolution of microstructure refinement and interface bonding

Shaowu Liu, Shen Wang, Yehao Zhang, Hongjian Wu, Wenbo Li et al. Materials Science in Additive Manufacturing 2026-07-23 10.36922/msam026220048
相关性: 本文涉及Ti6Al4V的冷喷涂增材制造，但主要关注与铝基体的异种材料结合，而非独立的Ti6Al4V力学与疲劳性能。
边界: The paper characterizes Ti6Al4V only as a coating on Al substrate, not as bulk AM Ti-6Al-4V, and provides no fatigue data or typical AM process (LPBF/SLM/EBM).
动机· 解决Ti6Al4V与Al异种金属熔焊时脆性相问题
方法· 激光辅助冷喷涂沉积Ti6Al4V于6061Al基体，分析微观组织与力学性能
结果· 600W激光辅助使孔隙率降至0.41%，抗拉强度提升40%至206.58 MPa
验证· 通过XRD、EBSD、TEM及拉伸硬度实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To avoid brittle intermetallic compounds in dissimilar Al/Ti joints by using solid-state laser-assisted cold spray deposition.
Method· Ti6Al4V was deposited on 6061Al substrates via laser-assisted cold spray; microstructure (XRD, EBSD, TEM) and mechanical properties were characterized.
Result· Laser assistance reduced porosity to 0.41% and refined grains; ultimate tensile strength reached 206.58 MPa with 2.17% elongation at 600 W.
Validation· Validation through microstructural analysis (XRD, EBSD, TEM) and mechanical testing (microhardness, tensile).
