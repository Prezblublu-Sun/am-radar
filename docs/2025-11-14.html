Design and optimisation of patient-specific porous femoral stems.

Sarah Safavi, Hans A Gray, Peter V S Lee Journal of orthopaedic surgery and research 2025-11-14 10.1186/s13018-025-06389-5
单位: Department of Biomedical Engineering, University of Melbourne, Parkville, VIC, 3010, Australia.
通讯: Peter V S Lee @ Department of Biomedical Engineering, University of Melbourne, Parkville, VIC, 3010, Australia. pvlee@unimelb.edu.au.
相关性: 该论文直接针对用户关注的髋关节假体应力屏蔽和微动问题，并采用患者特定设计与增材制造，与髋关节植入物和生物医学增材制造方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not involve AI/ML or surrogate model development, and is not related to bioprinting.
动机· 为减少髋关节置换术后因应力屏蔽导致的假体松动，设计低刚度多孔股骨柄。
方法· 制备不同孔隙率金刚石点阵试样测试弹性模量，结合有限元优化八种多孔股骨柄设计。
结果· 近端多孔和实心壳型设计在降低应力屏蔽同时满足疲劳和微动要求。
验证· 通过力学试验验证点阵结构性能，并用有限元模拟评估假体性能和疲劳标准。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To address stress shielding and implant loosening after total hip replacement, this study designs patient-specific porous femoral stems with reduced stiffness.
Method· Diamond lattice specimens with varying porosities were mechanically tested to obtain elastic moduli, and finite element optimization was performed on eight stem designs with linear or radial modulus distributions.
Result· Proximal porous and solid shell stems reduced stress shielding while meeting fatigue and micromotion criteria for bone integration.
Validation· Mechanical testing validated lattice properties, and finite element simulations predicted implant performance and fatigue compliance with ISO 7206-4.

DO WE NEED TO CEMENT FEMORAL STEMS IN OSTEOPETROSIS?

Jean‐Yves Chouinard, Julien Dаrtus, Vincent Massé, Xavier Zwiebel, Carol L. Richards et al. Orthopaedic Proceedings 2025-11-14 10.1302/1358-992x.2025.13.004
单位: CHU de Quebec - Universite Laval, Québec, Canada
通讯: Jean‐Yves Chouinard @ CHU de Quebec - Universite Laval, Québec, Canada
相关性: 该论文为临床回顾研究，未涉及生物打印、AI、有限元代理或增材制造，与用户研究方向不相关。
边界: No implant design optimization, no stress shielding quantification, no FEA modeling, no AI/ML, no AM fabrication.
动机· 探究骨硬化症患者全髋置换中骨水泥与非骨水泥股骨柄的生存率差异
方法· 加拿大多中心回顾性研究，分析22例全髋置换的临床数据
结果· 非骨水泥柄10年生存率优于骨水泥柄，再手术率更低
验证· 临床随访数据统计分析
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare implant survival and complication rates between cemented and cementless femoral stems in total hip arthroplasty for osteopetrosis patients.
Method· A retrospective Canadian multicenter study of 22 THA cases from 18 patients with mean 9.1-year follow-up.
Result· Cementless stems showed better 10-year survivorship (89% vs 71% for revision, p=0.18; 89% vs 52% for reoperation, p=0.03).
Validation· Clinical data analysis of multicenter patient records.

Metavalent Bonding-Induced Phonon Hardening and Giant Anharmonicity in BeO

Xuejie Li, Yuzhou Hao, Yujie Liu, Shengying Yue, Xiaolong Yang et al. arXiv preprint 2025-11-14 link
相关性: 与用户的研究方向（AI生物打印、髋关节植入物应力屏蔽、FEA替代模型、AM生物医学）完全无关。
边界: This paper studies phonon transport in BeO crystals, with no connection to AI/ML, bioprinting, hip implants, FEA surrogate, or biomedical additive manufacturing.
动机· 寻找具有超低热导率的材料，理解成键对晶格动力学的影响。
方法· 使用第一性原理计算对比共价键和亚金属键BeO的热输运性质。
结果· 亚金属键导致强非简谐性和超低热导率（24 W/mK），需考虑四声子散射。
验证· 无实验验证，仅理论计算与已知材料性质对比。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To identify materials with intrinsically low lattice thermal conductivity by understanding the role of bonding character on phonon transport.
Method· First-principles calculations comparing covalent zincblende and metavalent rocksalt BeO, including phonon renormalization and four-phonon scattering.
Result· Metavalent bonding induces giant anharmonicity and ultralow thermal conductivity (24 W/mK) in rs-BeO, requiring explicit inclusion of temperature-dependent effects.
Validation· Theoretical predictions without experimental validation; comparison with known trends in metavalent materials.

Impact of Nitrogen Atom Clusters and Vacancy Defects on Graphene: A Molecular Dynamics Investigation

Indranil Rudra, Md. Moktadir Billah Tahmid, Jahid Emon, Mohammad Jane Alam Khan arXiv preprint 2025-11-14 link
相关性: 本文研究石墨烯的力学性能，与AM力学性能方向无关。
边界: This paper does not involve additive manufacturing, biomedical implants, or any AM-specific mechanical testing; it is a molecular dynamics study of graphene defects.
动机· 研究氮掺杂与空位缺陷对石墨烯力学性能的影响。
方法· 通过分子动力学模拟比较氮原子簇与空位缺陷的效果。
结果· 氮簇显著降低力学性能，空位缺陷导致更早失效。
验证· 仅分子动力学数值模拟，无实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the comparative effects of nitrogen atom clusters and vacancy defects on the mechanical behavior of graphene.
Method· Molecular dynamics simulations were performed to compare mechanical responses of graphene with nitrogen clusters and equivalent vacancy defects.
Result· Nitrogen clusters degraded mechanical properties similarly to random doping, while vacancies caused higher stiffness but lower ductility and earlier failure.
Validation· Only numerical molecular dynamics simulations, no experimental validation.
