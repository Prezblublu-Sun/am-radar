АДИТИВНЕ ВИГОТОВЛЕННЯ ТИТАНОВИХ СПЛАВІВ МЕТОДОМ XBEAM З АНАЛІЗОМ МІКРОСТРУКТУРИ ТА МЕХАНІЧНИХ ВЛАСТИВОСТЕЙ

Світлана Вікторівна Кирилаха Journal of Rocket-Space Technology 2025-11-25 10.15421/452529
单位: Національний університет "Запорізька політехніка"
通讯: Світлана Вікторівна Кирилаха @ Національний університет "Запорізька політехніка"
相关性: 这是一篇关于电子束送丝增材制造Ti-6Al-4V力学性能的综述文章，与方向直接相关但缺乏原始实验数据。
边界: It is a review paper that does not present new experimental data; the mechanical results are compiled from literature rather than original testing.
动机· 综述电子束送丝增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的微观结构和力学性能。
方法· 通过文献回顾和SEM图像分析，评估工艺参数对组织和性能的影响。
结果· 报道了屈服强度~920 MPa，抗拉强度~995 MPa，延伸率~14%，硬度~340 HV。
验证· 基于文献数据与锻造及其他增材制造方法对比验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To review the microstructure and mechanical properties of Ti-6Al-4V fabricated by electron beam wire deposition (xBeam).
Method· Literature review and SEM-based microstructural analysis of the effect of process parameters.
Result· Reported yield strength ~920 MPa, UTS ~995 MPa, elongation ~14%, and hardness ~340 HV.
Validation· Validation through comparison with literature data from other AM methods and wrought material.

X-ray diffraction analysis and microstructures of laser powder bed fusion Ti6Al4V for application in aerospace structural components

Diali Phillip Mokoena, Thywill Dzogbewu, Amos Muiruri, Deon de Beer Springer Link (Chiba Institute of Technology) 2025-11-25 10.1051/matecconf/202541701003/pdf
相关性: 该论文仅涉及热处理后的微观结构表征，未提供力学性能数据，因此与用户需求的相关性有限。
边界: No mechanical testing (fatigue or tensile) data reported; only microstructural characterization without any mechanical performance results.
动机· 利用XRD分析LPBF Ti6Al4V在不同热处理后的相变。
方法· 对LPBF制备的Ti6Al4V进行应力消除和退火处理，并用XRD表征相组成。
结果· 退火后亚稳态α'马氏体分解为稳定的α+β层状组织。
验证· XRD实验数据验证了相变过程，但缺乏力学性能验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To use XRD as a tool to analyze phase evolution in LPBF Ti6Al4V subjected to different post-processing heat treatments.
Method· XRD analysis was performed on LPBF Ti6Al4V samples after stress-relieving and annealing heat treatments to identify phase composition.
Result· Annealing caused the decomposition of metastable α' martensite into a stable lamellar α+β microstructure.
Validation· The phase transformations were validated through XRD experimental data, but no mechanical performance validation was conducted.

Application of Laser and Cryogenic Surface Treatment for the Evolution of Surface Morphology in Additively Manufactured Ti-6Al-4V Alloy Samples

Dorota Laskowska, Monika Szada-Borzyszkowska, Błażej Bałasz, Wiesław Szada-Borzyszkowski, Izabela Bukała Materials 2025-11-25 10.3390/ma18235315
单位: Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Energy, Koszalin University of Technology, Śniadeckich 2, 75-620 Koszalin, Poland
通讯: Dorota Laskowska @ Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Energy, Koszalin University of Technology, Śniadeckich 2, 75-620 Koszalin, Poland  |  Monika Szada-Borzyszkowska @ Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Energy, Koszalin University of Technology, Śniadeckich 2, 75-620 Koszalin, Poland
相关性: 该论文研究了激光和干冰表面处理对SLM Ti-6Al-4V样品表面形貌的影响，属于后处理范畴，但未涉及力学性能测试。
边界: The paper only characterizes surface morphology and does not provide any mechanical performance (fatigue, tensile) data after post-processing.
动机· 改善增材制造Ti-6Al-4V合金的表面质量以提升性能
方法· 对SLM样品进行激光清洗和干冰喷射处理，并分析表面形貌
结果· 两种方法均能改善表面均匀性并去除污染物，但激光处理可能导致微裂纹
验证· 通过表面形貌和成分分析验证处理效果
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the surface quality of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V components by post-processing treatments.
Method· Laser cleaning and dry ice blasting were applied to SLM samples under various parameters, followed by surface topography and morphology analysis.
Result· Both treatments improved surface uniformity and removed contaminants, but laser treatment caused remelting and microcracks at certain focal lengths.
Validation· Surface morphology was characterized using optical microscopy and SEM to validate treatment effectiveness.

Investigating wear performance of surface treated additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V temporomandibular joint replacement implants

Sizwe Ntimane, Gerrie Booysen, Kobus van der Walt Springer Link (Chiba Institute of Technology) 2025-11-25 10.1051/matecconf/202541701004/pdf
相关性: 该论文研究了表面处理对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V颞下颌关节植入物的磨损性能，但未涉及疲劳或拉伸性能，与后处理控制方向的疲劳/机械性能核心关注点不直接相关。
边界: Does not investigate fatigue or tensile properties; focuses only on wear performance of an implant application (TMJ), which may fall under Layer 3.
动机· 改善增材制造Ti-6Al-4V颞下颌关节植入物的磨损性能。
方法· 采用氧助推扩散和氮化物热处理对AM Ti-6Al-4V进行表面处理，并进行销盘磨损试验。
结果· 表面处理后的试样磨损性能明显改善。
验证· 通过销盘磨损试验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the wear performance of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V temporomandibular joint implants through post-processing surface treatments.
Method· Oxygen boost diffusion and nitride heat treatment were applied to AM Ti-6Al-4V specimens, followed by pin-on-disc wear testing.
Result· Significant improvement in wear resistance was observed for treated components compared to as-built ones.
Validation· The wear performance was validated experimentally using pin-on-disc testing.
