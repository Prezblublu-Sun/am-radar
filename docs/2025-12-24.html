Experimental Investigation on Cutting Force and Hole Quality in Milling of Ti-6Al-4V

Laifa Zhu, Kai Zhang, Bin Liu, Feng Jiang, Xian Wu et al. Micromachines 2025-12-24 10.3390/mi17010019
单位: College of Mechanical Engineering and Automation, Huaqiao University, Xiamen 361021, China
通讯: Laifa Zhu @ College of Mechanical Engineering and Automation, Huaqiao University, Xiamen 361021, China
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的铣削加工，但未研究后处理对疲劳或力学性能的影响，与当前方向弱相关。
边界: Does not evaluate post-processing effect on fatigue or tensile properties; focuses on cutting process parameters rather than mechanical performance modification.
动机· 研究铣削参数对Ti-6Al-4V孔加工质量的影响
方法· 通过实验改变铣削深度、每齿进给量和切削速度进行加工测试
结果· 优化参数使切削力降低91.74%，显著改善孔质量和几何精度
验证· 实验测量切削力、毛刺和孔轴线偏差，并结合微观组织分析
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the influence of cutting parameters on milling force and hole quality in Ti-6Al-4V, aiming to improve precision in aerospace components.
Method· An experimental study varying milling depth, feed per tooth, and cutting speed, with measurements of force, burr formation, and geometrical accuracy.
Result· Optimized parameters reduced milling force by 91.74% and significantly decreased entrance burr width and hole-axis deviation.
Validation· Experimental cutting tests with mechanical and microstructural analysis, including surface observations.

Vibration Behaviour of Topologically Optimised Sacrificial Geometries for Precision Machining of Thin-Walled Components

Evren Yasa, Ozgur Poyraz, Finlay P. C. Parson, Anthony Molyneux, M. Baxter et al. Materials 2025-12-24 10.3390/ma19010070
单位: The University of Sheffield Advanced Manufacturing Research Centre North-West, Blackburn BB2 7HP, UK
通讯: Evren Yasa @ The University of Sheffield Advanced Manufacturing Research Centre North-West, Blackburn BB2 7HP, UK
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti6Al4V的机械加工后处理，但主要关注加工过程中的振动行为，而非后处理对疲劳或力学性能的影响。
边界: Does not evaluate how post-processing (machining) modifies fatigue or tensile mechanical properties; instead focuses on vibration/chatter during machining of thin-walled geometries.
动机· 研究薄壁Ti6Al4V增材制造零件加工时的振动行为。
方法· 通过拓扑优化设计四种牺牲几何结构，并进行模态测试和实际加工。
结果· 优化设计增强了模态性能，但加工中振动敏感性增加。
验证· 通过敲击测试和实际加工验证模态特性变化。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Investigate vibration behavior of thin-walled Ti6Al4V components produced by laser powder bed fusion during machining.
Method· Four stock envelope designs (constant, tapered, and two topologically optimized variants) were evaluated through tap testing and subsequent machining.
Result· Optimized geometries improved modal performance but exhibited more pronounced in situ modal changes during machining due to variable material removal.
Validation· Validated by tap testing and actual machining experiments.

Simulation of friction stir welding in butt welds of grade 5 titanium alloy and measurement of heat distribution

Iman Mohammadi, Bahman Mirzakhani Synthesis and Sintering 2025-12-24 10.53063/synsint.2025.54297
单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Arak University, Arak, 38156-8-8349, Iran
通讯: Iman Mohammadi @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Arak University, Arak, 38156-8-8349, Iran
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的搅拌摩擦焊模拟，而非增材制造，因此与用户关注的增材制造Ti-6Al-4V机械性能方向不相关。
边界: The paper studies friction stir welding (FSW), a joining process, not additive manufacturing; it does not address LPBF/SLM/EBM/DED mechanical or fatigue performance.
动机· 研究搅拌摩擦焊过程中的热分布和残余应力
方法· 使用ABAQUS和DFLUX子程序进行热-力耦合模拟
结果· 成功模拟了冷却和加热循环对残余应力的影响
验证· 通过实验焊接验证模拟结果
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate heat distribution and residual stresses during friction stir welding of Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Coupled thermal-mechanical simulation using ABAQUS with the DFLUX subroutine.
Result· The simulation captured the effect of thermal cycles on residual stress formation.
Validation· Experimental welding tests validated the simulation results.
