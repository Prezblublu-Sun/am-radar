Drum Shell-Inspired Fractal Lightweight Microlattice Structures with Tailored Mechanical and Transport Properties

Zhenbang Lei, Xuerui Xia, J J Zhang, Luobin Hu, XM. Wang et al. Journal of Composites Science 2026-07-17 10.3390/jcs10070374
单位: School of Advanced Manufacturing, Nanchang Universidty, Nanchang 330006, China
通讯: Lei Zhang @ School of Advanced Manufacturing, Nanchang Universidty, Nanchang 330006, China
相关性: 该论文专注于增材制造Ti-6Al-4V微格结构的力学性能（弹性模量、屈服强度、能量吸收），但完全未涉及疲劳性能，因此对方向L1的疲劳研究参考价值有限。
边界: This paper does not address fatigue life, S-N curves, or crack initiation/propagation, which are key aspects of the direction focus on mechanical and fatigue performance.
动机· 受鼓壳力学原理启发，提出仿生分形微格结构设计方法。
方法· 基于六方晶体结构引入一阶和二阶分形设计，利用LPBF制备Ti-6Al-4V试样，通过准静态压缩和CFD模拟表征。
结果· 二阶分形结构优化节点应力分布，弹性模量、屈服强度和能量吸收显著提升，但渗透性降低。
验证· 准静态压缩实验和CFD模拟验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Inspired by drum shell mechanics, this study proposes a fractal hierarchical microlattice design for lightweight structures combining mechanical and transport properties.
Method· First- and second-order fractal designs based on hexagonal crystal structure were fabricated by laser powder bed fusion (LPBF) in Ti-6Al-4V, and characterized via quasi-static compression and CFD simulations.
Result· The second-order fractal architecture improved elastic modulus, yield strength, and energy absorption while reducing permeability.
Validation· Experimental quasi-static compression and computational fluid dynamics simulations.

Systematic study of the interplay between laser beam shape, energy input, and hatch spacing in PBF-LB/M of Ti-6Al-4V: experiments and numerical modeling

Abid Ullah, Reshad Bakhtari, Jan Johannsen, Philipp Kohlwes, Kai Zhang et al. Materials & Design 2026-07-17 10.1016/j.matdes.2026.116617
单位: Institute for Industrialization of Smart Materials, Hamburg University of Technology, D-21079 Hamburg, Germany
通讯: Abid Ullah @ Institute for Industrialization of Smart Materials, Hamburg University of Technology, D-21079 Hamburg, Germany  |  Milan Brandt @ RMIT Centre for Additive Manufacturing, School of Engineering, RMIT University, Melbourne, VIC 3000, Australia
相关性: 该论文研究了Ti-6Al-4V的激光粉末床熔融工艺参数对致密化和显微硬度的影响，但缺少关键的拉伸和疲劳性能数据。
边界: This paper focuses on process parameters, densification, and microstructure, but provides no tensile or fatigue characterization, which is the core of Layer 1 mechanical/fatigue focus.
动机· 探索激光束形状、能量输入和扫描间距对Ti-6Al-4V致密化和显微组织的影响
方法· 采用单道实验、立方体试块和3D瞬态多物理场模拟，研究三种环状光束轮廓
结果· 所有光束轮廓在面能量密度≥2.8-3.0 J/mm²时达到近全密度，显微硬度稳定在360-395 HV0.2
验证· 通过单道实验和立方体密度测量验证模拟结果
English summary & terms
Motivation· To systematically understand the coupled effects of laser beam shape, hatch spacing, and energy input on densification and microstructure in PBF-LB/M of Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Single-track experiments, cube builds, and 3D transient multiphysics simulations were conducted for three ring-type beam profiles from core-weighted to ring-dominated.
Result· Near-full density (~99.9%) was achieved for all profiles at areal energy density ≥2.8–3.0 J/mm², and microhardness converged to 360–395 HV0.2 once density was high.
Validation· Experimental single-track and cube density measurements validated the simulation predictions for melt-pool geometry and lack-of-fusion defects.

Integrating ANOVA and grey synthetic relational degree for energy-efficiency decisions in tangential milling under linear and sinusoidal tool paths

Marie Madeleine Sarzi Inácio, Igor Cézar Pereira, Amauri Hassui, Rodrigo Henriques Lopes da Silva The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology 2026-07-17 10.1007/s00170-026-18664-8
单位: Department of Manufacturing and Materials Engineering, School of Mechanical Engineering, University of Campinas – UNICAMP, 200 Mendeleyev St., Barão Geraldo, Campinas, São Paulo, 13083-860, Brazil
通讯: Marie Madeleine Sarzi Inácio @ Department of Manufacturing and Materials Engineering, School of Mechanical Engineering, University of Campinas – UNICAM
相关性: 该论文关注铣削加工能效优化，与AM Ti-6Al-4V的后处理对疲劳/力学性能的影响无关。
边界: This paper studies energy efficiency in machining rather than post-processing treatments (e.g., heat treatment, HIP, surface finishing) and their effect on fatigue or mechanical performance of AM Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 提高铣削加工能效以促进制造可持续性
方法· 集成ANOVA和灰色综合关联度分析切削参数对能效的影响
结果· 径向切深和切削速度分别是线性与正弦路径的能效关键参数
验证· 通过全因子实验和功率分析仪测量验证决策逻辑
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve energy efficiency in milling by understanding and prioritizing cutting parameter effects.
Method· A hierarchical decision logic combining ANOVA and Grey Synthetic Relational Degree (GSRD) was used to interpret parameter sensitivities for linear and sinusoidal tool paths.
Result· Radial depth of cut dominated for linear paths while cutting speed was primary for sinusoidal paths, yielding up to 15% electricity reduction.
Validation· Full 2³ factorial experiments with power analyzer monitoring validated the parameter prioritization framework.
