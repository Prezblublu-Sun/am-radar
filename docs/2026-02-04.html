pH-Dependent Long-Term Degradation and Mechanical Integrity of LPBF-Fabricated Porous Ti-6Al-4V in Hank’s Solutions with Different pH Values

Wei-Gang Lv, Zi-Meng Xiao, Ze-Xin Wang, Sheng Lu, Dubovyy Oleksandr et al. Metals 2026-02-04 10.3390/met16020187
单位: School of Materials Science and Engineering, Jiangsu University of Science and Technology, Zhenjiang 212003, China
通讯: Wei-Gang Lv @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, Jiangsu University of Science and Technology, Zhenjiang 212003, China
相关性: 该论文研究了LPBF多孔Ti-6Al-4V在不同pH环境下的降解对压缩性能的影响，但未涉及疲劳性能，因此仅作为转移性背景参考。
边界: No fatigue testing, no S-N curve, no defect or anisotropic characterization; mechanical evaluation limited to degradation-affected compression only.
动机· 研究LPBF制备的多孔Ti-6Al-4V在不同pH模拟体液中的长期降解行为及其对力学完整性的影响。
方法· 将多孔样品在pH 3、5、7的Hank's溶液中浸泡90天，结合表征与压缩测试分析降解机理。
结果· 酸性环境加剧TiO2溶解与离子释放，压缩强度分别下降12.68、11.67和5.84 MPa，断裂为韧脆混合模式。
验证· 通过90天浸泡实验和压缩力学测试验证降解对性能的影响。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the long-term degradation behavior of LPBF-fabricated porous Ti-6Al-4V in Hank's solutions with varying pH and the consequent effect on mechanical integrity.
Method· Porous specimens were immersed in Hank's solutions at pH 3, 5, and 7 for 90 days, and characterized by multiple techniques followed by compression testing.
Result· Acidic conditions promoted TiO2 dissolution and ion release, reducing compressive strength by 12.68, 11.67, and 5.84 MPa for pH 3, 5, and 7, respectively, with a mixed ductile-brittle fracture mode.
Validation· Experimental immersion and compression mechanical testing validated the degradation-induced performance deterioration.

Effect of Gradient Layer Induced by Laser Shock Peening on Adhesion and Wear Resistance of AlCrN Coatings on TC4 Titanium Alloy

Ying Xu, Wenqian Yu, Xinlong Liao, Yanan Zhu, Boyong Su Materials 2026-02-04 10.3390/ma19030608
单位: Engineering Training Center, Nantong University, Nantong 226019, China
通讯: Boyong Su @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Nantong University, Nantong 226019, China
相关性: 该论文涉及激光冲击强化（LSP）后处理对TC4钛合金表面涂层性能的影响，但未研究疲劳性能，与方向焦点（疲劳/力学性能）关联较弱。
边界: This paper does not examine fatigue behavior; it focuses on wear resistance and coating adhesion of LSP-treated Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 为提高Ti-6Al-4V表面AlCrN涂层的结合强度与耐磨性能
方法· 采用激光冲击处理形成梯度涂层结构
结果· 激光冲击能量增大后界面结合强度从13.6N提升至43.3N，磨损率下降至7.67×10^-5 mm^3/(N·m)
验证· 通过微观形貌与元素分布分析及摩擦磨损实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the interfacial bonding strength and tribological performance of AlCrN coatings on TC4 titanium alloy.
Method· Laser shock peening was used to create a gradient AlCrN/TC4 transition layer, followed by secondary coating deposition.
Result· Increasing laser energy raised adhesion from 13.6N to 43.3N and reduced wear rate to 7.67×10^-5 mm^3/(N·m).
Validation· Systematic investigation of surface/cross-section morphologies, elemental distribution, and wear tests.

Effect of build direction in Ti6Al4V alloy manufactured by EB-PBF on the corrosion performance of PEO coatings

Carlos A. Vargas, Alejandro A. Zuleta, Juan G. Castaño, Maryory A. Gómez, Carlos A. Botero et al. The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology 2026-02-04 10.1007/s00170-025-17297-7
单位: Grupo Materiales Avanzados y Energía (MATyER), Facultad de ingeniería, Instituto Tecnológico Metropolitano –ITM, Medellín, Colombia
通讯: Carlos A. Vargas @ Grupo Materiales Avanzados y Energía (MATyER), Facultad de ingeniería, Instituto Tecnológico Metropolitano –ITM, Medellí
相关性: 该论文研究EB-PBF Ti6Al4V基体上PEO涂层的耐腐蚀性能，未涉及力学或疲劳性能，与筛选方向不直接相关。
边界: It does not address mechanical properties or fatigue of Ti-6Al-4V; instead, it focuses on corrosion performance of PEO coatings, which is outside the L1 scope.
动机· 研究EB-PBF Ti6Al4V增材制造方向对PEO涂层腐蚀性能的影响
方法· 在不同电流密度和处理时间下制备PEO涂层，并表征其形貌、相组成和腐蚀性能
结果· PEO涂层消除了未涂层EB-PBF Ti6Al4V的腐蚀各向异性，表现出均匀的保护性能
验证· 通过实验制备涂层并进行腐蚀测试验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates how build direction in EB-PBF Ti6Al4V affects the corrosion performance of PEO coatings.
Method· PEO coatings were produced under varying current densities and treatment times, and characterized for morphology, phase composition, and corrosion resistance.
Result· PEO treatment eliminated the corrosion anisotropy of uncoated EB-PBF Ti6Al4V, providing uniform protection regardless of build direction.
Validation· Experimental coating fabrication and corrosion testing validated the findings.

Differentiated mechanical properties between additively manufactured and as-cast TC4 titanium alloy subject to deep cryogenic treatment regulation

Z.J. Ji, C. Sun, H. Nan, J. Z. Wang, J.Q. Liao et al. Journal of Materials Research and Technology 2026-02-04 10.1016/j.jmrt.2026.01.240
单位: AECC Beijing Institute of Aeronautical Materials, Beijing 10095, China
通讯: G.R. Li @ Jiangsu University, Zhenjiang, Jiangsu Province, 212013, China
相关性: 该论文主要研究深冷处理对增材制造TC4合金力学性能的调控，而非直接关注打印态或工艺态Ti-6Al-4V的基础力学与疲劳性能。
边界: The paper's main contribution is the effect of deep cryogenic treatment (a post-processing step) on mechanical properties, not the as-built/process-state mechanical or fatigue behavior of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 解决激光增材制造TC4合金强度与塑性协同提升难题。
方法· 对增材制造和铸造TC4进行深冷处理实验，对比微观组织与力学性能。
结果· 深冷处理使增材制造TC4的强度提升3.5%-5%，延伸率提升33.9%。
验证· 通过拉伸测试和微观表征验证力学性能与组织演变。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To overcome the strength-ductility trade-off in laser additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· Deep cryogenic treatment was applied to both additively manufactured and as-cast TC4 specimens, followed by microstructural and mechanical characterization.
Result· DCT synergistically increased tensile strength, yield strength, and elongation of AM-TC4 by 3.5%, 5.0%, and 33.9%, respectively.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated by tensile tests and microstructural analysis via SEM, EBSD, and XRD.

A Comprehensive Investigation on Micro-Drilling Performance and Hole Quality of Ti6Al4V Alloy Fabricated by Electron Beam Melting

Murat Şen Arabian Journal for Science and Engineering 2026-02-04 10.1007/s13369-026-11087-3
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Marmara University, Istanbul, Turkey
通讯: Murat Şen @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Marmara University, Istanbul, Turkey
相关性: 该论文涉及EBM Ti6Al4V的微钻孔加工，但未研究后处理对疲劳或力学性能的改善，与用户方向不直接相关。
边界: This paper focuses on micro-drilling process parameters and hole quality, not on how post-processing modifies fatigue or mechanical performance of Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 研究EBM Ti6Al4V合金微钻孔的加工性能与孔质量
方法· 实验考察不同钻孔策略、主轴转速和进给率对推力和振动等的影响
结果· 最优参数为引孔策略、7500 r/min和5 μm/r，表面粗糙度2.3~3.5 μm
验证· 通过实验测量验证孔几何质量与加工条件的关系
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the micro-drilling performance and hole quality of Ti6Al4V alloy produced by electron beam melting under various drilling strategies.
Method· Experimental drilling tests were conducted with different spindle speeds, feed rates, and drilling strategies, measuring thrust force, vibration, tool temperature, and wear.
Result· Optimal hole quality was achieved using a pilot hole strategy at 7500 rpm and 5 µm/rev, with surface roughness Ra between 2.3 μm and 3.5 μm.
Validation· Experimental measurements of thrust force, vibration, tool temperature, and wear validated the process parameter optimization.
