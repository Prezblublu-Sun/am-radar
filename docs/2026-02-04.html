Effect of Gradient Layer Induced by Laser Shock Peening on Adhesion and Wear Resistance of AlCrN Coatings on TC4 Titanium Alloy

Ying Xu, Wenqian Yu, Xinlong Liao, Yanan Zhu, Boyong Su Materials 2026-02-04 10.3390/ma19030608
单位: Engineering Training Center, Nantong University, Nantong 226019, China
通讯: Boyong Su @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Nantong University, Nantong 226019, China
相关性: 该论文研究了激光冲击强化对TC4钛合金涂层结合力和耐磨性的影响，但未涉及疲劳或拉伸性能，与用户关注的后处理对疲劳/力学性能的改善方向相关但不够直接。
边界: No fatigue or tensile data; focuses on coating adhesion and wear resistance rather than bulk mechanical performance.
动机· 提高TC4钛合金上AlCrN涂层的结合力和耐磨性。
方法· 激光冲击强化形成梯度过渡层并再次沉积涂层。
结果· 梯度涂层结合力从13.6 N提高到43.3 N，磨损率降低。
验证· 通过划痕试验和磨损试验进行力学测试。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve adhesion and wear resistance of AlCrN coatings on TC4 titanium alloy.
Method· Laser shock peening was used to form a gradient transition layer, followed by secondary coating deposition.
Result· Gradient coating increased interfacial adhesion from 13.6 N to 43.3 N and reduced wear rate significantly.
Validation· Mechanical testing via scratch test and wear test.

A Comprehensive Investigation on Micro-Drilling Performance and Hole Quality of Ti6Al4V Alloy Fabricated by Electron Beam Melting

Murat Şen Arabian Journal for Science and Engineering 2026-02-04 10.1007/s13369-026-11087-3
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Marmara University, Istanbul, Turkey
通讯: Murat Şen @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Marmara University, Istanbul, Turkey
相关性: 该论文涉及EBM成形Ti6Al4V合金的微钻孔后处理工艺，但未研究其对疲劳或力学性能的影响，与关注后处理对力学性能影响的L2方向关联度低。
边界: No investigation of post-processing effect on fatigue or tensile mechanical performance; focuses solely on drilling process parameters and hole quality.
动机· 研究电子束熔融Ti6Al4V合金的微钻孔性能及孔质量优化。
方法· 采用不同钻孔策略、主轴转速和进给率进行实验，测量推力、振动、温度、磨损和孔质量。
结果· 导孔策略和低参数下获得最佳孔质量，推力随进给率和转速增大而增大。
验证· 通过孔几何形状优化参数并验证各评价指标。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the micro-drilling performance and hole quality of electron beam melted Ti6Al4V alloy under various drilling conditions.
Method· Experimental drilling tests with different strategies, spindle speeds, and feed rates, measuring thrust force, vibration, tool temperature, wear, and hole quality.
Result· Optimal hole quality achieved with pilot hole strategy at 7500 rpm and 5 µm/rev; thrust force and vibration increase with feed rate and spindle speed.
Validation· Process parameters optimized based on hole geometry and validated against multiple evaluation criteria.

Effect of build direction in Ti6Al4V alloy manufactured by EB-PBF on the corrosion performance of PEO coatings

Carlos A. Vargas, Alejandro A. Zuleta, Juan G. Castaño, Maryory A. Gómez, Carlos A. Botero et al. The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology 2026-02-04 10.1007/s00170-025-17297-7
单位: Grupo Materiales Avanzados y Energía (MATyER), Facultad de ingeniería, Instituto Tecnológico Metropolitano –ITM, Medellín, Colombia
通讯: Carlos A. Vargas @ Grupo Materiales Avanzados y Energía (MATyER), Facultad de ingeniería, Instituto Tecnológico Metropolitano –ITM, Medellí
相关性: 该论文主要研究EB-PBF Ti6Al4V的PEO涂层腐蚀性能，不涉及力学或疲劳性能。
边界: The paper focuses on corrosion performance of PEO coatings on EB-PBF Ti6Al4V, with no mechanical or fatigue testing, modeling, or analysis.
动机· 研究EB-PBF Ti6Al4V基体构建方向对PEO涂层腐蚀性能的影响。
方法· 在不同电流密度和时间下制备PEO涂层，分析形貌、相组成和腐蚀性能。
结果· 构建方向对涂层厚度和孔隙率影响微小，PEO处理消除了未涂层样品的腐蚀各向异性。
验证· 通过扫描电镜、X射线衍射和电化学测试进行实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the influence of build direction in EB-PBF Ti6Al4V on the corrosion performance of Plasma Electrolytic Oxidation coatings.
Method· PEO coatings were produced in Si-P electrolyte under varied current densities and treatment times, followed by characterization of morphology, phase composition, and corrosion resistance.
Result· Build direction had minimal effect on coating thickness and porosity, and PEO treatment eliminated corrosion anisotropy observed in uncoated EB-PBF Ti6Al4V.
Validation· Experimental validation via SEM, XRD, and electrochemical corrosion tests.
