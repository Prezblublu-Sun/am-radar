The effect of heat treatment on the structural and performance evolution of plasma electrolytic oxidation coatings

Fangquan Xi, Chao Deng, Zheng Zhang, Yang Liu, Jiyuan Chen et al. Journal of Materials Research and Technology 2025-10-18 10.1016/j.jmrt.2025.10.120
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering & Automation, Northeastern University, Shenyang 110819, China
通讯: Yang Liu @ Key Laboratory of Vibration and Control of Aero-Propulsion System Ministry of Education, Northeastern University, Shenya  |  Yuanyuan Kang @ School and Hospital of Stomatology, China Medical University, Liaoning Provincial Key Laboratory of Oral Diseases, ShenY
相关性: 该论文研究了热处理对Ti-6Al-4V合金上等离子电解氧化涂层疲劳性能的影响，虽非增材制造件，但其方法可转移至增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的后处理控制。
边界: This paper does not use additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V substrate; the starting microstructure and defects of AM parts would differ, limiting direct applicability.
动机· 改善生物医学植入物的机械耐久性，探究热处理对PEO涂层结构和性能的影响。
方法· 在不同温度下对Ti-6Al-4V合金上的PEO涂层进行热处理，表征相组成、表面特性和疲劳性能。
结果· 400°C热处理提高了涂层致密度和残余压应力，最佳疲劳抗力；600°C以上促进锐钛矿向金红石转变，降低疲劳寿命。
验证· 通过XRD、显微硬度、残余应力和疲劳实验验证了热处理温度的影响。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the mechanical durability of biomedical implants, this work investigates how heat treatment influences the structure and properties of plasma electrolytic oxidation (PEO) coatings on Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· PEO coatings on Ti-6Al-4V were heat-treated at various temperatures, and the evolution of phase composition, surface characteristics, and fatigue performance was evaluated.
Result· Heat treatment at 400°C improved coating densification and preserved residual compressive stresses, yielding the best fatigue resistance, while treatments above 600°C induced anatase-to-rutile transformation and microcracks, degrading fatigue life.
Validation· Validation was performed using X-ray diffraction, microhardness tests, residual stress analysis, and fatigue experiments.

Evaluation of the Physical, Mechanical, Tribological, Electrochemical, and Oxidation Resistance Behaviour of Spark Plasma Sintering-Synthesized Ceramic-Reinforced Ti6Al4V-Based Composites

John Olorunfemi Abe, Olawale Popoola, A.P.I. Popoola Journal of Bio- and Tribo-Corrosion 2025-10-18 10.1007/s40735-025-01059-9
单位: Department of Chemical, Metallurgical and Materials Engineering, Tshwane University of Technology, P.M.B. X680, Pretoria, South Africa
通讯: John Olorunfemi Abe @ Department of Chemical, Metallurgical and Materials Engineering, Tshwane University of Technology, P.M.B. X680, Pretoria
相关性: 涉及Ti6Al4V，但制备方法是火花等离子烧结，非增材制造，与方向不匹配。
边界: The paper uses spark plasma sintering (powder metallurgy) rather than additive manufacturing (LPBF/SLM/EBM/DED), so it does not address mechanical/fatigue performance of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 研究Ti6Al4V基复合材料在高温下的性能。
方法· 通过火花等离子烧结制备含不同陶瓷纳米颗粒的复合材料。
结果· 加入氮化硼等增强相提高了硬度、耐磨性和耐腐蚀性。
验证· 进行了密度、硬度、摩擦、电化学和氧化测试。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the performance of Ti6Al4V-based composites for high-temperature aerospace applications.
Method· Spark plasma sintering was used to synthesize composites with h-BN, TiN, and AlN nanoparticles.
Result· Reinforced composites showed enhanced microhardness, wear resistance, and corrosion resistance compared to unreinforced Ti6Al4V.
Validation· Experimental measurements of density, microhardness, tribological, electrochemical, and oxidation behavior.

Surface modification of Ti-13Nb-13Zr implant alloy by ZrO2 nano-particles coating using RF plasma magnetron sputtering

Hussein Khudhair Al-Kinani, A. Kianvash, Mohammad Rezvani, Taher Rabizadeh Results in Engineering 2025-10-18 10.1016/j.rineng.2025.107824
单位: Dept. of Materials Engineering, Institute of Mechanical Engineering, University of Tabriz, Tabriz 51664-16471, Iran
通讯: A. Kianvash @ Dept. of Materials Engineering, Institute of Mechanical Engineering, University of Tabriz, Tabriz 51664-16471, Iran
相关性: 该论文针对Ti-13Nb-13Zr合金的涂层改性，而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学或疲劳性能。
边界: This paper studies a different alloy (Ti-13Nb-13Zr) and focuses on surface coating for corrosion and biological properties, not on additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V mechanical or fatigue performance.
动机· 提高Ti-13Nb-13Zr植入合金的表面化学和生物学性能。
方法· 采用射频等离子体磁控溅射在合金表面沉积ZrO2纳米颗粒涂层。
结果· 150W功率下涂层具有最佳耐腐蚀性和羟基磷灰石形成能力。
验证· 通过XRD、SEM、电化学测试和SBF浸泡评价。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the chemical and biological properties of Ti-13Nb-13Zr implant alloy via surface coating.
Method· ZrO2 nanoparticle coating was deposited on the alloy surface using RF plasma magnetron sputtering at different powers.
Result· The coating deposited at 150 W exhibited the best corrosion resistance and highest hydroxyapatite formation after immersion in SBF.
Validation· The coatings were characterized by XRD, SEM, OCP, EIS, and SBF immersion tests.
