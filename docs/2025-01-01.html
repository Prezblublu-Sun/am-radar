Cementless long-stem fixation in periprosthetic clamshell fracture: a biomechanical investigation.

Lingqi Zhu, Li Xie, Mingchang He, Jianyun Miao, Liang Zhou et al. Frontiers in bioengineering and biotechnology 2025-01-01 10.3389/fbioe.2025.1604441
单位: Department of Orthopaedics, The 909th Hospital, School of Medicine, Xiamen University, Zhangzhou, Fujian, China.
通讯: Wenliang Zhai @ Department of Orthopaedics, The 909th Hospital, School of Medicine, Xiamen University, Zhangzhou, Fujian, China. [推断]
相关性: 该论文研究了无骨水泥长柄股骨假体在假体周围壳样骨折中的生物力学稳定性，与用户关注的股骨假体稳定性相关，但未涉及应力遮挡、增材制造或计算建模。
边界: This paper does not quantify stress shielding, does not involve additive manufacturing, and does not use computational modeling for implant design.
动机· 探究无骨水泥长柄假体治疗假体周围壳样骨折的生物力学强度
方法· 使用Sawbones股骨模型进行轴向压缩、扭转及失效测试
结果· 股骨内侧壁完整性对扭转稳定性关键，钢丝环扎可改善
验证· 人工骨模型的机械力学实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the biomechanical strength of cementless long-stem fixation in periprosthetic clamshell fractures.
Method· Mechanical testing of Sawbones femur models with axial compression, torsion, and failure tests.
Result· Medial wall integrity is critical for torsional stability; cerclage wiring improves torsional stability.
Validation· Experimental mechanical testing using Sawbones artificial bone models.

Radiographic Performance of a Novel Femoral Stem Design.

Jacob Hawkins, Emilie N Miley, Kendyll Coxen, Catalina Baez, Justin T Deen et al. The Iowa orthopaedic journal 2025-01-01 10.1016/j.artd.2022.01.020
单位: Orthopaedic Associates, Destin, Florida, USA.
通讯: Hari K Parvataneni @ Florida Orthopedic Institute, Gainesville, Florida, USA. [推断]
相关性: 该论文评估了一种新型股骨柄的影像学表现，但未涉及生物力学建模、有限元分析或增材制造，与用户的研究方向相关性较弱。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no FEA, no implant design or stress shielding analysis, and no AM or surrogate modeling.
动机· 评估新型股骨柄设计的影像学表现及骨整合模式。
方法· 回顾性分析592例全髋置换术后一年的影像学资料。
结果· 85.2%患者可见明确骨整合，假体翻修率仅0.2%。
验证· 临床放射学评估，无生物力学验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the radiographic performance and osseointegration patterns of a novel cementless femoral stem in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective single-center study reviewed radiographs of 592 primary or conversion THA cases at one-year follow-up.
Result· 85.2% of stems showed radiographic osseointegration with a 0.2% all-cause stem revision rate at one year.
Validation· Clinical radiographic assessment using spot weld criteria; no biomechanical or computational validation.

[Innovation in complex hip arthroplasty (Perthes sequel and developmental dysplasia of the hip): use of 3D models in surgical planning and simulation. Functional and radiographic results. Pilot study].

D Godoy-Monzón, E Fernández Sainz-Rozas, J M Pascual-Espinosa, J Jiménez-Baquero Acta ortopedica mexicana 2025-01-01 link
单位: Servicio de Ortopedia y Traumatología, Hospital de San Rafael, Hospitales Pascual. Cádiz, España.
通讯: J Jiménez-Baquero @ Servicio de Ortopedia y Traumatología, Hospital de San Rafael, Hospitales Pascual. Cádiz, España. [推断]
相关性: 该论文关注3D打印模型在髋关节置换术前规划中的临床应用，不涉及股骨柄的设计、有限元分析或制造工艺。
边界: This paper does not address femoral stem design, stress shielding, micromotion, or any mechanical or fatigue testing of implants.
动机· 评估3D打印模型在复杂髋关节置换术前规划中的效果。
方法· 对22例患者进行术前3D建模和手术模拟，并记录手术指标和随访结果。
结果· 3D模拟提高了假体放置准确性，Harris评分从术前37.5改善至术后90.0。
验证· 通过临床随访和影像学评估验证假体稳定性和功能恢复。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the outcomes of 3D-printed models for preoperative planning and simulation in complex total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Twenty-two patients with Perthes sequelae or hip dysplasia underwent CT-based 3D modeling and surgical simulation; surgery time, blood loss, complications, functional scores, and radiographic outcomes were recorded.
Result· The 3D simulation enabled accurate implant sizing and placement, with significant improvement in Harris Hip Score (from 37.5 to 90.0) and stable fixation at mean 40.7 months follow-up.
Validation· Validation was performed through clinical follow-up (functional scores, complications) and radiographic assessment (Moore's criteria for osteointegration).

Evaluating the risk factors for Lubinus SP II femoral stem fractures: A case series of 5 primary total hip arthroplasty patients with a mean follow-up of 7.5 years.

Alexis Panzures, Saim Faisel, Akkhash Sivakumar, Syed Ashoor Hasan Kazmi, Gareth Turnbull et al. SAGE open medical case reports 2025-01-01 10.1177/2050313X251366366
单位: University of Edinburgh, Edinburgh, UK.
通讯: Muhammad Adeel Akhtar @ University of Edinburgh, Edinburgh, UK.; Victoria Hospital, Kirkcaldy-NHS Fife, Scotland, UK.; University of St Andrews, [推断]
相关性: 该论文为临床病例研究，未涉及髋关节假体的生物力学建模、应力遮挡或增材制造设计。
边界: This paper is a clinical case series on stem fracture incidence and risk factors, with no implant design optimization, finite element analysis, mechanical testing, or additive manufacturing context.
动机· 评估Lubinus SP II股骨柄假体断裂的发生率与危险因素。
方法· 回顾性分析4244例初次全髋置换术，识别5例断裂并进行随访。
结果· 断裂率为0.1%，多发生于中段，与柄型号偏小、内翻位植入相关。
验证· 基于临床数据库的回顾性病例分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the incidence and risk factors of Lubinus SP II femoral stem fractures in a large cohort of primary total hip arthroplasty patients.
Method· A retrospective analysis of 4244 primary THAs identified 5 stem fractures; patient demographics, implant details, and radiographic parameters were collected with prospective follow-up.
Result· Stem fracture incidence was 0.1% (5/4240) at mean 7.5 years, predominantly mid-stem, and associated with undersized stems and varus alignment.
Validation· Clinical case series with prospective data collection from a single institution's arthroplasty database.

Impact of fracture reduction quality on clinical outcomes in hip arthroplasty for intertrochanteric fractures based on a novel radiographic evaluation system: a retrospective study.

Binquan Zhang, Jia Huo, Huijie Li Frontiers in medicine 2025-01-01 10.3389/fmed.2025.1637763
单位: Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Hebei Medical University Third Hospital, Shijiazhuang, Hebei, China.
通讯: Huijie Li @ Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Hebei Medical University Third Hospital, Shijiazhuang, Hebei, China. [推断]
相关性: 此论文为临床回顾性研究，关注骨折复位质量评估，与用户研究方向（AI生物打印、髋假体应力屏蔽、FEA代理模型、生物医学增材制造）无直接关联。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, no implant stress shielding analysis, no FEA, no AI/ML, no additive manufacturing, no implant design; purely clinical radiographic evaluation without contact mechanics or implant validation.
动机· 评估骨折复位质量对髋关节置换术临床结果的影响
方法· 基于四项放射学标准建立评价系统，回顾分析237例患者
结果· 高质量复位显著改善Harris评分并降低延迟愈合率
验证· 回顾性临床队列研究，统计调整骨折分型
English summary & terms
Motivation· To establish a standardized radiographic evaluation system for reduction quality in hip arthroplasty for intertrochanteric fractures and assess its correlation with clinical outcomes.
Method· A retrospective cohort of 237 patients was classified into optimal, acceptable, or poor reduction based on four radiographic criteria: greater trochanter alignment, lesser trochanter reduction, femoral stem stability, and postoperative femoral anteversion.
Result· Optimal reduction correlated with significantly better Harris Hip Scores (92.57 vs. 82.46), lower delayed healing (3.74% vs. 14.29%), and reduced abductor weakness (1.87% vs. 14.29%).
Validation· Retrospective clinical cohort study with statistical adjustment for AO/OTA fracture classification.
