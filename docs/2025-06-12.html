Overview of material constitutive law for additive manufacturing metal structures

Bing‐Lin Lai, Yiran Li, Tong Guo, George Vasdravellis, J.Y. Richard Liew Urban Lifeline 2025-06-12 10.1007/s44285-025-00046-3
单位: Key Laboratory of Concrete and Prestressed Concrete Structures of Ministry of Education, Southeast University, Nanjing, 211189, China
通讯: Bing‐Lin Lai @ Key Laboratory of Concrete and Prestressed Concrete Structures of Ministry of Education, Southeast University, Nanjing,
相关性: 该综述汇总了包括Ti-6Al-4V在内的多种增材制造金属的后处理对力学性能的影响，但缺乏针对Ti-6Al-4V后处理控制的新实验数据。
边界: Literature review without novel experimental data or quantitative comparison of post-processing effects on Ti-6Al-4V fatigue performance.
动机· 增材制造金属材料的力学性能尚未完全理解，需要系统总结。
方法· 汇总Ti6Al4V、17-4PH、304和316不锈钢的拉伸和低周疲劳数据，分析各向异性及后处理影响。
结果· 比较了单调拉伸和低周疲劳下增材制造金属的本构模型。
验证· 基于已发表文献的数据汇编和模型评估。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The mechanical properties of additively manufactured metals are not fully understood, motivating a systematic review of existing data and models.
Method· Compiles tensile and low-cycle fatigue data for Ti6Al4V, 17-4PH, 304 SS, and 316 SS from literature, analyzing effects of anisotropy, surface treatment, heat treatment, and other post-processing methods.
Result· Evaluates and compares constitutive models for monotonic tensile and low-cycle fatigue loading of AM metals.
Validation· Based on literature data compilation and model comparison without new experimental validation.

Effect of oxygen content in feedstock powders on microstructure and mechanical properties of ELI Ti-6Al-4V fabricated via laser powder bed fusion

Woo Hyeok Kim, Sangwoo Kim, Raj Narayan Hajra, Gargi Roy, Jeoung Han Kim Powder Metallurgy 2025-06-12 10.1177/00325899251348352
单位: Department of Materials Science &amp; Engineering, Hanbat National University, Daejeon, South Korea
通讯: Jeoung Han Kim @ Department of Materials Science &amp; Engineering, Hanbat National University, Daejeon, South Korea
相关性: 文章涉及热处理（热等静压）对Ti-6Al-4V微观组织和硬度的影响，与后处理方向相关。
边界: 主要变量为粉末氧含量，而非系统研究后处理工艺对力学性能（如疲劳）的影响。
动机· 研究激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V中氧含量对组织和性能的影响。
方法· 采用不同氧含量粉末进行LPBF成型并进行热等静压处理，通过光谱和XRD分析微观结构。
结果· 氧含量增加导致晶格畸变和硬度升高，热等静压后α板条厚度减小。
验证· 通过维氏硬度和微观结构表征验证氧含量和热等静压的影响。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how the oxygen content in ELI Ti-6Al-4V powders affects the microstructure and mechanical properties of LPBF-fabricated samples.
Method· Samples were fabricated with varying oxygen content powders and subjected to hot isostatic pressing; microstructural analysis was performed using spectroscopy and XRD.
Result· Higher oxygen content increased lattice distortion and Vickers hardness, while HIP treatment reduced alpha lath thickness.
Validation· Validation was conducted through Vickers hardness measurements and microstructural characterization.

INTELLIGENT HYBRID OPTIMIZATION OF WEDM FOR Ti-6Al-4V USING TAGUCHI DEEP LEARNING AND ANOVA FOR ENHANCED SURFACE INTEGRITY AND PREDICTION ACCURACY

Kannan Pattanam Ramamoorthy, S. Balasubramani, S. Dinesh, Monica Arul Materiali in tehnologije 2025-06-12 10.17222/mit.2025.1393
单位: Mahendra Institute of Technology, Namakkal 637503, Tamil Nadu, India
通讯: Kannan Pattanam Ramamoorthy @ Mahendra Institute of Technology, Namakkal 637503, Tamil Nadu, India
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的线切割放电加工参数优化，但未涉及增材制造后处理（如热处理、表面强化）对疲劳或力学性能的影响，与用户方向的直接相关性低。
边界: Not about post-processing of additive manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it focuses on wire EDM process parameters for surface roughness, not on modifying fatigue or mechanical performance via post-processing treatments like heat treatment, HIP, or shot peening.
动机· 优化Ti-6Al-4V线切割放电加工的表面质量。
方法· 结合Taguchi正交实验、深度神经网络和方差分析建立混合优化框架。
结果· 最优参数组合使表面粗糙度降至1.50 μm，ANN预测精度超过95%。
验证· 通过WEDM实验和ANN预测验证模型有效性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance surface integrity of Ti-6Al-4V by optimizing Wire Electrical Discharge Machining parameters through a hybrid approach.
Method· A hybrid framework integrating Taguchi's L27 orthogonal array, Deep Neural Networks, and Analysis of Variance was employed to model and optimize the effect of pulse-on time, pulse-off time, and wire feed rate on surface roughness.
Result· Optimal parameters (Ton=3 µs, Toff=4 µs, Wf=2 m/min) achieved a minimum Ra of 1.50 µm, and the ANN model predicted roughness with over 95% accuracy.
Validation· The approach was validated by conducting WEDM experiments and comparing measured surface roughness with ANN predictions, confirmed by ANOVA at p=0.05.
