Microstructural evolution and enhanced performance of B4C-reinforced Al-5Mg alloy via wire arc additive manufacturing: mechanical properties and wear mechanisms

单位: Engineering Research Center of Continuous Extrusion, Ministry of Education, Dalian Jiaotong University, Dalian, 116028, China
通讯: Guangzong Zhang @ Engineering Research Center of Continuous Extrusion, Ministry of Education, Dalian Jiaotong University, Dalian, 116028,  |  Renguo Guan @ Engineering Research Center of Continuous Extrusion, Ministry of Education, Dalian Jiaotong University, Dalian, 116028,
相关性: 该论文直接研究了电弧增材制造Al-5Mg合金的力学性能和磨损机制，与用户关注的增材制造力学性能方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not address biomedical implants, bone-implant interface, or stress shielding; it focuses on Al-Mg alloys for aerospace/automotive, not biomedical AM.

结果· 0.4 wt% B4C使抗拉强度提升7.1%，耐磨性提高57.4%。
验证· 通过拉伸、硬度、磨损试验及微观表征验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To overcome the inherent limitations in hardness and wear resistance of WAAM-processed Al-5Mg alloys, this study investigates the effect of interlayer B4C particle addition.
Method· Different weight fractions (0.2–0.6 wt%) of B4C particles were introduced into the interlayer during wire arc additive manufacturing of Al-5Mg alloy, and the resulting microstructures, mechanical properties, and tribological behavior were characterized.
Result· Addition of 0.4 wt% B4C reduced grain size by 52.8%, increased ultimate tensile strength by 7.1% to 300 MPa, and decreased wear rate by 57.4%.
Validation· The findings were validated through tensile testing, hardness measurements, wear tests, and microstructural analysis using SEM and EBSD.
