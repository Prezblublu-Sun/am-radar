相关性: 该研究直接关注增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的超声疲劳行为及构建取向影响，包含实验验证和不确定性量化，与用户方向高度相关。
边界: No post-processing or implant-specific focus; paper covers as-built fatigue characterization with uncertainty framework.
动机· 研究构建取向对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V超声疲劳行为的影响及校准不确定性。
方法· 采用超声疲劳试验、DIC和激光位移测量，结合蒙特卡洛模拟量化不确定性。

English summary & terms

Result· Horizontal builds exhibited 5-9% higher effective stiffness than vertical builds, and calibration uncertainty can cause two-order-of-magnitude variations in predicted very high cycle fatigue life.
Validation· Experimental data from five specimens per material-orientation combination and early fracture analysis confirmed the findings.
