arXiv preprint#3D printing#mechanical properties#metamaterials
💡 探索新型3D碳同素异形体Diamondiyne的力学性能，为轻质吸能超材料提供基础。

arXiv preprint#additive manufacturing#tungsten alloys#ductility

★ Microstructural evolution and enhanced performance of B4C-reinforced Al-5Mg alloy via wire…
Wenyi Zhang, Siqi Yin et al. · AM Mechanical Properties
Additive Manufacturing Frontiers#WAAM#Al-5Mg#B4C reinforcement
💡 解决WAAM制备Al-5Mg合金硬度和耐磨性差的问题。
Read more
方法: 在WAAM层间添加不同含量B4C颗粒，研究其微观结构和力学性能。
