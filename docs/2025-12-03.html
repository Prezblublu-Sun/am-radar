On the relation between microstructure and impact toughness of 17-4__PH stainless steel produced by powder bed fusion laser beam (PBF-LB)

Renata de Oliveira Melo, Jean-Bernard Vogt, Eric Nivet, Flore Villaret, Christophe Grosjean et al. arXiv preprint 2025-12-03 link
相关性: 该论文直接研究了增材制造17-4PH不锈钢的微观结构与冲击韧性关系，与用户研究方向高度相关。
边界: This paper focuses on a specific alloy and does not cover other AM materials or broader mechanical properties such as fatigue or wear.
动机· 探究增材制造17-4PH钢的强韧化机制。
方法· 对PBF-LB试样进行不同时效热处理，开展多尺度微观表征和力学测试。
结果· 增材制造试样拉伸强度优于传统制造，但冲击韧性显著降低；逆奥氏体相变提高韧性；SiO2夹杂和Cu析出相影响力学性能。
验证· 通过准静态拉伸和动态冲击测试验证微观结构与力学性能的关联。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To elucidate the relationship between microstructure and impact toughness in additively manufactured 17-4 PH stainless steel.
Method· Aging heat treatments were applied to PBF-LB specimens, followed by multiscale microstructural characterization and mechanical testing.
Result· PBF-LB specimens exhibited comparable tensile strength but significantly lower impact toughness; strain-induced reversed austenite improved toughness, while SiO2 inclusions and Cu-rich nanoprecipitates played contrasting roles.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through quasi-static tensile and dynamic impact tests combined with microstructural analysis.

Double Perovskites K2NbTaO6 and Rb2NbTaO6 from First-Principles: Towards Efficient Materials for Green Energy

Ouendadji Salima, Aissani Ali, El Haj Hassan Fouad, Benahmedi Lakhdar arXiv preprint 2025-12-03 link
相关性: 该论文研究双钙钛矿材料的电子和光学性质，与增材制造力学性能无关。
边界: This paper is a first-principles study of double perovskite compounds for energy applications, containing no additive manufacturing, mechanical testing, or biomedical context.
动机· 探索双钙钛矿用于绿色能源的潜力
方法· 基于第一性原理计算结构、电子、弹性等性质
结果· 材料呈半导体性，脆性，紫外区有吸收
验证· 仅理论计算，无实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the potential of double perovskite oxides for green energy applications requiring coupled optical and mechanical performance.
Method· First-principles computations were used to assess the structural, electronic, elastic, optical, and thermoelectric properties of K2NbTaO6 and Rb2NbTaO6.
Result· Both compounds are brittle semiconductors with band gaps around 2.6–2.8 eV and show high UV absorption.
Validation· No experimental validation; stability criteria were checked via Born criteria.

Layered XZnBi (X = Rb, Cs) with Pudding-Mold Bands, Complex Fermi Surfaces and Low Thermal Conductivity: A First-Principles Study of Thermoelectric Properties

Aadil Fayaz Wani, Nirma Kumari, SuDong Park, Byungki Ryu arXiv preprint 2025-12-03 link
相关性: 该论文研究热电材料性能，与用户研究方向无任何关联。
边界: This paper studies thermoelectric properties of Zintl compounds using first-principles, with no additive manufacturing, no biomedical implants, no mechanical testing, no AI/ML, and no FEA surrogate methods.
动机· 探究层状Zintl化合物XZnBi的热电性能。
方法· 第一性原理结合玻尔兹曼方程计算电子和热输运。
结果· RbZnBi和CsZnBi在900K时ZT最高达2.42（CRTA），考虑el-ph后降至0.52。
验证· 理论计算，无实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the thermoelectric performance of layered Zintl compounds XZnBi (X=Rb,Cs) through first-principles calculations.
Method· First-principles calculations combined with Boltzmann transport equations, including electron-phonon coupling and three-phonon scattering to evaluate transport coefficients and lattice thermal conductivity.
Result· High ZT values up to 2.42 at 900K under constant relaxation time approximation, reduced to 0.52 when electron-phonon coupling is included, with stacking order significantly affecting lattice thermal conductivity.
Validation· Theoretical predictions without experimental validation, relying on first-principles accuracy.
