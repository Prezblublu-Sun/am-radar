Enhancing ductility of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V via engineered porosity and post hot isostatic pressing treatment

Zi-Qi Zhao, M.Y. Xu, C.H. Wang, Lingyun Qian, Yongchun Li et al. Materials Science and Engineering A 2025-07-16 10.1016/j.msea.2025.148834
单位: Beijing Key Laboratory of Lightweight Metal Forming, Beijing, 100083, China
通讯: Peipei Li @ Beijing Key Laboratory of Lightweight Metal Forming, Beijing, 100083, China
相关性: 该论文直接研究了热等静压后处理对SLM Ti-6Al-4V延展性的影响，完全符合L2方向要求。
边界: Does not include fatigue testing, only monotonic tensile ductility.
动机· 通过可控孔隙度和热等静压处理增强增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的延展性。
方法· 采用激光粉末床熔融制备含设计孔隙的Ti-6Al-4V试样，随后进行热等静压处理。
结果· 热等静压后孔隙闭合，延展性显著提升，强度略有降低。
验证· 通过拉伸测试和微观结构表征验证延展性改善效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the ductility of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V by combining engineered porosity with hot isostatic pressing (HIP) post-treatment.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V specimens with controlled initial porosity were fabricated by laser powder bed fusion and subsequently subjected to HIP.
Result· HIP treatment closed the engineered pores and significantly enhanced ductility, with a slight reduction in strength.
Validation· Validation was performed through tensile testing and microstructural analysis before and after HIP.

Y2O3-Doped BioGlass Coatings for Ti-6Al-4V Alloy: Insights into Degradation, Bioactivity, Ion Release, Adhesion, and Corrosion Behavior

Yakup Say, Naim Aslan, Salih Cihangir, Oğuzkağan Murat, Ulaş Çaydaş Metallurgical and Materials Transactions A 2025-07-16 10.1007/s11661-025-07896-0
单位: Department of Machine and Metallic Technology, Munzur University, Tunceli, Türkiye
通讯: Yakup Say @ Department of Machine and Metallic Technology, Munzur University, Tunceli, Türkiye
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V合金的涂层改性，但未涉及股骨柄结构设计、增材制造或力学性能，与用户关注的Ti-6Al-4V股骨柄结构设计和服务性能方向无关。
边界: No femoral stem design, no additive manufacturing, no structural/mechanical analysis of an implant; this paper studies coatings on Ti-6Al-4V alloy, not a stem or implant structure.
动机· 提高Ti-6Al-4V合金的耐腐蚀性和生物活性，减少离子释放。
方法· 采用溶胶-凝胶法在Ti-6Al-4V合金上制备Y2O3掺杂的生物玻璃涂层。
结果· 10 wt% Y2O3掺杂使涂层附着力提高52%，但降低生物活性和耐腐蚀性，增加Ti离子释放。
验证· 通过附着力测试、降解实验、生物活性测试和离子释放测量进行实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the corrosion resistance, biocompatibility, and reduce ion release of Ti-6Al-4V alloy for orthopedic applications.
Method· Y2O3-doped BioGlass coatings were synthesized by sol-gel method and applied on Ti-6Al-4V substrates.
Result· The addition of 10 wt% Y2O3 increased adhesion strength by 52% but negatively affected bioactivity, corrosion resistance, and increased Ti ion release.
Validation· Experimental validation through adhesion tests, degradation studies, bioactivity assays, and ion release measurements.
