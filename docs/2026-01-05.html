FIR transmission and resistively detected cyclotron resonances in InSb

M. E. Bal, N. Deßmann, K. Saeedi, U. Zeitler arXiv preprint 2026-01-05 link
相关性: 该论文涉及半导体物理与太赫兹光谱，与用户的研究方向（AM力学性能）完全无关。
边界: This paper focuses on magneto-transmission and cyclotron resonances in InSb semiconductor physics, with no relation to additive manufacturing, mechanical testing, or biomedical implants.
动机· 研究InSb中远红外透射与光电压响应中的回旋共振现象。
方法· 搭建同时测量磁透射光谱和光导响应的实验装置，分析低频磁场下InSb的交流电导率。
结果· 观察到了与k·p微扰理论一致的回旋共振，提取有效质量为0.014me，并发现依赖通量的金属-绝缘体转变。
验证· 理论与实验对比，磁透射和光电压数据一致。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate cyclotron resonances in InSb via simultaneous far-infrared transmission and photoconductive measurements.
Method· An experimental setup was developed to acquire magneto-transmission spectra and measure photoconductive response at low temperatures and high magnetic fields.
Result· Cyclotron resonances consistent with k·p perturbation theory were observed, yielding an effective mass of 0.014m_e, and a fluence-dependent metal-insulator transition was detected.
Validation· Experimental magneto-transmission data and photovoltage signals were compared with theoretical predictions.

Anharmonic lattice dynamics study of phonon transport in layered and molecular-crystal indium iodides

Takuma Shiga, Yoshikazu Mizuguchi, Hiroshi Fujihisa arXiv preprint 2026-01-05 link
相关性: 该研究与用户方向（生物打印、髋关节植入物、有限元代理模型、增材制造）完全无关，属于固体物理和热传输领域。
边界: This paper studies phonon transport in indium iodides with no connection to biomedical implants, additive manufacturing, finite element analysis, or any biomechanical context.
动机· 研究碘化铟层状和分子晶体中低热导率的机制。
方法· 采用第一性原理非简谐晶格动力学计算。
结果· 两种材料热导率低于1 W/m-K，波状传热影响因组成而异。
验证· 理论计算与实验证据对比。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand the origin of low lattice thermal conductivity in layered and molecular-crystal indium iodides.
Method· First-principles anharmonic lattice dynamics calculations are used to analyze both particle-like and wave-like phonon transport.
Result· Both InI and InI3 exhibit thermal conductivities below 1 W/m-K over a wide temperature range, with wave-like contributions significant only in InI3.
Validation· Comparison with experimental indications of stacking faults and partial disorder in the rhombohedral phase.
