Study on the influence of tool structure on the gyroscopic milling performance of CFRP/Ti-6Al-4V stacked materials

Hui Xiao, Tao Chen, Chunhui Li, Guangyue Wang, Zhilong Yu et al. Chinese Journal of Mechanical Engineering 2026-02-01 10.1016/j.cjme.2026.100236
单位: School of Mechanical and Power Engineering, Harbin University of Science and Technology, Harbin 150080, China
通讯: Tao Chen @ School of Mechanical and Power Engineering, Harbin University of Science and Technology, Harbin 150080, China
相关性: 该论文研究CFRP/Ti-6Al-4V叠层材料的加工，而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的后处理对力学性能的影响。
边界: This paper focuses on machining of CFRP/Ti-6Al-4V stacks, not on post-processing treatments (e.g., heat treatment, HIP, surface modification) of AM Ti-6Al-4V to improve fatigue/mechanical performance.
动机· 研究工具结构对CFRP/Ti-6Al-4V叠层材料回旋铣削性能的影响。
方法· 使用三种不同刀尖圆弧半径的铣刀进行回旋铣削对比实验，测量切削温度、轴向力和孔质量。
结果· 增大主轴转速、减小轨道转速和进给速度可降低轴向力；增大刀尖圆弧半径可降低切削温度和轴向力，改善孔出口质量。
验证· 通过实验对比三种刀具在不同工艺参数下的铣削结果进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the influence of tool corner radius on the gyroscopic milling performance of CFRP/Ti-6Al-4V stacked materials to improve hole quality.
Method· Comparative experiments were conducted using three milling cutters with different corner radii on a self-developed gyroscopic milling actuator, with a multi-point thermocouple configuration for temperature measurement.
Result· Increasing spindle speed while decreasing orbital speed and feed rate reduces axial force; larger tool corner radius decreases cutting temperature and axial force, and improves hole exit quality.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed by testing the three cutters under various machining parameters and measuring cutting temperature, axial force, and hole quality.
