Short vs standard stems perioperative blood loss and complication risk

Nikolai Ramadanov, Maximilian Voss, Maximilian Heinz, Robert Prill, Dakota Fuchs et al. European journal of medical research 2026-02-23 10.1186/s40001-026-04070-z
单位: Center of Orthopaedics and Traumatology, Brandenburg Medical School, University Hospital Brandenburg/Havel, 14770, Brandenburg/Havel, Germany. nikolai.ramadanov@gmail.com
通讯: Nikolai Ramadanov @ Center of Orthopaedics and Traumatology, Brandenburg Medical School, University Hospital Brandenburg/Havel, 14770, Brand
相关性: 该论文比较了短柄与标准柄在初次全髋置换中的失血和并发症，未涉及应力遮挡、微动或植入物力学建模，属于临床结局研究。
边界: This paper is a retrospective clinical cohort study on blood loss and complications, containing no biomechanical modeling, implant design, FEA, or additive manufacturing relevant to femoral stem mechanics.
动机· 探讨短柄与标准柄在全髋置换中失血和并发症的差异
方法· 回顾性队列研究，多变量回归分析调整混杂因素
结果· 短柄独立降低术中及总失血量，隐性失血和并发症无显著差异
验证· 临床回顾性队列数据验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare perioperative blood loss and complications between short and standard femoral stems in primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective cohort study using multivariable regression to adjust for confounders such as age, sex, BMI, and ASA class.
Result· Short stems were independently associated with lower intraoperative and total blood loss, but not with hidden blood loss or complication rates.
Validation· Clinical data from a retrospective cohort of elective primary THA patients.

Role of octahedral tilting induced acoustic softening on limiting thermal transport in SrSnO3

Yuzhou Hao, Turab Lookman, Xiangdong Ding, Jun Sun, Zhibin Gao arXiv preprint 2026-02-23 10.1103/76nt-hw3h
相关性: 该论文研究的是钙钛矿氧化物的热输运性质，与用户的生物医学植入物、AI生物打印、FEA代理模型或增材制造方向完全无关。
边界: This paper studies thermal transport in perovskite oxides, with no biomedical application, no implant mechanics, no FEA surrogate, no additive manufacturing context.
动机· 探究八面体倾斜对SrSnO3热导率的影响机制。
方法· 通过第一性原理计算分析不同相中声子谱和热输运性质。
结果· 发现八面体倾斜导致声学软化，显著降低热导率。
验证· 理论计算与实验数据对比验证趋势。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the mechanism by which octahedral tilting influences lattice thermal conductivity in SrSnO3, revealing an unexpected acoustic softening effect.
Method· First-principles calculations of phonon dispersions and thermal transport across orthorhombic, tetragonal, and cubic phases of SrSnO3.
Result· Octahedral tilting in the tetragonal phase induces acoustic phonon softening, reducing lattice thermal conductivity from 7.48 to 6.06 W/mK with a mere 1-degree tilt increase.
Validation· Computational results are compared with available experimental thermal conductivity data for consistency.

Tunable dislocations overcome mechano-functional tradeoff in perovskite oxides

Jiawen Zhang, Wenjun Lu, Xufei Fang arXiv preprint 2026-02-23 link
相关性: 该论文研究钙钛矿氧化物中位错对力学和功能性能的调控，与增材制造力学性能方向无关。
边界: This paper does not involve additive manufacturing or AM-specific mechanical properties; it focuses on dislocation engineering in bulk perovskite oxides.
动机· 探索位错工程对陶瓷力学与功能性能的协同调控
方法· 在KTaO₃中引入不同密度位错，测试力学与热导性能
结果· 发现脆-韧-脆非单调转变，中间密度位错实现高延展性
验证· 通过单轴压缩实验和热导率测量验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To overcome the mechano-functional tradeoff in perovskite oxides via dislocation engineering.
Method· Seeded dislocations of varying densities were introduced into KTaO₃ single crystals, followed by mechanical and thermal conductivity testing.
Result· A non-monotonic brittle-ductile-brittle transition was observed, with intermediate dislocation densities achieving >20% strain.
Validation· Mechanical compression tests and thermal conductivity measurements validated the dislocation density-dependent behavior.

Mechanical and Structural Contributions to Anisotropy in Granular Materials

Mehdi Pouragha, Gertraud Medicus, Selvarajah Premnath, Siva Sivathayalan arXiv preprint 2026-02-23 link
相关性: 与用户研究方向无关，属于岩土力学领域，未涉及任何生物医学工程内容。
边界: This paper addresses anisotropy in granular materials with no application to biomedical implants, bioprinting, or FEA surrogate methods for biomechanics.
动机· 分离颗粒材料中力学各向异性和结构各向异性的贡献。
方法· 发展一阶线性化方法，利用两个独立取向度量量化各向异性成分。
结果· 力学各向异性始终更强，且随偏应力增大其相对优势下降。
验证· 通过空心圆柱试验和多方向加载验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This work aims to decouple the mechanical and structural contributions to anisotropy in granular materials.
Method· A first-order linearization of the incremental stress-strain response is developed using two independent orientation measures to quantify each component from macroscopic data.
Result· Mechanical anisotropy is consistently stronger than structural anisotropy, but its relative dominance decreases as the deviatoric stress increases.
Validation· The method is validated using hollow-cylinder tests with varied loading directions and compared to an isotropic hypoplastic model.
