Effects of annealing-tailored microstructure on mechanical properties and deformation behaviors of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V Alloy

Yi Ren, Canghai Yin, Yongxun Li, Wenzhe Nie, Shan Tang et al. Journal of Alloys and Compounds 2025-12-23 10.1016/j.jallcom.2025.185782
单位: School of Aeronautics, Chongqing Jiaotong University, Chongqing 400074, China
通讯: Yi Ren @ School of Aeronautics, Chongqing Jiaotong University, Chongqing 400074, China
相关性: 该论文研究了退火后处理对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V合金微观结构和力学性能的影响，与方向2中的退火热处理直接相关。
边界: 本文聚焦于退火热处理对Ti-6Al-4V合金力学性能的影响，属于后处理研究，但未涉及其他后处理如HIP、喷丸等。
动机· 探究退火调控微观结构对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V力学性能的影响
方法· 对不同退火温度和时间处理后的试样进行微观表征和力学测试
结果· 退火处理显著改变了α/β相比例和晶粒尺寸，优化了强度与塑性的平衡
验证· 通过拉伸试验和显微硬度测量验证微观结构-性能关系
English summary & terms
Motivation· This work investigates how annealing-tailored microstructure influences the mechanical properties of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· Specimens were subjected to various annealing temperatures and durations, followed by microstructural characterization and mechanical testing.
Result· Annealing significantly altered α/β phase fraction and grain size, achieving a balanced improvement in strength and ductility.
Validation· Tensile tests and microhardness measurements were used to validate the microstructure-property relationships.

Probing the Phase Composition and Surface Roughness in the Biological Response of Additively Manufactured Titanium Alloy Bioimplants

Lu Feng Yang, Yanhao Hou, Duo Meng, Axieh Bagasol, Fan Wu et al. ACS Omega 2025-12-23 10.1021/acsomega.5c08853
单位: Department of Materials
通讯: Weiguang Wang @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, School of Engineering  |  Wajira Mirihanage @ Department of Materials
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的后处理（HIP）但对力学性能无研究，仅关注生物响应。
边界: no mechanical fatigue or tensile data; focus is biological performance not mechanical post-processing effect.
动机· 探究表面粗糙度和相组成对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V植入物生物性能的影响。
方法· 通过LPBF和HIP制备样品，进行细胞活力测试。
结果· 表面粗糙度相较于相组成对生物性能影响更大，HIP处理提高细胞活力。
验证· 体外细胞实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study investigates how surface roughness and phase composition influence the biological performance of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V implants.
Method· Samples were fabricated via laser powder bed fusion (LPBF) and hot isostatic pressing (HIP), followed by cell viability assays.
Result· Surface roughness dominates over phase composition in determining biological response, and HIP-treated samples show enhanced cell viability compared to as-built LPBF samples.
Validation· Validation was performed through in vitro cell culture experiments.

Influence of cross-shear and contact stress on the wear of UHMWPE on boost-diffusion-treated additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V

L. Creme, R.M. Cowie, J. van der Merwe, L M Jennings, T.H. Becker Tribology International 2025-12-23 10.1016/j.triboint.2025.111619
通讯: J. van der Merwe @ Department of Mechanical and Mechatronic Engineering, Stellenbosch University, South Africa  |  T.H. Becker @ Centre for Materials Engineering, University of Cape Town, South Africa
相关性: 本文涉及增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的Boost扩散后处理，但关注的是UHMWPE的磨损行为而非钛合金本身的疲劳或力学性能，与后处理控制方向关联弱。
边界: This paper studies wear of UHMWPE against BD-treated Ti-6Al-4V, not fatigue, tensile, or porosity closure of the Ti-6Al-4V itself, and thus does not directly address the post-processing control of mechanical performance as defined in this direction.
动机· 探究增材制造Ti-6Al-4V经Boost扩散处理后与UHMWPE对磨的磨损行为。
方法· 采用多方向销盘磨损试验，改变交叉剪切比和接触应力。
结果· 初始磨损高因表面粗糙，后续磨损率降低且表面无退化。
验证· 销盘磨损实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the wear behavior of UHMWPE against boost-diffusion-treated additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V under varying cross-shear and contact stress conditions.
Method· Multidirectional pin-on-plate wear tests were conducted with cross-shear ratios from 0 to 0.3 and contact stresses from 0.56 to 14 MPa.
Result· Initial high wear due to surface roughness was followed by polishing of the BD-treated surface, reducing friction and wear rate; no surface degradation was observed.
Validation· Experimental pin-on-plate wear testing with surface characterization.

SYNTHESIS AND INVESTIGATION OF TITANIUM-GOLD (TI-AU) BASED THIN COATINGS FOR BIOMEDICAL APPLICATIONS WITH ENHANCED PHYSICAL-MECHANICAL PROPERTIES

D. K. Yeskermessov, C. C. Lukose, M. Birkett, S. A. Pazylbek, A. S. Yeskermessova et al. Herald of Kazakh-British technical university 2025-12-23 10.55452/1998-6688-2025-22-4-374-385
单位: D. Serikbayev East Kazakhstan Technical University»
通讯: D. K. Yeskermessov @ D. Serikbayev East Kazakhstan Technical University»
相关性: 该论文研究Ti-Au涂层而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学或疲劳性能。
边界: The paper does not address additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it focuses on PVD-deposited Ti-Au coatings on Ti6Al4V substrates, which is outside the scope of AM mechanical/fatigue performance.
动机· 开发具有优异物理力学性能的Ti-Au基薄涂层用于生物医学应用。
方法· 采用直流/射频磁控溅射在Ti6Al4V基体上沉积Ti-Au涂层，添加Ag和Cu，并研究其微观结构和性能。
结果· PVD改性提高了Ti6Al4V基体的耐磨性并降低了摩擦系数，归因于Ti3Au相的形成。
验证· 通过SEM、EDX、XRD、AFM、纳米压痕和摩擦学测试进行表征。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop Ti-Au-based thin coatings with enhanced physical-mechanical properties for biomedical applications.
Method· Ti-Au coatings were deposited on Ti6Al4V substrates by DC and RF magnetron sputtering, with Ag and Cu additions, and characterized for microstructure and properties.
Result· PVD modification improved wear resistance and reduced coefficient of friction of the Ti6Al4V substrate, attributed to the formation of the Ti3Au phase.
Validation· Characterization via SEM, EDX, XRD, AFM, nanoindentation, and tribological tests.

Method· Brazing experiments were conducted with different groove distances prepared by fiber laser, followed by mechanical testing and microstructural characterization.
Result· Groove distance significantly influences the interfacial microstructure and joint strength.
Validation· Tensile testing and microstructural analysis were used to validate the findings.

Prediction and multi-objective optimization of heat affected zone in femtosecond laser machining of Ti-6Al-4V alloy using GA-enhanced machine learning

Hao Yang, MingRui Lu, Chongyang Han, Dongyu Han, Dongming Li et al. Measurement Science and Technology 2025-12-23 10.1088/1361-6501/ae309b
单位: School of Electromechanical Engineering, Dalian Minzu University, Dalian 116600, People’s Republic of China
通讯: Hao Yang @ School of Electromechanical Engineering, Dalian Minzu University, Dalian 116600, People’s Republic of China
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的飞秒激光加工热影响区预测，但未涉及增材制造件的后处理，与方向无关。
边界: The paper does not address post-processing of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it studies femtosecond laser drilling of wrought alloy for cooling holes, with no mechanical testing or fatigue data.
动机· 精确控制飞秒激光钻孔热影响区厚度以保证结构完整性。
方法· 结合高斯支持向量回归和遗传算法进行预测与多目标优化。
结果· 实现了热影响区厚度的可靠预测和参数优化。
验证· 利用实验数据验证模型预测精度。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To precisely control the heat-affected zone thickness during femtosecond laser drilling of Ti-6Al-4V for ensuring structural integrity of turbine blade cooling holes.
Method· A hybrid approach integrating Gaussian support vector regression (G-SVR) with genetic algorithm (GA) for prediction and multi-objective optimization of HAZ thickness.
Result· The G-SVR-GA framework achieves reliable prediction accuracy and provides optimized laser parameter sets for desired HAZ thickness.
Validation· Experimental validation was conducted by comparing model predictions with measured HAZ thickness from laser drilling tests.
