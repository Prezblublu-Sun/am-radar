In Situ Synchrotron Radiation Computed Tomography Study on Fatigue Damage Evolution of Additively Manufactured Ti-6Al-4V Alloy

Hui Wang, Guangcheng Fan, Yu Xiao Crystals 2026-03-11 10.3390/cryst16030195
单位: School of Engineering Science, University of Science and Technology of China, Hefei 230027, China
通讯: Hui Wang @ School of Engineering Science, University of Science and Technology of China, Hefei 230027, China
相关性: 该论文直接研究了激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V合金的疲劳损伤演化，属于L1方向的核心内容。
边界: The paper covers exactly the core focus: experimental fatigue characterization of LPBF Ti-6Al-4V with in situ tomography.
动机· 揭示激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V合金的疲劳损伤演化机制。
方法· 利用原位同步辐射计算机断层扫描技术观察疲劳裂纹扩展。
结果· 发现裂纹桥接与偏转现象，局部高密度区与裂纹扩展方向一致。
验证· 通过疲劳实验和SR-CT原位观测验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand the fatigue damage evolution mechanism in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V alloy using in situ 3D imaging.
Method· In situ synchrotron radiation computed tomography (SR-CT) was employed during fatigue testing to observe crack propagation and local density variations.
Result· Crack bridging and deflection were observed, and local high-density zones (LHDZs) correlated with crack propagation direction and basket-weave α-phase microstructure.
Validation· Experimental validation via in situ fatigue testing with SR-CT imaging and quantitative crack growth analysis.

单位: National Demonstration Center for Experimental Mechanical Engineering Education (Shandong University)
通讯: Yanle Li @ National Demonstration Center for Experimental Mechanical Engineering Education (Shandong University)
相关性: 论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V作为基底的双金属结构，但主要关注异种合金界面结合性能而非Ti-6Al-4V本身的力学与疲劳行为。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the poor bonding performance and fracture toughness of bimetallic structures under extreme temperature gradients.

Result· The hybrid process enhances bonding strength by 31.4% and increases the stress intensity factor to 38.1 MPa·m¹/² through multi-regional microstructure coordination.
Validation· Mechanical bending tests and microstructural analysis were conducted to validate the fracture toughness and bonding enhancement.
