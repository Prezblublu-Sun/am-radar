An Experimental Study on Tensile Characteristics of Ti-6Al-4V Thin Struts Made by Laser Powder-Bed Fusion: Effects of Strut Geometry and Linear Energy Density

Rabiul Islam, Beytullah Aydogan, Kevin Chou Metals 2025-09-11 10.3390/met15091009
单位: Department of Industrial Engineering, Speed School of Engineering, University of Louisville, Louisville, KY 40292, USA
通讯: Rabiul Islam @ Department of Industrial Engineering, Speed School of Engineering, University of Louisville, Louisville, KY 40292, USA
相关性: 该论文直接研究了Ti-6Al-4V LPBF薄杆的拉伸性能与几何及工艺参数的关系，完全符合L1方向对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V力学性能的要求。
边界: No fatigue or cyclic loading data; limited to monotonic tensile properties.
动机· 研究LPBF Ti-6Al-4V薄杆的几何和工艺参数对力学性能的综合影响。
方法· 设计不同数量、尺寸、截面形状和线能量密度的Ti-6Al-4V杆，微CT测孔隙率，拉伸测试力学性能。
结果· 杆数增加降低强度；线能量密度最显著，最佳0.2 J/mm；最小尺寸时尺寸效应明显；低能量下方杆劣于圆杆。
验证· 通过拉伸实验和微CT孔隙率测量进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the combined effects of strut geometry, configuration, and processing conditions on the mechanical properties of thin-strut L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V struts with varying number, size, cross-sectional shape, and linear energy density were fabricated, characterized by micro-CT for porosity, and tested under tension.
Result· Increasing strut number significantly reduces tensile strength; linear energy density is the most influential factor with an optimum near 0.2 J/mm; size effects are minimal except at the smallest size; square struts perform worse at low energy densities.
Validation· Experimental tensile testing and micro-CT porosity measurement.

The creep properties and mechanism of Ti-6Al-4V with ultra-low β contents fabricated by wire arc additive manufacturing

单位: Jiangsu Key Laboratory of 3D Printing Equipment and Manufacturing, School of Electrical and Automation Engineering, Nanjing Normal University, Nanjing, 210023, China
通讯: Zixiang Li @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Tsinghua University, Beijing, 100084, China
相关性: 该论文直接研究增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的蠕变力学性能，与方向要求的力学性能评估高度相关。
边界: The paper focuses on high-temperature creep, not on fatigue or room-temperature tensile properties which are the primary mechanical tests listed in the direction.
动机· 研究超低β含量WAAM Ti-6Al-4V的高温蠕变行为。
方法· 在600°C和700°C进行蠕变实验，并用EBSD和TEM分析微观结构。
结果· WAAM样品的蠕变抗力优于锻造样品，归因于紧密连接的α相和低β含量。
验证· 与商业锻造样品进行对比实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the creep behavior of wire-arc additive manufactured Ti-6Al-4V with ultra-low β content under high-temperature, high-stress conditions.
Method· Creep tests were performed at 600°C and 700°C under various stresses, and microstructural evolution was characterized using EBSD and TEM.
Result· WAAM samples exhibited superior creep resistance compared to forged samples, attributed to the interconnected α-phase network and low β-phase content.
Validation· Experimental creep data of WAAM samples were compared against commercially forged Ti-6Al-4V under identical conditions.

Exploring β Stabilizing Elements of α+β Ti-Al Alloys for Facilitating In-Situ Martensite Decomposition in PBF-LB

Takuma Teramae, Ammarueda Issariyapat, Shota Kariya, Junko Umeda, Katsuyoshi Kondoh Journal of the Japan Society of Powder and Powder Metallurgy 2025-09-11 10.2497/jjspm.25-00020
单位: Graduate School of Engineering, The University of Osaka
通讯: Takuma Teramae @ Graduate School of Engineering, The University of Osaka
相关性: 该论文关注合金成分设计而非后处理工艺，与L2方向无关。
边界: Paper investigates alloy composition (Cr, Mn, Fe) to facilitate in-situ martensite decomposition, not a post-processing treatment applied to AM Ti-6Al-4V; no fatigue or mechanical testing results.
动机· PBF-LB制造的Ti-6Al-4V因马氏体组织导致塑性差，需从合金设计角度改善。
方法· 通过CALPHAD设计不同β稳定元素（Cr、Mn、Fe）的Ti-Al合金，对比Ti-Al-V。
结果· Mn能有效促进β相形成并抑制有害金属间化合物，是最佳β稳定剂。
验证· 通过粉末冶金制备合金，进行超相变热处理和水淬及600°C时效，XRD/SEM分析相组成。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Ti-6Al-4V fabricated by laser powder bed fusion suffers from limited ductility due to martensitic microstructure; this work addresses that via alloy composition design rather than post-processing.
Method· Using CALPHAD, alloys with Cr, Mn, or Fe as beta stabilizers (replacing V) were designed with identical beta transus, then fabricated by powder metallurgy, supertransus heat-treated, water-quenched, and aged at 600°C.
Result· Mn was the most effective beta stabilizer, promoting beta phase formation and suppressing detrimental intermetallic compounds, enabling in-situ α+β microstructure.
Validation· Microstructural characterization by XRD and SEM confirmed phase evolution; no mechanical tests reported.
