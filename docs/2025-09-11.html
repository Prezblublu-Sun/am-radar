An Experimental Study on Tensile Characteristics of Ti-6Al-4V Thin Struts Made by Laser Powder-Bed Fusion: Effects of Strut Geometry and Linear Energy Density

Rabiul Islam, Beytullah Aydogan, Kevin Chou Metals 2025-09-11 10.3390/met15091009
单位: Department of Industrial Engineering, Speed School of Engineering, University of Louisville, Louisville, KY 40292, USA
通讯: Rabiul Islam @ Department of Industrial Engineering, Speed School of Engineering, University of Louisville, Louisville, KY 40292, USA
相关性: 直接针对激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V薄支柱的拉伸力学性能，与L1方向高度契合。
边界: This paper does not cover fatigue performance (S-N, crack initiation/growth) but focuses solely on tensile behavior, which is only part of the L1 scope.
动机· 探究支柱几何和线能量密度对LPBF Ti-6Al-4V薄支柱拉伸性能的联合影响。
方法· 设计不同数目、尺寸、截面形状及线能量密度的支柱，通过微CT和拉伸测试表征。
结果· 支柱数目增加降低强度；尺寸效应微弱；圆形与方形截面性能相近，低LED下方形成劣化；最佳LED约0.2J/mm。
验证· 采用微CT测量孔隙率并配合拉伸试验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the combined influence of strut geometry and linear energy density on the tensile mechanical properties of thin-strut L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V specimens.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V struts with varying number, size, cross-sectional shape, and five levels of linear energy density were fabricated by L-PBF, characterized by micro-CT for porosity, and tested under tension.
Result· Increasing strut number reduces tensile strength; size effects are minimal except at the smallest size; circular and square shapes behave similarly except at the lowest LED; maximum strength occurs near 0.2 J/mm LED.
Validation· Mechanical testing combined with micro-CT porosity analysis.

Influence of layer thickness and post-processes on the quasi-static and fatigue behaviour of Ti-6Al-4V specimens with embedded structural health monitoring system produced by laser powder bed fusion

Michaël Hinderdael, Antonio Cutolo, Nima Razavi, Filippo Berto, Brecht Van Hooreweder Structural Health Monitoring 2025-09-11 10.1177/14759217251360334
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Vrije Universiteit Brussel, Brussel, Belgium
通讯: Michaël Hinderdael @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Vrije Universiteit Brussel, Brussel, Belgium
相关性: 该论文研究了激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V中嵌入结构健康监测系统后，层厚和后处理对准静态及疲劳性能的影响，直接相关于Ti-6Al-4V基础力学与疲劳特性。
边界: The paper primarily focuses on the embedded SHM system and its impact on mechanical properties, rather than a comprehensive standalone characterization of Ti-6Al-4V mechanical or fatigue behavior without such embedded features.
动机· 在LPBF Ti-6Al-4V中嵌入SHM系统，研究层厚和后处理对其力学性能的影响。
方法· 通过准静态拉伸、疲劳测试（试样及部件级），比较不同层厚和后处理条件。
结果· 揭示了层厚和后处理对嵌入通道结构力学性能的影响。
验证· 实验验证，包括准静态和疲劳试验。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the influence of layer thickness and post-processing on the quasi-static and fatigue behavior of Ti-6Al-4V specimens with embedded structural health monitoring capillaries produced by laser powder bed fusion.
Method· Combined quasi-static tensile testing, coupon-level fatigue testing, and component-level fatigue testing with variations in layer thickness and post-process conditions.
Result· The results demonstrate the effects of layer thickness and post-processing on the mechanical performance and fatigue life of the embedded capillary system.
Validation· Experimental validation through mechanical testing of quasi-static and fatigue properties.

Use Of In-Process Monitoring To Evaluate Porosity Generated During The Laser-Powder Bed Fusion Printing Of TI-6AL-4V

Andrea Villano, Fatemeh Golpayegani, Cathal Hoare, Denis P. Dowling 2025-09-11 10.59499/ep256767956
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的LPBF打印过程监测与孔隙率对力学性能的影响，但主要关注过程监控而非疲劳性能。
边界: The paper's primary focus is on in-process monitoring for porosity detection, not on detailed mechanical or fatigue characterization, and fatigue performance is not addressed.
动机· 利用过程监测识别LPBF打印Ti-6Al-4V中孔隙缺陷的形成

验证· 通过力学测试和微观结构表征验证监测数据的有效性
English summary & terms
Motivation· To determine if in-process monitoring can detect porosity defects as they form during LPBF of Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· An optical emission sensor monitored meltpool radiation during printing of Ti-6Al-4V spinal cages, and the data were correlated with porosity, microstructure, and mechanical strength.
Result· The in-process monitoring system successfully identified porosity and microstructural variations at ridges and edges compared to bulk regions.
Validation· Experiments included morphological, crystallographic, and mechanical testing to validate the correlations.

Optimizing Post-Processing Of Additive Metal Alloys For Enhanced Mechanical And Corrosion Performance

REM Surface Engineering, Agustín Díaz, REM Surface Engineering, J. C. Boykin, REM Surface Engineering et al. 2025-09-11 10.59499/ep256764598
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的后处理（热处理和表面抛光）对力学性能的影响，但涵盖多种合金，针对性不强。
边界: The paper covers multiple alloys (IN-625, AlF357, etc.) and does not provide a deep, systematic study focused solely on Ti-6Al-4V post-processing.
动机· 解决增材制造金属合金表面及近表面缺陷导致的力学和腐蚀性能不足问题。
方法· 对多种增材制造合金（含Ti-6Al-4V）进行热处理和化学/化学机械抛光。
结果· 后处理显著降低孔隙率、消除表面缺陷，并提升拉伸强度、疲劳性能和耐腐蚀性。
验证· 通过力学测试（拉伸、疲劳）和腐蚀实验验证后处理效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To address surface and near-surface defects in additively manufactured metal alloys that degrade mechanical strength and corrosion resistance.
Method· Heat treatments and chemical/chemical-mechanical polishing were applied to several AM alloys, including Ti-6Al-4V, IN-625, AlF357, and others.
Result· Post-processing effectively reduced porosity, eliminated surface defects, and improved tensile strength, fatigue life, and corrosion resistance across alloys.
Validation· Mechanical testing (tensile, fatigue) and corrosion experiments were conducted to validate the improvements.

单位: School of Aerospace, Transport and Manufacturing, Cranfield University, Bedfordshire, MK43 0AL UK
通讯: Alex Bishop @ School of Aerospace, Transport and Manufacturing, Cranfield University, Bedfordshire, MK43 0AL UK
相关性: 该论文研究了Ti-6Al-4V的大气压等离子体刻蚀后处理工艺，但未涉及力学性能测试，因此与后处理对疲劳性能影响的方向相关性较弱。
边界: No mechanical testing (fatigue, tensile, or microstructure analysis) to evaluate the effect of post-processing on mechanical performance.
动机· 开发用于Ti-6Al-4V表面加工的大气压等离子体刻蚀技术。
方法· 采用SF6作为刻蚀气体，在大气压下对Ti-6Al-4V进行等离子体刻蚀。
结果· 实现了0.5-2 mm³/min的材料去除速率，且表面损伤小。
验证· 通过实验测量材料去除速率并分析表面形貌验证刻蚀效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop an atmospheric pressure plasma etching process for Ti-6Al-4V as an alternative finishing method for complex-shaped components.
Method· SF6 etchant is used in an atmospheric pressure plasma setup to remove material from Ti-6Al-4V surfaces.
Result· Material removal rates of 0.5–2 mm³/min are achieved with minimal surface damage.
Validation· Experimental measurements of material removal rates and surface characterization confirm the effectiveness of the etching process.

Exploring β Stabilizing Elements of α+β Ti-Al Alloys for Facilitating In-Situ Martensite Decomposition in PBF-LB

Takuma Teramae, Ammarueda Issariyapat, Shota Kariya, Junko Umeda, Katsuyoshi Kondoh Journal of the Japan Society of Powder and Powder Metallurgy 2025-09-11 10.2497/jjspm.25-00020
单位: Graduate School of Engineering, The University of Osaka
通讯: Takuma Teramae @ Graduate School of Engineering, The University of Osaka
相关性: 该论文研究合金成分设计而非后处理工艺，对机械性能影响缺乏实验数据。
边界: The paper focuses on alloy composition design (substituting V with Cr, Mn, Fe) rather than evaluating post-processing treatments on mechanical performance of Ti-6Al-4V; no fatigue or tensile results are reported.
动机· 探索β稳定元素促进激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V中原位马氏体分解
方法· 采用CALPHAD设计合金成分，粉末冶金制备，进行超相变热处理和水淬
结果· 锰是最有效的β稳定剂，能促进β相形成并抑制有害金属间化合物
验证· 仅微观结构表征，无力学测试
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate alternative β stabilizing elements (Cr, Mn, Fe) that facilitate in-situ martensite decomposition during PBF-LB of Ti-6Al-4V, addressing the issue of limited ductility.
Method· Alloy compositions with identical β transus temperatures were designed via CALPHAD, fabricated by powder metallurgy, and subjected to supertransus heat treatment and water quenching, followed by isothermal aging.
Result· Manganese was identified as the most effective β stabilizer for promoting α+β microstructure formation while suppressing detrimental intermetallics.
Validation· Microstructural characterization using XRD and SEM only; no mechanical testing performed.
