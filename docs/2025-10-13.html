Effect of hot isostatic pressing on the microstructure and mechanical properties of porous Ti-6Al-4V alloy manufactured by laser powder bed fusion

Markéta Straková, Jiří Kubásek, Jonáš Divín, Jan Pinc, Dalibor Vojtěch Materials Science in Additive Manufacturing 2025-10-13 10.36922/msam025260055
相关性: 该论文直接研究了热等静压对LPBF制造的多孔Ti-6Al-4V合金力学性能的影响，完全符合后处理调控方向。
边界: Does not include fatigue testing; only monotonic tensile, compression, and bending properties are reported.
动机· 探究热等静压对LPBF多孔Ti-6Al-4V微观结构和力学性能的影响。
方法· 通过LPBF制备金刚石和螺旋二十四面体点阵结构并施加HIP处理，进行拉伸、压缩和弯曲测试。
结果· HIP降低了强度和硬度，但显著提高了延展性（从6%到17%）和弯曲性能。
验证· 通过力学测试对比HIP前后试样的性能变化。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study aims to determine how hot isostatic pressing alters the microstructure and mechanical behavior of laser powder bed fused porous Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Diamond and gyroid porous lattice specimens were fabricated by LPBF, subjected to HIP, and characterized by tensile, compression, and bending tests alongside solid reference samples.
Result· HIP reduced porosity, ultimate tensile strength, and hardness but increased ductility from 6% to 17% and enhanced flexural strength of gyroid structures from 280 MPa to 340 MPa.
Validation· Mechanical testing of as-printed and HIP-treated specimens provided direct experimental validation.

Effect of gradient direction on the compressive and energy absorption characteristics of Ti-6Al-4V metamaterials

Uday Kumar Jonnala, Y. Ravi Kumar, Gibson Ian, Chinnapat Panwisawas Materials & Design 2025-10-13 10.1016/j.matdes.2025.114938
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, National Institute of Technology, Warangal, India
通讯: Y. Ravi Kumar @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, National Institute of Technology, Warangal, India  |  Chinnapat Panwisawas @ School of Engineering and Materials Science, Queen Mary University of London, London E1 4NS, United Kingdom
相关性: 该论文研究了Ti-6Al-4V TPMS梯度点阵的压缩性能，为骨科植入物设计提供了材料性能参考，但未直接涉及股骨柄结构设计。
边界: The paper does not model or test a femoral stem implant; it studies generic TPMS lattice cubes without implant geometry or loading scenario.
动机· 探究梯度方向对Ti-6Al-4V TPMS点阵力学性能的影响，以优化骨科植入物设计。
方法· 通过激光粉末床熔融制备一维、二维和三维梯度Gyroid和Diamond点阵，进行准静态压缩实验。
结果· 三维梯度Gyroid在30%相对密度下弹性模量3.36GPa，屈服强度107.93MPa，能量吸收优于一维梯度。
验证· 实验验证：准静态压缩测试对比不同梯度方向的力学响应和失效模式。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of gradient direction on the compressive and energy absorption properties of Ti-6Al-4V TPMS lattices for potential orthopedic implant applications.
Method· One-dimensional, two-dimensional, and three-dimensional graded gyroid and diamond TPMS lattices were fabricated by laser powder-bed fusion and tested under quasi-static compression.
Result· The 3D graded gyroid exhibited the highest elastic modulus (3.36 GPa) and yield strength (107.93 MPa) at 30% relative density, with superior energy absorption compared to 1D and 2D gradients.
Validation· Experimental validation via quasi-static compression testing and comparison of mechanical responses across different gradient directions.

Ti-Al-V/Zn-Al-Cu Composite Materials Prepared by Zinc Melt Infiltration Technology

Veronika Balejová, Alena Michalcová, Martina Bašistová, Petr Lichý, Dalibor Vojtěch Materials 2025-10-13 10.3390/ma18204690
单位: Department of Metals and Corrosion Engineering, University of Chemistry and Technology, Prague, Technická 5, 166 28 Prague, Czech Republic
通讯: Alena Michalcová @ Department of Metals and Corrosion Engineering, University of Chemistry and Technology, Prague, Technická 5, 166 28 Prag
相关性: 该论文涉及SLM Ti-6Al-4V的复合材料制备，但主要关注锌熔渗，而非基体Ti-6Al-4V本身的力学或疲劳性能。
边界: The paper does not characterize the mechanical or fatigue performance of as-built or process-state Ti-6Al-4V; instead it focuses on a zinc-infiltrated composite, and the only mechanical test is compression on the composite, not tensile or fatigue data for Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 通过锌熔渗制备生物相容性良好的TiAlV/ZnAlCu复合材料
方法· 采用真空吸入和离心铸造两种方法将锌合金熔渗入SLM Ti-6Al-4V多孔骨架
结果· 压缩测试表明复合材料力学性能取决于浸渗方法和几何结构
验证· 通过压缩实验对比复合材料和Ti-6Al-4V增强体的力学性能
English summary & terms
Motivation· To fabricate a TiAlV/ZnAlCu composite with improved biocompatibility for potential bioimplant applications.
Method· Molten zinc alloy was infiltrated into SLM Ti-6Al-4V reinforcements of three geometries using vacuum suction and centrifugal casting.
Result· Compression tests show that the mechanical properties of the composite depend on the infiltration method and reinforcement geometry.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated via compression testing and compared with the as-built Ti-6Al-4V reinforcement.

Microstructure and properties of TiNiTa shape memory alloy coating by laser additive manufactured on the surface of Ti6Al4V

Han Yan, Zong Chang Zhao, Ran Cai, Yongguang Wang, Z.Y. Gao et al. Scientific Reports 2025-10-13 10.1038/s41598-025-19536-z
单位: Hunan Automotive Engineering Vocational University, Zhuzhou, 412000, China
通讯: Han Yan @ Hunan Automotive Engineering Vocational University, Zhuzhou, 412000, China
相关性: 该论文主要关注TiNiTa涂层性能，而非Ti-6Al-4V本身的力学或疲劳性能，因此与用户研究方向不直接相关。
边界: This paper focuses on a TiNiTa coating on Ti6Al4V, not on the mechanical or fatigue performance of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V itself.
动机· 通过激光增材制造在Ti6Al4V表面制备TiNiTa形状记忆涂层。
方法· 使用激光增材制造技术合成涂层，并进行微观结构、硬度、耐磨和耐腐蚀测试。
结果· 涂层具有细小枝晶结构，硬度867.4 HV，耐磨性比基体高42倍，耐腐蚀性良好。
验证· 实验测试与数值模拟结合验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To fabricate a TiNiTa shape memory alloy coating on Ti6Al4V substrate via laser additive manufacturing.
Method· The coating was synthesized by laser additive manufacturing and characterized for microstructure, microhardness, wear resistance, and corrosion resistance.
Result· The coating exhibited fine dendritic grains, a microhardness of 867.4 HV, wear resistance 42 times higher than the substrate, and good corrosion resistance.
Validation· Experimental measurements were complemented by numerical simulation of the molten pool.
