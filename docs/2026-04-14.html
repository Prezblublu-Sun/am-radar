Topology Optimization Customized Femoral Hip Implant.

Siddharth Tiwari, N. K. Jain International Journal of Drug Delivery Technology 2026-04-14 10.25258/ijddt.16.11s.78
单位: Research Scholar, Department of Mechanical Engineering, NIT, Raipur, India
通讯: Siddharth Tiwari @ Research Scholar, Department of Mechanical Engineering, NIT, Raipur, India
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V髋关节植入物的拓扑优化，但未涉及任何后处理或热等静压工艺。
边界: This paper focuses on topology optimization of a hip implant using FEA, but does not address Ti-6Al-4V post-processing, HIP, surface treatments, or any experimental validation.
动机· 优化股骨髋关节假体以减少应力遮挡和松动
方法· 使用有限元分析进行拓扑优化，更改头径和锥角
结果· 获得0.405mm变形和246.38 MPa应力，满足性能要求
验证· 仅仿真验证，无实验
English summary & terms
Motivation· To reduce aseptic loosening and stress shielding in femoral hip implants through design optimization.
Method· Finite element analysis-based topology optimization of head size and taper angle.
Result· Achieved deformations of 0.405 mm and stresses of 246.38 MPa within acceptable limits.
Validation· Simulation-based verification only, no experimental validation.

Damage dose dependence of deuterium retention in high-temperature self-ion irradiated tungsten

Mikhail Zibrov, Thomas Schwarz-Selinger, Michael Klimenkov, Ute Jäntsch arXiv preprint 2026-04-14 link
相关性: 该论文涉及核聚变材料中氘的保留，与用户的增材制造力学性能、生物打印、髋关节植入物等研究方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on deuterium retention in ion-irradiated tungsten for nuclear fusion applications, with no connection to additive manufacturing, biomedical implants, or mechanical property prediction.
动机· 研究高温自离子辐照钨中氘保留随损伤剂量的变化。
方法· 使用20 MeV自离子在1350 K辐照钨样品，随后进行D等离子体暴露和核反应分析。
结果· 高剂量下D浓度超过800 K辐照值，达1.7 at.%，未饱和。
验证· 通过透射电镜和热脱附谱验证缺陷和捕获位点。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the damage dose dependence of deuterium retention in tungsten after high-temperature self-ion irradiation.
Method· Recrystallized tungsten samples were irradiated with 20 MeV self-ions at 1350 K, then exposed to deuterium plasma and analyzed using nuclear reaction analysis and thermal desorption spectroscopy.
Result· Deuterium concentration increased with damage dose, reaching 1.7 at.% at 2.3 dpa without saturation, exceeding values from lower temperature irradiations.
Validation· Transmission electron microscopy confirmed void formation, and reaction-diffusion simulations reproduced thermal desorption spectra.

Depth-Resolved Thermal Conductivity of HFCVD Diamond Films via Square-Pulsed Thermometry

Kexin Zhang, Xiaosong Han, Ershuai Yin, Xin Qian, Junjun Wei et al. arXiv preprint 2026-04-14 link
相关性: 该论文研究金刚石薄膜热导率，与生物医学植入物、生物打印或FEA代理模型无关。
边界: This paper does not address any biomedical implant, bioprinting, FEA surrogate, or AM for biomedical context; it focuses on thermal management in electronics.
动机· 高功率电子器件热管理需高导热金刚石薄膜
方法· 采用方脉冲热法结合EBSD/TEM重构导热系数深度分布
结果· 导热系数从界面60 W/mK增至表面200 W/mK，对应晶粒粗化
验证· 通过频率相关热穿透深度测量和微观结构表征验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· High-thermal-conductivity diamond films on SiC are needed for thermal management in high-power electronics.
Method· Square-pulsed thermometry combined with EBSD and TEM was used to reconstruct the depth-dependent thermal conductivity profile.
Result· Thermal conductivity increased from ~60 W/mK near the interface to ~200 W/mK at the surface, correlating with grain coarsening.
Validation· The depth-resolved profile was validated by frequency-dependent thermal penetration measurements and microstructural characterization.
