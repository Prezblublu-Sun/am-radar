Application-driven optimization of Ti-6Al-4V alloy via customized heat treatments

Andrea Trombetta, Cesare Certini, Luca Marco Pasini, Marco Boniardi, Luca Rosaspina et al. Frattura ed Integrità Strutturale 2026-04-14 10.3221/igf-esis.77.06
单位: ATSolution82, via Aldo Galli 7, 22100 Como Italy
通讯: Andrea Trombetta @ ATSolution82, via Aldo Galli 7, 22100 Como Italy
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V合金的热处理，但未涉及增材制造工艺，因此与L2方向（AM Ti-6Al-4V后处理）不直接相关
边界: The paper studies heat treatments of conventional Ti-6Al-4V, not additively manufactured material, and thus does not address post-processing of AM Ti-6Al-4V as required by the direction.
动机· 为改善Ti-6Al-4V合金的力学性能，研究不同热处理方法的影响。
方法· 对Ti-6Al-4V进行退火、固溶时效、β退火和β固溶过时效处理，并进行显微组织和力学性能表征。
结果· STA处理获得最高强度，BA处理改善断裂韧性，BSTOA处理提高高周疲劳极限。
验证· 通过光学和扫描电镜、拉伸、硬度、冲击、断裂力学和疲劳试验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the mechanical performance of Ti-6Al-4V alloy by investigating the effects of various heat treatments.
Method· Four heat treatments (annealed, solution-treated and aged, β-annealed, β-solution-treated and overaged) were applied to Ti-6Al-4V, and microstructural characterization and mechanical testing were conducted.
Result· STA treatment yielded the highest strength and hardness, BA treatment improved fracture toughness, and BSTOA treatment provided the highest high-cycle fatigue limit.
Validation· Optical and scanning electron microscopy, tensile, hardness, impact, fracture mechanics, and fatigue tests were used for validation.
