Tip-of-Stem Pain Is a Rare Complication Following Revision Total Knee Arthroplasty at a Mean 6.9-Year Follow-Up.

Adam Bridger, Faran Chaudhry, Aazad Abbas, David Backstein, Jesse Isaac Wolfstadt The Journal of arthroplasty 2026-03-31 10.1016/j.arth.2026.01.018
单位: Temerty Faculty of Medicine, University of Toronto, Toronto, Ontario, Canada.
通讯: Jesse Isaac Wolfstadt @ Division of Orthopaedics, Department of Surgery, Temerty Faculty of Medicine, University of Toronto, Toronto, Ontario, C [推断]
相关性: 该论文为临床回顾性研究，不涉及增材制造、植入物设计或生物力学建模，与用户研究方向无关。
边界: this paper does not involve implant design, additive manufacturing, biomechanics modeling, or stress shielding analysis; it is purely a clinical outcomes study.
动机· 研究翻修全膝关节置换术后尖端痛的发生率及相关因素
方法· 回顾性分析910例患者，比较有无尖端痛的影像学参数
结果· 尖端痛发生率3%，胫骨尖端距峡部距离短和胫骨柄长≥100mm相关
验证· 临床回顾性研究
English summary & terms
Motivation· Investigate incidence and radiographic associations of tip-of-stem pain after revision TKA.
Method· Retrospective review of 910 patients comparing stem length, isthmus width, and distance to isthmus between pain and no-pain groups.
Result· Tip-of-stem pain occurred in 3.0% and was associated with shorter tibial tip-isthmus distance and longer tibial stems (≥100 mm).
Validation· Clinical retrospective study with statistical analysis using Mann-Whitney U-tests, t-tests, and Chi-square tests.

Iron spin crossover in ferropericlase and its effect on lower-mantle thermal conductivity

Alexander F. Goncharov, Irina Chuvashova, Eric Edmund, JungFu Lin, Zena Younes et al. arXiv preprint 2026-03-31 link
相关性: 与增材制造力学性能研究方向无关，属于地球物理学领域。
边界: This paper studies thermal conductivity of Earth's lower-mantle minerals, with no focus on additive manufacturing, mechanical properties, or structural materials.
动机· 测量下地幔主要矿物铁方镁石的热导率
方法· 利用激光闪光法及X射线自由电子激光加热技术在金刚石压砧中测量
结果· 发现60-100 GPa间热导率因铁自旋转变而显著降低
验证· 与先前的布里奇曼石结果结合构建地幔热导率剖面
English summary & terms
Motivation· Thermal conductivity of Earth's lower mantle controls heat transfer and mantle dynamics, but direct measurements at relevant pressures and temperatures are lacking.
Method· Direct measurements of thermal conductivity in single-crystal ferropericlase were performed using optical laser flash and X-ray free-electron laser heating in diamond-anvil cells up to 2200 K and 130 GPa.
Result· A marked reduction in conductivity between 60 and 100 GPa at ~1700 K is consistent with the iron spin crossover, providing the first conductivity data for ferropericlase at lower-mantle conditions.
Validation· The results were combined with previous data on bridgmanite to construct a lower-mantle thermal conductivity profile, validated against geophysical constraints.

Thermal Conductivity and Temperature-Induced Band Gap Renormalization in Crystalline and Amorphous Ga$_2$O$_3$

Rustam Arabov, Jiaxuan Li, Xiaotong Chen, Nikita Rybin, Alexander Shapeev arXiv preprint 2026-03-31 link
相关性: 与增材制造及力学性能方向完全无关。
边界: This paper does not address any aspect of additive manufacturing, mechanical properties, or biomedical implants; it is a pure semiconductor physics study.
动机· 研究Ga2O3的导热和电子-声子相互作用。
方法· 利用机器学习势（MTP）耦合第一性原理计算。
结果· 非晶Ga2O3的晶格热导率约为β-Ga2O3的十分之一。
验证· 理论计算，无实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate lattice thermal conductivity and electron-phonon interactions in crystalline and amorphous Ga2O3.
Method· A machine-learned moment tensor potential coupled with first-principles calculations was employed.
Result· Amorphous Ga2O3 exhibits a lattice thermal conductivity approximately one order of magnitude lower than that of crystalline β-Ga2O3.
Validation· The study is purely computational with no experimental validation.
