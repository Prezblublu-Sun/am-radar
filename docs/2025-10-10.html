Optimization of laser powder bed fusion process to enhance mechanical properties of as-built Ti–6Al–4V samples

Junghoon Lee, Arif Hussain, Rae Eon Kim, Xingyi Li, Young-Sam Kwon et al. Journal of Materials Research and Technology 2025-10-10 10.1016/j.jmrt.2025.10.018
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Pohang University of Science and Technology, (POSTECH). Pohang 37673, Republic of Korea
通讯: Dongsik Kim @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Pohang University of Science and Technology, (POSTECH). Pohang 37673, Republic of
相关性: 该论文直接针对激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V的力学性能优化，符合L1方向核心要求。
边界: 论文未涉及疲劳性能，仅关注拉伸性能，但仍在L1方向范围内。
动机· 通过优化激光粉末床熔融工艺参数，实现无需后处理的Ti-6Al-4V试样满足ASTM B348标准。
方法· 系统分析体能量密度和扫描间距对相对密度、表面粗糙度、微观组织和力学性能的影响，确定最优参数组合。
结果· 优化参数下获得相对密度99.9%，抗拉强度1214 MPa，延伸率11.7%的优异综合力学性能。
验证· 通过实验测试验证了力学性能满足ASTM B348标准。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To achieve as-built Ti-6Al-4V samples with tensile strength and elongation meeting ASTM B348 standards through LPBF process optimization.
Method· Systematic analysis of the effects of volumetric energy density and hatch distance on relative density, surface roughness, microstructure, and mechanical properties to identify optimal parameters.
Result· Optimal parameters (67 J/mm³, 60 μm) yielded 99.9% relative density, 1214 MPa UTS, and 11.7% elongation.
Validation· Mechanical testing confirmed that the as-built properties satisfy ASTM B348 requirements.

Study on the microstructure and properties of Ti-6Al-4V + Cu functionally graded alloy by laser directed energy deposition

Chunlun Chen, Zhenlin Zhang, Luyun Li, Yongsheng Zhao, Yan Liu et al. Journal of Alloys and Compounds 2025-10-10 10.1016/j.jallcom.2025.184332
单位: School of Materials Science and Engineering, Southwest Jiaotong University, Key Laboratory of Advanced Materials Technology Education, Ministry of Education, Chengdu 610031, China
通讯: Zhenlin Zhang @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, Southwest Jiaotong University, Key Laboratory of Advanced Materials Technol  |  Hui Chen @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, Southwest Jiaotong University, Key Laboratory of Advanced Materials Technol
相关性: 该论文研究了Ti-6Al-4V中添加铜的功能梯度合金，而非纯Ti-6Al-4V，不符合L1方向对材料的具体要求。
边界: The material is not pure Ti-6Al-4V; it includes copper additions, making it a functionally graded alloy outside the scope of Ti-6Al-4V base mechanical/fatigue evaluation.
动机· 为提高Ti-6Al-4V的抗菌性和耐磨性，通过添加铜制备功能梯度合金。
方法· 采用激光定向能量沉积（LDED）制备不同铜含量的Ti-6Al-4V+Cu梯度合金。
结果· 铜的添加改变了微观结构，提高了硬度和抗菌性，但降低了延展性。
验证· 通过显微组织观察、硬度测试和抗菌实验验证性能变化。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the antibacterial and wear resistance of Ti-6Al-4V, copper was added to fabricate a functionally graded alloy.
Method· A Ti-6Al-4V + Cu functionally graded alloy was produced using laser directed energy deposition with varying copper content.
Result· Copper addition altered the microstructure, enhanced hardness and antibacterial properties, but reduced ductility.
Validation· Microstructural observation, hardness testing, and antibacterial assays were used to validate property changes.

Design Optimization of Femoral Hip Implants with Circular Perforations

José L. Serna-Landivar, Daniela Milagros Antícona Valderrama, William C. Algoner, Andrés Eduardo De la Cruz Herrera, Marleni Mendoza Damas et al. International Journal of Online and Biomedical Engineering (iJOE) 2025-10-10 10.3991/ijoe.v21i12.57489
单位: Universidad Tecnológica del Perú, Lima, Peru
通讯: José L. Serna-Landivar @ Universidad Tecnológica del Perú, Lima, Peru
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V材料但聚焦于髋关节假体设计优化，未涉及增材制造工艺或Ti-6Al-4V的力学性能表征。
边界: The paper does not address additive manufacturing of Ti-6Al-4V; it focuses on implant design with conventional manufacturing, lacking mechanical characterization of AM Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 设计轻量化髋关节假体而不牺牲强度。
方法· 使用有限元分析评估带圆形穿孔的股骨柄设计。
结果· 钛合金V2构型实现最大减重19.7%且安全裕度可接受。
验证· 仅通过仿真模拟，无实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To design lightweight femoral hip implants without compromising strength.
Method· Finite element simulations in ANSYS Workbench evaluated circular perforations in femoral stems under physiological loading.
Result· The Ti-6Al-4V V2 configuration achieved a 19.7% mass reduction while maintaining acceptable safety margins.
Validation· The study relied solely on simulation case studies without experimental validation.
