A novel small punch creep test to determine norton creep properties of LPBF Ti64 alloy

Furui Shi, Mathieu Lalé, Feng Yu, Martin Abendroth, Bernard Viguier et al. Frontiers in Materials 2025-12-09 10.3389/fmats.2025.1689246
单位: College of Mechanical and Automotive Engineering, Ningbo University of Technology, Ningbo, China
通讯: Feng Yu @ College of Mechanical and Automotive Engineering, Ningbo University of Technology, Ningbo, China
相关性: 该论文针对LPBF Ti64合金的蠕变性能测试方法，虽然不直接涉及疲劳，但属于力学性能表征，与研究方向相关。
边界: The paper focuses on creep properties, which are not explicitly listed in the direction's focus on tensile, fatigue, and hardness; it does not provide direct fatigue or tensile data.
动机· 开发适用于LPBF Ti64合金的小冲杆蠕变测试方法以获取Norton蠕变参数。
方法· 结合代表应力-应变法和逆有限元分析，从小冲杆测试和蠕变测试中提取参数。
结果· 实验验证表明该方法与理论一致，准确性高。
验证· 通过实验SPCT和UCT对比验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a novel small punch creep test method for LPBF Ti64 alloy to determine Norton creep parameters efficiently.
Method· A combined representative stress-strain method and inverse finite element analysis is used to extract creep parameters from small punch test data.
Result· The method shows strong agreement with uniaxial creep test results, confirming its accuracy.
Validation· Experimental validation through small punch creep tests and comparison with uniaxial creep tests.

Mechanical Properties and Fracture Behavior of Hot Isostatically Pressed TiC/TC4 Composites

Zhiyu Sun, Jinyi Duan, Xiang Wu, Xiaofei Mo, Hai Nan et al. Materials 2025-12-09 10.3390/ma18245529
单位: Cast Titanium Alloy R&D Center, Beijing Institute of Aeronautical Materials, Beijing 100095, China
通讯: Yuankui Cao @ State Key Laboratory of Powder Metallurgy, Central South University, Changsha 410083, China
相关性: 论文涉及热等静压处理TC4基复合材料，但未涉及增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的后处理对疲劳性能的影响。
边界: The paper studies HIP-fabricated TiC/TC4 composites, not post-processing of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V, and does not report fatigue data.
动机· 克服传统铸造钛基复合材料成分偏析和强化相粗大的问题
方法· 通过热等静压制备TiC/TC4复合材料并进行力学测试
结果· 室温抗拉强度1058 MPa，伸长率17%，600°C抗拉强度约500 MPa
验证· 室温及高温拉伸实验验证力学性能
English summary & terms
Motivation· To overcome composition segregation and coarse reinforcement phases in conventionally cast titanium matrix composites.
Method· TiC/TC4 composites were fabricated via hot isostatic pressing and characterized for tensile and fracture behavior.
Result· The composites achieved room-temperature tensile strength of 1058 MPa, yield strength of 958 MPa, and total elongation of 17%, with high-temperature strength of ~500 MPa at 600°C.
Validation· Mechanical testing including room-temperature and high-temperature tensile tests was performed.

A Dynamic Time Warping-Transfer Learning Approach to Transferring Knowledge in Stress-strain Behaviors from Polymers to Metals: An Affordable and Generalizable Additive Manufacturing Part Qualification Framework

Duan, Chenglong, Wu, Dazhong ArXiv.org 2025-12-09 link
相关性: 该论文主要关注一种通用的迁移学习方法，将聚合物应力应变知识转移到金属材料，Ti6Al4V仅作为其中一个测试案例，不专注于Ti6Al4V的力学或疲劳性能。
边界: Paper does not focus on Ti-6Al-4V mechanical/fatigue characterization; it uses Ti-6Al-4V as one of three test datasets for a general ML framework transferring knowledge from polymers to metals.
动机· 提出一种低成本增材制造部件鉴定方法，通过迁移聚合物知识预测金属应力应变。
方法· 利用动态时间规整选择最优聚合物数据集，训练LSTM模型预测金属应力应变曲线。
结果· 在AlSi10Mg、Ti6Al4V和碳钢上取得良好预测精度（MAPE 12.41%）。
验证· 基于文献中的公开实验数据集进行数值验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Develop a cost-effective part qualification framework for metal additive manufacturing by transferring knowledge from polymer stress-strain behaviors.
Method· Use dynamic time warping to select the optimal polymer dataset and train an LSTM model to predict metal stress-strain curves.
Result· The DTW-TL model achieves high prediction accuracy on AlSi10Mg, Ti6Al4V, and carbon steel datasets with an average MAPE of 12.41%.
Validation· Validated on existing experimental stress-strain datasets from the literature.
