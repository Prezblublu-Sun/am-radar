Ultra-cryogenic and room temperature normalized impact toughness of laser additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V and Ti-5Al-5Mo-5V-3Cr

Lia Pribnow, Mika León Altmann, Thomas Wegener, A. Irretier, Rainer Fechte‐Heinen et al. International Journal of Plasticity 2026-01-31 10.1016/j.ijplas.2026.104629
单位: University of Bremen, Bremen, Germany
通讯: Lia Pribnow @ University of Bremen, Bremen, Germany
相关性: 该论文直接研究了热等静压（HIP）后处理对激光增材制造Ti-6Al-4V冲击韧性的影响，完全符合方向要求。
边界: This paper does not cover fatigue performance, only impact toughness, and it also includes a second alloy (Ti-5553) which is outside the primary focus.
动机· 为评估液氢温度下增材制造钛合金的冲击性能，首次研究超低温冲击韧性。
方法· 采用Charpy冲击试验在20K和293K下测试Ti-6Al-4V和Ti-5553，并考虑热等静压处理。
结果· Ti-6Al-4V在20K下韧性保持不变但断裂机制改变；热等静压Ti-5553在20K下韧性翻倍。
验证· 通过实验Charpy冲击试验验证，使用原位冷却装置。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the ultra-cryogenic impact performance of additively manufactured titanium alloys for liquid hydrogen applications, this study investigates Charpy impact toughness at 20 K for the first time.
Method· Fully instrumented Charpy impact tests were conducted at 20 K and 293 K on Ti-6Al-4V and Ti-5Al-5Mo-5V-3Cr, considering hot isostatic pressing (HIP) and varying process parameters.
Result· Ti-6Al-4V showed similar toughness at both temperatures but different fracture mechanisms, while HIP-treated Ti-5553 exhibited doubled toughness at 20 K compared to 293 K.
Validation· Experimental validation via Charpy impact testing with in-situ cryogenic cooling.

Influence of annealing temperature on microstructure evolution and strength-ductility trade-off in warm-rolled Ti-6Al-4V thin strips

Huifang Lan, Zhipeng Guo, Shuai Tang, Jianping Li Materials Today Communications 2026-01-31 10.1016/j.mtcomm.2026.114747
单位: State Key Laboratory of Rolling and Automation, Northeastern University, Shenyang, Liaoning 110819, P R China
通讯: Huifang Lan @ State Key Laboratory of Rolling and Automation, Northeastern University, Shenyang, Liaoning 110819, P R China
相关性: 该论文研究退火温度对Ti-6Al-4V显微组织和力学性能的影响，但使用温轧材料而非增材制造材料，对增材制造后处理控制具有参考价值。
边界: The paper uses wrought (warm-rolled) Ti-6Al-4V, not additive-manufactured (AM) Ti-6Al-4V, so it does not directly address post-processing of AM material as required by the direction.
动机· 研究退火温度对温轧Ti-6Al-4V薄带显微组织和力学性能的影响。
方法· 对90%压下率温轧Ti-6Al-4V在不同温度退火，表征显微组织并测试拉伸性能。
结果· 700°C退火获得最佳强度-塑性平衡，归因于双峰显微组织。
验证· 通过拉伸测试和微观结构表征验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of annealing temperature on the recrystallization behavior and mechanical properties of warm-rolled Ti-6Al-4V strips.
Method· Annealing at various temperatures after 90% warm rolling, followed by microstructural characterization and tensile testing.
Result· Annealing at 700°C produced an optimal combination of strength and ductility due to a bimodal microstructure.
Validation· Experimental mechanical testing and microstructural analysis.

Regulation Mechanism of Friction Stir Processing Parameters on Microstructure and Properties of Laser Metal Deposited TC4 Titanium Alloy

K Max Zhang, Yan Li, Binghan Li, Weijun Liu, Xingwei Jiang et al. Advanced Engineering Materials 2026-01-31 10.1002/adem.202501524
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering Shenyang University of Technology Shenyang China
通讯: K Max Zhang @ School of Mechanical Engineering Shenyang University of Technology Shenyang China  |  Weijun Liu @ School of Mechanical Engineering Shenyang University of Technology Shenyang China
相关性: 该论文涉及对激光金属沉积TC4钛合金进行摩擦搅拌加工后处理，但方向要求关注增材制造态或工艺态的力学与疲劳性能，而该文未提供疲劳数据或拉伸性能。
边界: This paper focuses on friction stir processing as a post-treatment, not on as-built or process-state mechanical/fatigue properties; no fatigue data, no tensile data, no S-N curve.
动机· 利用摩擦搅拌加工改善激光熔覆沉积TC4钛合金的组织和性能
方法· 系统研究摩擦搅拌加工参数（转速和行进速度）对TC4合金微观结构和力学性能的影响
结果· 摩擦搅拌加工有效细化晶粒，消除孔隙，提高硬度至345.2 HV1，降低磨损率
验证· 通过XRD、硬度、磨损实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the microstructure and mechanical properties of laser metal deposited TC4 titanium alloy via friction stir processing.
Method· Systematic investigation of friction stir processing parameters (rotational speed 250–1050 rpm, travel speed 50–210 mm min−1) on microstructural evolution and mechanical performance.
Result· Friction stir processing effectively refines grains, eliminates porosity, increases microhardness from 305.2 to 345.2 HV1, and improves wear resistance.
Validation· Experimental validation through XRD, microhardness measurements, and wear testing.

Abrasive Water Jet Machining (AWJM) of Titanium Alloy—A Review

Aravinthan Arumugam, Alokesh Pramanik, Amit Rai Dixit, Animesh Kumar Basak Designs 2026-01-31 10.3390/designs10010013
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Engineering Institute of Technology, Perth, WA 6005, Australia
通讯: Aravinthan Arumugam @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Engineering Institute of Technology, Perth, WA 6005, Australia
相关性: 该综述涉及钛合金的磨料水射流加工，但未专注于增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的疲劳或机械性能改性，相关性有限。
边界: This paper is a general review of AWJM of titanium alloys and does not focus on additive-manufactured Ti-6Al-4V, nor does it provide experimental or modeling data on fatigue or mechanical performance after post-processing.
动机· 综述磨料水射流加工钛合金的参数与效果。
方法· 收集并分析现有文献，总结工艺参数对加工响应的影响。
结果· 指出喷射压力和磨料流量是影响材料去除率和表面质量的关键因素。
验证· 基于已发表研究的归纳，无新实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This review aims to consolidate knowledge on abrasive water jet machining parameters and their effects on titanium alloy processing, particularly Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· A systematic review of existing literature on AWJM of titanium alloys, focusing on parametric influences and optimization.
Result· Jet pressure and abrasive flow rate are identified as the most influential parameters for material removal rate and surface quality.
Validation· The paper is a review without original experimental or modeling validation.

XFEM Prediction of Fatigue Crack Propagation in Ti-6Al-4V Compressor Blades under ThermoMechanical Loads

Ananya Singh, Alexander H. Miller, S. O'connor American Journal of Data Science and Analysis 2026-01-31 10.71465/ajdsa3600
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, The University of Melbourne, Parkville VIC 3010, Australia
通讯: Ananya Singh @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, The University of Melbourne, Parkville VIC 3010, Australia
相关性: 该论文研究非增材制造的Ti-6Al-4V压气机叶片疲劳裂纹扩展，与增材制造方向不相关。
边界: The paper does not involve additive manufacturing; it simulates fatigue crack propagation in conventionally manufactured compressor blades, thus it does not address the specified direction on additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 预测热机械载荷下压气机叶片疲劳裂纹扩展行为
方法· 采用扩展有限元法(XFEM)结合修正Paris定律模拟裂纹扩展
结果· 热梯度显著影响应力强度因子，加速特定区域裂纹扩展
验证· 数值仿真验证，无实验数据
English summary & terms
Motivation· To predict fatigue crack propagation in Ti-6Al-4V compressor blades under complex thermomechanical loading.
Method· The extended finite element method (XFEM) with a modified Paris law was used to simulate arbitrary crack paths.
Result· Thermal gradients alter stress intensity factors, leading to accelerated crack growth in critical blade regions.
Validation· The study is purely numerical with no experimental validation.
