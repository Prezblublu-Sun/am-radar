Using marker bands in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V to measure small fatigue crack growth rates and equivalent initial damage sizes

Christopher Bodger, Ben Main, Yi Rye Choi, Isaac Field, Simon Barter International Journal of Fatigue 2025-08-06 10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2025.109224
单位: Capability Acquisition and Sustainment Group, Brindabella Park, Canberra, Australia
通讯: Simon Barter @ RMIT University, Melbourne, Victoria, Australia
相关性: 该论文直接针对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的疲劳裂纹扩展和初始损伤尺寸测量，符合层1基础力学与疲劳关注点。
边界: The paper focuses on marker band technique for crack growth measurement rather than providing conventional S-N curves or tensile properties.
动机· 验证标志带技术在增材制造Ti-6Al-4V中用于测量小裂纹扩展速率和等效初始损伤尺寸的可行性。
方法· 设计了适用于AM Ti-6Al-4V的标志带，通过变幅加载产生可识别的疲劳条纹。
结果· 成功在AM Ti-6Al-4V中实现标志带，可提取裂纹扩展数据和等效初始损伤尺寸分布。
验证· 通过实验疲劳测试验证了标志带技术对裂纹扩展速率测量的有效性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To demonstrate the viability of using marker bands to measure small fatigue crack growth rates and equivalent initial damage sizes in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V for certification and sustainment.
Method· Marker bands were introduced by applying periodic high-to-low load sequences during fatigue testing of AM Ti-6Al-4V specimens, creating distinguishable bands on fracture surfaces.
Result· The marker band technique successfully provided crack growth rate data and equivalent initial damage size distributions for AM Ti-6Al-4V.
Validation· The marker band method was validated through comparison with conventional crack growth measurement techniques on AM Ti-6Al-4V specimens.

Fatigue performance improvement using laser shock peening in high strength ductile metallic materials

Sarah George, R.B. Tait, A.A. Becker, Andrea Carlisle Journal of the Southern African Institute of Mining and Metallurgy 2025-08-06 10.17159/2411-9717/1619/2025
单位: University of Cape Town, South Africa
通讯: Sarah George @ University of Cape Town, South Africa
相关性: 该论文研究了激光冲击强化对Ti-6Al-4V疲劳性能的提升，虽然材料并非增材制造，但后处理方法和结果对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的后处理控制具有参考价值。
边界: Does not specify use of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; materials are likely wrought, not AM, and the paper includes multiple alloys without exclusive focus on Ti-6Al-4V post-processing.
动机· 通过激光冲击强化提升金属材料的疲劳寿命。
方法· 对AA7075-T6、Ti-6Al-4V等材料进行激光冲击处理并开展疲劳测试与断口分析。
结果· Ti-6Al-4V的疲劳寿命提升超过5倍。
验证· 疲劳试验与断口形貌观察验证了强化效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve fatigue life of high-strength ductile metallic materials through laser shock peening (LSP).
Method· Fatigue testing and fractography were conducted on LSP-treated AA7075-T6, Ti-6Al-4V, and AISI316 stainless steel specimens.
Result· LSP resulted in over a five-fold increase in fatigue life for Ti-6Al-4V, with material-dependent efficacy.
Validation· Experimental fatigue tests and fractographic analysis confirmed the performance improvement.

通讯: Andreas Greuling @ Department of Prosthetic Dentistry and Biomedical Materials Science, Hannover Medical School, Hannover, Germany

Validation· In vitro osteoblast adhesion, morphology, and membrane integrity assays were performed.

Shape characterisation for mounted additive manufacturing powders

Courtney Gallagher, Sagar Nikam, Emmett Kerr, Shaun McFadden Results in Engineering 2025-08-06 10.1016/j.rineng.2025.106605
单位: School of Computing, Engineering and Intelligent Systems, Ulster University, Derry/Londonderry, BT48 7JL, UK
通讯: Shaun McFadden @ School of Computing, Engineering and Intelligent Systems, Ulster University, Derry/Londonderry, BT48 7JL, UK
相关性: 该论文研究的是Ti-6Al-4V粉末形貌表征，但未涉及增材制造后的力学或疲劳性能，与本方向直接需求不匹配。
边界: No mechanical or fatigue characterization of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; focuses solely on powder morphology before the AM process.
动机· 发展一种易用的粉末形貌表征方法，以辅助增材制造粉末质量控制。
方法· 使用标准金相设备和自动图像分析算法从2D截面图像测量粉末的圆形度、凸度和坚固度。
结果· 与3D基准比较，2D方法误差较小（圆形度<7.4%，凸度<2.2%，坚固度<3.9%），超声处理粉末形状最佳。
验证· 通过商业3D分析系统进行基准对比验证2D方法的准确性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study aims to develop an accessible methodology for routine powder shape characterization to support quality control in additive manufacturing.
Method· Standard metallographic equipment and automated image analysis were used to measure particle circularity, convexity, and solidity from 2D cross-sectional images of mounted powders.
Result· The 2D method showed reasonable accuracy (<7.4% error for circularity) compared to commercial 3D analysis, with ultrasonically processed powder exhibiting the highest shape descriptors.
Validation· Validation was performed by comparing 2D measurements to benchmark 3D data from a commercial particle analyzer.

Paint Removal Performance and Sub-Surface Microstructural Evolution of Ti6Al4V Alloy Using Different Process Parameters of Continuous Laser Cleaning

Haoye Zeng, Biwen Li, Liangbin Hu, Yun Zhang, Ruiqing Li et al. Coatings 2025-08-06 10.3390/coatings15080916
单位: College of Mechanical Engineering, University of South China, Hengyang 421001, China
通讯: Chaochao Zhou @ School of Nuclear Science and Technology, University of South China, Hengyang 421001, China  |  Pinghu Chen @ College of Mechanical Engineering, University of South China, Hengyang 421001, China
相关性: 该论文研究激光清洗工艺而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学与疲劳性能。
边界: The paper focuses on laser cleaning of paint from Ti6Al4V, not on mechanical or fatigue characterization of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 研究连续激光清洗去除Ti6Al4V合金表面涂层的工艺参数影响。
方法· 使用高斯连续激光在不同功率和扫描速度下清洗120μm涂层，分析去除率、表面粗糙度、微观结构和硬度。
结果· 最佳参数为100W和550mm/min，表面粗糙度接近原始基材，显微硬度仅增加3.41%。
验证· 通过EBSD和硬度测试验证基材损伤程度。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of continuous-wave laser cleaning parameters on removing paint coatings from Ti6Al4V alloy without damaging the substrate.
Method· A Gaussian continuous-wave laser was applied at varying power (100-200 W) and scanning speed (520-610 mm/min) to remove a 120 μm paint layer, with characterization of removal rate, surface roughness, microstructure, and hardness.
Result· Optimal cleaning was achieved at 100 W and 550 mm/min, yielding surface roughness similar to the original substrate (55 nm vs. 56 nm) and only a 3.41% increase in microhardness (347 HV).
Validation· Experimental validation via EBSD and microhardness testing confirmed minimal substrate damage under the optimized parameters.

Comparative tool wear study on drilling of CFRP/Ti stacks using TiAlN and AlTiSiN coated drill bits

S. Samsudeensadham, V. Krishnaraj Engineering Research Express 2025-08-06 10.1088/2631-8695/adf8b3
单位: PSG College of Technology, Coimbatore, India
通讯: S. Samsudeensadham @ PSG College of Technology, Coimbatore, India  |  V. Krishnaraj @ PSG College of Technology, Coimbatore, India
相关性: 该论文研究CFRP/Ti叠层钻孔刀具磨损，不涉及Ti-6Al-4V后处理对疲劳或力学性能的影响。
边界: The paper focuses on drilling tool wear and hole quality, not on any post-processing treatment (e.g., heat treatment, HIP, surface modification) that modifies the fatigue or mechanical performance of Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 比较TiAlN与AlTiSiN涂层钻头在CFRP/Ti叠层钻孔中的刀具磨损性能。
方法· 在不同切削速度和进给率下进行干式钻孔实验，测量推力、毛刺高度和刀具磨损。
结果· TiAlN涂层钻头耐磨性更好，AlTiSiN涂层钻头毛刺更小、表面质量更优。
验证· 通过100孔钻孔实验和在线测力系统验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study evaluates the drilling performance of TiAlN and AlTiSiN-coated solid carbide tools in CFRP/Ti-6Al-4V stacks under dry conditions.
Method· Drilling experiments were conducted at cutting speeds of 15–30 m/min and feed rates of 0.025–0.1 mm/rev, measuring thrust force, burr height, and flank wear.
Result· TiAlN-coated drills showed superior wear resistance, while AlTiSiN-coated drills produced lower burr formation and better surface finish.
Validation· Validation was performed through a 100-hole drilling test and online dynamometer measurements.
