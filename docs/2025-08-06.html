Data Driven Insights into Composition Property Relationships in FCC High Entropy Alloys

Nicolas Flores, Daniel Salas Mula, Wenle Xu, Sahu Bibhu, Daniel Lewis et al. arXiv preprint 2025-08-06 link
相关性: 该论文不涉及增材制造工艺或生物医学领域，而是关于高熵合金成分与力学性能的机器学习建模。
边界: No additive manufacturing process or biomedical implant context; focuses on composition-property modeling for structural HEAs without AM-specific mechanical characterization.
动机· 利用机器学习从有限数据中揭示高熵合金成分与力学性能的关系
方法· 采用基于编码器-解码器的模型结合贝叶斯多目标超参数优化
结果· 模型在屈服强度和UTS/YS比等六个性能上达到或优于传统回归方法
验证· 基于BIRDSHOT系统的纳米压痕数据集进行数值验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To uncover composition-property relationships in high entropy alloys despite limited and heterogeneous data using advanced machine learning methods.
Method· Encoder-decoder based chemistry-property models with Bayesian multi-objective hyperparameter optimization are evaluated for mapping alloy composition to six mechanical properties.
Result· The models achieve competitive or superior performance compared to conventional regressors, particularly for yield strength and UTS/YS ratio.
Validation· Models are validated on the BIRDSHOT nanoindentation dataset by comparing predictions to measured mechanical properties.

Intrinsic Layer-Dependent Surface Energy and Exfoliation Energy of van der Waals Materials

Lin-Lin Wang, Jiaqiang Yan, Yong Han, Claire C. Wang, Jian-Xiang Qiu et al. arXiv preprint 2025-08-06 10.1021/acs.jpclett.5c01632
相关性: 与增材制造力学性能方向无关，涉及二维材料表面能，不涉及增材制造或植入物力学。
边界: This paper focuses on 2D van der Waals materials and their exfoliation energies, with no connection to additive manufacturing, mechanical properties of implants, or biomedical contexts.
动机· 计算二维范德华材料的层依赖表面能和剥离能
方法· 使用密度泛函理论，考虑范德华相互作用
结果· 单层表面能最小，且减少幅度随层内原子层数增加而减小
验证· 理论计算，无实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To calculate the intrinsic layer-dependent surface and exfoliation energies of van der Waals materials for understanding exfoliation effectiveness.
Method· Density functional theory with exchange-correlation functionals that account for explicit van der Waals interactions.
Result· Single van der Waals layers have the smallest surface energy, and the reduction diminishes with increasing atomic layers within the layer.
Validation· Theoretical calculations only, no experimental validation.

Tailored Thermal and Mechanical Performance of Biodegradable PLA-P(VDF-TrFE) Polymer Blends

G Suresh, B. Satyanarayana, C. Thirmal, Kaushal Jagarlamudi, T Komala et al. arXiv preprint 2025-08-06 10.1002/app.70307
相关性: 该论文研究PLA-P(VDF-TrFE)聚合物共混薄膜的热机械性能，与用户的AM金属植入物力学性能方向无关。
边界: This paper does not involve LPBF/AM for metallic biomedical implants, does not address hip implant stress shielding or osseointegration, and does not include any FEA or surrogate modeling of mechanical behavior.
动机· 开发具有可调热机械性能的聚合物共混材料。
方法· 通过改变P(VDF-TrFE):PLA比例制备薄膜，并表征其结构、热和力学性能。
结果· 25:75共混比因PLA结晶增强表现出最优机械强度，50:50共混比平衡了模量和电活性相。
验证· 通过DSC、FTIR和拉伸测试验证结构与性能关系。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To design biodegradable polymer blends with tailored thermal and mechanical performance for functional applications including sensors and 3D printing.
Method· Free-standing blend films of P(VDF-TrFE) and PLA were fabricated at varying ratios and characterized by thermal analysis, FTIR, and tensile testing to study structure-property relationships.
Result· The 25:75 blend exhibited superior mechanical strength due to enhanced PLA crystallinity and chain alignment, while the 50:50 blend balanced tensile modulus and electroactive phase content.
Validation· Mechanical testing, DSC, and FTIR were used to correlate composition with crystallinity, beta-phase fraction, and mechanical performance.
