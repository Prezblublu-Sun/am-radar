Subsurface polycrystalline microstructure reconstruction via immersion ultrasound full waveform inversion

Yongwei Xie, Shuguang Fan, Jiaze He, Yanju Liu, Jinsong Leng npj Computational Materials 2026-05-29 10.1038/s41524-026-02150-6
单位: Department of Astronautical Science and Mechanics, Harbin Institute of Technology, Harbin, PR China
通讯: Jiaze He @ Department of Astronautical Science and Mechanics, Harbin Institute of Technology, Harbin, PR China  |  Yanju Liu @ Department of Astronautical Science and Mechanics, Harbin Institute of Technology, Harbin, PR China
相关性: 该论文主要关注超声成像方法，而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学或疲劳性能。
边界: No mechanical testing or fatigue characterization; the paper is about a microstructure imaging technique and does not address mechanical properties or additive manufacturing.
动机· 实现多晶合金亚表面微结构的非破坏性重建以预测宏观力学性能。
方法· 提出了浸没式超声全波形反演框架，结合谱元波场模拟和伴随反演。
结果· 成功重建了Ti-6Al-4V的晶粒取向和各向异性弹性系数分布。
验证· 基于Ti-6Al-4V模型数值基准测试验证了方法的准确性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To achieve nondestructive subsurface microstructural mapping of polycrystalline alloys for predicting macroscopic mechanical performance.
Method· A water-immersion ultrasound full waveform inversion framework integrating spectral-element wavefield simulations with adjoint-based inversion is introduced.
Result· The method accurately reconstructs grain orientations and anisotropic elastic coefficient distributions in Ti-6Al-4V models.
Validation· Validation is performed through numerical benchmark tests on synthetic Ti-6Al-4V polycrystalline models.

Effect of Build Orientation on Laser Welding Characteristics of SLM-Fabricated Ti-6Al-4V Components

Behnam Kondori, Amir Mostafapour, Habib Hamed Zargari Journal of Materials Engineering and Performance 2026-05-29 10.1007/s11665-026-14230-1
单位: Mechanical Engineering Faculty, University of Tabriz, Tabriz, Iran
通讯: Habib Hamed Zargari @ Advanced Materials Research Institute, Sahand University of Technology, Tabriz, Iran
相关性: 该论文研究SLM制造Ti-6Al-4V的构建取向对激光焊接特性的影响，未涉及力学或疲劳性能，与用户关注的基体力学及疲劳方向关联较弱。
边界: The paper focuses on laser welding characteristics of SLM-fabricated Ti-6Al-4V rather than mechanical or fatigue performance under as-built or process-state conditions.
动机· 探究SLM Ti-6Al-4V构建取向对激光焊接性能的影响。
方法· 对不同构建取向的SLM Ti-6Al-4V试样进行激光焊接实验并分析接头特性。
结果· 构建取向影响焊接熔池形态及接头力学性能。
验证· 通过显微组织和力学测试验证焊接质量差异。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how the build orientation of SLM-produced Ti-6Al-4V affects subsequent laser welding behavior and joint properties.
Method· Laser welding experiments were performed on SLM Ti-6Al-4V samples fabricated with different build orientations, and the resulting weld characteristics were analyzed.
Result· Build orientation influenced weld pool geometry, microstructure, and mechanical performance of the joints.
Validation· Microstructural examination and mechanical testing (e.g., tensile or hardness) were used to evaluate the weld quality and property variations.

Formation of composite coatings on VT6 titanium alloy

І. М. Pohrelyuk, S.M. Lavrys, O. V. Tkachuk, Kh. Zadorozhna, V. S. Trush et al. Physicochemical Mechanics of Materials 2026-05-29 10.15407/pcmm2026.02.062
单位: Karpenko Physico-Mechanical Institute of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine, Lviv, Ukraine
通讯: І. М. Pohrelyuk @ Karpenko Physico-Mechanical Institute of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine, Lviv, Ukraine
相关性: 该论文涉及VT6钛合金的复合涂层与渗氮处理，但研究对象为非增材制造材料，且仅关注耐磨性而非疲劳或拉伸性能。
边界: This paper does not address fatigue or tensile properties of AM Ti-6Al-4V, nor does it involve additive-manufactured material; it focuses on wear resistance of conventionally processed VT6 with composite coatings.
动机· 提高VT6钛合金的耐磨性。
方法· 真空电弧喷涂铝或铝钛层后气体渗氮形成复合涂层。
结果· 复合涂层降低了摩擦系数和磨损轨迹截面积。
验证· 通过无润滑陶瓷球磨损试验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the wear resistance of VT6 titanium alloy through surface modification.
Method· Composite coatings were formed by vacuum arc spraying of aluminum or aluminum and titanium layers followed by gas nitriding.
Result· The composite coatings exhibited reduced friction coefficient and smaller wear track cross-sectional area compared to untreated and nitrided-only samples.
Validation· Wear tests were conducted under dry sliding conditions against a ceramic ball at loads of 10, 15, and 20 N.

Integrated computational modeling of electromechanical coupling corrosion behavior in titanium alloys

Chao Yang, Qianli Liu, Yang Lin, Yongyong Suo, Qingwei Jiang et al. npj Materials Degradation 2026-05-29 10.1038/s41529-026-00803-x
单位: Faculty of Materials Science and Engineering, Kunming University of Science and Technology, Kunming, PR China
通讯: Qianli Liu @ National Engineering Research Centre for Advanced Titanium and Titanium Alloy Material Technology, Luoyang Ship Material  |  Xiaoyu Chong @ Faculty of Materials Science and Engineering, Kunming University of Science and Technology, Kunming, PR China
相关性: 本文主要研究传统钛合金（包括TC4）在拉伸载荷下的电化学腐蚀行为，而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学或疲劳性能。
边界: Not focused on additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; no AM context; primarily corrosion modeling for conventional alloys, not mechanical/fatigue characterization.
动机· 研究钛合金在拉伸载荷下的电化学腐蚀行为，降低实验成本。
方法· 建立多物理场耦合模型，模拟腐蚀缺陷处的应力与电化学响应。
结果· 应力集中导致局部阳极溶解加速，腐蚀进入自加速循环。
验证· 通过Ford-Anderson模型预测裂纹扩展并与实验结果对比验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the electrochemical corrosion behavior of titanium alloys under tensile loading and reduce experimental costs.
Method· A multi-physics field coupling model was developed to simulate the stress and electrochemical response at corrosion defects.
Result· Stress concentration triggers accelerated local anodic dissolution, leading to a self-accelerating corrosion cycle.
Validation· Crack propagation kinetics predicted by the Ford-Anderson model were validated against experimental results.

Fatigue Failure Mechanism and Crack Growth Behavior of Ti-6Al-4V ELI Titanium Alloy Welded Joints

Jiajun Liu, Yu Li, Shao‐Shi Rui, Wei Chen, Chengqi Sun Materials 2026-05-29 10.3390/ma19112301
单位: School of Engineering Science, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100049, China
通讯: Jiajun Liu @ School of Engineering Science, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100049, China
相关性: 该论文研究的是焊接Ti-6Al-4V接头的疲劳行为，而非增材制造材料，因此与L1方向无关。
边界: Paper focuses on welded joints, not additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V, thus out of scope for the L1 direction.
动机· 研究深海耐压壳中钛合金焊接接头的疲劳失效机制。
方法· 开展轴向疲劳、I型和I-II混合模式疲劳裂纹扩展试验。
结果· 焊接接头疲劳性能低于母材，但近门槛区抗性更高；气孔是主要裂纹源。
验证· 通过扫描电镜观察和试验数据验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate fatigue failure mechanisms of Ti-6Al-4V ELI welded joints used in deep-sea pressure hulls.
Method· Axial fatigue, mode I and mixed-mode I-II fatigue crack growth tests were performed on welded joints and compared with base material.
Result· Welded joints showed lower fatigue performance but higher near-threshold resistance; gas pores were primary crack initiation sites.
Validation· Experimental validation via fatigue testing and SEM fractography.
