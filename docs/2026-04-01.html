Tensile and fatigue properties of additively manufactured titanium alloy: Effects of heat treatment and surface processing

Jinliang Zhang, Bo Song, Qian Yan, Chaoqun Wu, Yusheng Shi Chinese Journal of Mechanical Engineering 2026-04-01 10.1016/j.cjme.2026.100312
单位: Key Laboratory of Advanced Manufacturing Technology for High Performance Parts, Ministry of Education, School of Mechanical and Electronic Engineering, Wuhan University of Technology, Wuhan 430070, Ch
通讯: Bo Song @ State Key Laboratory of Materials Processing and Die and Mould Technology, School of Materials Science and Engineering,
相关性: 该论文直接研究了热和表面后处理对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V疲劳和拉伸性能的影响，与后处理控制方向完全一致。
边界: This paper does not investigate the effect of hot isostatic pressing (HIP) or laser shock peening; it focuses on heat treatment and shot blasting/machining only.
动机· 提高LPBF Ti-6Al-4V的拉伸和疲劳性能，使其达到锻件水平
方法· 对LPBF Ti-6Al-4V试样进行热处理和表面处理（喷丸、机加工）并测试力学性能
结果· 表面处理大幅提升强度和疲劳极限，结合热处理后进一步改善
验证· 通过拉伸和疲劳实验对比处理前后的力学性能
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the tensile and fatigue properties of LPBF Ti-6Al-4V to match those of wrought counterparts.
Method· Heat treatment and surface processing (shot blasting and machining) were applied to LPBF Ti-6Al-4V specimens, followed by tensile and fatigue testing.
Result· Surface processing greatly enhanced strength and fatigue limit, which was further improved by subsequent heat treatment.
Validation· Mechanical tests (tensile and fatigue) comparing as-built, heat-treated, and surface-processed conditions.

Experimental investigation of the fatigue performance of Ti-6Al-4V titanium alloy subjected to shot peening

Xiao Zhang, Yi Zuo, Zhuotao Fan Journal of Physics Conference Series 2026-04-01 10.1088/1742-6596/3207/1/012124
单位: Shanghai Aircraft Design and Research Institute, Commercial Aircraft Corporation of China, Ltd., Shanghai, 200135, P. R. China
通讯: Xiao Zhang @ Shanghai Aircraft Design and Research Institute, Commercial Aircraft Corporation of China, Ltd., Shanghai, 200135, P. R.
相关性: 该论文研究了喷丸处理对Ti-6Al-4V疲劳性能的影响，但未明确为增材制造材料，相关方法可转移至增材制造后处理评估。
边界: The paper does not specify the manufacturing route; it likely uses wrought Ti-6Al-4V, not additive-manufactured Ti-6Al-4V, thus it lacks direct relevance to AM post-processing.
动机· 探究不同喷丸强度对Ti-6Al-4V疲劳性能的影响规律。
方法· 对Ti-6Al-4V板材进行五种强度喷丸处理，开展疲劳试验并计算细节疲劳额定值。
结果· 喷丸显著提升疲劳性能，中等强度时增益最大达48.50%，过高或过低均使增益下降。
验证· 通过不同喷丸强度下的疲劳试验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how shot peening intensity affects the fatigue performance of Ti-6Al-4V titanium alloy.
Method· Fatigue tests were conducted on Ti-6Al-4V plates subjected to five different shot peening intensities, and the detail fatigue rating (DFR) was calculated to quantify improvement.
Result· Shot peening significantly enhances fatigue resistance, with a maximum DFR gain of 48.50%; an intermediate peening intensity yields the optimal improvement.
Validation· Experimental fatigue testing with multiple intensity levels.

Microstructure and mechanical properties of additively enhanced titanium alloy lightweight high-speed motor shaft

Yue Wang, Zehao Xu, Y LIU, Xiaotian Wei, Sikai Fu et al. Journal of Physics Conference Series 2026-04-01 10.1088/1742-6596/3221/1/012023
单位: North China Electric Power University
通讯: Yue Wang @ North China Electric Power University
相关性: 论文研究的是Ti-6Al-4V基体上的激光熔覆涂层，而非基体本身的力学或疲劳性能，与方向关注的Ti-6Al-4V增材制造力学性能不直接相关。
边界: The paper is about a laser-cladded Al-based composite coating on Ti-6Al-4V, not about the mechanical or fatigue behavior of the base Ti-6Al-4V material itself.
动机· 提升高速电机轴的力学性能和耐磨性
方法· 采用激光熔覆技术在钛合金电机轴上制备Al基复合涂层
结果· 涂层硬度从320HV增至530-610HV，摩擦系数显著降低
验证· 通过显微硬度和磨损试验验证涂层性能
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the mechanical properties and wear resistance of high-speed motor shafts.
Method· An Al-based composite coating was deposited on a Ti-6Al-4V motor shaft using laser cladding additive manufacturing.
Result· The coating achieved a microhardness increase from 320 HV to 530-610 HV and a significantly reduced friction coefficient compared to the substrate.
Validation· Validation was performed through microhardness measurements and wear tests.

Comparative analysis between traditional topology optimization and lattice structure optimization for additive manufacturing

Muhammad Muzammil Idrees reposiTUm (TU Wien) 2026-04-01 10.34726/hss.2026.139126
单位: TU Wien
通讯: Muhammad Muzammil Idrees @ TU Wien
相关性: 该论文研究了Ti-6Al-4V材料的点阵结构与拓扑优化对比，但未涉及任何股骨柄或骨科植入物设计，与用户方向无关。
边界: The paper does not study a femoral/hip stem or any orthopaedic implant structure; it uses a generic tensile specimen and lacks biomechanical context, stress shielding, or implant-specific loading.
动机· 比较拓扑优化与点阵结构在减重设计中的力学性能差异。
方法· 对六种点阵（BCC、FCC、Graph-Diamond、Gyroid、Schwarz、Diamond）与一种拓扑优化实体进行有限元拉伸对比。
结果· 拓扑优化实体承载能力最强（82.5 kN），TPMS Diamond点阵次之（33.5 kN），而支柱类点阵因应力集中性能较差。
验证· 仅有限元分析，无实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare the mechanical performance of topology-optimized solid versus various lattice structures under tensile loading for weight reduction.
Method· A controlled numerical comparison of six lattice geometries (three strut-based, three TPMS-based) and one topology-optimized solid, all with identical mass and volume, using linear elastic FEA with Ti-6Al-4V material.
Result· The topology-optimized solid exhibited the highest load capacity (82.5 kN), followed by the TPMS Diamond (33.5 kN), while strut-based lattices showed significant bending and stress localization leading to lower capacities (7-20 kN).
Validation· Numerical benchmark with mesh convergence study, no experimental validation.
