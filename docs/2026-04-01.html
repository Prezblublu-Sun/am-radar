Revealing the microstructure-defect evolution and fracture behavior of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V alloy by in-situ X-ray computed tomography

单位: School of Aeronautics, Chongqing Jiaotong University, Chongqing 400074, China
通讯: Yi Ren @ Chongqing Key Laboratory of Green Aviation Energy and Power, Chongqing Jiaotong University, Chongqing, 400074, China  |  Weiyou Chen @ Aero-engine Thermal Environment and Structure Key Laboratory of Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, College
相关性: 该论文直接针对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V合金的断裂行为，通过原位X射线断层扫描揭示微观结构与缺陷演化，为力学性能研究提供关键实验数据。
边界: No cyclic fatigue life or S-N data; focuses on monotonic fracture mechanisms.

方法· 利用原位X射线断层扫描在力学测试中实时观察缺陷演化。
结果· 揭示了缺陷的形成、扩展及其对断裂路径的影响。
验证· 通过原位实验观测结合微观组织表征进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand the interplay between microstructure and defects during fracture of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· In-situ X-ray computed tomography was employed during mechanical testing to capture real-time defect evolution.
Result· The evolution of defects and their influence on fracture initiation and propagation were revealed.
Validation· In-situ experimental observations were validated against post-test microstructural characterization.

Tensile and fatigue properties of additively manufactured titanium alloy: Effects of heat treatment and surface processing

Jinliang Zhang, Bo Song, Qian Yan, Chaoqun Wu, Yusheng Shi Chinese Journal of Mechanical Engineering 2026-04-01 10.1016/j.cjme.2026.100312
单位: Key Laboratory of Advanced Manufacturing Technology for High Performance Parts, Ministry of Education, School of Mechanical and Electronic Engineering, Wuhan University of Technology, Wuhan 430070, Ch
通讯: Bo Song @ State Key Laboratory of Materials Processing and Die and Mould Technology, School of Materials Science and Engineering,
相关性: 该论文直接研究了热处理和表面处理（喷丸、机加工）对激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V合金拉伸和疲劳性能的影响，完全符合后处理控制方向。
边界: No coverage of cyclic plasticity modeling or microstructural characterization beyond basic comparison; limited to standard treatments without novel post-processing techniques.
动机· 增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学性能不及锻件，需通过后处理改善。
方法· 对激光粉末床熔融和锻态Ti-6Al-4V试样进行热处理及喷丸/机加工处理，测试拉伸和疲劳性能。
结果· 表面处理显著提升拉伸和疲劳强度，热处理进一步提高疲劳极限。
验证· 通过对比不同处理状态的力学性能实验数据验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· As-built LPBF Ti-6Al-4V exhibits inferior tensile and fatigue properties compared to forgings, necessitating post-processing improvements.
Method· Heat treatment and surface treatments (shot blasting and machining) were applied to LPBF and forged Ti-6Al-4V specimens, followed by tensile and fatigue testing.
Result· Surface treatments greatly improved tensile and fatigue strength, and heat treatment further enhanced the fatigue limit.
Validation· Experimental mechanical testing comparing as-built, heat-treated, and surface-treated conditions.

Experimental investigation of the fatigue performance of Ti-6Al-4V titanium alloy subjected to shot peening

Xiao Zhang, Yi Zuo, Zhuotao Fan Journal of Physics Conference Series 2026-04-01 10.1088/1742-6596/3207/1/012124
单位: Shanghai Aircraft Design and Research Institute, Commercial Aircraft Corporation of China, Ltd., Shanghai, 200135, P. R. China
通讯: Xiao Zhang @ Shanghai Aircraft Design and Research Institute, Commercial Aircraft Corporation of China, Ltd., Shanghai, 200135, P. R.
相关性: 该论文直接研究了喷丸处理对Ti-6Al-4V疲劳性能的影响，提供了不同强度下的实验数据，与后处理控制方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not include microstructural analysis or porosity closure data, focusing only on fatigue performance and DFR quantification.
动机· 研究喷丸处理对Ti-6Al-4V钛合金疲劳性能的影响。
方法· 对Ti-6Al-4V板进行不同强度的喷丸处理，开展疲劳试验并计算DFR。
结果· 喷丸处理最大提高DFR达48.50%，中等强度效果最佳。
验证· 通过疲劳试验验证喷丸强化效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To experimentally investigate the effect of shot peening on the fatigue performance of Ti-6Al-4V titanium alloy.
Method· Fatigue tests were conducted on Ti-6Al-4V plates subjected to five different shot peening intensities, and the detailed fatigue rating (DFR) was calculated.
Result· Shot peening significantly improved fatigue resistance, with a maximum DFR gain of 48.50%, and an intermediate peening intensity yielded the optimal enhancement.
Validation· The findings are validated through systematic fatigue experiments across multiple peening intensities.

单位: TU Wien
通讯: Muhammad Muzammil Idrees @ TU Wien
相关性: 该论文未涉及任何股骨柄或骨科植入物设计，而是对Ti-6Al-4V矩形块进行拓扑优化与点阵结构的通用拉伸性能数值比较。
边界: Does not address femoral stem design, hip implant geometry, or any orthopaedic biomechanical criteria such as stress shielding, micromotion, or ISO fatigue testing; instead focuses on generic tensile comparison of lattice and topology-optimized solids in a rectangular block.
动机· 比较拓扑优化与点阵结构在减重设计中的力学性能差异。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare the mechanical performance of topology optimization and various lattice structures under identical mass constraints for lightweight design.
Method· Linear elastic finite element analysis was conducted on six lattice geometries (three strut-based, three TPMS-based) and one topology-optimized solid, all constrained to the same mass within a rectangular design volume.
Result· The topology-optimized solid exhibited the highest tensile load capacity, followed by TPMS lattices, with strut-based lattices showing earlier failure due to stress localization.
Validation· No experimental validation; the study relies solely on numerical simulations.

CNN fusion of stylus traces with cutting parameters for accurate and economical roughness estimation of Ti-6Al-4V dry turning

Lidiya Priyadarsini K, Anupam Haldar, Abhishek Mandal, Rajat Sen Engineering Research Express 2026-04-01 10.1088/2631-8695/ae5ecf
相关性: 该论文研究钛合金干车削的表面粗糙度预测，而非后处理对力学性能的影响，与用户方向无关。
边界: This paper does not study any post-processing treatment (e.g., heat treatment, shot peening, HIP) nor its effect on mechanical performance (fatigue, tensile) of Ti-6Al-4V; it focuses on surface roughness prediction in machining, which is a quality control method rather than a post-processing modification.
动机· 针对钛合金干车削中表面粗糙度预测准确度低的问题。
方法· 首次将卷积神经网络直接应用于触针轮廓迹线进行粗糙度预测，并与标量回归模型比较。
结果· CNN+SVR混合模型预测精度显著提升（R²=0.724，MAE=0.192μm）。
验证· 采用27种加工条件下的135个样本，通过组感知交叉验证避免数据泄露。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To address poor accuracy in surface roughness prediction for dry turning of Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Convolutional neural networks are applied directly to stylus profile traces for Ra prediction, compared with scalar regression models.
Result· The CNN+SVR hybrid model achieves significantly improved prediction accuracy (R²=0.724, MAE=0.192 μm).
Validation· 135 profile samples from 27 machining conditions are used with a group-aware cross-validation strategy to prevent data leakage.
