Implant Survival and Clinical Results of a Cementless Femoral Stem: Minimum 30-Year Follow-Up of 348 Primary Total Hip Arthroplasties

Andre Lunz, Maximilian Neuendorff, Mustafa Hariri, Johannes Weishorn, Tilman Walker et al. The Journal of Arthroplasty 2026-04-01 10.1016/j.arth.2026.04.014
单位: Department of Orthopaedics, Heidelberg University Hospital, Heidelberg, Germany
通讯: Andre Lunz @ Department of Orthopaedics, Heidelberg University Hospital, Heidelberg, Germany
相关性: 该论文提供超30年临床随访结果，但未涉及力学建模、应力遮挡或增材制造。
边界: This paper does not include any biomechanical modeling, stress shielding quantification, micromotion analysis, or additive manufacturing of the stem.
动机· 评估骨水泥型股骨柄超30年生存率及临床功能。
方法· 回顾性分析348例初次全髋置换，平均随访33年。
结果· 33年假体生存率为79.2%，无菌松动和假体周围骨折为主要失败原因。

English summary & terms
Motivation· Long-term evidence beyond 25 years for cementless femoral stems is lacking; this study evaluates survival at a minimum of 30 years.
Method· A retrospective review of 348 primary cementless THAs using a single straight stem was performed, with prospective follow-up for at least 30 years.
Result· At 33 years, stem survival for any revision was 79.2%, and for aseptic loosening 91.6%; stem undersizing (canal fill <90%) was a significant risk factor for aseptic loosening.
Validation· Kaplan-Meier survival analyses with confidence intervals and patient-reported outcomes (Harris Hip Score) were used to validate implant performance.
