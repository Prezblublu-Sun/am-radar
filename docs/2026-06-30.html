Effect of Repeated Powder Reuse on the Quality and Mechanical Properties of Ti-6Al-4V Fabricated by Laser Powder Bed Fusion

Jae H. Kim, Seong-Jin Park, Hyomoon Joo, Jae‐Keun Hong Journal of Welding and Joining 2026-06-30 10.5781/jwj.2026.44.3.7
单位: Lightweight Materials Research Division, Korea Institute of Materials Science, Changwon, 51508, Korea
相关性: 该论文直接研究了激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V粉末重复使用对力学性能的影响，属于L1方向的核心内容。
边界: This paper does not model or predict mechanical/fatigue behavior; it provides only experimental characterization and a practical reuse limit based on oxygen and ductility.
动机· 评估LPBF Ti-6Al-4V粉末重复使用对制品质量与力学性能的影响。
方法· 将气体雾化Ti-6Al-4V粉末重复使用多达15次，检测粉末形貌、氧含量、硬度、拉伸性能及微观组织。
结果· 重复使用15次后硬度升至411 HV，伸长率从10.7%降至7.4%，氧含量超标，提出了6次重复使用上限。
验证· 通过硬度测试、拉伸试验、SEM-EDS和光学显微镜进行实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of repeated powder reuse on the quality and mechanical properties of Ti-6Al-4V fabricated by laser powder bed fusion.
Method· Gas-atomized Ti-6Al-4V powder was reused up to 15 build cycles, and changes in powder morphology, oxygen content, hardness, tensile properties, and microstructure were characterized.
Result· Hardness increased, elongation decreased from 10.7% to 7.4%, oxygen exceeded the ASTM limit after 6 cycles, and a practical reuse limit of 6 cycles was proposed.
Validation· Experimental validation through mechanical testing (hardness, tensile), SEM-EDS, and optical microscopy.

Integrating Mechanical Testing and Finite Element Analysis for Failure Risk Prediction in Ti-6Al-4V Parts Produced by Laser Powder Bed Fusion

Franka Hendra, Riki Effendi, Supriyono Supriyono Jurnal POLIMESIN 2026-06-30 10.30811/jpl.v24i3.8905
单位: Universitas Pamulang
相关性: 该论文通过力学测试与有限元分析相结合预测LPBF Ti-6Al-4V部件的疲劳失效，直接支持用户对力学与疲劳性能的研究方向。
边界: 论文重点在于开发预测模型而非提供新的实验疲劳数据或S-N曲线，机械表征可能仅限于模型构建。
动机· 针对激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V部件疲劳失效风险预测的挑战。
方法· 结合力学测试与有限元分析，建立多元回归预测模型。
结果· 模型能高精度预测疲劳寿命，R²接近0.95，RMSE低。
验证· 通过k折交叉验证评估模型精度。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To address the challenge of predicting fatigue failure risk for Ti-6Al-4V parts produced by Laser Powder Bed Fusion.
Method· An integrated experimental-computational approach combining tensile and fatigue testing with finite element simulations and multiple regression analysis.
Result· The developed model achieves high accuracy in predicting fatigue life with R² values near 0.95 and low RMSE.
Validation· The model is validated using k-fold cross-validation (k=5) with performance metrics R² and RMSE.

Computational Investigation of Friction Stir Processing of Ti-6Al-4V Alloy for Biomedical Applications Using FEM and Taguchi Design

Nebojša Zdravković, Dragan Džunić, Živana Jovanović Pešić, Dalibor Nikolić Computation 2026-06-30 10.3390/computation14070150
单位: Department of Medical Statistics and Informatics, Faculty of Medical Sciences, University of Kragujevac, Svetozara Markovića 69, 34000 Kragujevac, Serbia
通讯: Dalibor Nikolić @ Institute for Information Technologies, University of Kragujevac, Liceja Kneževine Srbije 1A, 34000 Kragujevac, Serbia
相关性: 该论文研究的是Ti-6Al-4V合金的搅拌摩擦加工（FSP）热场模拟，而非增材制造状态下的力学或疲劳性能，因此对L1方向参考价值有限。
边界: This paper focuses on a post-processing surface modification technique (FSP) and thermal-microstructural outcomes, not on mechanical/fatigue performance of as-built or process-state Ti-6Al-4V. No tensile, hardness, or fatigue data are reported.
动机· 通过有限元模拟优化Ti-6Al-4V搅拌摩擦加工参数，避免熔化并保持细晶组织。
方法· 采用三维有限元模型和Taguchi L9正交阵列，分析转速、行进速度、肩部直径和搅拌针直径的影响。
结果· 行进速度是影响温度的最显著参数（贡献63.1%），最优参数组合可控制温度低于β转变温度。
验证· 与文献中实验报道的温度范围对比验证趋势。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To computationally optimize friction stir processing parameters for Ti-6Al-4V, ensuring temperatures remain below melting and β-transus to preserve fine microstructure.
Method· A three-dimensional finite element model combined with Taguchi L9 orthogonal array was used to evaluate the effects of rotational speed, traverse speed, shoulder diameter, and pin diameter.
Result· Traverse speed was the dominant parameter (63.1% contribution), and the optimal condition (400 rpm, 100 mm/min, 18 mm shoulder) maintained temperatures below the β-transus.
Validation· Predicted temperatures were compared with experimental ranges reported in literature for friction stir welding/processing of Ti-6Al-4V.

Modelling and computational optimization of different neural network architectures for prediction of depth of cut in abrasive water jet machining of Ti6Al4V

Yakub Iqbal Mogul, Jaimon Dennis Quadros, G. Ganesan Subramanian, Prashanth Thalambeti, Ibtisam Mogul et al. Scientific Reports 2026-06-30 10.1038/s41598-026-54813-5
单位: National Centre for Motorsport Engineering, School of Engineering and Built Environment, University of Greater Manchester, Deane Road, Bolton, BL3 5AB, UK
通讯: Jaimon Dennis Quadros @ National Centre for Motorsport Engineering, School of Engineering and Built Environment, University of Greater Mancheste
相关性: 本文研究的是Ti6Al4V的磨料水射流加工中切深预测的神经网络优化，未涉及后处理对疲劳或力学性能的影响，因此与用户方向（后处理对力学性能的影响）关联较弱。
边界: This paper focuses on predicting depth of cut in abrasive water jet machining of Ti6Al4V, but does not study the effect of post-processing on mechanical performance (e.g., fatigue, tensile strength) or microstructural changes.
动机· 优化神经网络模型以精确预测Ti6Al4V磨料水射流加工的切深。
方法· 基于Taguchi L27实验设计，使用单隐藏层和深度神经网络，比较四种激活函数。
结果· 深度神经网络结合高斯激活函数预测精度最高，测试RMSE降低12.5%，R²提高4.2%。
验证· 通过SEM观察加工表面形貌验证预测结果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This work aims to optimize neural network architectures for accurately predicting depth of cut in abrasive water jet machining of Ti6Al4V.
Method· Experiments were performed using a Taguchi L27 orthogonal array, and two neural network models (single hidden layer and deep) with four activation functions were optimized on a dataset generated from regression equation.
Result· The deep neural network with Gaussian activation function achieved the best prediction, reducing testing RMSE by 12.5% and improving R² by 4.2%.
Validation· Scanning electron microscopy confirmed the predicted depth of cut by revealing micro-cutting and micro-ploughing features on machined surfaces.
