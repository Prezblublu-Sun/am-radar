Achieving strength-ductility synergy in laser-manufactured titanium alloys: a wire-powder synchronous feeding strategy with O-Fe microalloying

Yuhang Duan, Jianfeng Wang, Zhuanni Gao, Shuwen Huang, Chao Feng et al. Microstructures 2026-05-15 10.20517/microstructures.2025.131
相关性: 该论文直接针对激光增材制造Ti-6Al-4V合金的力学性能，通过微合金化显著提升强度和塑性，与方向L1高度相关。
边界: This paper does not report fatigue properties, S-N curves, or cyclic loading behavior, which are part of the direction focus.
动机· 克服激光制造钛合金强度-延展性难以兼顾的长期难题
方法· 提出丝粉同步送进策略结合O-Fe微合金化调控组织
结果· 获得抗拉强度1266.6 MPa、延伸率15.5%的优异综合力学性能
验证· 通过多尺度表征和定量分析验证微合金化对晶粒细化和性能提升的作用
English summary & terms
Motivation· To overcome the strength-ductility trade-off in laser-manufactured titanium alloys via a novel wire-powder synchronous feeding strategy with O-Fe microalloying.
Method· The strategy combines wire and powder feeding with controlled O-Fe additions to refine the microstructure during solidification.
Result· The optimized Ti-6Al-4V-0.5O-2.3Fe alloy achieved a tensile strength of 1266.6 MPa and an elongation of 15.5%, representing substantial improvements over the base alloy.
Validation· Multiscale characterization and quantitative analyses confirmed grain refinement, acicular α/α′ modification, and stress relief at phase boundaries.

Effects of Stress Relief Heat Treatment Process on Thermal Analysis and Kinetics of Ti-6Al-4V Alloys

Fatih Altun, Gözde Altuntaş, Abdulcelil Bayar, Ramazan Asmatulu, Bülent Bostan et al. 2026-05-15 10.33599/nasampe/s.26.160
单位: Wichita State University
通讯: Fatih Altun @ Wichita State University
相关性: 该论文研究了应力消除热处理对Ti-6Al-4V合金热分析和动力学的影响，但未涉及机械性能，与用户关注的疲劳/机械性能方向关联较弱。
边界: This paper does not report any mechanical performance data (fatigue, tensile, hardness) after stress relief, focusing instead on thermal oxidation and phase transformation kinetics.
动机· 研究应力消除热处理对3D打印Ti-6Al-4V合金热分析和动力学的影响。
方法· 采用激光粉末床熔融制备样品，在700°C进行2小时应力消除处理，通过热重分析、SEM和Kissinger方程计算活化能。
结果· 应力消除后，马氏体转变为α+β相，质量增加减少10%，活化能降低50%。
验证· 通过TGA和SEM实验验证热处理对氧化和相变的影响。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of stress relief heat treatment on the thermal analysis and kinetics of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V alloys.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V samples were produced by laser powder bed fusion and subjected to stress relief at 700°C for 2 hours, followed by thermogravimetric analysis, SEM, and activation energy calculation using the Kissinger equation.
Result· Stress relief transformed martensite to α+β phase, reduced mass gain by 10%, and decreased activation energy by 50%.
Validation· Experimental validation through TGA and SEM analysis.

X-ray observation of individual Ti-6Al-4V spherical powder particle impact in an in-situ operando laser directed energy deposition system

相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的定向能量沉积过程，但聚焦于单个粉末颗粒的撞击观察，未提供力学或疲劳性能数据。
边界: This paper does not provide mechanical or fatigue characterization of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it focuses on single particle impact dynamics rather than bulk material properties.
动机· 观察激光定向能量沉积中单个钛合金粉末颗粒的撞击过程
方法· 使用原位X射线成像技术观察颗粒撞击
结果· 揭示了颗粒撞击过程中的变形和飞溅行为
验证· 实验观察验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To observe the impact of individual Ti-6Al-4V powder particles during laser directed energy deposition.
Method· In-situ operando X-ray imaging was used to capture single particle impacts.
Result· The deformation and splashing behavior during particle impact were revealed.
Validation· Experimental observation provided direct evidence.
