Functional and radiological outcome of fully hydroxyapatite-coated femoral stem in uncemented total hip replacement

Mahantesh Magadum, Deepak D Chitragar, Shreyas Zalariya, Dr. Mohamed Abdelgelil Mohamed International Journal of Orthopaedics Sciences 2026-03-01 10.22271/ortho.2026.v12.i3a.3908
相关性: 该论文报告了全髋关节置换中使用羟基磷灰石涂层股骨柄的临床与影像学结果，但未涉及生物力学建模、有限元分析或增材制造，与用户研究方向关联较弱。
边界: This paper does not include any biomechanical modeling (e.g., stress shielding, micromotion), finite element analysis, or additive manufacturing processes, and focuses solely on clinical and radiological outcomes without computational or mechanistic insights.
动机· 评估全羟基磷灰石涂层股骨柄在非骨水泥全髋置换中的功能和影像学结果。
方法· 纳入49例患者的前瞻性和回顾性观察研究，随访36个月。
结果· 平均Harris髋关节评分91分，93.9%近端骨长入良好，无柄下沉或松动。
验证· 通过临床随访和影像学评估验证植入物固定效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the functional and radiological outcomes of fully hydroxyapatite-coated femoral stems in uncemented total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A combined prospective and retrospective observational study was conducted on 49 patients undergoing THA with HA-coated stems, with a mean follow-up of 36 months.
Result· The mean Harris Hip Score was 91, with excellent proximal bone ingrowth in 93.9% and no cases of stem subsidence or loosening.
Validation· Clinical assessment and radiographic analysis were used to confirm osseointegration and implant stability.

Taper-Slip Cemented Femoral Stem Designs Are Associated With Higher Periprosthetic Fracture Risk Compared to Composite-Beam in a United States Health Care System.

Foster Chen, Heather A Prentice, Brian H Fasig, Elizabeth W Paxton, Kevin T Hug et al. The Journal of arthroplasty 2026-03-01 10.1016/j.arth.2025.07.006
单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Washington Permanente Medical Group, Seattle, Washington.
通讯: Matthew P Kelly @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Southern California Permanente Medical Group, Harbor City, California. [推断]
相关性: 该论文纯临床研究，与用户的研究方向（AI生物打印、髋关节假体应力遮挡、FEA替代模型、AM生物医学植入物）无关。
边界: This paper is a clinical registry study with no biomechanical modeling, no finite element analysis, no surrogate modeling, no additive manufacturing, and no AI/ML, thus completely off-topic from the user's directions.
动机· 比较锥形滑移与复合梁型骨水泥股骨柄的翻修风险差异
方法· 基于美国医疗系统关节置换登记处的回顾性队列研究，采用Cox比例风险回归分析
结果· 锥形滑移设计假体周围骨折翻修风险更高，但无菌性松动风险无显著差异
验证· 使用大规模临床登记数据并进行多变量校正
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare the revision risk for periprosthetic fracture and aseptic loosening between taper-slip and composite-beam cemented femoral stem designs in a large US healthcare system.
Method· A retrospective cohort study was conducted using data from the Total Joint Replacement Registry, including 4,631 primary cemented THAs, with multivariable Cox proportional hazards regression for covariate adjustment.
Result· The taper-slip design was associated with a significantly higher risk of revision for periprosthetic fracture (HR=3.51) but no significant difference in aseptic loosening revision risk compared to composite-beam stems.
Validation· The study utilized a large clinical registry with long-term follow-up (up to 19 years) and adjusted for multiple covariates.

单位: The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center, Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Columbus, OH, USA.
通讯: Nicholas J Greco @ The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center, Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Columbus, OH, USA. [推断]
相关性: 该论文仅比较全髋关节置换术中的手术入路临床结果，未涉及任何生物力学建模、应力分析或增材制造内容。

结果· 两组围手术期并发症、30天再入院和90天急诊就诊无显著差异。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare early perioperative complications of direct anterior approach versus alternative approaches in total hip arthroplasty performed by DAA fellowship-trained surgeons.

Result· No significant differences were found in perioperative complications, 30-day readmissions, or 90-day ED visits between groups, and radiographic acetabular cup and femoral stem alignment were similar.
Validation· Validation was through retrospective clinical data analysis with univariate and multivariate regression.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the biomechanical performance of a cemented femoral stem for canine total hip replacement across two breeds.

Validation· Mechanical tests were correlated with radiographic and morphometric parameters in a cadaveric model.
