Defects analysis in LPBF printing based on up-skin and down-skin angles using machine learning

Zohreh Azimifar, Ali Razani, Sagar Patel, Martine McGregor, Mihaela Vlasea Proceedings of the Holistic Innovation in Additive Manufacturing (HI-AM) Conference 2025-10-31 10.15353/hi-am.v1i1.6846
单位: Department of Mechanical and Mechatronics Engineering, University of Waterloo, Waterloo, Canada
通讯: Zohreh Azimifar @ Department of Mechanical and Mechatronics Engineering, University of Waterloo, Waterloo, Canada
相关性: 该论文专注于LPBF Ti-6Al-4V的缺陷检测，但未涉及力学性能或疲劳测试，因此对方向L1的机械性能研究帮助有限。
边界: This paper does not report any mechanical or fatigue properties of Ti-6Al-4V; it focuses solely on defect detection and morphology without linking to mechanical performance or fatigue behavior.
动机· 分析LPBF制造Ti-6Al-4V中上下表面缺陷的几何特征与分布。
方法· 使用YOLO→U-Net流水线结合CT扫描数据进行缺陷检测与聚类统计。
结果· 实现了基于表面取向的切片级缺陷几何分析。
验证· 通过X射线CT扫描实验数据验证缺陷检测效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To analyze surface-connected defects in LPBF Ti-6Al-4V relative to up-skin and down-skin orientation angles.
Method· A YOLO→U-Net pipeline with clustering and statistical morphology was applied to CT-scanned data from Ti-6Al-4V parts and lattice structures.
Result· Slice-level defect geometry analysis with respect to surface-normal direction was achieved.
Validation· Experimental validation using X-ray CT scanning of manufactured Ti-6Al-4V parts.

Design and mechanical analysis of additively manufactured primitive flexures

Jonah Leinwand, Mihaela Vlasea, Stewart McLachlin Proceedings of the Holistic Innovation in Additive Manufacturing (HI-AM) Conference 2025-10-31 10.15353/hi-am.v1i1.6831
单位: Department of Mechanical and Mechatronics Engineering, University of Waterloo, Waterloo, Ontario, N2L 3G1, Canada
通讯: Jonah Leinwand @ Department of Mechanical and Mechatronics Engineering, University of Waterloo, Waterloo, Ontario, N2L 3G1, Canada
相关性: 该论文针对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V柔性结构的力学性能进行了实验测试，但主要面向颈椎人工椎间盘替代应用，而非标准力学/疲劳表征。
边界: Does not provide tensile or S-N fatigue data; focuses on flexural stiffness for a specific implant design, not general mechanical characterization.
动机· 为增材制造柔性构件提供力学设计数据，以支持人工颈椎间盘等植入物设计。
方法· 设计多种柔性构件（改变厚度、悬垂角、弯曲次数），用激光粉末床熔融制造Ti6Al4V样品，进行循环弯曲和压缩测试。
结果· 厚度≥1mm可复制颈椎轴向压缩刚度，柔性区引入后刚度损失可忽略。
验证· 通过DIC和关节模拟器的力学测试验证设计参数对刚度的影响。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To provide mechanical design data for additively manufactured flexural members to support compliant implant design, such as cervical disc replacements.
Method· Family of primitive flexures with varying thickness, overhang angle, and bend count were designed and fabricated in Ti6Al4V using laser powder bed fusion, then tested under cyclic bending and compression to failure.
Result· Flexures with 1 mm thickness achieved cervical-spine-like axial stiffness, and compliant zones introduced negligible stiffness reduction while increasing structural compliance.
Validation· Mechanical testing with a joint motion simulator and digital image correlation validated the deformation response and stiffness trends.

动机· 改善Ti6Al4V高速铣削中的散热和加工性能
方法· 对比了浇注冷却和微量润滑冷却策略对不同铣削参数下切削力、刀具磨损和表面粗糙度的影响

English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to enhance heat dissipation and machining performance of Ti6Al4V during high-speed milling by comparing cooling strategies.
Method· Experiments were conducted at various spindle speeds and feed rates using flood and minimum quantity lubrication (MQL) cooling with uncoated carbide tools.
Result· MQL cooling reduced cutting force, tool wear, and surface roughness compared to flood cooling, especially at medium to low feed rates.
Validation· Validation was performed through direct measurement of cutting force, tool wear, and surface roughness in machining trials.

Influence of isothermal post-treatment on tribocorrosion behaviour of laser powder bed fusion-processed β-type Ti-Nb alloy

Adnan Akman, Yohan Douest, Jaqueline Gehlmann, Avinash Hariharan, Christian Haase et al. Materials Today Communications 2025-10-31 10.1016/j.mtcomm.2025.114222
单位: Institute of Materials Science, TU Dresden, Helmholtzstr. 7, 01069 Dresden, Germany
通讯: Adnan Akman @ Institute of Materials Science, TU Dresden, Helmholtzstr. 7, 01069 Dresden, Germany
相关性: 该论文研究Ti-Nb合金而非Ti-6Al-4V，且未涉及疲劳性能，与后处理控制方向无关。
边界: The paper focuses on Ti-42Nb alloy, not Ti-6Al-4V, so it does not meet the material requirement for the L2 post-processing direction.
动机· 研究热处理对LPBF Ti-Nb合金力学及摩擦腐蚀性能的影响
方法· 对LPBF Ti-42Nb进行等温时效处理，测试显微组织、力学和摩擦腐蚀行为
结果· 等温处理引入析出相，提高力学性能但降低耐摩擦腐蚀性
验证· 通过显微组织表征和摩擦腐蚀实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· Investigate the effect of isothermal post-treatment on the tribocorrosion behavior of laser powder bed fusion-processed β-type Ti-Nb alloys.
Method· LPBF-fabricated Ti-42Nb samples were subjected to isothermal age-hardening at 300°C and 450°C for 30 hours, and their mechanical, corrosion, and tribocorrosion properties were evaluated.
Result· Isothermal heat treatment introduced ω and α'' precipitates, enhancing mechanical strength but reducing tribocorrosion resistance due to brittle precipitate detachment.
Validation· Microstructural analysis (XRD, SEM) and tribocorrosion tests under OCP and potentiostatic conditions in PBS were conducted.
