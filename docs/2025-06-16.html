Aplicación del Small Punch Test en la caracterización mecánica de Ti-6Al-4V obtenido por fabricación aditiva

José Francisco Moreno Ballester, Vicente Amigó Borrás, Ángel Vicente Escuder, José Ramón Blasco Puchades 2025-06-16 10.4995/ceipm2025.2025.21181
单位: Universitat Politècnica de València, Instituto Universitario de Tecnología de Materiales, Valencia
通讯: José Francisco Moreno Ballester @ Universitat Politècnica de València, Instituto Universitario de Tecnología de Materiales, Valencia
相关性: 该论文研究EBM Ti-6Al-4V的小冲杆测试方法，为小样本力学表征提供参考。
边界: does not provide standard tensile or fatigue data; focuses on method validation rather than comprehensive mechanical characterization
动机· 为克服增材制造Ti-6Al-4V力学表征的样品限制，探索小冲杆测试的适用性。
方法· 通过小冲杆测试获得载荷-位移数据，并建立与拉伸强度的关联模型。
结果· 小冲杆测试能有效估计强度，但EBM样品因缺陷和各向异性导致分散性较大。
验证· 与锻造Ti-6Al-4V的传统拉伸试验进行对比验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study investigates the applicability of the Small Punch Test for mechanical characterization of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V when material is limited.
Method· Small Punch Test was performed on EBM Ti-6Al-4V specimens, and correlations between maximum load and ultimate tensile strength were established with normalization by thickness and displacement.
Result· SPT showed good correlation for wrought Ti-6Al-4V but higher scatter for EBM specimens due to anisotropy and surface defects.
Validation· Validation was conducted by comparing SPT-derived strength estimates with conventional tensile test results for wrought Ti-6Al-4V.

Comparison of ultrasonic surface treatment methods applied to additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V alloy

S. K. Sundukov, R. I. Nigmetzyanov, Vyacheslav Mikhailovich Prikhodko, D. S. Fatyukhin, V.K. Koldyushov Metal Working and Material Science 2025-06-16 10.17212/1994-6309-2025-27.2-6-28
相关性: 该论文直接研究Ti-6Al-4V合金增材制造后的超声表面处理，虽未提供疲劳数据，但表面粗糙度和显微硬度的变化对后处理工艺选择有参考价值。
边界: Does not directly measure tensile or fatigue performance; mechanical characterization is limited to microhardness and surface roughness.
动机· 研究不同超声处理方法对SLM成型Ti-6Al-4V表面性能的影响。
方法· 比较空蚀、空蚀-磨粒和超声表面塑性变形三种处理。
结果· 三种方法均降低粗糙度，超声表面塑性变形硬度提高35%但产生裂纹。
验证· 通过高速摄像、粗糙度测试和金相显微镜验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To determine the influence of different ultrasonic treatment types on surface properties of SLM Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Comparative testing of cavitation-erosion, cavitation-abrasive, and ultrasonic surface plastic deformation treatments.
Result· All treatments reduce roughness; USPD increases microhardness by 35% but introduces cracks.
Validation· High-speed imaging, roughness measurement, and microstructural analysis.

Thermo mechanical Finite Element Analysis of the plasma Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing process in DEFORM® 13

Marcel Czipin, Alexander Wenda, Karin Hartl, Emre Akalin, Martin Stockinger Journal of Advanced Joining Processes 2025-06-16 10.1016/j.jajp.2025.100321
单位: Chair of Metal Forming, University of Leoben, Franz-Josef-Straße 18, AT-8700, Leoben, Austria
通讯: Marcel Czipin @ Chair of Metal Forming, University of Leoben, Franz-Josef-Straße 18, AT-8700, Leoben, Austria
相关性: 该论文研究了Ti-6Al-4V线弧增材制造的热机械有限元模拟，并评估了七种热处理策略对机械各向异性的影响，但与疲劳性能的直接关联较弱。
边界: No experimental fatigue or tensile data; the study focuses on process simulation and grain size prediction, not quantitative mechanical performance after post-processing.
动机· 通过有限元模拟预测线弧增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的热历程和变，并评估热处理对力学各向异性的影响。
方法· 在DEFORM中建立热-力-微观组织耦合模型，采用双椭球热源和逐层激活，对比七种热处理工艺。
结果· 模拟与实验吻合良好，固溶+水淬+时效热处理最有效降低各向异性。
验证· 热电偶验证热历程，3D扫描验证变形，金相验证晶粒尺寸。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to simulate the thermal history, deformation, and residual stress of wire arc additive manufacturing of Ti-6Al-4V and to evaluate post-processing heat treatments to mitigate mechanical anisotropy.
Method· A thermo-mechanical finite element model was developed in DEFORM® using a normalized double-ellipsoid heat source and element activation, and seven heat treatment strategies were compared.
Result· The simulation predictions agreed well with experimental measurements, and solution annealing followed by water quenching and low-temperature aging was identified as the most effective treatment for reducing anisotropy.
Validation· The model was validated against thermocouple measurements for thermal history, a 3D scan for deformation, and an as-built micrograph for grain size distribution.

Effect of Thermal Oxidation Parameters on Morphological Properties of Thermal Oxide Coatings on Ti6Al4V Titanium Alloy

Muhammad Arslan, Ameeq Farooq Nature's symphony 2025-06-16 10.69547/ns.31.05
单位: Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, China
通讯: Muhammad Arslan @ Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, China
相关性: 论文涉及Ti6Al4V合金的热氧化处理，但未涉及力学性能，且不针对增材制造材料，与用户关注的疲劳/力学性能方向关联弱。
边界: This paper does not investigate mechanical performance (fatigue, tensile) of Ti-6Al-4V after post-processing, nor does it concern additive-manufactured material.
动机· 研究热氧化参数对Ti6Al4V合金氧化涂层形貌的影响。
方法· 采用退火、正火和空淬三种热处理，在850和900°C下对Ti6Al4V进行氧化。
结果· 900°C退火产生均匀致密的涂层，厚度195 µm，增重126 mg/cm²。
验证· 通过SEM、XRD和重量测量表征涂层形貌和厚度。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to investigate how thermal oxidation parameters affect the morphology of oxide coatings on Ti6Al4V alloy.
Method· Ti6Al4V samples were subjected to annealing, normalizing, and air-quenching at 850 and 900 °C, followed by characterization of oxide coating morphology, thickness, and weight gain.
Result· Annealing at 900 °C produced a uniform, dense oxide coating (mainly rutile TiO2) with a thickness of 195 µm and weight gain of 126 mg/cm², outperforming other treatments.
Validation· Experimental characterization (SEM, XRD, gravimetry) was used to evaluate coating properties.

单位: Instituto de Estudos Avançados, Departamento de Ciência e Tecnologia Aeroespacial
通讯: Rafael Humberto Mota de Siqueira @ Instituto de Estudos Avançados, Departamento de Ciência e Tecnologia Aeroespacial
相关性: 该论文主要研究Ti6Al4V激光表面重熔后处理对表面粗糙度、残余应力和微观结构的影响，未涉及任何力学或疲劳性能测试，与用户关注的增材制造Ti-6Al-4V基础力学和疲劳性能方向不匹配。
边界: This paper focuses on laser surface remelting (LSM) post-processing of Ti6Al4V and reports only surface roughness, XRD, and residual stress; it provides no mechanical testing (tensile, fatigue, hardness) and thus does not contribute to the base mechanical/fatigue characterization required by Layer 1.
动机· 控制SLM Ti6Al4V的表面粗糙度以提高机械可靠性。
方法· 采用Yb:光纤激光对Ti6Al4V进行表面重熔处理。
结果· 表面粗糙度降低56%，残余应力变为压应力，晶粒尺寸增加44%。
验证· 通过粗糙度测量、XRD和残余应力分析进行表征。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To control the surface roughness of SLM-produced Ti6Al4V components, which is critical for mechanical reliability.
Method· Laser surface remelting (LSM) was applied using a Yb:fiber laser at 130 W, 50 mm/s scan speed, 0.1 mm hatch spacing, and argon shielding.
Result· Surface roughness (Ra) was reduced by 56%, compressive residual stresses were induced, and grain size increased by 44% due to coalescence during remelting.
Validation· Surface roughness measurements, X-ray diffraction for phase identification and texture, and residual stress analysis were conducted.

Development of Two-Color Pyrometer for Film Cooling and Transpiration Cooling at High Temperature Conditions

Bo‐Wei Chen, J. K. Chang, Szu-Chi Huang, Shih-Hsun Chen, Yao-Hsien Liu 2025-06-16 10.1115/gt2025-152615
单位: National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University , ,
通讯: Bo‐Wei Chen @ National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University , ,
相关性: 文中所用一种材料为增材制造Ti-6Al-4V，但研究核心为高温冷却技术及温度测量，与力学/疲劳性能无关。
边界: The paper does not investigate any mechanical or fatigue properties of Ti-6Al-4V; its focus is on pyrometry and cooling performance, not on tensile, fatigue, or other mechanical characterization.
动机· 发展高温环境下不依赖发射率的表面温度测量技术
方法· 使用双色高温计通过干涉滤光片（1250/1550nm）及黑体炉标定，测量加肋冷却及发散冷却模型表面温度
结果· 成功测量不同材料（含Ti-6Al-4V）表面温度，测量不确定度小于5%
验证· 风洞实验（主流温度>900°C）验证，与黑体炉标定结果对比
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a non-invasive temperature measurement technique that is independent of surface emissivity for high-temperature cooling studies.
Method· A two-color pyrometer using an industrial camera with interference filters at 1250 nm and 1550 nm, calibrated with a blackbody furnace, was applied to measure surface temperature on film-cooled and transpiration-cooled flat plates.
Result· The technique successfully measured surface temperatures on Ti-6Al-4V and other additively manufactured specimens with an uncertainty below 5%.
Validation· Experimental validation was conducted in a high-temperature wind tunnel (mainstream >900°C) with blackbody calibration.
