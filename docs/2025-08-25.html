Evaluation of the size and orientation-dependent mechanical properties of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V: The role of microstructure, defects, and surface roughness and its implications for lattice structures

Massimiliano Casata, S. Perosanz, Yuanbo T. Tang, T. Wilkinson, Roger C. Reed et al. Results in Engineering 2025-08-25 10.1016/j.rineng.2025.106781
单位: E.T.S. de Ingeniería Aeronáutica y del Espacio, Universidad Politécnica de Madrid, Plaza Cardenal Cisneros 3, 28040, Madrid, Spain
通讯: Daniel Barba @ E.T.S. de Ingeniería Aeronáutica y del Espacio, Universidad Politécnica de Madrid, Plaza Cardenal Cisneros 3, 28040, Mad
相关性: 该论文系统研究了增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的尺寸效应和取向效应对力学性能的影响，与您关注的Ti-6Al-4V基础力学性能方向高度相关。
边界: Does not include fatigue testing or S-N data; focuses solely on static tensile properties and microstructure.
动机· 探究激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V中样品尺寸和取向对力学性能的影响
方法· 通过缺陷分析、几何特征、微观组织表征以及计算建模系统评估尺寸效应
结果· 小尺寸试样孔隙率增加，但经准确评估后强度反而提升，归因于α板条细化
验证· 采用X射线断层扫描和力学实验验证尺寸效应
English summary & terms
Motivation· To systematically investigate how sample size and orientation affect the mechanical properties of LPBF Ti-6Al-4V through microstructure, defects, and surface roughness.
Method· Defect analysis, geometry characterization, microstructure examination, and computational modeling were performed to extract accurate mechanical properties.
Result· Smaller specimens showed increased porosity but higher strength (UTS +3.87%, YS +6.65%) after correcting for load-bearing area, attributed to α-lath refinement.
Validation· Experimental validation via X-ray tomography and tensile tests confirmed the size effects.

Design-Informed Evaluation and Fretting Damage Mapping of Additive-Manufactured Ti-6Al-4V Components

Ahmad Sadeghian, Saeed Adibnazari Designs 2025-08-25 10.3390/designs9050100
单位: School of Science and Engineering, Sharif University of Technology, International Campus, Kish Island 79417-76655, Iran
通讯: Saeed Adibnazari @ Department of Aerospace Engineering, Sharif University of Technology, Tehran 1458889694, Iran
相关性: 该论文直接研究SLM制造的Ti-6Al-4V的微动疲劳行为，包含实验和损伤表征，完全符合L1层方向。
边界: 该论文专注于微动疲劳这一特定疲劳模式，未涵盖常规疲劳（如S-N曲线）或拉伸性能，但仍在L1层疲劳框架内。
动机· 研究SLM制造Ti-6Al-4V合金的微动疲劳行为及损伤机理。
方法· 进行微动疲劳实验，结合SEM观察损伤，建立微动疲劳图。
结果· 揭示了不同载荷下损伤演化规律，提出了设计评估框架。
验证· 通过实验和SEM观察验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the fretting fatigue behavior of SLM Ti-6Al-4V and characterize damage mechanisms.
Method· Fretting fatigue tests on SLM specimens under various axial stresses and contact loads, with SEM damage analysis.
Result· A fretting map was developed, linking loading conditions to damage evolution, and a design-informed evaluation framework was proposed.
Validation· Experimental fretting fatigue tests and SEM observations.

Detection of sub-superficial defects by infrared thermography in parts made by powder bed fusion with electron beam

Silvio Defanti, Simone De Giorgi, Giovanni Rizza, Giulia Colombini, Lucia Denti et al. Progress in Additive Manufacturing 2025-08-25 10.1007/s40964-025-01313-z
单位: Department of Engineering “Enzo Ferrari”, University of Modena and Reggio Emilia, Modena, Italy
通讯: Silvio Defanti @ Department of Engineering “Enzo Ferrari”, University of Modena and Reggio Emilia, Modena, Italy
相关性: 该论文关注缺陷检测方法而非机械/疲劳性能，与用户关注的Ti-6Al-4V疲劳性能无直接关联。
边界: No mechanical or fatigue testing or modeling; only defect detection using thermography.
动机· 探索焦耳热红外热成像作为CT替代法检测PBF-EB钛合金零件亚表面缺陷。
方法· 在Ti6Al4V样品中引入缺陷，用共聚焦显微镜和CT表征，开发自动热成像检测协议。
结果· 热成像有效检测缺陷，烧结粉末不影响检测，建立了缺陷可检测性图。
验证· 通过CT验证缺陷尺寸并与热成像对比确认方法可行性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study investigates Joule heating infrared thermography as a cost-effective alternative to CT for detecting subsurface defects in PBF-EB Ti-6Al-4V parts.
Method· Intentional defects of various sizes and depths were created in Ti-6Al-4V samples, surface morphology characterized by confocal microscopy, CT provided accurate defect dimensions, and an automated thermographic inspection protocol was developed.
Result· Thermographic detection is effective despite surface roughness; sintered powder within defects does not hinder detection, and defect detectability maps tailored to anisotropic features were initiated.
Validation· Validation via CT scans confirmed defect dimensions and the assumption that sintered powder can be treated as voids.
