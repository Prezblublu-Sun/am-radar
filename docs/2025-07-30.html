Constitutive relationship and deformation mechanism of SLM Ti-6Al-4V alloy under high strain rate

Chaorun Si, Shuo Wang, Haodong Sun, Yi Feng Journal of Alloys and Compounds 2025-07-30 10.1016/j.jallcom.2025.182659
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi’an 710072, China
通讯: Chaorun Si @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi’an 710072, China
相关性: 该论文直接针对激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V的力学性能，符合L1层对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V力学/疲劳性能的关注。
边界: 该论文未涉及疲劳寿命或S-N曲线，主要关注高应变率下的本构关系而非循环载荷疲劳行为。
动机· 研究SLM Ti-6Al-4V合金在高应变率下的本构关系和变形机制
方法· 结合实验测试与理论建模，分析不同应变率下的力学响应
结果· 建立了高应变率下的本构模型，揭示了应变率强化机制
验证· 通过动态压缩实验验证模型准确性
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the constitutive behavior and deformation mechanisms of SLM-processed Ti-6Al-4V alloy under high strain rate loading.
Method· Experimental dynamic compression tests combined with constitutive modeling were employed to characterize the strain rate sensitivity.
Result· A constitutive relationship was established, revealing significant strain rate strengthening and changed deformation mechanisms.
Validation· The model was validated by comparing predicted stress-strain curves with experimental data from split Hopkinson pressure bar tests.

Residual Stress and Corrosion Performance in L-PBF Ti6Al4V: Unveiling the Optimum Stress Relieving Temperature via Microcapillary Electrochemical Characterisation

Lorenzo D’Ambrosi, Katya Brunelli, Francesco Cammelli, Reynier I. Revilla, Arshad Yazdanpanah Metals 2025-07-30 10.3390/met15080855
单位: Department of Industrial Engineering, University of Padova, Via Marzolo 9, 35131 Padova, Italy
通讯: Katya Brunelli @ Department of Industrial Engineering, University of Padova, Via Marzolo 9, 35131 Padova, Italy  |  Arshad Yazdanpanah @ Department of Industrial Engineering, University of Padova, Via Marzolo 9, 35131 Padova, Italy
相关性: 该论文研究了L-PBF Ti6Al4V的应力消除热处理对残余应力和耐腐蚀性的影响，但未涉及疲劳或拉伸力学性能，因此对您的方向（后处理控制力学性能）仅具有参考价值。
边界: No fatigue or tensile testing; focuses on corrosion and residual stress rather than mechanical performance (fatigue, tensile).
动机· 寻找最优低温应力消除温度，在减小残余应力的同时保持L-PBF Ti6Al4V的耐腐蚀性。
方法· 在400、600、800°C进行应力退火，通过XRD、SEM和微毛细管电化学方法表征微观结构和腐蚀行为。
结果· 600°C退火有效降低残余应力且不影响腐蚀性能，800°C则因相变损害钝化膜。
验证· 通过模拟口腔环境的电化学测试验证腐蚀行为，并XRD验证残余应力。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to identify the optimal low-temperature stress relieving heat treatment that minimizes residual stresses while preserving corrosion resistance in L-PBF Ti6Al4V.
Method· Stress relieving at 400, 600, and 800°C was applied, and microstructure, residual stress, and electrochemical behavior were characterized using XRD, SEM, and microcapillary electrochemical techniques under simulated oral conditions.
Result· Heat treatment at 600°C effectively reduces residual stress with minimal impact on corrosion resistance, whereas 800°C leads to α'→α+β transformation and significantly degrades passive film integrity.
Validation· Experimental validation through X-ray diffraction for residual stress and microcapillary electrochemical testing for corrosion performance.

In-situ investigation and analytical modeling of crack initiation and propagation in Ti–6Al–4V under low-cycle fatigue conditions on microscale

Christopher M. Wuensch International Journal of Fatigue 2025-07-30 10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2025.109148
单位: Institute of Test and Simulation for Gas Turbines, German Aerospace Center, Forschungsallee 1, Augsburg, 86159, Germany
通讯: Christopher M. Wuensch @ Institute of Test and Simulation for Gas Turbines, German Aerospace Center, Forschungsallee 1, Augsburg, 86159, Germany
相关性: 该论文研究的是传统工艺制造的Ti-6Al-4V的低周疲劳裂纹行为，未涉及增材制造，与用户关注的增材制造Ti-6Al-4V力学性能方向不匹配。
边界: Does not address additive manufacturing; material is conventionally manufactured Ti-6Al-4V, not LPBF/SLM/EBM/DED.
动机· 研究Ti-6Al-4V在低周疲劳条件下的裂纹萌生与扩展行为。
方法· 采用原位低周疲劳实验和解析建模相结合的方法。
结果· 裂纹在β相和晶界萌生，滑移带形成，扩展中吸收能量持续增加。
验证· 通过原位力学测试验证裂纹行为。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand the crack initiation and propagation mechanisms in Ti-6Al-4V under low-cycle fatigue conditions.
Method· In-situ low-cycle fatigue experiments combined with analytical modeling of crack growth.
Result· Crack initiation occurs preferentially at β phases and grain boundaries, and the absorbed energy increases continuously with crack propagation.
Validation· The study validates findings through in-situ experimental measurements of crack growth.

Effect of Y2O3 content on microstructure and mechanical properties of ODS Ti–6Al–4V alloys fabricated by in situ atomization and SPS

单位: Functional Materials and Components Group, Korea Institute of Industrial Technology, Gangneung, 25440, Republic of Korea
通讯: Hyung-Ki Park @ Functional Materials and Components Group, Korea Institute of Industrial Technology, Gangneung, 25440, Republic of Korea
相关性: 该论文采用气雾化和放电等离子烧结，而非增材制造技术，与L1方向无关。
边界: The fabrication method is gas atomization and spark plasma sintering (SPS), not additive manufacturing (LPBF/SLM/EBM/DED), making it off-topic for the Ti-6Al-4V AM mechanical/fatigue focus.
动机· 研究Y2O3含量对ODS Ti-6Al-4V合金微观结构和力学性能的影响。
方法· 通过真空电弧重熔、电极感应熔炼气雾化和放电等离子烧结制备ODS Ti64合金。
结果· 1 wt% Y2O3的ODS Ti64合金实现了最佳强度、硬度和延展性平衡。
验证· 通过硬度测试和压缩试验验证力学性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Investigate the effect of Y2O3 content on the microstructure and mechanical properties of oxide dispersion strengthened (ODS) Ti-6Al-4V alloys.
Method· ODS Ti64 powders were produced via vacuum arc remelting and electrode induction melting gas atomization, then consolidated by spark plasma sintering (SPS).
Result· The ODS Ti64 alloy with 1 wt% Y2O3 exhibited optimal balance of strength, hardness, and ductility.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through hardness measurements and compression tests.

动机· 改善Ti-6Al-4V合金表面性能以适合植入物应用。
方法· 采用电火花加工(EDM)改变表面形态并测试性能。
