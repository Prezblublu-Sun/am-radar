High-temperature strength and microstructural evolution of Ti-6Al-4V alloy fabricated by wire Arc additive manufacturing under elevated temperature

Soobin Kim, Dong-Hyuck Kam, Kee‐Ahn Lee Scientific Reports 2025-12-15 10.1038/s41598-025-31895-1
单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Inha University, Incheon, 22212, Republic of Korea
通讯: Soobin Kim @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Inha University, Incheon, 22212, Republic of Korea
相关性: 该论文直接研究了WAAM Ti-6Al-4V的高温压缩力学性能，与L1层机械与疲劳性能方向高度相关。
边界: Does not cover fatigue or tensile testing; focuses on high-temperature compression behavior rather than room-temperature fatigue or standard mechanical properties.
动机· 研究WAAM Ti-6Al-4V在高温下的力学性能及微观结构演变。
方法· 对WAAM和热轧Ti-6Al-4V进行室温和300-700°C压缩试验，并分析微观结构。
结果· WAAM合金在所有温度下屈服强度均高于热轧态，700°C时出现快速流动软化。
验证· 通过压缩实验与微观表征验证了高温性能差异及微观结构演化机制。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the high-temperature compressive mechanical properties of Wire Arc Additively Manufactured Ti-6Al-4V compared to hot-rolled material.
Method· Compression tests were conducted at room temperature, 300°C, 500°C, and 700°C, along with microstructural characterization using SEM/EBSD.
Result· The WAAM alloy exhibited higher yield strength at all temperatures due to fine α-lath structure and high-angle grain boundaries, but at 700°C it experienced rapid flow softening from kinking and dynamic recrystallization.
Validation· Experimental compression testing and microstructural analysis validated the temperature-dependent strength and deformation behavior.

Study on Growth Life Prediction of Surface Crack in Metallic Materials under Nonproportional Loading

Yuichi Ono, Hiroki KAKIDA, Kaito Watanabe, Akari INOUE Journal of the Society of Materials Science Japan 2025-12-15 10.2472/jsms.74.749
单位: Department of Mechanical and Physical Engineering, Tottori University
通讯: Yuichi Ono @ Department of Mechanical and Physical Engineering, Tottori University
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的疲劳裂纹扩展实验，但主要提出一种统一预测公式，并非专门针对Ti-6Al-4V的力学性能。
边界: The paper covers Ti-6Al-4V only as one of several test materials and its main contribution is a generic crack growth prediction method, not a detailed Ti-6Al-4V mechanical/fatigue characterization.
动机· 研究非比例加载下金属材料表面裂纹的生长寿命预测方法。
方法· 对SUS304和Ti-6Al-4V进行疲劳裂纹扩展实验，并用数字图像相关测量裂纹开口位移。
结果· 提出基于σθa,max+k'τθa,max的有效应力范围公式，裂纹生长寿命预测误差约±30%。
验证· 通过两种材料的疲劳实验验证所提经验公式。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a method for predicting surface crack growth life in metallic materials under nonproportional loading conditions.
Method· Fatigue crack propagation tests were conducted on SUS304 and Ti-6Al-4V, and crack opening displacement was measured using digital image correlation to derive effective stress intensity factor ranges.
Result· An empirical formula using σθa,max + k'τθa,max with k'=1/2 uniformly correlates the effective stress range across materials, allowing crack growth life prediction within ±30% error.
Validation· The proposed formula was validated by comparing predicted and experimental crack growth lifetimes for both stainless steel and titanium alloy.

Result· Solution heat treatment at 950°C followed by air cooling and aging eliminated heterogeneous microstructure and precipitated fine β-transformed structures, enhancing strength-ductility synergy.
Validation· Tensile tests and digital image correlation (DIC) analysis were used to evaluate mechanical behavior and strain distribution.
