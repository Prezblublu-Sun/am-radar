Improving the Ductility of Electron Beam Welded Joint between Additive Manufactured and Forged Ti‐6Al‐4V Alloys through Heat Treatment

Xinyu Liu, Yuhe Tian, Weijun He, Bin Jiang Advanced Engineering Materials 2025-12-15 10.1002/adem.202502394
单位: College of Materials Science and Engineering Chongqing University Chongqing 400044 China
通讯: Weijun He @ Chongqing Key Laboratory of High‐performance Corrosion‐resistant Alloys Chongqing University Chongqing 400044 China
相关性: 该论文涉及对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V与锻件焊接接头进行热处理，虽非单独研究后处理对AM材料性能的影响，但为后处理改善力学性能提供了参考。
边界: The paper focuses on a welded joint between AM and forged Ti-6Al-4V rather than solely on post-processing of AM material, and does not include fatigue testing.
动机· 激光熔覆沉积Ti-6Al-4V部件与锻件焊接后性能不均匀，亟需改善接头塑性。
方法· 采用固溶+时效热处理调控电子束焊接头的显微组织，并通过DIC分析拉伸应变分布。
结果· 950℃固溶空冷+时效消除显微组织不均匀性，析出β转变结构提升强度-塑性协同。
验证· 通过拉伸试验和DIC应变测量验证热处理效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Welded joints between additively manufactured and forged Ti-6Al-4V exhibit heterogeneous microstructures that degrade tensile ductility, requiring post-weld heat treatment to improve performance.
Method· Post-weld solution-aging heat treatments with varying solution temperatures and cooling rates were applied to electron beam welded joints, and digital image correlation was used to map local strain distributions during tensile testing.
Result· Solution treatment at 950°C followed by air cooling and aging eliminated microstructural heterogeneity and precipitated fine β-transformed structures, enhancing strength-ductility synergy.
Validation· Tensile tests combined with digital image correlation provided quantitative validation of the heat treatment effect on mechanical properties.

Investigation of Thermal Conductivity in Advanced Metallic Materials via Computational Fluid Dynamics

Rareş AVĂDANEI, Florin-Bogdan MARIN, Mihaela MARIN The Annals of “Dunarea de Jos” University of Galati Fascicle IX Metallurgy and Materials Science 2025-12-15 10.35219/mms.2025.4.04
单位: “Dunarea de Jos” University of Galati, Romania
通讯: Rareş AVĂDANEI @ “Dunarea de Jos” University of Galati, Romania
相关性: 该论文研究Ti6Al4V合金的热传导性能，与骨科植入物股骨柄的结构设计和力学性能无关。
边界: No stem structural design, no mechanical testing, no biomechanical analysis, no fatigue or stress shielding; purely thermal conductivity simulation.
动机· 研究Ti6Al4V合金的热传导性能
方法· 采用CFD双尺度建模，结合BCC晶格和宏观实体
结果· 热传播延迟且不均匀，归因于低热导率
验证· 数值模拟，无实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the transient thermal behavior of Ti6Al4V alloy for heat dissipation applications.
Method· A dual-scale CFD model combining a BCC lattice structure and macroscopic solid domain.
Result· Heat propagation is delayed and uneven due to the alloy's low thermal conductivity of 6.5 W/m·K.
Validation· Numerical simulation without experimental validation.

Effect of process parameters on surface integrity in laser powder bed fusion of Ti-6Al-4V alloy

Sabine Le Roux, Mehdi Salem, Mohammad Shabbar, Buchibabu Vicharapu, N. Muthu et al. Scientific Reports 2025-12-15 10.1038/s41598-025-27513-9
单位: Institut Clément Ader (ICA), Université de Toulouse, CNRS, IMT Mines Albi, UPS, INSA, ISAE-SUPAERO, Campus Jarlard, Albi, 81013, France
通讯: Sabine Le Roux @ Institut Clément Ader (ICA), Université de Toulouse, CNRS, IMT Mines Albi, UPS, INSA, ISAE-SUPAERO, Campus Jarlard, Albi
相关性: 该论文研究的是增材制造工艺参数对表面完整性的影响，未涉及任何后处理工艺，因此与后处理控制方向无关。
边界: The paper focuses on the effect of LPBF process parameters on surface roughness and residual stresses in the as-built state; it does not study any post‑processing treatment (e.g., heat treatment, HIP, machining) and thus does not fall under Layer 2 post‑processing control.
动机· 研究激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V合金工艺参数对表面完整性的影响。
方法· 通过改变激光功率和扫描速度调整能量密度，测量表面粗糙度和残余应力。
结果· 高能量密度降低表面粗糙度，残余应力与表面形貌相关。
验证· 实验测量与计算结果对比验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to elucidate the evolution of surface topography and residual stresses during LPBF of Ti-6Al-4V as a function of build height and process energy input.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V samples were fabricated at volumetric energy densities from 21.8 to 65.4 J·mm⁻³ by varying laser power and scanning speed, and surface roughness and residual stresses were measured using focus variation microscopy and computed tomography.
Result· Higher energy densities resulted in stable melt pools and reduced surface roughness, and residual stresses significantly influenced surface morphology.
Validation· Computed and measured residual stresses agreed well for sound builds, validating the numerical model.
