Physics-based analytical modeling of materials properties in metal additive manufacturing

Wei Huang, Ruoqi Gao, Hamid Garmestani, Steven Y. Liang Journal of Manufacturing Processes 2025-09-23 10.1016/j.jmapro.2025.09.019
单位: George W. Woodruff School of Mechanical Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology, 801 Ferst Drive, Atlanta, 30332, GA, USA
通讯: Wei Huang @ George W. Woodruff School of Mechanical Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology, 801 Ferst Drive, Atlanta, 30332, G  |  Steven Y. Liang @ George W. Woodruff School of Mechanical Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology, 801 Ferst Drive, Atlanta, 30332, G
相关性: 该论文直接针对激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V的力学性能（弹性模量、屈服强度）进行物理建模与实验验证，非常适合Layer 1的关注点。
边界: Paper does not cover fatigue life or S-N curves, which are part of the direction's scope but not mandatory for Layer 1.
动机· 为预测激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V的微观结构依赖力学性能建立物理建模框架。
方法· 集成热模型、JMAK动力学、CET准则及自洽模型，预测晶粒尺寸、织构及各向异性弹性模量。
结果· 预测弹性模量109-117 GPa，屈服强度850-900 MPa，实验验证良好且对层/行参数不敏感。
验证· 通过熔池尺寸、极图强度及力学性能实验数据验证模型。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a computationally efficient physics-based modeling framework for predicting microstructure-dependent mechanical properties of LPBF Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· The framework integrates a 3D thermal model, JMAK kinetics, CET criteria, Bunge texture calculation, and a self-consistent model for anisotropic elastic constants and Hall–Petch yield strength.
Result· Predicted elastic modulus (109–117 GPa) and yield strength (850–900 MPa) agree well with experimental ranges (100–140 GPa and 850–1050 MPa), showing stability across processing parameters.
Validation· The thermal model, texture pole figures, and mechanical properties are validated against experimental measurements.

Numerical investigation of the effect of defect population on the fatigue strength anisotropy of alloys fabricated by L-PBF

Sai Sreenivas Penkulinti, Nicolas Saintier, Matthieu Bonneric, Benoît Verquin, Thierry Palin‐Luc et al. International Journal of Fatigue 2025-09-23 10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2025.109294
单位: Arts et Metiers Institute of Technology, CNRS, Bordeaux INP, I2M, UMR 5295, F-33400 Talence, France
通讯: Nicolas Saintier @ Arts et Metiers Institute of Technology, CNRS, Bordeaux INP, I2M, UMR 5295, F-33400 Talence, France
相关性: 该论文直接研究了L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V合金中缺陷对疲劳强度各向异性的影响，基于有限元模拟，完全符合L1方向。
边界: It does not provide new experimental data; it relies on literature for validation.
动机· 研究L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V合金缺陷对疲劳强度各向异性的影响。
方法· 采用有限元模拟，分析熔合缺陷和气体孔隙在多轴比例加载下的作用。
结果· 疲劳强度对缺陷取向高度敏感，各向异性在拉伸加载下尤为明显。
验证· 与文献实验数据对比验证了数值结果的准确性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how defect populations affect the fatigue strength anisotropy of L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V under multiaxial loading.
Method· Finite element simulations were performed for two defect types (lack of fusion and gas pores) under proportional multiaxial loading.
Result· Fatigue strength is highly dependent on defect orientation and morphology, with higher anisotropy under tensile loading and for LoF defects.
Validation· Numerical fatigue strength anisotropy was validated against experimental data from the literature.

Design Methods Used to Minimize Residual Stresses in Direct Laser Deposition

S.Yu. Ivanov Труды Санкт-Петербургского государственного морского технического университета 2025-09-23 10.52899/24141437_2025_03_375
单位: Institute of Laser and Welding Technologies of the Saint Petersburg State Marine Technical University
通讯: S.Yu. Ivanov @ Institute of Laser and Welding Technologies of the Saint Petersburg State Marine Technical University
相关性: 该论文对Ti-6Al-4V直激光沉积残余应力的模拟与减少方法有参考价值，但缺少实验机械性能验证。
边界: No experimental validation of the proposed methods; results are purely numerical without mechanical property measurements such as fatigue or tensile data.
动机· 针对Ti-6Al-4V直激光沉积残余应力问题，提出几何与成分改性方法。
方法· 通过有限元模拟不同端部圆角及纯钛过渡层的残应力与塑性应变。
结果· 凹形圆角和软夹层能有效减少塑性变形，避免开裂。
验证· 数值模拟验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To mitigate residual stresses and deformations in direct laser deposited Ti-6Al-4V large structures.
Method· A finite element model simulates residual stresses and plastic strains in deposits with flat/concave fillets or a pure titanium gradient interlayer.
Result· Concave fillets and a soft interlayer significantly reduce plastic deformation, especially in the stronger alloy region.
Validation· The study relies on numerical simulation without experimental confirmation.

Corrosion-Resistant Plasma Electrolytic Oxidation Composite Coatings on Ti6Al4V for Harsh Acidic Environments

Michael Garashchenko, Yuliy Yuferov, Konstantin Borodianskiy Journal of Composites Science 2025-09-23 10.3390/jcs9100515
单位: Department of Chemical Engineering, Ariel University, Ariel 40700, Israel
通讯: Konstantin Borodianskiy @ Department of Chemical Engineering, Ariel University, Ariel 40700, Israel
相关性: 该论文研究Ti6Al4V的PEO涂层耐腐蚀性能，不涉及增材制造或力学/疲劳性能。
边界: Paper does not address additive manufacturing (LPBF/SLM/EBM/DED) nor mechanical/fatigue properties of Ti-6Al-4V; it focuses on corrosion resistance of coatings for acidic environments.
动机· 提升Ti6Al4V在强酸环境中的耐腐蚀性
方法· 通过等离子体电解氧化在Ti6Al4V上制备复合陶瓷涂层
结果· 磷酸盐电解液加氧化铝纳米颗粒的涂层耐蚀性最佳
验证· 电化学测试表征腐蚀电流密度和极化电阻
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the corrosion resistance of Ti6Al4V alloy in harsh acidic environments relevant to energy storage systems.
Method· Composite ceramic coatings were synthesized on Ti6Al4V via plasma electrolytic oxidation using silicate-, phosphate-, and sulfate-based electrolytes with and without α-alumina nanoparticles.
Result· Coatings produced in phosphate electrolyte with Al2O3 nanoparticles exhibited the highest polarization resistance (113 kΩ·cm2) and lowest corrosion current density (143 nA/cm2) in 5 M H2SO4.
Validation· Corrosion performance was validated through electrochemical polarization and impedance measurements in concentrated sulfuric acid.
