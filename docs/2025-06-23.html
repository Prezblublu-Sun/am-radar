Mechanical Properties and Material Characteristics of 3D-Printed Titanium Capsules for Cancer Drug Delivery Applications

K. Kazimierska-Drobny, Grzegorz Szala, Janusz Musiał, Marek Macko, Tomasz Karasiewicz et al. Materials 2025-06-23 10.3390/ma18132969
单位: Faculty of Mechatronics, Kazimierz Wielki University, Mikolaj Kopernik 1 Street, 85-074 Bydgoszcz, Poland
通讯: K. Kazimierska-Drobny @ Faculty of Mechatronics, Kazimierz Wielki University, Mikolaj Kopernik 1 Street, 85-074 Bydgoszcz, Poland
相关性: 该论文针对Ti-6Al-4V合金的DMLS增材制造件进行了压缩强度和表面粗糙度的实验研究，虽然应用为药物胶囊，但力学数据对基础力学性能有参考价值。
边界: No fatigue testing or tensile properties; focus on drug delivery capsules rather than standard structural mechanical characterization.
动机· 评估3D打印钛合金胶囊作为抗癌药物载体的力学和材料性能。
方法· 通过DMLS制备Ti-6Al-4V胶囊，测试不同打印角度和几何参数的压缩强度及表面粗糙度。
结果· 45°打印的胶囊压缩强度最高，且损伤后无尖锐边缘，表面粗糙度有利于组织整合。
验证· 对36个胶囊进行压缩实验，并用t检验进行统计分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study aims to evaluate the mechanical and material properties of 3D-printed titanium capsules for potential use as anticancer drug carriers.
Method· Capsules made of Ti-6Al-4V via DMLS were tested for compressive strength and surface roughness under different printing angles (0°, 45°, 90°) and loading directions.
Result· Capsules printed at 45° exhibit the highest compressive strength, and damage does not produce sharp edges, while high surface roughness promotes tissue integration.
Validation· Compression tests were performed on 36 capsules, and statistical analysis used Student's t-test.

The Impact of As-Built Surface Characteristics of Selective-Laser-Melted Ti-6Al-4V on Early Osteoblastic Response for Potential Dental Applications

Muhammad Hassan Razzaq, Olugbenga Ayeni, Selin Köklü, Kagan Berk, Usama Zaheer et al. Journal of Functional Biomaterials 2025-06-23 10.3390/jfb16070230
单位: Additive Manufacturing Technologies Application and Research Center—EKTAM, Gazi University, Ankara 06560, Türkiye
通讯: Tayebeh Ameri @ Chair for Composite Materials, Department of Materials Science, Faculty of Engineering, Kiel University, Kaiserstraße 2,  |  Sinan Şen @ Department of Orthodontics, University Hospital Schleswig Holstein, Arnold-Heller-Strasse 3, 24105 Kiel, Germany
相关性: 该论文研究SLM工艺参数对Ti-6Al-4V表面特性的影响，未涉及任何后处理工艺，且关注生物响应而非力学性能，与后处理控制方向无关。
边界: This paper does not study any post-processing treatment (heat treatment, HIP, machining, peening, etc.) nor does it report mechanical performance (fatigue, tensile). It focuses on as-built surface tailoring via process parameters for biological response.
动机· 探索SLM直接调控Ti-6Al-4V表面特性以替代后处理用于牙科植入物
方法· 通过改变激光扫描速度调节体积能量密度实现多尺度表面形貌
结果· 中等能量密度下获得双尺度粗糙度和亲水性，促进成骨细胞增殖
验证· 通过MG-63细胞培养验证表面生物活性
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to tailor surface characteristics of SLM Ti-6Al-4V by adjusting process parameters to eliminate post-processing for dental implants.
Method· Volumetric energy density was controlled via laser scanning speed to create dual-scale surface roughness and alter hydrophilicity.
Result· Moderate VED (50-100 W·mm3/s) produced macro-roughness 25.5-27.6 µm and micro-roughness 58.8-64.2 nm, reducing water contact angle to ~62° and enhancing osteoblast proliferation 1.5-fold.
Validation· Biological assays with MG-63 cells confirmed improved early cellular responses compared to machined Ti-6Al-4V.

Tailored Cutting Fluid Supply in Additively Manufactured Milling Tools for Aerospace Applications

Tobias Kelliger, Markus Meurer, Thomas Bergs 2025-06-23 10.1115/msec2025-151844
单位: RWTH Aachen University , ,
通讯: Tobias Kelliger @ RWTH Aachen University , ,
相关性: 本文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的加工，但并非关于AM Ti-6Al-4V的后处理研究。
边界: No post-processing treatment of AM Ti-6Al-4V is investigated; the paper focuses on cutting tool design for machining Ti-6Al-4V, not on modifying the material itself.
动机· 提高钛合金加工的刀具寿命和工艺可靠性。
方法· 利用激光粉末床熔融制造带有内部冷却通道的铣刀，并设计不同喷嘴几何形状。
结果· 多出口喷嘴设计显著提高刀具寿命，优于传统刀具。
验证· 通过粗糙铣削实验测量刀具磨损、切削力和切屑形状。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve tool life and process reliability in machining titanium alloys by optimizing cutting fluid supply.
Method· Laser powder bed fusion was used to fabricate indexable milling tools with internal cooling channels, and various nozzle geometries were systematically designed and tested.
Result· Multiple outlet nozzles on the rake face enhanced convective cooling and reduced chip size, significantly increasing tool life over conventional tools.
Validation· Experimental rough milling of Ti-6Al-4V was conducted with measurements of tool wear, cutting forces, and chip morphology, supported by CFD simulations.

相关性: 该论文研究与钛合金加工刀具相关，而非钛合金后处理工艺，因此与用户方向无关。

English summary & terms
Motivation· Develop a sustainable cutting tool for machining Ti-6Al-4V by incorporating internal cooling.
Method· A turning tool with an integrated vortex tube for internal cooling was designed and experimentally tested.
Result· The internally cooled tool reduced cutting temperature and improved tool life, contributing to sustainable machining.
Validation· Experimental machining tests validated the cooling performance and sustainability benefits.
