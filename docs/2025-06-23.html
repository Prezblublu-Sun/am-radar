Mechanical Properties and Material Characteristics of 3D-Printed Titanium Capsules for Cancer Drug Delivery Applications

K. Kazimierska-Drobny, Grzegorz Szala, Janusz Musiał, Marek Macko, Tomasz Karasiewicz et al. Materials 2025-06-23 10.3390/ma18132969
单位: Faculty of Mechatronics, Kazimierz Wielki University, Mikolaj Kopernik 1 Street, 85-074 Bydgoszcz, Poland
通讯: K. Kazimierska-Drobny @ Faculty of Mechatronics, Kazimierz Wielki University, Mikolaj Kopernik 1 Street, 85-074 Bydgoszcz, Poland
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的DMLS工艺和压缩性能，但主要聚焦于药物递送胶囊的应用，未涉及疲劳性能，与用户关注的方向关联度较低。
边界: No fatigue data; focus is on drug delivery capsules rather than base mechanical/fatigue characterization of Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 评估3D打印钛胶囊作为抗癌药物载体的力学与材料特性
方法· 通过DMLS制备Ti-6Al-4V胶囊，测试不同打印角度（0°,45°,90°）下的压缩强度并分析表面粗糙度
结果· 45°打印角度下胶囊压缩强度最高，且强度远高于人骨组织，损伤后无尖锐边缘
验证· 对36个胶囊进行压缩实验，结合SEM和粗糙度分析进行统计验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the mechanical and material properties of 3D-printed titanium capsules as potential carriers for anticancer drugs.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V capsules were fabricated by DMLS with printing angles of 0°, 45°, and 90°, and their compressive strength, surface roughness, and damage behavior were characterized.
Result· Capsules printed at 45° exhibited the highest compressive strength, significantly exceeding human bone tissue values, with no sharp edges or fragments after failure.
Validation· Validation was performed through compressive testing of 36 capsules with statistical analysis (Student's t-test) and SEM/roughness evaluation.

Ultraprecision Machining of Additively Manufactured Ti-5Al-5Mo-5V-3Cr Alloy for Biomedical Applications

Laymin Hoe, Yunfa Guo, Y. Lee, Kevin Lizarraga, David Yan 2025-06-23 10.1115/msec2025-151281
单位: National University of Singapore , ,
通讯: Laymin Hoe @ National University of Singapore , ,
相关性: 该论文研究的是Ti-5553合金的超精密加工，与Ti-6Al-4V股骨柄结构设计无关。
边界: The paper does not address Ti-6Al-4V femoral stem design, porous architecture, stress shielding, or stem fatigue; it focuses on machinability of a different alloy (Ti-5553) and surface finishing.
动机· 改善β稳定钛合金植入物表面质量以促进骨整合
方法· 对激光粉末床熔融制备的Ti-5553进行超精密加工，研究切削深度影响
结果· 后刀面磨损显著，切削力逐渐增大，表面粗糙度随切削距离增加而恶化
验证· 通过切削实验测量刀具磨损、切削力、切屑形貌和表面粗糙度
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve surface quality of β-stabilized titanium alloys for better osseointegration in biomedical implants.
Method· Ultraprecision machining experiments were conducted on laser powder bed fusion (LPBF)-built Ti-5Al-5Mo-5V-3Cr (Ti-5553) alloy at varying depths of cut.
Result· Flank wear dominated over rake wear; cutting and thrust forces increased gradually; surface roughness increased with cutting distance due to tool wear.
Validation· Experimental validation through measurement of tool wear, cutting forces, chip morphology, and surface roughness.

The Impact of As-Built Surface Characteristics of Selective-Laser-Melted Ti-6Al-4V on Early Osteoblastic Response for Potential Dental Applications

Muhammad Hassan Razzaq, Olugbenga Ayeni, Selin Köklü, Kagan Berk, Usama Zaheer et al. Journal of Functional Biomaterials 2025-06-23 10.3390/jfb16070230
单位: Additive Manufacturing Technologies Application and Research Center—EKTAM, Gazi University, Ankara 06560, Türkiye
通讯: Tayebeh Ameri @ Chair for Composite Materials, Department of Materials Science, Faculty of Engineering, Kiel University, Kaiserstraße 2,  |  Sinan Şen @ Department of Orthodontics, University Hospital Schleswig Holstein, Arnold-Heller-Strasse 3, 24105 Kiel, Germany
相关性: 论文研究的是选区激光熔化Ti-6Al-4V的原始表面特性对成骨细胞响应的影响，不涉及任何后处理工艺，与方向需求不符。
边界: No post-processing treatment studied; paper investigates as-built surfaces and biological response for dental applications, not mechanical performance after post-processing.
动机· 通过SLM工艺参数直接调控Ti-6Al-4V表面形貌，避免后处理，用于牙科种植体。
方法· 改变激光扫描速度调节体能量密度，制备不同粗糙度的SLM样品。
结果· 中等VED样品具有双尺度粗糙度，亲水性增强，促进MG-63细胞增殖和铺展。
验证· MG-63成骨细胞体外培养实验验证生物相容性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To eliminate post-processing by tailoring the surface characteristics of SLM Ti-6Al-4V through process parameters for dental implant applications.
Method· Volumetric energy density was varied by adjusting laser scanning speed to produce dual-scale surface roughness on SLM samples.
Result· Moderate VED samples exhibited optimized dual-scale roughness, enhanced hydrophilicity, and improved MG-63 osteoblast proliferation and cytoskeletal organization.
Validation· In vitro biological assays with MG-63 cells confirmed enhanced early cellular responses on as-built SLM surfaces.
