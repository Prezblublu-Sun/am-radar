Tian‐Hao Ma, Yuxin Wang, Le Chang, Wei Zhang, Jianping Zhao et al. Materials 2025-11-11 10.3390/ma18225122
单位: School of Mechanical and Power Engineering, Nanjing Tech University, Nanjing 211816, China
通讯: Changyu Zhou @ School of Mechanical and Power Engineering, Nanjing Tech University, Nanjing 211816, China
相关性: 该论文直接针对L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V的多轴疲劳行为，包含四种构建方向和多轴非比例加载，符合L1方向需求。
边界: The paper focuses on multiaxial low-cycle fatigue under non-proportional loading, which is a specific subset of fatigue behavior; it does not cover high-cycle fatigue or tensile properties extensively.
动机· 研究L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V在多轴非比例加载下的疲劳行为及构建方向影响。

结果· VAE-ANN模型结合宏微观特征将寿命预测误差控制在10%以内。
验证· 通过多轴低周疲劳实验验证模型预测准确性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the multiaxial low-cycle fatigue behavior of L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V under proportional and non-proportional loading with varying building directions.
Method· Strain-controlled multiaxial fatigue tests were conducted for four building directions; fatigue life was predicted using FS, KBMP, and a hybrid VAE-ANN model incorporating macro-micro features.
Result· The VAE-ANN model achieved high accuracy with life predictions within 10% error, outperforming analytical models and capturing building direction effects.
Validation· Experimental multiaxial low-cycle fatigue tests validated the predictive models, with fracture surface characterization supporting failure mechanisms.

通讯: Md. Bengir Ahmed Shuvho @ School of Electrical and Mechanical Engineering, The University of Adelaide, Adelaide, SA, 5005, Australia

Effect of chromium addition on the microstructure and mechanical properties of laser cladding Ti-Al-SiC composite coatings

Tao Liu, Yunwei Guo, Jingfa Lei, Chen Lu, Lu Wang et al. Scientific Reports 2025-11-11 10.1038/s41598-025-23071-2
单位: School of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, Anhui Jianzhu University, Hefei, 230601, China
通讯: Tao Liu @ School of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, Anhui Jianzhu University, Hefei, 230601, China
相关性: 论文研究的是激光熔覆Ti-Al-SiC-Crx涂层，而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V本身的力学疲劳性能，因此与用户关注的方向关联性较弱。
边界: The paper does not study the mechanical or fatigue performance of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V itself; instead, it focuses on a composite coating deposited on Ti-6Al-4V, with no data on the base material's fatigue life, anisotropy, defects, or post-processing effects relevant to the direction.
动机· 研究Cr含量对激光熔覆Ti-Al-SiC涂层微观结构和力学性能的影响。
方法· 在Ti-6Al-4V基体上激光熔覆不同Cr含量的涂层，通过XRD、SEM、EBSD表征，进行压缩和磨损测试。
结果· Cr含量增加促进Ti4Cr/Cr2AlC析出，细化晶粒，提高屈服强度、峰值应力和显微硬度，但摩擦系数增大。
验证· 实验验证包括XRD、SEM、EBSD、压缩测试、纳米压痕和磨损测试。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of chromium addition on the microstructure and mechanical properties of laser-cladded Ti-Al-SiC composite coatings on Ti-6Al-4V substrate.
Method· Ti-Al-SiC-Crx coatings with varying Cr content were fabricated by laser cladding and characterized using XRD, SEM, EBSD, compression tests, nanoindentation, and wear tests.
Result· Increasing Cr content promotes precipitation of Ti4Cr and Cr2AlC, refines grain size, and enhances yield strength, peak stress, and microhardness, but increases the friction coefficient.
Validation· Experimental validation through microstructural characterization, compression testing, nanoindentation, and wear testing.

Tribological compatibility of cost-effective TiAlVN coatings deposited using Ti6Al4V alloy targets

Junfeng Shen, Bi Wu, Peng Wang, Huan Zhao, Deli Duan Surface Science and Technology 2025-11-11 10.1007/s44251-025-00106-4
单位: Institute of Metal Research, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Shenyang, China
通讯: Junfeng Shen @ Institute of Metal Research, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Shenyang, China
相关性: 该论文研究的是TiAlVN涂层，并非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学或疲劳性能，与研究方向无关。
边界: The paper focuses on TiAlVN coatings deposited via sputtering, not on the mechanical or fatigue performance of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V (LPBF/SLM/EBM/DED).
动机· 探索低成本Ti6Al4V靶材制备TiAlVN涂层的可行性及摩擦学性能。
方法· 反应磁控溅射沉积TiAlVN涂层，测试其硬度和与四种对偶材料的摩擦学性能。
结果· 涂层硬度30.8 GPa，与WC-Co对磨时磨损率最低，建立了摩擦学兼容性预测模型。
验证· 通过实验和外部数据验证模型，R²=0.94。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the tribological performance and cost-effectiveness of TiAlVN coatings deposited using Ti6Al4V alloy targets instead of conventional sintered targets.
Method· TiAlVN coatings were deposited by reactive magnetron sputtering, and their hardness and wear behavior were tested against four different counterpart materials.
Result· The coating achieved a hardness of 30.8 GPa, with the lowest wear rate against WC-Co, and a predictive model for tribological compatibility was developed.
Validation· The model was validated against experimental data and external datasets, achieving an R² of 0.94.

Validation· Numerical simulation with assumed parameters.
