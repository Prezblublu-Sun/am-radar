相关性: 该论文针对Ti-6Al-4V ELI的疲劳寿命预测建立了多尺度有限元框架，直接符合方向要求。

English summary & terms

Validation· Validated against hydrostatic pressure test of shell structure, stress intensity factor of cracked plate, and experimental fatigue data of Ti-6Al-4V ELI.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how different laser beam profiles (Gaussian, core-ring, ring-dominated) affect porosity and tensile response of PBF-LB/M Ti-6Al-4V at room and elevated temperatures.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of shot peening on the tribological performance of additively manufactured Ti6Al4V alloy.
Method· Shot peening with ceramic beads and CrNi steel shots was applied to DMLS-fabricated Ti6Al4V, and wear behavior was evaluated using ball-on-disc tests with an Al2O3 counterbody under dry sliding conditions.
Result· Shot peening increased surface hardness by up to 42% and reduced roughness, leading to lower friction coefficients and wear rates, with distinct wear mechanisms depending on peening media.
Validation· Validation was performed through SEM and EDS analyses correlating wear mechanisms with phase redistribution.
