Multi-Scale Finite Element Simulation Framework for Deformation and Damage of Large Structure Under Complex Loadings

Cheng Li, Chengqi Sun Materials 2026-07-01 10.3390/ma19132800
单位: State Key Laboratory of Nonlinear Mechanics, Institute of Mechanics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100190, China
通讯: Chengqi Sun @ School of Engineering Science, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100049, China
相关性: 该论文针对Ti-6Al-4V ELI的疲劳寿命预测建立了多尺度有限元框架，直接符合方向要求。
边界: No as-built mechanical property data reported; validation uses published experimental results.
动机· 开发适用于大型结构复杂载荷下变形与损伤分析的多尺度仿真框架
方法· 建立嵌套子模型有限元框架，从宏观至微观逐级传递位移解，结合晶体塑性方法和疲劳指示参数模型
结果· 壳体和裂纹板应力应变误差小于5%，Ti-6Al-4V ELI剪切疲劳寿命预测误差低于10%
验证· 通过壳体静压实验和含裂纹板应力强度因子计算验证，并与Ti-6Al-4V ELI疲劳实验数据对比
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a multi-scale simulation framework for deformation and damage analysis of large structures under complex loadings.
Method· A nested sub-modeling finite element framework is established, transferring displacement solutions from global to local scales, integrated with crystal plasticity and a fatigue indicator parameter model.
Result· Stress and strain errors in shell and cracked plate are within 5%, and predicted shear fatigue lives of Ti-6Al-4V ELI agree with experiments within 10% error.
Validation· Validated against hydrostatic pressure test of shell structure, stress intensity factor of cracked plate, and experimental fatigue data of Ti-6Al-4V ELI.

Effect of open-loop and closed-loop in-situ process control on microstructure and mechanical properties of L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V

Md Shafiqur Rahman Jame, Ankita Roy, Dang Toan Truong, Haojun You, Abdulaziz Alfaifi et al. Journal of Manufacturing Processes 2026-07-01 10.1016/j.jmapro.2026.06.071
相关性: 该论文直接研究了L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V的工艺控制（开环与闭环）对微观结构和力学性能的影响，符合L1方向对机械性能的关注。
边界: The paper does not explicitly address fatigue performance, but it is still relevant as it covers tensile properties and microstructure which are part of L1 focus.
动机· 研究开环与闭环原位过程控制对L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V微观结构和力学性能的影响。
方法· 对比开环和闭环两种控制策略，进行L-PBF成型，然后表征微观结构和力学性能。
结果· 闭环控制能显著改善微观结构均匀性和力学性能（如强度、延展性）。
验证· 通过拉伸测试、硬度测试和显微组织观察进行实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of open-loop versus closed-loop in-situ process control on the microstructure and mechanical properties of L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Two control strategies were implemented during L-PBF, followed by microstructural characterization and mechanical testing (tensile and hardness).
Result· Closed-loop control improved microstructural homogeneity and enhanced mechanical properties including strength and ductility.
Validation· Experimental validation through tensile tests, hardness measurements, and microscopy analysis.

Structural and Morphological Analysis of Shot Peening Effects on Tribological Wear of DMLS‐Manufactured Ti6Al4V Titanium Alloy

Wojciech Okuniewski, Mariusz Walczak, David Vališ, Kamil Pasierbiewicz physica status solidi (RRL) - Rapid Research Letters 2026-07-01 10.1002/pssr.70218
单位: Department of Materials Engineering Faculty of Mechanical Engineering Lublin University of Technology Lublin Poland
通讯: Wojciech Okuniewski @ Department of Materials Engineering Faculty of Mechanical Engineering Lublin University of Technology Lublin Poland  |  Mariusz Walczak @ Department of Materials Engineering Faculty of Mechanical Engineering Lublin University of Technology Lublin Poland
相关性: 该论文研究了喷丸处理对DMLS制造Ti6Al4V合金表面硬度、粗糙度和摩擦磨损性能的影响，虽然未直接针对疲劳性能，但喷丸作为后处理工艺对表面状态的改善与疲劳性能密切相关。
边界: Does not include fatigue or tensile testing; focuses on tribological wear rather than the tensile/fatigue properties emphasized in the direction focus.
动机· 研究喷丸处理对增材制造Ti6Al4V合金摩擦磨损性能的影响
方法· 采用陶瓷珠和CrNi钢珠对DMLS制备的Ti6Al4V进行喷丸，通过球盘摩擦试验评估耐磨性
结果· 喷丸使表面硬度提高约42%，粗糙度降低，摩擦系数和磨损率显著下降，且不同喷丸介质导致不同的磨损机制
验证· 通过SEM和EDS分析验证喷丸诱导的相相关磨损机制
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of shot peening on the tribological performance of additively manufactured Ti6Al4V alloy.
Method· Shot peening with ceramic beads and CrNi steel shots was applied to DMLS-fabricated Ti6Al4V, and wear behavior was evaluated using ball-on-disc tests with an Al2O3 counterbody under dry sliding conditions.
Result· Shot peening increased surface hardness by up to 42% and reduced roughness, leading to lower friction coefficients and wear rates, with distinct wear mechanisms depending on peening media.
Validation· Validation was performed through SEM and EDS analyses correlating wear mechanisms with phase redistribution.

Strength and corrosion behavior of Zn/Ti-6Al-4V interpenetrating phase composites: Effect of heat treatment of the LPBF Ti-6Al-4V

Ramavath Suman, D. Mohanta, D. Punera, M. Ramakrishna, Srikant Gollapudi Materialia 2026-07-01 10.1016/j.mtla.2026.102816
相关性: 涉及Ti-6Al-4V的热处理，但主要研究的是复合材料而非单独Ti-6Al-4V的疲劳/力学性能。
边界: Focuses on strength and corrosion behavior of Zn/Ti-6Al-4V composite, not on fatigue or mechanical performance of Ti-6Al-4V alone after post-processing.
动机· 研究热处理对LPBF Ti-6Al-4V基Zn复合材料的强度与腐蚀行为的影响。
方法· LPBF制备Ti-6Al-4V骨架经热处理后浸渗Zn形成互穿相复合材料。
结果· 热处理改变了Ti-6Al-4V的微观结构，进而影响复合材料的力学和腐蚀性能。
验证· 通过拉伸测试和腐蚀实验验证材料性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates how heat treatment of LPBF Ti-6Al-4V affects the mechanical strength and corrosion behavior of Zn/Ti-6Al-4V interpenetrating phase composites.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V scaffolds were fabricated by LPBF, heat-treated under different conditions, and then infiltrated with Zn to form interpenetrating phase composites.
Result· The heat treatment altered the Ti-6Al-4V microstructure, which significantly influenced the composite's tensile strength and corrosion resistance.
Validation· Mechanical testing (tensile) and corrosion experiments were performed to validate the material properties.
