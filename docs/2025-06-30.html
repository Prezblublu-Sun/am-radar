Effect of Support Structure on Residual Stress Distribution in Ti-6Al-4V Alloy Fabricated by Laser Powder Bed Fusion

Seung-yeon Lee, Haeum Park, Min Jae Baek, Dong Jun Lee, Jae Wung Bae et al. Hanguk bunmal jaeryo hakoeji 2025-06-30 10.4150/jpm.2025.00087
相关性: 该论文研究了支撑结构对激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V合金残余应力的影响，与L1方向的机械性能与残余应力主题高度相关。
边界: The paper does not provide extensive fatigue life or S-N data, focusing instead on residual stress distribution due to support structure design.
动机· 研究支撑结构设计对激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V合金残余应力的影响，以减少变形。
方法· 通过不同支撑结构设计制备样品，测量残余应力和力学性能。
结果· 支撑结构显著影响残余应力分布，优化设计可降低应力。
验证· 实验测量残余应力和力学性能验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of support structure design on residual stress in LPBF Ti-6Al-4V to minimize distortion.
Method· Samples with different support structures were fabricated and residual stress and mechanical properties were measured.
Result· Support structure design significantly affects residual stress distribution; optimized designs reduce residual stress.
Validation· Experimental characterization of residual stress and mechanical properties.

Finite element analysis of hip implant using additive manufacturing materials in different loading conditions

Abdulla All Noman, M. S. Shaari, Mohd Akramin Mohd Romlay, Kim Seah Tan JOURNAL OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING AND SCIENCES 2025-06-30 10.15282/jmes.19.2.2025.6.0834
单位: Faculty of Mechanical and Automotive Engineering Technology, Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah 26600, Pekan, Pahang, Malaysia. Phone: +6094315366
通讯: Abdulla All Noman @ Faculty of Mechanical and Automotive Engineering Technology, Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah 26600, Pekan,
相关性: 该论文主要研究髋关节植入物的有限元分析，而非Ti-6Al-4V增材制造的基础力学与疲劳性能。
边界: This paper focuses on hip implant design and FEA, not on base mechanical or fatigue characterization of Ti-6Al-4V AM material.
动机· 通过有限元分析评估基于增材制造的髋关节植入物在多种载荷下的力学性能。
方法· 使用CAD设计植入物并在Ansys中对Ti-6Al-4V材料进行四种载荷下的应力应变分析。
结果· 坐、站、走、跑四种载荷下最大变形和等效应力均小于材料屈服强度，设计安全。
验证· 仿真案例验证，无实验数据。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the mechanical performance of an additively manufactured hip implant under various physiological loading conditions using finite element analysis.
Method· A CAD model of the hip implant was analyzed in Ansys using Ti-6Al-4V material properties under four loading conditions (sitting, standing, walking, jogging).
Result· The maximum deformation, von Mises stress, and shear stress were highest during jogging but remained below the material's yield and shear strengths, indicating a safe design.
Validation· The study is a simulation case without experimental validation.

Machining of Micro Holes In Titanium Alloy By Electro Discharge Machining

Anuj, Hari Parshad - International Journal of Leading Research Publication. 2025-06-30 10.70528/ijlrp.v6.i6.1634
相关性: 该论文研究的是钛合金电火花加工微孔，并非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的后处理对疲劳/力学性能的影响。
边界: The paper concerns conventional machining of Ti-6Al-4V, not post-processing of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V, and does not evaluate mechanical performance (fatigue, tensile).
动机· 研究电火花加工参数对钛合金微孔质量的影响。
方法· 采用Taguchi L9正交阵列实验，使用Cu-W复合电极。
结果· Cu-W电极降低电极损耗30%，双策略加工减少锥度并改善圆度。
验证· 通过SEM和显微分析验证加工质量。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the influence of EDM parameters on the quality of micro holes in Ti-6Al-4V using a Cu-W composite electrode.
Method· A Taguchi L9 orthogonal array was employed to systematically vary pulse current, on-time, off-time, voltage, and flushing pressure.
Result· The Cu-W electrode reduced tool wear rate by 30%, and a two-pass strategy decreased taper from 1.4° to 0.8° and improved roundness.
Validation· Microscopic and SEM analyses confirmed recast layer thickness and crack formation trends.
