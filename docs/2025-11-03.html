Effect of Laser Surface Melting on the Microstructure and Corrosion Resistance of Laser Powder Bed Fusion and Wrought Ti-6Al-4V Alloys

A. Lekatou, Vaia Sarika, Bohdan Efremenko, Yu. G. Chabak, V. G. Efremenko et al. Coatings 2025-11-03 10.3390/coatings15111285
单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering, University of Ioannina, 45110 Ioannina, Greece
通讯: V. G. Efremenko @ Institute of Materials Research, Slovak Academy of Sciences, 04001 Kosice, Slovakia
相关性: 该论文主要研究激光表面熔化对Ti-6Al-4V腐蚀行为的影响，而非力学或疲劳性能，与用户方向关联度低。
边界: No mechanical fatigue data, no tensile properties, no S-N curve; main focus is corrosion not mechanical performance.
动机· 研究激光表面熔化对LPBF和锻造Ti-6Al-4V腐蚀行为的影响
方法· 对LPBF和锻造试样进行激光表面熔化处理，表征微观结构并测试在模拟体液中的腐蚀性能
结果· 激光表面熔化提高了显微硬度但降低了耐腐蚀性，归因于表面粗糙度增加和马氏体形成
验证· 通过显微硬度测试和电化学腐蚀实验进行验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study investigates how laser surface melting affects the corrosion behavior of additively manufactured and wrought Ti-6Al-4V in simulated body fluid.
Method· Laser surface melting was applied to both LPBF and wrought Ti-6Al-4V, followed by microstructural characterization and corrosion tests in simulated body fluid at 37°C.
Result· LSM increased microhardness to 655–680 HV due to lattice expansion and dislocation density, but reduced corrosion resistance due to increased surface roughness and martensite formation.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed through microhardness measurements and electrochemical corrosion tests including cyclic polarisation.

An enhanced characteristic time-based heat input model for simulating laser heating in additive manufacturing

Mahsa Heidari, Louis N.S. Chiu, Ming Liu, Aijun Huang, Bernard Rolfe et al. Journal of Materials Processing Technology 2025-11-03 10.1016/j.jmatprotec.2025.119129
单位: Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Monash University, Clayton VIC3800, Australia
通讯: Wenyi Yan @ Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Monash University, Clayton VIC3800, Australia
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V增材制造的仿真模型，但核心是热输入模型而非力学或疲劳性能表征。
边界: This paper focuses on a computational heat input model for thermal simulation of DED, not on mechanical/fatigue characterization or modeling of Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 提高增材制造热模拟的计算效率
方法· 通过体积平均曝光时间改进特征时间热输入模型
结果· 温度历史误差降至2.6%，计算时间仅为Goldak模型的23%
验证· 通过实验温度、残余应力和变形数据验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve computational efficiency while maintaining accuracy in thermal simulation of additive manufacturing.
Method· Enhanced the characteristic time-based heat input model by volume-averaging laser exposure time over the semi-elliptical cylinder.
Result· Achieved temperature history errors of 2.6% (thin-wall) and 7.6% (cone), with runtime reduced to 23% and 19.3% of the Goldak model respectively.
Validation· Validated against experimental temperature, residual stress (contour method), and distortion measurements.

Antimicrobial Sol–Gel Glassy Surfaces for Modification of Dental Implant Abutments to Reduce Microbial Adhesion

Özlem Çölgeçen, Murat Akarsu, Esin Akarsu, Ataç Uzel, Feyzan Özdal Kurt et al. Gels 2025-11-03 10.3390/gels11110882
单位: Department of Prosthodontics, Faculty of Dentistry, İzmir Katip Çlebi University, 35640 Izmir, Türkiye
通讯: Özlem Çölgeçen @ Department of Prosthodontics, Faculty of Dentistry, İzmir Katip Çlebi University, 35640 Izmir, Türkiye
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti6Al4V基体上的抗菌涂层，但未涉及增材制造或Ti-6Al-4V的力学/疲劳性能。
边界: The paper does not address additive manufacturing (LPBF/SLM/EBM/DED) of Ti-6Al-4V, nor does it characterize the base mechanical or fatigue performance of the alloy; it focuses on sol-gel coatings for dental abutments.
动机· 减少种植体周围炎的微生物定植。
方法· 在Ti6Al4V上制备含银/铜的溶胶-凝胶玻璃涂层。
结果· 涂层具有低粗糙度、高硬度、良好粘附力和抗菌性。
验证· 通过表面粗糙度、硬度、粘附力、细胞毒性和微生物粘附测试验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To reduce microbial colonization associated with peri-implantitis by developing durable antimicrobial coatings on Ti6Al4V dental abutments.
Method· Sol-gel derived glassy coatings with ionic silver or silver/copper were dip-coated onto Ti6Al4V substrates and cured at 450°C; half of specimens underwent thermal aging.
Result· Coatings exhibited low surface roughness, hydrophilic surfaces, improved hardness, strong adhesion, and significantly reduced adhesion of oral microorganisms while remaining biocompatible.
Validation· Surface roughness, contact angle, hardness, adhesion strength, scratch resistance, cytotoxicity (agar diffusion and MTT), and microbial adhesion tests were conducted.
