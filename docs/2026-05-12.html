Integrating TAGUCHI Design with MCDM Frameworks for Multi-Objective Optimization of Ti6AL4V Grinding Via Ester-Based Minimum Quantity Lubrication (MQL)

Quốc Tuần Nguyễn, Thị Thu Hằng Nguyễn Journal of Machine Engineering 2026-05-12 10.36897/jme/220939
相关性: 该论文研究的是Ti6Al4V的磨削工艺优化，而非增材制造，因此与用户关注的Ti-6Al-4V增材制造力学与疲劳性能方向无关。
边界: The paper does not study additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it is purely about grinding process optimization of wrought Ti-6Al-4V, lacking any mechanical/fatigue characterization or AM context.
动机· 优化Ti6Al4V磨削参数以提高表面质量
方法· 采用Taguchi方法结合MCDM框架多目标优化
结果· 确定了最佳磨削条件，表面粗糙度达0.828μm
验证· 实验验证磨削力与表面粗糙度
English summary & terms
Motivation· To optimize grinding parameters for Ti6Al4V to improve surface quality and process stability.
Method· Taguchi experimental design combined with multiple MCDM and weighting techniques for multi-objective optimization.
Result· Optimal grinding conditions achieved a minimum surface roughness of 0.828 μm and low normal force of 7.39 N.
Validation· Experimental measurements of surface roughness and cutting force components validated the optimization.

Effect of Surface Roughness and Residual Stress on Fatigue Performance of Ti-6Al-4V

Ruminda Wimalasiri Engineer Journal of the Institution of Engineers Sri Lanka 2026-05-12 10.4038/engineer.v59i2.7746
相关性: 该论文未涉及增材制造工艺，无法直接用于增材制造Ti-6Al-4V疲劳性能研究。
边界: The paper does not address additive manufacturing; it focuses on conventionally manufactured Ti-6Al-4V implants, which is outside the L1 direction for additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 研究制造工艺对Ti-6Al-4V髋关节假体表面粗糙度和残余应力的影响，以消除体内疲劳失效。
方法· 制备与真实植入物相同条件的沙漏型试样，进行旋转弯曲疲劳试验，并推导数学方程。
结果· 发现制造工艺显著影响表面粗糙度和应力状态，进而影响疲劳性能。
验证· 通过旋转弯曲疲劳试验验证数学方程的有效性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study investigates the effects of manufacturing practices on surface roughness and residual stress of Ti-6Al-4V hip prostheses to eliminate in-vivo fatigue failures.
Method· Hourglass-shaped samples were prepared under conditions identical to actual implant manufacturing, and rotating-bending fatigue tests were conducted, and a mathematical equation was derived.
Result· Clear variations in surface roughness and stress states due to manufacturing practices and considerable variations in fatigue performance were observed.
Validation· The validation was performed through rotating-bending fatigue testing of the samples.

Variation of Fatigue Threshold Limit of Ti-6Al-4Vat Different Stress Ratios

Ruminda Wimalasiri Engineer Journal of the Institution of Engineers Sri Lanka 2026-05-12 10.4038/engineer.v59i2.7755
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering , The Open University of Sri Lanka
通讯: Ruminda Wimalasiri @ Department of Mechanical Engineering , The Open University of Sri Lanka
相关性: 论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V疲劳性能，但未涉及增材制造工艺（LPBF/SLM/EBM/DED），与用户关注的增材制造Ti-6Al-4V力学与疲劳方向不匹配。
边界: Does not concern additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; no mention of LPBF, SLM, EBM, or DED processes.
动机· 研究表面粗糙度和残余应力对Ti-6Al-4V疲劳阈值的影响。
方法· 采用旋转弯曲试验测试不同表面粗糙度（0.2-52 μm）和退火状态的试样。
结果· 获得了不同应力比下的阈值曲线，发现ΔKth随表面粗糙度和残余应力变化。
验证· 通过实验数据验证阈值曲线的有效性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Investigate how surface roughness and residual stress affect fatigue threshold limits in Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Rotary-bending tests were conducted on samples with four surface roughness levels (0.2, 6, 21, 52 μm) under annealed and non-annealed conditions.
Result· Threshold curves for four stress ratios were developed, showing clear variation of ΔKth with surface roughness and residual stress.
Validation· Experimental validation via mechanical testing of Ti-6Al-4V specimens.

Simulation of part distortion in milling operation of forged aerospace components using boolean subtraction

G. Mauthner, Christian Daniel Gommel, Thomas Gruber, Severin Maier, Dražen Breščaković et al. Journal of Machine Engineering 2026-05-12 10.36897/jme/220938
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的铣削变形模拟，但研究对象为锻造件而非增材制造件，且未涉及任何增材制造后处理对机械性能的影响。
边界: This paper studies machining distortion of forged Ti-6Al-4V components, not post-processing of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it does not address fatigue or mechanical performance modification by post-processing.
动机· 预测锻造航空零部件铣削时的变形问题
方法· 利用布尔减法集成CAM数据至FEM进行逐步材料去除模拟
结果· 开发了自动化工艺评估工具并通过演示件验证
验证· 实验室环境下使用航空相关演示件进行验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To predict part distortion during milling of forged aerospace components caused by residual stress release.
Method· A finite element simulation approach integrating CAM data via Boolean subtraction for step-by-step material removal.
Result· An automated tool for process evaluation was developed and demonstrated on aerospace components.
Validation· Laboratory validation using aerospace-relevant demonstration parts.
