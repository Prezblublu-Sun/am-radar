Multi-Scale Microstructure-Informed Modeling of Residual Stresses and Distortions in Additive Friction Stir Deposition

Mayank Agarwal, Mohamed Elleithy, Rakesh K. Kapania, Pınar Acar, Wei Zhao 2026-01-08 10.2514/6.2026-1670
单位: Virginia Polytechnic Institute and State University
通讯: Mayank Agarwal @ Virginia Polytechnic Institute and State University
相关性: 该论文提出了一个多尺度框架来预测Ti-6Al-4V增材制造中的残余应力和变形，与L1层的力学和疲劳性能相关，但主要贡献在于建模方法而非直接实验数据。
边界: no experimental mechanical or fatigue testing; the paper focuses on modeling residual stresses and distortions via a surrogate neural network, not on reporting tensile, yield, or fatigue properties.
动机· 高效预测Ti-6Al-4V增材摩擦搅拌沉积过程中的残余应力和变形
方法· 结合晶体塑性模型和神经网络代理的多尺度热-力学仿真框架
结果· 神经网络代理方法将计算时间从71小时缩短至1分钟
验证· 与全物理晶体塑性模拟结果进行对比验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To efficiently and accurately predict residual stresses and distortions in Ti-6Al-4V structures manufactured by Additive Friction Stir Deposition (AFSD).
Method· A multi-scale thermo-mechanical framework integrating crystal plasticity (DAMASK) with a surrogate feedforward neural network to replace computationally expensive full-field simulations.
Result· The surrogate model reduced computational time from 71 hours to 1 minute for a low-fidelity analysis while preserving accuracy.
Validation· The neural network predictions were validated against high-fidelity DAMASK crystal plasticity simulations.

Texture evolution and mechanical anisotropy reduction in boron-microalloyed Ti-6Al-4V sheets during cold rolling and annealing

Ying Han, Yazheng Wang, Yun Meng, Lei Cheng, Wei Yu et al. Materials Science and Engineering A 2026-01-08 10.1016/j.msea.2025.149703
单位: Institute of Engineering Technology, University of Science and Technology Beijing, Beijing, 100083, China
通讯: Wei Yu @ Institute of Engineering Technology, University of Science and Technology Beijing, Beijing, 100083, China
相关性: 该论文研究了硼微合金化Ti-6Al-4V板材在冷轧和退火过程中的织构与力学各向异性，虽针对传统加工材料，但其后处理工艺对AM Ti-6Al-4V具有参考价值。
边界: The paper investigates conventionally processed sheets, not AM Ti-6Al-4V, so the findings may not directly apply to as-built microstructures; additionally, it lacks fatigue data, which is a key focus of the direction.
动机· 研究冷轧和退火对硼微合金化Ti-6Al-4V板材织构与力学各向异性的影响
方法· 对含硼Ti-6Al-4V板材进行冷轧和退火处理，并分析其显微组织和力学性能
结果· 冷轧和退火有效降低了力学各向异性并改变了织构特征
验证· 通过拉伸测试和电子背散射衍射表征验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how cold rolling and annealing modify texture and reduce mechanical anisotropy in boron-microalloyed Ti-6Al-4V sheets.
Method· Cold rolling and annealing post-treatments were applied, followed by microstructural characterization and mechanical testing.
Result· The combined cold rolling and annealing significantly reduces mechanical anisotropy and alters crystallographic texture.
Validation· Experimental validation through tensile tests and EBSD analysis.

单位: University of New South Wales Canberra at ADFA
通讯: Daniel Handford @ University of New South Wales Canberra at ADFA
相关性: 该论文主要关注光纤布拉格光栅传感器在激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V结构中的应用，而非力学或疲劳性能表征。
边界: This paper focuses on sensor application and strain measurement rather than reporting mechanical properties or fatigue behavior of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 监测高超声速飞行器热结构中的应变
方法· 在EBM制造的Ti-6Al-4V上应用光纤布拉格光栅传感器

English summary & terms
Motivation· To enable strain monitoring in hypersonic aerostructures subjected to high temperatures using fiber Bragg grating sensors.
Method· Fiber Bragg grating sensors were installed on electron-beam melted Ti-6Al-4V specimens, and adhesive compounds were evaluated.
Result· The study characterized the measurement behavior of the sensors under static loading, considering surface roughness effects.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed through static loading tests on additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V samples.

The Aerothermal, and Structural Design of the Common Front End Flight Vehicle

Jason Smith, Dan Handford, Hilbert Van Pelt, Andrew Neely, SA Brown 2026-01-08 10.2514/6.2026-0105
单位: University of New South Wales Canberra at ADFA
通讯: Jason Smith @ University of New South Wales Canberra at ADFA
相关性: 该论文将Ti-6Al-4V作为飞行器热结构材料，但未提供增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学或疲劳性能数据。
边界: The paper does not focus on mechanical/fatigue characterization of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it uses Ti-6Al-4V as a material for a flight vehicle structure and does not provide systematic mechanical or fatigue data.
动机· 为高超声速飞行试验平台进行热结构设计，评估材料可行性。
方法· 通过气动热和结构仿真，结合增材制造Ti-6Al-4V原型制造与测试。
结果· 确定4mm壁厚Ti-6Al-4V热结构适用于原型机，并完成集成验证。
验证· 制造并测试了增材制造Ti-6Al-4V原型模块，验证了设计可行性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To design a hot structure for a high-speed flight testbed, assessing suitable materials for extreme aerothermal conditions.
Method· The study employed aerothermal and structural simulations, followed by additive manufacturing of Ti-6Al-4V prototypes via EBM for proof-of-concept testing.
Result· A 4 mm wall thickness Ti-6Al-4V hot structure was found sufficient, and the prototype modules were successfully manufactured and integrated.
Validation· Proof-of-concept features were manufactured and tested, with machined assembly features ensuring required tolerances.

Tuning the Mechanical and Antibacterial Properties of ZrO2 Thin Films by Varying Deposition Angle and Orientation for Biomedical Applications

Asma Gzaiel, Khalil Aouadi, A Besnard, Y. Pinot, Corinne Nouveau et al. Micro 2026-01-08 10.3390/micro6010004
单位: Arts et Metiers Institute of Technology, LaBoMaP, F-71250 Cluny, France
通讯: A Besnard @ Université Marie et Louis Pasteur, SUPMICROTECH, CNRS, Institut FEMTO-ST, F-25000 Besançon, France
相关性: 该论文主要研究ZrO2薄膜，而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学或疲劳性能。
边界: The paper focuses on ZrO2 thin films deposited on Ti6Al4V substrates by sputtering, not on additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V mechanical/fatigue characterization or modeling.
动机· 在Ti6Al4V基底上沉积ZrO2薄膜以兼顾力学与抗菌性能
方法· 采用斜角沉积直流磁控溅射并改变入射角与取向
结果· 薄膜硬度与模量超过Ti6Al4V基底，高入射角时抗菌率超90%
验证· 通过纳米压痕和抗菌实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To engineer ZrO2 thin films on Ti6Al4V substrates that combine excellent mechanical integrity with antibacterial activity.
Method· ZrO2 films were deposited by DC magnetron sputtering at varying oblique angles and orientations, characterized by XRD, SEM, AFM, and nanoindentation.
Result· Films deposited at incidence angles between 7° and 50° exhibited hardness and modulus exceeding Ti6Al4V, while films at the highest angle achieved >90% antibacterial inhibition against Staphylococcus aureus.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated by nanoindentation, and antibacterial efficacy by inhibition rate tests.

Structural design and multiphase biomechanical evaluation of a topology-optimized metal 3D-printed dual-compression plate for patellar fractures  

Chi-Yang Liao, Shao-Fu Huang, Ya-Han Chan, Hsuan-Wen Wang, Yupin Yang et al. International Journal of Bioprinting 2026-01-08 10.36922/ijb025480494
相关性: 本研究聚焦于髌骨骨折接骨板，而非股骨柄假体，与用户关注的Ti-6Al-4V股骨柄结构设计无关。
边界: The paper addresses a patellar plate for fracture fixation, not a femoral/hip stem implant, and therefore does not cover stem structural design, stress shielding, or stem fatigue per the direction focus.
动机· 髌骨骨折传统固定方式力学不足，需设计新型接骨板。
方法· 通过有限元分析和拓扑优化设计双压缩接骨板，并用SLM制造。
结果· 优化板在静力、疲劳和动态测试中表现优于传统固定。
验证· 根据ASTM F382进行四点弯曲、疲劳和动态膝关节测试。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Conventional fixation for patellar fractures lacks sufficient mechanical stability, especially for transverse and comminuted types.
Method· A dual-compression patellar plate was designed via finite element analysis and topology optimization, then fabricated by selective laser melting.
Result· The optimized plate demonstrated superior static, fatigue, and dynamic performance compared with conventional fixation.
Validation· Mechanical validation included four-point bending (ASTM F382), fatigue testing, and dynamic knee-extension experiments.
