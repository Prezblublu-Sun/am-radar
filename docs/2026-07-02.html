Impact of Segmentation Methods on Predicting Fatigue-Initiating Pores from X-ray Computed Tomography Data

Justin P. Miner, Michelle Hobdari, Henry Stanton, Sneha Prabha Narra Integrating materials and manufacturing innovation 2026-07-02 10.1007/s40192-026-00467-0
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Carnegie Mellon University, 5000 Forbes Ave, Pittsburgh, PA, 15213, USA
通讯: Sneha Prabha Narra @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Carnegie Mellon University, 5000 Forbes Ave, Pittsburgh, PA, 15213, USA
相关性: 该论文直接研究增材制造Ti-6Al-4V疲劳起裂孔的CT图像分割方法，对疲劳性能预测具有实际意义。
边界: 该论文专注于分割方法的影响，未涉及疲劳寿命数值或S-N曲线等标准机械性能数据。
动机· 评估不同CT图像分割方法对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V疲劳起裂孔预测的影响。
方法· 采用四种分割方法处理旋转弯曲疲劳试样的X-µCT数据，并用修正Murakami参数识别临界孔。
结果· 2.5D U-Net在所有案例中正确预测起裂孔，而体素指标Dice不反映疲劳相关性。
验证· 基于NIST AMBench 2025挑战的四组已知疲劳起裂孔案例进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study evaluates how different X-ray computed tomography segmentation methods affect the prediction of fatigue-initiating pores in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Four segmentation methods (global binary, adaptive, hysteresis, 2.5D U-Net) were applied to X-µCT scans of PBF-LB Ti-6Al-4V rotating bending fatigue specimens, using a modified √A parameter accounting for the bending stress gradient to locate the critical pore.
Result· Threshold-based methods predicted the fatigue-initiating pore correctly in three of four cases, while the U-Net succeeded in all four, demonstrating that voxel-wise metrics like Dice coefficient do not correlate with fatigue relevance.
Validation· The approach was validated against four test cases from the NIST AMBench 2025 challenge with known fatigue-initiating pores.

Effect of wall thickness on the microstructure and properties of Ti-6Al-4V schwarz diamond structure prepared by selective laser melting

Xinyu Zhang, Chun Guo, WenQing Li, Guangcan Huang, Qingcheng Lin et al. Materials Science and Technology 2026-07-02 10.1177/02670836261463942
单位: Anhui Science and Technology University
通讯: Chun Guo @ Anhui Science and Technology University
相关性: 这篇论文直接研究了SLM制备的Ti-6Al-4V金刚石结构的力学性能，与层1关注的基本机械性能高度相关。
边界: No fatigue life or S-N data; only monotonic mechanical properties (tensile, compression, impact) are reported.
动机· 研究壁厚对SLM制备的Ti-6Al-4V schwarz金刚石结构性能的影响。
方法· 制备不同壁厚的schwarz金刚石结构，进行微观组织和力学性能测试。
结果· 壁厚增加提高了拉伸、压缩和冲击性能，0.6mm壁厚拉伸强度达165.42 MPa。
验证· 通过实验测试验证力学性能与微观结构关系。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of wall thickness on the microstructure and mechanical properties of Ti-6Al-4V schwarz diamond structures fabricated by selective laser melting.
Method· Schwarz diamond structures with varying wall thicknesses (0.3-0.6 mm) were manufactured and characterized for porosity, microstructure, and mechanical properties under tensile, compressive, and impact loading.
Result· Increasing wall thickness enhanced tensile strength (from 68.06 to 165.42 MPa), compressive strength, and impact energy absorption, with needle-like α' martensite microstructure throughout.
Validation· Experimental mechanical testing and microstructure analysis were performed on the fabricated samples.
