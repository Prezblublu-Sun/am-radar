STUDY OF THE EFFECT OF PRELIMINARY ULTRASONIC SURFACE MODIFICATION OF TI-6AL-4V ALLOY SUBSTRATE ON THE FORMATION OF A NITRIDED LAYER

Zarina Aringozhina, Nurtoleu Magazov, Auezhan Amanov, Arnur Askhatov, Elaman Batanov Journal of Surface Engineering and Coating Technology 2026-02-09 10.66310/zeed5485
单位: D. Serikbayev East Kazakhstan State Technical University
通讯: Zarina Aringozhina @ D. Serikbayev East Kazakhstan State Technical University
相关性: 该论文主要研究Ti-6Al-4V合金基体的表面改性，不涉及增材制造及力学/疲劳性能。
边界: Paper focuses on surface modification of Ti-6Al-4V via UNSM and nitriding, not on mechanical/fatigue performance of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; substrate manufacturing method is unspecified and likely not additive.
动机· 探究超声纳米表面改性对Ti-6Al-4V合金渗氮层形成的影响。
方法· 通过超声纳米表面改性预处理后再进行离子渗氮，系统改变工艺参数。
结果· 优化后的UNSM预处理显著促进氮扩散，形成致密均匀的TiN/Ti2N层，表面硬度和耐磨性提升。
验证· 采用SEM、XRD、硬度测试和摩擦磨损实验进行表征。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how ultrasonic nanocrystalline surface modification (UNSM) influences the formation of nitride layers on Ti-6Al-4V alloy during ion-plasma nitriding.
Method· Systematic variation of UNSM parameters (vibration amplitude, static load, processing temperature) followed by ion-plasma nitriding to assess microstructural and mechanical evolution.
Result· Optimized UNSM pre-treatment significantly enhances nitrogen diffusion, producing dense and uniform TiN/Ti2N layers with up to 25% increase in surface hardness and improved wear resistance.
Validation· Confirmed by scanning electron microscopy, X-ray diffraction, microhardness testing, and tribological wear tests.

通讯: Takuma Teramae @ Graduate School of Engineering, The University of Osaka, 2-1 Yamadaoka, Suita, Osaka 565-0871, Japan  |  Katsuyoshi Kondoh @ Joining and Welding Research Institute, The University of Osaka,11-1 Mihogaoka, Ibaraki, Osaka 567-0047, Japan
相关性: 该论文通过Mn合金化实现原位马氏体分解，而非后处理工艺，与Ti-6Al-4V后处理控制方向无关。
边界: The paper does not investigate any post-processing treatment for Ti-6Al-4V; instead it focuses on alloy design (Mn addition) to achieve in-situ martensite decomposition during PBF-LB, which is a process parameter or material composition approach, not a post-processing route.
动机· 通过Mn合金化促进PBF-LB钛合金中马氏体原位分解。
方法· 对比Ti-2Mn与Ti-6Al-4V的马氏体分解行为及力学性能。
结果· Ti-2Mn实现高效分解，强度为纯钛2.5倍，延伸率达7.8%。
验证· 微观组织表征与室温拉伸测试。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To address the undesirable α' martensitic microstructure in PBF-LB Ti alloys by alloy design with Mn addition that promotes in-situ decomposition.
Method· The microstructure evolution and solute partitioning of Ti-2Mn and Ti-6Al-4V were compared across different volumetric energy densities.
Result· Ti-2Mn exhibited more efficient martensite decomposition, achieving approximately 2.5 times the strength of pure Ti with uniform elongation up to 7.8% without post-heat treatment.
Validation· Microstructural characterization (SEM, EDS) and uniaxial tensile testing were performed.

Rapid Optimization of Microstructure–Hardness Relationship in Ti-Al-V-Zr-Nb-Mo Alloy via Gradient Heat Treatment

Lijuan Zhu, Chun Feng, Yuanlong Liang, Wei Luo, Kai Zhang et al. Materials 2026-02-09 10.3390/ma19040660
单位: State Key Laboratory of Oil and Gas Equipment, Tubular Goods Research Institute of CNPC, Xi’an 710077, China
通讯: Chun Feng @ CNPC Bohai Equipment Manufacturing Co., Ltd., Tianjin 300457, China  |  Huiqun Liu @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, Central South University, Changsha 410083, China
相关性: 该论文研究的是Ti-6Al-4V衍生合金的热处理与硬度关系，但未涉及增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的疲劳或力学性能，且合金成分与标准Ti-6Al-4V有差异。
边界: This paper does not study additive-manufactured Ti-6Al-4V, nor does it report fatigue or tensile mechanical performance; it only examines hardness of a non-standard Ti alloy variant.
动机· 快速优化Ti-Al-V-Zr-Nb-Mo合金的热处理窗口，建立微观结构与硬度的关系。
方法· 采用梯度固溶和时效处理，结合显微组织表征分析α/β相含量与硬度关联。
结果· 在特定温度下获得峰值硬度，最优Vαp/Vβt比约为3:7，细小的αs析出提高硬度。
验证· 通过硬度测试和微观组织观察验证了梯度热处理方法的有效性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To efficiently establish the microstructure–hardness relationship for a Ti-6Al-4V variant alloy and identify optimal heat treatment parameters.
Method· A gradient heat treatment approach combining solution treatment and aging was used to rapidly correlate phase fractions with hardness.
Result· Peak hardness over 350 HV1 was achieved at 502°C and 551°C aging, with an optimal Vαp/Vβt ratio near 3:7 and fine αs precipitation.
Validation· Experimental hardness measurements and microstructural analysis validated the predicted relationships.
