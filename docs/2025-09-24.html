Adhesive layer formation and its dual role in tribological performance and surface integrity of Ti-6Al-4V: Implications for the machining process

Nan Xu, Dongze Wang, Chris Taylor, Jack Secker, Gregory de Boer et al. Tribology International 2025-09-24 10.1016/j.triboint.2025.111228
单位: Institute of Functional Surfaces, School of Mechanical Engineering, University of Leeds, Leeds LS2 9JT, United Kingdom
通讯: Nan Xu @ Institute of Functional Surfaces, School of Mechanical Engineering, University of Leeds, Leeds LS2 9JT, United Kingdom  |  Dongze Wang @ Institute of Functional Surfaces, School of Mechanical Engineering, University of Leeds, Leeds LS2 9JT, United Kingdom  |  Ardian Morina @ Institute of Functional Surfaces, School of Mechanical Engineering, University of Leeds, Leeds LS2 9JT, United Kingdom
相关性: 该论文研究的是Ti-6Al-4V切削过程中的粘附层形成，而非增材制造后的后处理工艺，与用户方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on machining tribology of Ti-6Al-4V, not on post-processing treatments for additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V, and does not address fatigue or mechanical performance modification.
动机· Ti-6Al-4V加工中粘附层对刀具和工件的影响尚不明确。
方法· 实验结合边界元法模拟WC/Co-Ti64销盘滑动接触下的粘附层行为。
结果· 粘附层降低摩擦但恶化表面完整性，高MQL抑制粘附层但增加磨损。
验证· 通过销盘摩擦实验和BEM模拟验证粘附层力学效应。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The formation of adhesive layers on tool flanks during Ti-6Al-4V machining remains poorly understood despite its critical impact on tool degradation and surface integrity.
Method· This study combines experimental pin-on-disc sliding tests and boundary element method (BEM) simulations to investigate adhesive layer behavior under dry and MQL conditions.
Result· Thick adhesive layers reduce friction and tool-side wear but increase surface roughness and work-hardened layer thickness, while high MQL suppresses layer formation, increasing wear yet improving surface quality.
Validation· Experimental pin-on-disc tests and comparative BEM simulations with 316L stainless steel validate the mechanical effects of adhesive layers.

An Experimental Investigation on EDM Machines with Advanced Machine Learning to Predict High MRR For Aerospace Application

S. Arunkumar, K. Martin Sagayam International Research Journal on Advanced Engineering Hub (IRJAEH) 2025-09-24 10.47392/irjaeh.2025.0553
单位: Research Scholar, Dept. of ECE, Karunya Institute of Technology and Science, Coimbatore, India
通讯: S. Arunkumar @ Research Scholar, Dept. of ECE, Karunya Institute of Technology and Science, Coimbatore, India
相关性: 该论文研究EDM加工钛合金的MRR预测，未涉及后处理对疲劳或力学性能的影响。
边界: Paper does not investigate post-processing effects on mechanical performance (fatigue, tensile) of Ti-6Al-4V; it focuses solely on MRR prediction during EDM machining.
动机· 提高EDM加工中材料去除率预测的准确性
方法· 使用四种机器学习算法预测MRR
结果· 预测结果与实际值偏差约3%
验证· 实验数据与预测对比
English summary & terms
Motivation· Accurate prediction of Material Removal Rate (MRR) in die-sinker EDM for aerospace alloys to aid production planning.
Method· Applied ANN, Random Forest, SVM, and XGBoost to predict MRR from machining parameters (current, ton, toff, duty cycle, flushing) for Ti-6Al-4V and Inconel 718.
Result· The predictions showed good agreement with experimental results, with a deviation of approximately 3%.
Validation· Experimental MRR measurements were compared to model predictions.

Cryogenic assisted abrasive waterjet machining of Ti-6Al-4V alloy: Thermo-mechanical optimization and AI-based surface integrity prediction

Shuaikang Chang, Wenchuan Liu, Jiren Tang, Mengyan Fan Sustainable materials and technologies 2025-09-24 10.1016/j.susmat.2025.e01685
单位: State Key Laboratory of Coal Mine Disaster Dynamics and Control, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400044, China
通讯: Wenchuan Liu @ State Key Laboratory of Coal Mine Disaster Dynamics and Control, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400044, China  |  Jiren Tang @ State Key Laboratory of Coal Mine Disaster Dynamics and Control, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400044, China
相关性: 该论文涉及常规Ti-6Al-4V合金的低温辅助磨料水射流加工，未明确针对增材制造材料，与AM后处理控制方向无关。
边界: The paper does not specify additive-manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; the work is on conventional Ti-6Al-4V machining, thus outside the AM post-processing scope.
动机· 优化低温辅助磨料水射流加工参数并预测表面完整性。
方法· 结合热力学优化和人工智能预测模型。
结果· 实现了加工参数优化和表面完整性预测。
验证· 基于常规Ti-6Al-4V合金的实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To optimize cryogenic assisted abrasive waterjet machining parameters and predict surface integrity for Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· Combined thermo-mechanical optimization with AI-based prediction models.
Result· Achieved parameter optimization and surface integrity prediction.
Validation· Experimental validation on conventional Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
