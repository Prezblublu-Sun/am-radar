Cryogenic assisted abrasive waterjet machining of Ti-6Al-4V alloy: Thermo-mechanical optimization and AI-based surface integrity prediction

Shuaikang Chang, Wenchuan Liu, Jiren Tang, Mengyan Fan Sustainable materials and technologies 2025-09-24 10.1016/j.susmat.2025.e01685
单位: State Key Laboratory of Coal Mine Disaster Dynamics and Control, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400044, China
通讯: Wenchuan Liu @ State Key Laboratory of Coal Mine Disaster Dynamics and Control, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400044, China  |  Jiren Tang @ State Key Laboratory of Coal Mine Disaster Dynamics and Control, Chongqing University, Chongqing 400044, China
相关性: 该论文研究了Ti-6Al-4V合金的低温辅助磨料水射流加工，这是一种后处理工艺，但未直接评估疲劳或力学性能，仅关注表面完整性预测，因此对层2方向的直接参考价值有限。
边界: The paper does not directly measure fatigue or mechanical performance after post-processing; it focuses on surface integrity prediction and process optimization without tensile/fatigue data.
动机· 提高Ti-6Al-4V合金磨料水射流加工的表面完整性
方法· 采用低温辅助磨料水射流加工并优化热力学参数，结合AI预测表面完整性
结果· 低温辅助改善了表面完整性，AI模型准确预测了表面粗糙度和残余应力
验证· 通过实验测量表面粗糙度和残余应力验证了模型
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve surface integrity of Ti-6Al-4V alloy during abrasive waterjet machining by introducing cryogenic assistance.
Method· Thermo-mechanical optimization of cryogenic assisted abrasive waterjet machining parameters combined with AI-based prediction of surface integrity indicators.
Result· Cryogenic assistance improved surface finish and reduced residual stress, and the AI model accurately predicted surface roughness and residual stress.
Validation· Experimental measurements of surface roughness and residual stress validated the model predictions.

Adhesive layer formation and its dual role in tribological performance and surface integrity of Ti-6Al-4V: Implications for the machining process

Nan Xu, Dongze Wang, Chris Taylor, Jack Secker, Gregory de Boer et al. Tribology International 2025-09-24 10.1016/j.triboint.2025.111228
单位: Institute of Functional Surfaces, School of Mechanical Engineering, University of Leeds, Leeds LS2 9JT, United Kingdom
通讯: Nan Xu @ Institute of Functional Surfaces, School of Mechanical Engineering, University of Leeds, Leeds LS2 9JT, United Kingdom  |  Dongze Wang @ Institute of Functional Surfaces, School of Mechanical Engineering, University of Leeds, Leeds LS2 9JT, United Kingdom  |  Ardian Morina @ Institute of Functional Surfaces, School of Mechanical Engineering, University of Leeds, Leeds LS2 9JT, United Kingdom
相关性: 该论文涉及钛合金加工过程中的粘附层形成，但未直接研究后处理对疲劳或力学性能的影响。
边界: The paper focuses on tribological behavior during machining, not on how post-processing modifies fatigue or mechanical performance of AM Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 探究钛合金加工中粘附层形成及其双重作用
方法· 采用销盘滑动接触实验和边界元法模拟
结果· 粘附层降低摩擦和工具磨损但恶化工件表面完整性
验证· 实验与边界元模拟验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the formation of adhesive layers during Ti-6Al-4V machining and their dual role in tribological performance and surface integrity.
Method· Pin-on-disc sliding contact experiments and boundary element method (BEM) simulations were conducted under dry and MQL conditions.
Result· Adhesive layers reduce friction and tool wear but compromise workpiece surface integrity by increasing roughness and work-hardened layer thickness.
Validation· Experimental pin-on-disc tests and BEM simulations confirmed the mechanistic findings.

An Experimental Investigation on EDM Machines with Advanced Machine Learning to Predict High MRR For Aerospace Application

S. Arunkumar, K. Martin Sagayam International Research Journal on Advanced Engineering Hub (IRJAEH) 2025-09-24 10.47392/irjaeh.2025.0553
单位: Research Scholar, Dept. of ECE, Karunya Institute of Technology and Science, Coimbatore, India
通讯: S. Arunkumar @ Research Scholar, Dept. of ECE, Karunya Institute of Technology and Science, Coimbatore, India
相关性: 本文研究电火花加工（EDM）的切削参数和材料去除率预测，不涉及增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的后处理及其力学性能。
边界: This paper does not address post-processing of additive manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it focuses on EDM machining of Ti-6Al-4V and Inconel 718 for material removal rate prediction without any connection to AM, fatigue, or mechanical performance.
动机· 研究电火花加工中参数对材料去除率的影响以优化生产计划
方法· 使用ANN、RF、SVM和XGBoost四种算法预测MRR
结果· 预测结果与实测偏差约3%
验证· 通过实验数据训练模型并与实际结果对比
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study aims to predict Material Removal Rate (MRR) in die-sinker EDM for aerospace materials to aid production planning.
Method· Four supervised machine learning algorithms (ANN, Random Forest, SVM, XGBoost) were trained on machining parameters to predict MRR.
Result· Predicted MRR deviated by approximately 3% from experimental values.
Validation· The models were validated against actual experimental MRR data.
