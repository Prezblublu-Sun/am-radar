Cellular Automata and Phase-Field Modeling of Microstructure Evolution in Metal Additive Manufacturing: Recent Advances, Hybrid Frameworks, and Pathways to Predictive Control

单位: AGH University of Krakow, Faculty of Metals Engineering and Industrial Computer Science, al. Mickiewicza 30, 30-059 Krakow, Poland

边界: This is a review of CA/PF modeling methods for microstructure evolution across multiple alloys, not a focused study on Ti-6Al-4V mechanical or fatigue performance; it does not provide experimental or simulation data specifically on Ti-6Al-4V tensile, fatigue, or anisotropy.

English summary & terms
Motivation· This review synthesizes recent advances in cellular automata and phase-field modeling for predicting grain-scale microstructure evolution during metal additive manufacturing.
Method· The paper surveys CA, PF, and hybrid CA-PF frameworks, including algorithmic innovations like GPU acceleration and machine learning.
Result· Hybrid frameworks balance computational efficiency and thermodynamic fidelity, but challenges remain in scalability, calibration, and multi-physics coupling.
Validation· Validated applications across multiple alloys (including Ti-6Al-4V) are reported through EBSD and mechanical testing comparisons.

Microstructure and corrosion behavior of laser powder bed fusion Ti-6Al-4V alloy for orthopedic implant application

Marichamy M, Chockalingam K, N. Arunachalam, Velkannan V Rapid Prototyping Journal 2026-01-21 10.1108/rpj-08-2025-0390
单位: Thiagarajar College of Engineering Department of Mechanical Engineering, , Madurai,
通讯: Marichamy M @ Thiagarajar College of Engineering Department of Mechanical Engineering, , Madurai,
相关性: 该论文仅关注LPBF Ti-6Al-4V的腐蚀行为，未涉及股骨柄的结构设计或力学性能。
边界: This paper focuses exclusively on corrosion resistance of LPBF Ti-6Al-4V in simulated body fluids, with no structural design, lattice architecture, stress shielding analysis, fatigue testing, or FEA of femoral stems.
动机· 确定LPBF工艺参数对Ti-6Al-4V在模拟体液中腐蚀性能的影响。
方法· 设计实验矩阵改变激光功率、扫描速度和间距，并进行电化学测试。
结果· 特定参数组（试样S8）在HBSS中线性极化电阻达127,855 Ω·cm²，腐蚀速率低至0.07365 mpy。
验证· 通过开路电位、动电位极化和电化学阻抗谱在HBSS和NaCl溶液中验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To quantitatively establish the relationship between LPBF process parameters and the corrosion resistance of Ti-6Al-4V in simulated body fluids for orthopedic implant applications.
Method· A designed experimental matrix varying laser power, scan speed, and hatch distance was used to fabricate specimens, followed by electrochemical tests (OCP, potentiodynamic polarization, EIS) in HBSS and 3.5% NaCl.
Result· Specimen S8 achieved the best corrosion performance with a linear polarization resistance of 127,855 Ω·cm² in HBSS and corrosion current density as low as 0.0317 µA/cm².
Validation· Experimental validation via multiple electrochemical techniques in two simulated body fluid environments.
