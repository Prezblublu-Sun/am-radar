In-situ X-ray investigation of ductile failure in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V alloy under different stress triaxiality

单位: School of Aeronautics, Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi’an 710072, China
通讯: Wei Huang @ National Key Laboratory of Strength and Structural Integrity, Xi’an 710072, China
相关性: 该论文直接研究增材制造Ti-6Al-4V在不同应力三轴度下的延性破坏，属于L1层机械性能研究。
边界: This paper does not focus on fatigue life or S-N curves, but rather on ductile failure mechanisms under varying stress triaxiality.
动机· 探索增材制造Ti-6Al-4V在不同应力三轴度下的延性破坏机理
方法· 采用原位X射线成像技术实时观察试样在拉伸过程中的损伤演化
结果· 揭示了应力三轴度对孔洞形核、长大和聚合的影响规律
验证· 通过原位X射线实验与力学测试数据对比验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand the ductile failure mechanisms of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V under varying stress triaxiality.
Method· In-situ X-ray imaging was employed to observe real-time damage evolution during tensile testing.
Result· The influence of stress triaxiality on void nucleation, growth, and coalescence was revealed.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed by correlating in-situ X-ray observations with mechanical test data.

Microstructure and mechanical properties of Ti-6Al-4V alloy processed by additive friction stir deposition over a wide temperature range

Meet Gor, Matthew Barnett, P.P. Bhattacharjee, Daniel Fabijanic Materials Science and Engineering A 2025-08-27 10.1016/j.msea.2025.149038
单位: Department of Materials Science and Metallurgical Engineering, IIT Hyderabad, Kandi, Sangareddy, Telangana 502284, India
通讯: Meet Gor @ Department of Materials Science and Metallurgical Engineering, IIT Hyderabad, Kandi, Sangareddy, Telangana 502284, India  |  P.P. Bhattacharjee @ Department of Materials Science and Metallurgical Engineering, IIT Hyderabad, Kandi, Sangareddy, Telangana 502284, India  |  Daniel Fabijanic @ Institute for Frontier Materials, Deakin University, Geelong, Victoria 3216, Australia
相关性: 该论文研究了增材摩擦搅拌沉积Ti-6Al-4V在不同温度下的微观结构和力学性能，直接关联到钛合金增材制造基础力学与疲劳层。
边界: The paper does not include fatigue testing or fatigue life data, which are key aspects of the L1 direction's specific focus, but it provides tensile properties and microstructure characterization relevant to mechanical performance of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 探究不同增材摩擦搅拌沉积温度对Ti-6Al-4V合金微观结构和力学性能的影响。
方法· 在700°C至1000°C温度范围内进行AFSD沉积，表征微观组织并测试拉伸性能。
结果· 800°C和900°C沉积出现完全转变的β组织，700°C得到双态组织，拉伸性能与锻件相当。
验证· 通过拉伸力学测试验证沉积态合金的力学性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to investigate the effect of deposition temperature on the microstructure and mechanical properties of Ti-6Al-4V processed by additive friction stir deposition (AFSD).
Method· AFSD was performed at 700°C, 800°C, 900°C, and 1000°C, followed by microstructural characterization and tensile testing of the as-deposited material.
Result· Processing at 800°C and 900°C unexpectedly produced a fully transformed β microstructure similar to that at 1000°C, while 700°C yielded a bimodal structure; tensile properties met forged standards.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through standard tensile tests comparing results to ASTM forging requirements.

Surface Engineering of EB-PBF Ti6Al4V via Anodization: Multifunctional Improvements Through TiO2 Nanotube Arrays

单位: Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Politecnico di Torino, Corso Duca Degli Abruzzi 24, 10129 Torino, Italy
通讯: Abdollah Saboori @ Department of Management and Production Engineering, Politecnico di Torino, Corso Duca degli Abruzzi 24, 10129 Torino, I
相关性: 该论文针对EB-PBF Ti6Al4V的阳极氧化后处理，涉及表面硬度和附着力等力学性能，但缺乏疲劳或拉伸数据，与用户关注的疲劳/力学性能方向部分相关。
边界: The paper does not include fatigue testing or tensile properties; it focuses on hardness, scratch adhesion, corrosion resistance, and bioactivity, not on cyclic or monotonic mechanical performance under structural loads.
动机· 通过阳极氧化在EB-PBF Ti6Al4V表面生成TiO2纳米管，提升生物医用性能。
方法· 采用不同电压阳极氧化和后续热处理，系统表征形貌、相结构和力学性能。
结果· 低电压生成更均匀致密的纳米管，硬度更高；高电压导致纳米管更大更无序，力学性能略降。
验证· 通过SEM、AFM、XRD、EDS、纳米压痕和电化学测试验证表面改性效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the surface properties of EB-PBF Ti6Al4V for biomedical implants through anodization-induced TiO2 nanotube arrays.
Method· Ti6Al4V samples were anodized at 40V and 60V for 2h followed by heat treatment at 550°C, and characterized by SEM, AFM, XRD, EDS, nanoindentation, nanoscratch, and electrochemical tests.
Result· Lower voltage produced uniform, compact nanotubes with higher hardness and adhesion, while higher voltage led to thicker, disordered nanotubes with reduced mechanical performance.
Validation· Characterization techniques (SEM, AFM, XRD, EDS, nanoindentation, nanoscratch) and electrochemical corrosion tests confirmed the structural and property changes.

Research on the Johnson–Cook Constitutive Model and Failure Behavior of TC4 Alloy

Jiaxuan Zhu, Huidong Zhi, Tong Huang, Ning Ding, Zhaoming Yan Metals 2025-08-27 10.3390/met15090951
单位: School of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, North University of China, Taiyuan 030051, China
通讯: Zhaoming Yan @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, North University of China, Taiyuan 030051, China
相关性: 论文研究TC4合金的力学行为，但未涉及增材制造，因此与用户关注的增材制造Ti-6Al-4V力学与疲劳性能不直接相关。
边界: Does not specify additive manufacturing; material source likely conventional, not AM Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 研究TC4合金在不同温度和应变率下的力学响应与损伤演化。
方法· 通过准静态和动态拉伸实验，结合SEM和ABAQUS仿真，修正Johnson-Cook模型。
结果· 观察到温度软化效应、缺口尺寸效应和应变率依赖性，修正模型与实验高度吻合（相关系数0.97）。
验证· 通过实验载荷-位移曲线与仿真对比，以及断口形貌分析验证模型。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This work aims to characterize the mechanical response and damage evolution of TC4 alloy under coupled temperature and strain rate conditions.
Method· Quasi-static and dynamic tensile tests were conducted using a universal testing machine and Split Hopkinson Tensile Bar, and the Johnson-Cook model was modified based on experimental data; a 3D simulation was built in ABAQUS.
Result· Temperature softening, notch size effect, and strain-rate-dependent hardening were observed; the modified model predicted load–displacement curves with a correlation coefficient of 0.97.
Validation· The model was validated by comparing simulated load–displacement curves with experimental results and by SEM fractography revealing ductile fracture mechanisms.

Mechanical and thermal responses of random triply periodic minimal surface structures

Maytawee Maneein, Thanakit Yuthchaiming, Chutima Pankul, Atikom Sombatmai, Teerapong Poltue et al. Materials & Design 2025-08-27 10.1016/j.matdes.2025.114638
相关性: 该论文研究随机TPMS结构的力学和热响应，但未涉及股骨柄或骨科植入物，与方向无关。
边界: The paper does not address femoral stem design, orthopaedic implants, stress shielding, or any implant-specific mechanical/biomechanical analysis.
动机· 研究随机TPMS结构在力学和热响应上的性能变化。
方法· 通过Voronoi方法生成随机Gyroid结构，进行有限元模拟和压缩实验。
结果· 随机性增加弹性模量、峰值应力和能量吸收，但降低热导率。
验证· 压缩实验与有限元模拟对比验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the mechanical and thermal responses of stochastic TPMS structures fabricated by laser powder bed fusion.
Method· Voronoi-based randomness was introduced into Gyroid structures, and FE simulations with damage modeling along with compressive testing were conducted.
Result· Increasing stochasticity improved elastic modulus, peak stress, and energy absorption by up to 54%, while reducing thermal conductivity by 8-16%.
Validation· Experimental compression tests validated the FE simulation results.
