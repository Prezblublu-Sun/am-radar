Surface Engineering of EB-PBF Ti6Al4V via Anodization: Multifunctional Improvements Through TiO2 Nanotube Arrays

Alireza Moradi, Sanae Tajalli, Amir Behjat, Abdollah Saboori, Luca Iuliano Coatings 2025-08-27 10.3390/coatings15090993
单位: Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Politecnico di Torino, Corso Duca Degli Abruzzi 24, 10129 Torino, Italy
通讯: Abdollah Saboori @ Department of Management and Production Engineering, Politecnico di Torino, Corso Duca degli Abruzzi 24, 10129 Torino, I
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的后处理（阳极氧化+热处理），但未研究疲劳或拉伸性能，仅侧重于生物医学应用中的硬度和耐腐蚀性，因此与方向聚焦的疲劳/力学性能改性关系较弱。
边界: No fatigue or tensile data; mechanical evaluation limited to hardness and adhesion, and the study targets biomedical application rather than structural mechanical performance.
动机· 研究EB-PBF制造Ti6Al4V合金通过阳极氧化和热处理进行表面改性以提升生物医学性能。
方法· 在不同电压下阳极氧化生成TiO2纳米管阵列，随后550°C热处理，并通过SEM、AFM、XRD、EDS及纳米压痕等表征。
结果· 较低电压生成均匀致密纳米管，硬度更高，耐腐蚀性增强，生物活性改善。
验证· 纳米压痕、纳米划痕、电化学测试及体外生物活性实验。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate anodization and heat treatment as post-processing for EB-PBF Ti6Al4V to enhance surface properties for biomedical applications.
Method· Ti6Al4V samples were anodized at 40 V and 60 V to form TiO2 nanotube arrays, then heat-treated at 550°C; characterization included SEM, AFM, XRD, EDS, nanoindentation, nanoscratch, and electrochemical tests.
Result· Lower voltage produced compact nanotubes with higher hardness and better corrosion resistance; bioactivity was improved due to apatite formation.
Validation· Mechanical testing via nanoindentation and nanoscratch, electrochemical corrosion tests, and in vitro bioactivity assays.

Research on the Johnson–Cook Constitutive Model and Failure Behavior of TC4 Alloy

Jiaxuan Zhu, Huidong Zhi, Tong Huang, Ning Ding, Zhaoming Yan Metals 2025-08-27 10.3390/met15090951
单位: School of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, North University of China, Taiyuan 030051, China
通讯: Zhaoming Yan @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, North University of China, Taiyuan 030051, China
相关性: 该论文研究的是非增材制造的TC4合金力学性能，与增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的机械/疲劳方向不相关。
边界: This paper focuses on conventionally manufactured TC4 alloy without any additive manufacturing process, defects, or AM-specific features.
动机· 研究TC4合金在准静态和动态加载下的力学响应及损伤演化。
方法· 通过准静态拉伸和SHTB动态拉伸实验，修正Johnson-Cook本构模型和失效准则。
结果· 修正模型准确预测载荷-位移曲线，相关系数0.97，断口为韧性微孔聚集型断裂。
验证· 采用ABAQUS仿真与实验对比验证模型准确性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To characterize the mechanical response and failure behavior of TC4 alloy under quasi-static and dynamic loading conditions.
Method· Quasi-static tensile tests at various temperatures and dynamic tensile tests using SHTB were conducted, and the Johnson-Cook model was modified and parameterized.
Result· The modified Johnson-Cook model accurately predicted load-displacement curves (correlation 0.97), and fracture surfaces showed ductile microvoid-dominated failure.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed by comparing simulated load-displacement curves with measured data.

Specific cutting force in dry turning of Ti6Al4V ELI using different grades of carbide inserts

Grzegorz Struzikiewicz Advances in Science and Technology – Research Journal 2025-08-27 10.12913/22998624/207932
相关性: 该论文研究的是干式车削钛合金的切削力，不涉及任何后处理工艺（如热处理、表面处理）对疲劳或力学性能的影响，因此与方向无关。
边界: Paper does not address any post-processing treatment (heat treatment, shot peening, HIP, etc.) or its effect on fatigue/mechanical performance of Ti-6Al-4V; it focuses solely on cutting forces during dry turning, which is a primary machining process.
动机· 研究干车削Ti6Al4V ELI时切削参数对切削力的影响，以优化加工效率。
方法· 使用不同牌号硬质合金刀片进行干车削试验，测量切削力并采用Taguchi方法分析。
结果· 切削力主要受切削层横截面积影响，涂层刀片显著降低切削力，表面硬化倾向更低。
验证· 通过36组切削试验验证，并采用响应曲面法优化参数。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study aims to investigate the effect of turning parameters on specific cutting force for Ti6Al4V ELI to improve machining efficiency and tool life.
Method· Dry turning experiments were conducted with two grades of carbide inserts (H13A and 1115) while varying cutting speed, depth of cut, and feed; specific cutting force was measured and analyzed using the Taguchi method and response surface methodology.
Result· Specific cutting force depends primarily on the cross-sectional area of the cut; coated inserts reduce cutting force by an average of 282.7 MPa and show lower tendency for built-up edge and surface hardening.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed through 36 cutting tests, and optimal parameters (v_c=80 m/min, a_p=1.0 mm, f=0.2 mm/rev) were determined using desirability function.
