Machine Learning-Driven Optimization of Computer Numerical Control Milling Parameters for Enhanced Surface Integrity and Burr Control in Additive Manufacturing

Zihni Alp Çevik, Gencay Sarıışık Journal of Tribology 2025-06-27 10.1115/1.4069030
单位: Besni Vocational School, Adiyaman University Department of Mechatronics, ,   ,
通讯: Zihni Alp Çevik @ Besni Vocational School, Adiyaman University Department of Mechatronics, ,   ,
相关性: 该论文研究了增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的CNC铣削后处理工艺对表面完整性和毛刺的影响，与L2方向（后处理控制）直接相关。
边界: Does not directly measure fatigue or mechanical properties (e.g., tensile, fatigue life), focusing only on surface roughness and burr formation.
动机· 优化增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的铣削参数以改善表面完整性并控制毛刺。
方法· 通过实验与六种机器学习算法建模并优化切削参数。
结果· 切削深度对毛刺影响最大，梯度提升算法预测毛刺精度最高（R²>0.98）。
验证· 实验测量不同参数下的表面粗糙度和毛刺尺寸进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To optimize machining parameters for enhanced surface integrity and burr control in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Combined experimental design with six machine learning algorithms to model and optimize cutting speed, feed rate, and depth of cut.
Result· Cutting depth dominated burr formation (>90% contribution), and gradient boosting achieved highest burr prediction accuracy (test R² > 0.98).
Validation· Experimental measurements of surface roughness (0.07-0.22 µm) and burr size (0.09-1.46 mm) validated the models.

Evaluation of the possibility of obtaining spherical powders of titanium alloy Ti 6Al 4V by supersonic reverse polarity plasma torch for use in additive manufacturing

Shiyi Gao, Volodymyr Korzhyk, Dmytro Strohonov, O.S. Tereshchenko, O.I. Demianov et al. Advances in Science and Technology – Research Journal 2025-06-27 10.12913/22998624/205855
相关性: 该论文仅涉及Ti-6Al-4V粉末制备，未涉及增材制造后的力学或疲劳性能。
边界: The paper does not provide any mechanical testing or fatigue data for additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V components; it focuses solely on powder production characterization.
动机· 探索通过等离子雾化制备球形Ti-6Al-4V粉末用于增材制造。
方法· 采用直流反向极性等离子炬超音速射流雾化1.0 mm实心线材。
结果· 获得球形度高、缺陷少的粉末，主要粒径分布为-140 μm占96 wt%。
验证· 通过粒径分析和球形度检测验证粉末质量。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the feasibility of producing spherical Ti-6Al-4V powders via plasma atomization for additive manufacturing feedstock.
Method· A supersonic reverse polarity DC plasma torch atomizes a 1.0 mm solid wire.
Result· Spherical powders with high sphericity and low defect content were obtained, with a main fraction of -140 μm at 96 wt%.
Validation· Particle size analysis and sphericity measurement confirmed the powder quality.

边界: No mechanical or fatigue characterization; focuses on powder deposition, melt pool dynamics and microstructure evolution only.
动机· 建立集成仿真框架以预测增材制造过程

English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop an integrated simulation framework capable of predicting powder deposition, melt pool dynamics, and microstructure evolution during additive manufacturing.
Method· A multiphysics model coupling heat transfer, fluid flow, and phase-field methods is implemented to simulate the entire process chain.
Result· The framework successfully reproduces melt pool geometry, thermal history, and final microstructure for Ti-6Al-4V.
Validation· Model predictions are validated against experimental measurements and literature data.

Piezodynamic Behavior of HA-BT Osteoconductive Coatings Under LIPUS Stimulation in Lab-on-a-Chip Model: A Promising Strategy for Bone Regeneration

Karime Carrera Gutiérrez, Oscar Morales, Irene Leal‐Berumen, Edmundo Berumen-Nafarrate, Carlos A. Poblano-Salas et al. Coatings 2025-06-27 10.3390/coatings15070765
单位: Centro de Investigación en Materiales Avanzados (CIMAV), Miguel de Cervantes 120, Chihuahua 31109, Chihuahua, Mexico
通讯: Roberto Gómez Batres @ Facultad de Medicina y Ciencia Biomédicas, Universidad Autónoma de Chihuahua, Circuito Universitario 31109, Campus UACH  |  Víctor M. Orozco-Carmona @ Centro de Investigación en Materiales Avanzados (CIMAV), Miguel de Cervantes 120, Chihuahua 31109, Chihuahua, Mexico
相关性: 该论文研究HA-BT涂层在Ti6Al4V植入体上的压电性能，而非股骨柄的结构设计或力学性能。
边界: This paper focuses on piezoelectric coatings for bone regeneration, not on structural design, fatigue, stress shielding, or mechanical testing of Ti-6Al-4V femoral stems.
动机· 开发具有骨仿生压电特性的HA-BT涂层以促进骨再生。
方法· 通过高能球磨和大气等离子喷涂制备不同BT含量的HA-BT复合涂层。
结果· 含30%BT的涂层在电化学稳定性和生物学响应方面表现最佳。
验证· 采用电化学测试、细胞毒性实验和压电力显微镜验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop HA-BT composite coatings that mimic the piezoelectric properties of bone for enhanced bone regeneration.
Method· HA-BT coatings with varying BT content were fabricated via high-energy ball milling and atmospheric plasma spraying.
Result· Coatings with up to 30% BT showed optimal electrochemical stability and biological response, while higher BT content induced cellular stress.
Validation· Validation was performed through electrochemical tests, MTT assays, and piezoresponse force microscopy.
