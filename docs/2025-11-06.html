Proportional Multiaxial Fatigue Behavior and Life Prediction of Laser Powder Bed Fusion Ti-6Al-4V with Critical Plane-Based Building Direction Variations

Tian‐Hao Ma, Yuxin Wang, Wei Zhang, Jianping Zhao, Changyu Zhou Materials 2025-11-06 10.3390/ma18215056
单位: School of Mechanical and Power Engineering, Nanjing Tech University, Nanjing 211816, China
通讯: Changyu Zhou @ School of Mechanical and Power Engineering, Nanjing Tech University, Nanjing 211816, China
相关性: 该论文直接研究了激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V的多轴疲劳行为及寿命预测，完全符合L1力学与疲劳方向的需求。
边界: This paper does not cover post-processing treatments (e.g., HIP, heat treatment) or femoral stem implant applications; it is a pure as-built mechanical/fatigue characterization.
动机· 探究激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V在不同构建方向下的多轴低周疲劳行为。
方法· 基于临界平面法设计四种构建方向，进行比例应变控制的多轴疲劳试验，并提出KBMP-λ寿命预测模型。
结果· 材料呈现三阶段软化特征，裂纹从内部未熔合缺陷萌生，寿命预测误差在20%以内。
验证· 通过多轴疲劳实验数据验证模型精度。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the proportional multiaxial low-cycle fatigue behavior of laser powder bed fusion Ti-6Al-4V with varying building orientations based on critical plane considerations.
Method· Multiaxial fatigue tests under proportional strain-controlled loading were performed on specimens built in four orientations, and a KBMP life prediction model incorporating building direction parameters (λ) was developed.
Result· The material exhibits a three-stage cyclic softening response, fatigue cracks initiate from internal lack-of-fusion defects, and the proposed KBMP-λ model predicts fatigue life within 20% error.
Validation· The model was validated against experimental multiaxial fatigue test data.

Microstructural Evolution and Properties of In Situ Synthesized Ceramic-Phase Reinforced Ti-Al Composite Coatings Fabricated by Coaxial Powder Feeding Laser Cladding

Zhide Wang, Haitao Zhao, Lijie Liu, Yulin Chen, Haojie Chen Coatings 2025-11-06 10.3390/coatings15111297
单位: School of Materials Science and Engineering, Shenyang Ligong University, Shenyang 110159, China
通讯: Haitao Zhao @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, Shenyang Ligong University, Shenyang 110159, China  |  Lijie Liu @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, Shenyang Ligong University, Shenyang 110159, China
相关性: 该论文研究的是TC4表面激光熔覆制备Ti-Al-Si复合涂层的微观结构和性能，而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学或疲劳性能，与用户关注方向不符。
边界: The paper studies in-situ synthesized ceramic-reinforced Ti-Al-Si composite coatings on Ti6Al4V substrates via laser cladding, not the mechanical or fatigue performance of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 提高TC4表面硬度和耐磨性
方法· 同轴送粉激光熔覆制备Ti-Al-Si复合涂层
结果· 添加25%Si时硬度最高(820.83 HV),磨损率最低
验证· 硬度测试和磨损实验
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the surface hardness and wear resistance of Ti6Al4V alloy.
Method· Coaxial powder feeding laser cladding was used to fabricate Ti-Al-Si composite coatings with in situ synthesized Ti5Si3 ceramic phases.
Result· The coating with 25 wt% Si content achieved a maximum microhardness of 820.83 HV and the lowest wear rate.
Validation· Vickers microhardness testing and wear weight loss measurements.

Effect on deep cryogenic cycling treatment and combined double-sided laser shock peening with coating of microstructure evolution and mechanical properties of annealing Ti-6Al-4V alloy

M. Saravanakumar, V. Paramasivam, R. Selvabharathi Canadian Metallurgical Quarterly 2025-11-06 10.1080/00084433.2025.2555102
单位: PSNA College of Engineering and Technology
通讯: M. Saravanakumar @ PSNA College of Engineering and Technology
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V合金的后处理，但未针对增材制造件，与用户关注的AM Ti-6Al-4V后处理方向不直接相关。
边界: The paper does not address additive-manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it studies post-processing on conventional wrought/annealed alloy, which is outside the scope of AM-specific post-processing effects.
动机· 探究深冷循环与激光冲击喷丸复合处理对Ti-6Al-4V合金组织与力学性能的影响。
方法· 对退火态Ti-6Al-4V合金进行深冷循环处理和双面激光冲击喷丸涂层处理。
结果· 复合处理显著细化了显微组织并提高了强度与硬度。
验证· 通过显微表征和室温拉伸/硬度测试验证效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to investigate the combined effect of deep cryogenic cycling and double-sided laser shock peening with coating on the microstructure and mechanical properties of annealed Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· Annealed Ti-6Al-4V alloy was subjected to deep cryogenic cycling followed by double-sided laser shock peening with a protective coating.
Result· The combined treatment significantly refined the microstructure and enhanced the strength and hardness of the alloy.
Validation· Validation was performed through microstructural characterization and room-temperature tensile and hardness tests.
