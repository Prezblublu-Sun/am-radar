Statistical average strain energy density fatigue estimation of strut-based metamaterials via synthetic as-built CAD digital twins

Simone Murchio, Raffaele De Biasi, Marcello Laurenti, Nicolò Bonato, Simone Carmignato et al. npj Metamaterials 2026-06-02 10.1038/s44455-026-00030-z
单位: Department of Chemical Engineering Materials Environment, La Sapienza University of Rome, Roma, Italy
通讯: Simone Murchio @ Department of Chemical Engineering Materials Environment, La Sapienza University of Rome, Roma, Italy  |  M. Benedetti @ Department of Industrial Engineering, University of Trento, Trento, Italy
相关性: 该论文直接针对L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V点阵结构的疲劳性能预测，提出了一种基于合成数字孪生的高效方法，与用户关注的力学和疲劳方向高度相关。
边界: The paper focuses on strut-level metamaterial fatigue rather than bulk solid Ti-6Al-4V mechanical properties, but it remains within the L1 scope for additive manufacturing Ti-6Al-4V fatigue.
动机· 为克服L-PBF制造Ti-6Al-4V点阵结构疲劳性能预测的困难。
方法· 提出基于立体显微镜图像的合成CAD数字孪生框架，结合有限元分析和统计应变能密度准则。
结果· 合成模型与CT重建形貌吻合，能有效估计疲劳寿命和预测失效位置。
验证· 与CT重建结果及已有实验疲劳数据对比验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To address the challenge of predicting fatigue performance in L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V lattice structures due to manufacturing imperfections.
Method· The authors propose a framework using stereomicroscopy images to generate synthetic as-built CAD digital twins, coupled with finite element analysis and a statistical strain energy density criterion.
Result· The synthetic models show close morphological agreement with CT-based reconstructions and can estimate fatigue life and failure locations within experimental scatter bands.
Validation· Validation is performed by comparing synthetic models to CT reconstructions and against experimental fatigue data from literature.

Effects of Ambient Oxygen Concentration on Microstructural Evolution and Mechanical Properties of Wire Arc Additively Manufactured Ti-6Al-4V Thin-Walled Components

Shuo Meng, Zhe Zhao, Hongwei Ji, Guangkuo Qin, Yefei Zhou et al. Materials 2026-06-02 10.3390/ma19112347
单位: College of Mechanical Engineering, Yanshan University, Qinhuangdao 066004, China
通讯: Shuo Meng @ College of Mechanical Engineering, Yanshan University, Qinhuangdao 066004, China
相关性: 该论文研究了WAAM Ti-6Al-4V中氧浓度对显微组织和拉伸性能的影响，属于L1方向但不涉及疲劳性能。
边界: No fatigue life or S-N data; focuses on tensile properties and microstructure under varying oxygen levels, not cyclic loading or fatigue crack initiation/growth.
动机· 探究WAAM Ti-6Al-4V中环境氧浓度对显微组织和力学性能的影响
方法· 在可控氧浓度（1,500,1000 ppm）及空气环境下制备薄壁WAAM试样并进行表征与拉伸测试
结果· 控氧范围内氧含量增加使强度上升、塑性下降；空气环境导致严重氧化和性能急剧劣化
验证· 通过OM、SEM、XRD和拉伸、硬度实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how ambient oxygen concentration during WAAM of Ti-6Al-4V affects its microstructure and mechanical properties.
Method· Thin-walled WAAM specimens were fabricated under controlled oxygen levels (1, 500, 1000 ppm) and in ambient air, followed by microstructural characterization and tensile/hardness testing.
Result· Controlled oxygen uptake increased tensile strength (880→940 MPa) and hardness (320→350 HV) while reducing elongation (11.5%→9.5%); ambient air caused severe embrittlement (295 MPa, 1.9%).
Validation· Experimental validation via OM, SEM, XRD, tensile tests, and hardness measurements.

Physics-informed few-shot learning for cross-material relative density prediction in laser powder bed fusion

Prashant Dhakal, Jae Gwang Kim, Aolin Hou, Xiaofei Wu, Shiren Wang Journal of Intelligent Manufacturing 2026-06-02 10.1007/s10845-026-02889-1
单位: Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering, Texas A&M University, College Station, TX, 77843, USA
通讯: Shiren Wang @ Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering, Texas A&M University, College Station, TX, 77843, USA
相关性: 该论文关注LPBF过程中Ti-6Al-4V的相对密度预测，但未涉及力学性能或疲劳行为，与研究方向的核心（力学与疲劳）关联性较低。
边界: The paper predicts relative density via cross-material few-shot learning but does not characterize or model mechanical properties (tensile, fatigue, etc.) or fatigue behavior of Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 为新型合金在有限数据下快速预测LPBF相对密度。
方法· 提出物理信息KNN，利用八维物理约束特征空间实现跨材料预测。
结果· PIKNN在Ti6Al4V上10-shot准确率达58%，优于基线方法。
验证· 使用公开的六种合金1579个LPBF样本进行跨材料验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Enable rapid relative density prediction for new alloys in LPBF with limited experimental data.
Method· Propose Physics-Informed K-Nearest Neighbors (PIKNN) using an eight-dimensional physics-constrained feature space for cross-material prediction.
Result· PIKNN achieves 58.0% accuracy on Ti6Al4V (10-shot) and outperforms prototypical networks and SVM baselines.
Validation· Validated on a public dataset of 1,579 LPBF prints across six alloys under a cross-material paradigm.

单位: Department of Mechatronics Engineering, Kyungnam University, Changwon 51767, Republic of Korea
通讯: Tae‐Hyeon Kim @ Department of Mechatronics Engineering, Kyungnam University, Changwon 51767, Republic of Korea
相关性: 论文主要研究在Ti-6Al-4V基板上沉积Al2O3陶瓷的工艺与微观结构，而非Ti-6Al-4V本身的力学或疲劳性能，与方向焦点无关。
边界: The paper does not investigate the mechanical or fatigue properties of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it focuses on ceramic bead deposition on a Ti-6Al-4V substrate.
动机· 探索在Ti-6Al-4V上沉积Al2O3陶瓷层的工艺基础
方法· 通过DED-LB工艺改变激光功率和扫描速度，分析单道沉积的几何形貌、稀释率和微观结构
结果· 确定了最佳工艺参数为300 W激光功率和400 mm/min扫描速度，稀释率约40.27%
验证· 利用SEM-EDS和XRD对界面微观结构和物相进行了表征
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study establishes baseline process conditions for depositing Al2O3 protective layers on Ti-6Al-4V substrates via DED-LB.
Method· Single Al2O3 beads were deposited at varying laser power (300–700 W) and scan speed (300–700 mm/min), and bead geometry, dilution, and microstructure were characterized.
Result· Optimal parameters (300 W, 400 mm/min, 3 g/min powder feed) yielded the lowest practical dilution of ~40.27% with acceptable bead morphology.
Validation· Microstructural and phase analyses (SEM-EDS, XRD) confirmed the interfacial characteristics under the selected condition.

Geometry and Surface Feature Evaluation in E-PBF Process Using In-Operando Electron Emission Signal

Abdulaziz Alfaifi, Omer A. Alshammery, Toan D. Truong, Haojun You, Mohsen Taheri Andani Materials 2026-06-02 10.3390/ma19112362
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Texas A&M University, College Station, TX 77843, USA
通讯: Abdulaziz Alfaifi @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Texas A&M University, College Station, TX 77843, USA
相关性: 该论文主要关注E-PBF过程中的原位监测方法，而非Ti-6Al-4V的力学或疲劳性能。
边界: The paper focuses on in-situ process monitoring for geometry and surface topography reconstruction, not on mechanical or fatigue properties of Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 探索E-PBF过程中利用电子发射信号原位监测几何与表面形貌的可行性。
方法· 记录点熔过程中的电子发射信号，并应用熔池膨胀校正重建几何特征。
结果· 重建误差低于2.2%，与轮廓仪高度剖面相关性达0.67-0.87。
验证· 通过光学显微镜和轮廓仪测量作为真值验证信号重建准确性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate whether the in-operando electron emission signal during E-PBF spot-melting can directly indicate geometric and topographical features of fabricated parts.
Method· The electron emission signal was recorded during spot-melting of a Ti-6Al-4V spur gear, and a melt-pool dilation correction was applied to reconstruct geometry and surface topography.
Result· After correction, the reconstruction achieved agreement deviations below 2.2% at key features and showed strong correlation (Pearson 0.67–0.87) with profilometer height profiles.
Validation· Ground truth optical microscopy and profilometry measurements were used to validate the signal-based reconstructions.

The Influence of Mechanical and Microstructural Characteristics on the Durability of a Femoral Implant Made of Different Alloys

Ivan Panfilov, EVGENIY SADYRIN, A. L. Nikolaev, Pavel Antipov, Andrey Vasiliev et al. Journal of Functional Biomaterials 2026-06-02 10.3390/jfb17060275
单位: Department of Theoretical and Applied Mechanics, Agribusiness Faculty, Don State Technical University, 1 Gagarin Square, 344003 Rostov-on-Don, Russia
通讯: Ivan Panfilov @ Department of Theoretical and Applied Mechanics, Agribusiness Faculty, Don State Technical University, 1 Gagarin Square,
相关性: 这篇论文是关于股骨植入物的，未涉及增材制造，与用户研究方向不相关。
边界: This paper does not focus on additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it investigates a femoral implant made from conventional Ti-6Al-4V and CoCrMo alloys, with no additive manufacturing processing.
动机· 研究股骨植入体的力学与微观结构特征对其耐久性的影响。
方法· 利用纳米压痕、SEM、EDS等实验表征Ti-6Al-4V和CoCrMo合金，并通过有限元分析评估动态载荷下的应力应变状态。
结果· 动态载荷下CoCrMo的承载能力比Ti-6Al-4V低约30%和21%。
验证· 实验表征结合有限元模拟，但未涉及增材制造验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the influence of mechanical and microstructural characteristics on the durability of a femoral implant made of Ti-6Al-4V and CoCrMo alloys.
Method· Nanoindentation, SEM, EDS, and finite element analysis were used to characterize and simulate the mechanical response under dynamic loading.
Result· Under dynamic loading, the load-bearing capacity of CoCrMo is approximately 30% and 21% lower than that of reference and experimentally characterized Ti-6Al-4V, respectively.
Validation· The study combined experimental characterization (nanoindentation, microscopy) with numerical simulation, but no additive manufacturing context was considered.
