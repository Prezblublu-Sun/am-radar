Crystal plasticity-based modelling of crack initiation and propagation driven by microstructural porosity in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V

S.S. Biriaie, H. Ben Boubaker, M. Nouari, P. Lohmuller, P. Laheurte Mechanics of Materials 2026-05-22 10.1016/j.mechmat.2026.105734
单位: Université de Lorraine, CNRS, LEM3, Arts et Métiers ParisTech, Metz 57070, France
通讯: H. Ben Boubaker @ Université de Lorraine, CNRS, LEM3, Arts et Métiers ParisTech, Metz 57070, France
相关性: 本文直接针对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的裂纹萌生与扩展，通过晶体塑性建模研究微孔缺陷的影响，符合L1方向的力学与疲劳性能需求。
边界: No experimental mechanical test data (e.g., tensile or fatigue S-N curves) provided; purely computational study without quantitative experimental validation.
动机· 研究增材制造Ti-6Al-4V中微孔驱动裂纹萌生与扩展的机制。
方法· 采用晶体塑性有限元方法建立微孔缺陷模型，模拟疲劳裂纹演化。
结果· 揭示了孔隙特征对裂纹路径和寿命的影响规律。
验证· 通过数值模拟与理论比较验证模型可靠性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand the role of microporosity in initiating and propagating fatigue cracks in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· A crystal plasticity finite element framework is developed to model crack initiation and propagation driven by microstructural pores.
Result· The model captures the effect of pore size, shape, and distribution on crack formation and growth.
Validation· The simulation results are validated through numerical benchmarks and mesh sensitivity studies.

ROLE OF CRITICAL ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING DEFECTS IN MICROSTRUCTURE FORMATION AND MECHANICAL BEHAVIOR OF TI–6AL–4V ALLOY

Svitlana Kyrylakha Collection of Scientific Publications NUS 2026-05-22 10.15589/znp2026.1(503).1.4
相关性: 该综述系统总结了后处理（热处理、热等静压）对Ti-6Al-4V疲劳性能的影响，与您的方向直接相关但缺乏原创实验数据。
边界: This paper is a review of existing literature, not a novel experimental or modeling study on a specific post-processing route; it synthesizes prior work without providing new data.
动机· 系统性分析增材制造Ti-6Al-4V合金中关键缺陷对疲劳行为的影响
方法· 综述机械测试、断口分析、CT及热处理研究，聚焦缺陷特征与疲劳关系
结果· 气孔和未熔合缺陷主导裂纹萌生，后处理（退火、热等静压）改善微观结构并提高裂纹扩展抗力
验证· 基于已有文献数据的综述性分析，无新实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· This review systematically analyzes how critical additive manufacturing defects affect the fatigue behavior and reliability of Ti-6Al-4V components.
Method· The study synthesizes recent works on mechanical testing, fractography, CT, and heat treatment to correlate defect characteristics with fatigue performance.
Result· Gas porosity and lack-of-fusion defects are identified as primary crack initiation sites, and post-processing treatments such as annealing and HIP improve microstructure and crack growth resistance.
Validation· The paper is a literature review without new experimental or numerical validation.

Study on Surface Micro-Texture of Ti2AlNb-based Alloy Cladding Layer by Ultrasonic and Temperature Field Assisted Milling

Ruikang LI, Guofu Gao, Wenbin MA Academic Journal of Emerging Technologies 2026-05-22 10.63313/ajet.9059
单位: School of Mechanical and Power Engineering, Henan Polytechnic University, Jiaozuo, 454003, China
通讯: Ruikang LI @ School of Mechanical and Power Engineering, Henan Polytechnic University, Jiaozuo, 454003, China
相关性: 论文主要研究Ti2AlNb合金而非Ti-6Al-4V，且关注表面纹理而非力学性能。
边界: The paper does not study Ti-6Al-4V mechanical or fatigue performance; it focuses on Ti2AlNb cladding layer surface texture.
动机· Ti2AlNb基合金难加工，需改善表面质量
方法· 激光熔覆制备Ti2AlNb层后超声-温度场辅助铣削
结果· 200℃预热+2μm振幅获得最优表面形貌
验证· 通过表面粗糙度测量和SEM观察验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· Ti2AlNb-based alloy is difficult to machine, requiring improved surface quality.
Method· A Ti2AlNb cladding layer was laser-cladded on Ti-6Al-4V, then ultrasonically and thermally assisted milled.
Result· Optimal surface morphology and texture were achieved at 200°C preheating with 2μm ultrasonic amplitude.
Validation· Surface roughness measurements and SEM observation were used for validation.
