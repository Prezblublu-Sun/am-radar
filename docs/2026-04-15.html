Comparison of the Effects of LSP Treatment on Wrought and Additive Manufactured Ti-6Al-4V Samples

Irvin Alejandro Guillen-Virgen, Gilberto Gomez-Rosas, Eduardo Castañeda-Paredes, Martha Guadalupe Arredondo Bravo, O. Klimova-Korsmik et al. Materials 2026-04-15 10.3390/ma19081582
单位: University Centre for Exact Sciences and Engineering (CUCEI), University of Guadalajara, Blvd. Marcelino García Barragan #1421, Guadalajara 44430, Mexico
通讯: Irvin Alejandro Guillen-Virgen @ University Centre for Exact Sciences and Engineering (CUCEI), University of Guadalajara, Blvd. Marcelino García Barragan
相关性: 该论文直接研究了激光冲击强化（LSP）这一后处理工艺对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V力学性能的影响，包括残余应力、显微硬度和微观结构，完全符合方向要求。
边界: Does not include fatigue testing or long-term cyclic data, but covers residual stress and microhardness as mechanical performance indicators.
动机· 比较激光冲击强化对锻造和增材制造Ti-6Al-4V合金表面完整性的影响。
方法· 采用特定激光参数分别处理锻造和增材制造试样，并进行残余应力、显微硬度和微观结构表征。
结果· 两种工艺试样均获得高达-800 MPa的残余压应力、22%的显微硬度提升和晶粒细化。
验证· 通过实验测量残余应力、显微硬度和微观结构验证效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare the effects of laser shock peening (LSP) on the surface integrity of wrought and additive manufactured Ti-6Al-4V alloys.
Method· LSP treatment was applied to both wrought and AM Ti-6Al-4V samples using identical parameters, followed by characterization of residual stress, microhardness, and microstructure.
Result· LSP induced compressive residual stresses up to -800 MPa, increased microhardness by 22%, and refined grain size from 18.16 μm to 5.54 μm in both manufacturing routes.
Validation· Experimental measurements of residual stress, microhardness, and microstructure were conducted to validate the effectiveness of LSP.

Motivation· To develop an efficient method for calibrating DEM parameters of AM powders using a rotating drum experiment and Froude number scaling.

Validation· Validation was performed by comparing DEM simulations with experimental data from a revolution device for powder flow.

Validation· Performance was validated through contact angle measurement, bacterial colony counting, and artificial saliva immersion tests.
