Comparison of the Effects of LSP Treatment on Wrought and Additive Manufactured Ti-6Al-4V Samples

Irvin Alejandro Guillen-Virgen, Gilberto Gomez-Rosas, Eduardo Castañeda-Paredes, Martha Guadalupe Arredondo Bravo, O. Klimova-Korsmik et al. Materials 2026-04-15 10.3390/ma19081582
单位: University Centre for Exact Sciences and Engineering (CUCEI), University of Guadalajara, Blvd. Marcelino García Barragan #1421, Guadalajara 44430, Mexico
通讯: Irvin Alejandro Guillen-Virgen @ University Centre for Exact Sciences and Engineering (CUCEI), University of Guadalajara, Blvd. Marcelino García Barragan
相关性: 该论文直接研究激光冲击强化对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的残余应力、显微硬度及微观结构的影响，完全符合后处理控制方向。
边界: No tensile or fatigue data reported; only residual stress, microhardness, and microstructure.
动机· 比较激光冲击强化对锻造和增材制造Ti-6Al-4V合金的影响。
方法· 对两种制备方式的样品进行相同参数LSP处理，测试残余应力、显微硬度和微观结构。
结果· LSP后残余压应力达-800 MPa，显微硬度提升22%，晶粒从18.16 μm细化至5.54 μm。
验证· 通过残余应力、硬度和金相实验验证结果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare the effects of laser shock peening (LSP) on the surface integrity of wrought and additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· Samples from both manufacturing routes were treated with identical LSP parameters (6 ns pulse, 1064 nm, 2500 pulses/cm², 0.750 J, 10 Hz) and characterized by residual stress, microhardness, and microstructure analysis.
Result· LSP induced compressive residual stresses up to −800 MPa, increased microhardness by 22%, and refined grain size from 18.16 μm to 5.54 μm.
Validation· Experimental measurements of residual stress, microhardness, and grain size were used to validate the treatment effectiveness.

Preparation of Composite Resin Coatings and Its Performance Improvement on Ti-Based Dental Implants

Siqi Zhu, Chao Yao, Chao Yao, Xiaopan Li, Yifan Yuan et al. Coatings 2026-04-15 10.3390/coatings16040475
单位: College of Mechanical Engineering, Jiangsu University of Science and Technology, Zhenjiang 212000, China
通讯: Chao Yao @ Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Jinan Stamotological Hospital, Jinan 250001, China  |  Jing Xia @ College of Mechanical Engineering, Jiangsu University of Science and Technology, Zhenjiang 212000, China  |  Chun Yao @ Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Jinan Stamotological Hospital, Jinan 250001, China
相关性: 论文主要关注钛基牙种植体的生物活性和抗菌性能，未涉及增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学或疲劳性能。
边界: No mechanical or fatigue testing of Ti-6Al-4V; focuses on coating bioactivity and antibacterial properties, not on additive manufacturing or base material mechanical performance.
动机· 提高钛合金牙种植体的生物活性和抗菌能力，延长使用寿命
方法· 结合表面微织构与改性光固化复合树脂涂层对TC4进行表面改性
结果· 微织构增大接触面积42.5%，复合树脂涂层抗菌率达74.6%并具有再矿化能力
验证· 通过接触角测量、细菌培养和人工唾液浸泡实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to improve the bioactivity and antibacterial properties of titanium dental implants to prevent interfacial loosening and failure.
Method· Surface microtexturing combined with a light-cured composite resin coating containing Ti-HA/TiO2 was applied to Ti-6Al-4V substrates.
Result· Microtexturing increased coating-substrate contact area by 42.5%, and the composite coating achieved an antibacterial rate of 74.6% with visible mineralization deposits after 3 days in artificial saliva.
Validation· Validation was performed through contact angle measurement, bacterial colony counting, and immersion tests in artificial saliva.

Multiphysics investigation of thermo-mechanical behavior and mechanisms in small-hole tapping of Ti6Al4V alloy

Wei Gao, 梁新富, Lei Lin, Xiangkun Meng, Wenjing Zhang et al. Mechanical sciences 2026-04-15 10.5194/ms-17-427-2026
单位: Aerospace Precision Products Co., Ltd. Tianjin 300300, China
通讯: Wei Gao @ Aerospace Precision Products Co., Ltd. Tianjin 300300, China
相关性: 该论文研究Ti6Al4V攻丝过程的热力学行为，未涉及增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的后处理对疲劳或力学性能的影响。
边界: The paper does not study post-processing effects on mechanical performance of AM Ti-6Al-4V; it focuses on tapping process mechanics in conventional Ti6Al4V without any experimental validation of fatigue or tensile properties.
动机· 揭示Ti6Al4V合金小孔攻丝过程中的热机械行为及机理。
方法· 采用热力耦合有限元模拟分析攻丝过程的力、温度和压力场。
结果· 得到轴向力三阶段变化、径向力周期性载荷及温度场分布规律。
验证· 通过多物理场仿真验证，无实验对比。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to reveal the thermo-mechanical behavior and mechanisms during small-hole tapping of Ti6Al4V alloy.
Method· A thermo-mechanical coupled finite element simulation was employed to analyze cutting forces, contact pressure, torque, and temperature distributions.
Result· The axial force shows a three-stage pattern, radial force exhibits periodic alternating loads, and localized high-temperature zones form at cutting edges.
Validation· The work is validated only through simulation; no experimental measurements are provided.
