Porosity Measurement for HIPped AM Ti6Al4V Samples – A Practical Comparison between Archimedes Method and Micro-CT

Osman Tuna Gökgöz, Hüdayim Başak, Olcay Ersel Canyurt, Katri Kakko, Chad Beamer Savunma Bilimleri Dergisi 2025-12-10 10.17134/khosbd.1811963
单位: GAZİ ÜNİVERSİTESİ
通讯: Osman Tuna Gökgöz @ GAZİ ÜNİVERSİTESİ
相关性: 本文仅比较孔隙率测量方法，未提供热等静压对力学性能影响的直接数据。
边界: This paper does not measure fatigue or mechanical performance; it only compares porosity measurement techniques (Archimedes vs Micro-CT) for HIPped Ti-6Al-4V, so it does not directly address how post-processing modifies mechanical properties.
动机· 比较热等静压AM Ti-6Al-4V样品孔隙率测量方法的实用性
方法· 使用阿基米德法和微CT对四种不同后处理条件的样品进行孔隙率测量与对比
结果· 两种方法结果一致，阿基米德法快速经济，微CT提供孔隙空间分布信息
验证· 通过两种测量方法的相互验证确保结果可靠
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to compare the practical effectiveness of Archimedes method and micro-CT for measuring porosity in HIPped additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V samples.
Method· Porosity was measured using both Archimedes method (all 16 samples) and micro-CT (one representative per group) for four post-processing conditions: three HIP variants and as-built.
Result· Both techniques showed consistent correlation; Archimedes method provides faster bulk density, while micro-CT reveals pore morphology, size, and spatial distribution.
Validation· The agreement between Archimedes and micro-CT measurements validates the reliability of both methods for porosity assessment.

Machinability Data to Sustainability Metrics: Cradle-to-Gate Life Cycle Assessment of Lubrication-Assisted Drilling of Cold Metal Transfer-Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing Ti-6Al-4V

Prameet Vats, Tarang Lotwala, Navneet Khanna, Avinash Kumar, Kishor Kumar Gajrani Journal of Tribology 2025-12-10 10.1115/1.4070625
单位: Indian Institute of Information Technology, Design and Manufacturing Department of Mechanical Engineering, , Kancheepuram,   , ,
通讯: Prameet Vats @ Indian Institute of Information Technology, Design and Manufacturing Department of Mechanical Engineering, , Kancheepura
相关性: 本文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的后处理（钻孔），但未研究其对疲劳或力学性能的影响，与L2重点不符。
边界: This paper does not investigate the effect of post-processing on fatigue or mechanical performance; it focuses solely on machinability and life cycle assessment.
动机· 评估WAAM Ti-6Al-4V钻孔加工中润滑策略的可持续性。
方法· 使用单/混合纳米流体在WAAM和锻态Ti-6Al-4V上进行钻孔实验，结合从门到门的生命周期评估。
结果· 混合纳米流体降低能耗约30%、切削力超40%、磨损约50%，但WAAM加工碳排放高于锻态。
验证· 通过钻孔实验和LCA验证润滑效果与环境影响。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to assess the sustainability of lubrication strategies during drilling of WAAM Ti-6Al-4V, bridging machinability with environmental impacts.
Method· Drilling experiments were conducted on WAAM and wrought Ti-6Al-4V using mono- and hybrid nanofluids (graphene nanoplatelets and hexagonal boron nitride in jojoba oil), coupled with a cradle-to-gate life cycle assessment.
Result· Hybrid nanofluids reduced power consumption by nearly 30%, cutting forces by over 40%, and flank wear by around 50% compared to dry conditions, while WAAM components produced higher CO2 emissions than wrought counterparts.
Validation· Experimental drilling tests and LCA were used to validate the improvements in machinability and environmental performance.
