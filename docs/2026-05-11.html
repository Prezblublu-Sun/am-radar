Investigation into the microstructure and fatigue property of selective electronic beaming melted TC4 alloy with different heating treatments

Jiawang Lu, Shanglei Yang, Junhang Bi Physica Scripta 2026-05-11 10.1088/1402-4896/ae6bbf
单位: Shanghai University of Engineering Science - Songjiang Campus, Shanghai University of Engineering Science, Shanghai, Shanghai, 201620, China
通讯: Jiawang Lu @ Shanghai University of Engineering Science - Songjiang Campus, Shanghai University of Engineering Science, Shanghai, Sha
相关性: 该论文研究了电子束选区熔化成形TC4钛合金经固溶时效处理后的微观组织与疲劳性能，与用户关注的AM Ti-6Al-4V后处理控制方向直接相关。
边界: The paper is fully core: it directly studies the effect of a post-processing treatment (solution and aging) on Ti-6Al-4V microstructure and fatigue performance.
动机· 电子束选区熔化成形TC4钛合金的力学性能可靠性是其批量生产的前提。
方法· 系统研究了固溶时效处理对TC4合金微观组织和疲劳性能的影响，包括不同温度下的组织演变。
结果· 固溶时效处理提高了合金的拉伸强度、塑性和疲劳寿命，950°C时篮筐组织转变为层状组织。
验证· 通过微观组织观察、硬度测试、拉伸和疲劳实验验证了性能变化。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The reliability of mechanical properties of electron beam selective melting (SEBM) Ti-6Al-4V is a prerequisite for mass production, motivating the study of post-processing effects.
Method· A systematic investigation was conducted on the effect of solution and aging treatment (SAT) on the microstructure and fatigue properties of SEBM TC4 titanium alloy, with varying solution temperatures.
Result· SAT enhanced tensile strength, plasticity, and fatigue life; at 950°C the basketweave structure transformed to a lamellar structure, accompanied by coarsening and reduced hardness.
Validation· Validation was performed through microstructural analysis, hardness testing, and uniaxial tensile and fatigue experiments.

Synergistic enhancement of tensile and high-cycle fatigue properties in wire direct energy deposition Ti-6Al-4V via hot isostatic pressing + solution treatment and aging treatment

Jianfei Zhang, Siyu Zhou, Tianwu Zhang, Bin WU, Guang Yang International Journal of Fatigue 2026-05-11 10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2026.109719
相关性: 该论文直接研究了热等静压和固溶时效处理对激光定向能量沉积Ti-6Al-4V合金拉伸和高周疲劳性能的协同增强作用，完全符合后处理控制方向。
边界: The paper does not address any Layer 3 implant structure or biomedical context; it is purely a post-processing study on DED Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 探究热等静压与固溶时效复合处理对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V力学性能的协同增效机制。
方法· 对线材定向能量沉积Ti-6Al-4V进行热等静压+固溶+时效处理，并测试拉伸和高周疲劳性能。
结果· 复合处理显著提升了材料的抗拉强度和疲劳寿命，实现了微观组织优化与缺陷愈合。
验证· 通过标准拉伸和疲劳试验验证力学性能改善。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the synergistic effect of combining hot isostatic pressing with solution and aging treatments on the mechanical properties of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Wire directed energy deposited Ti-6Al-4V specimens were subjected to a combined post-processing route of hot isostatic pressing followed by solution treatment and aging, and then characterized for tensile and high-cycle fatigue behaviors.
Result· The combined treatment yielded a simultaneous improvement in tensile strength and high-cycle fatigue life due to enhanced microstructure and porosity closure.
Validation· Mechanical validation was performed via standard tensile and high-cycle fatigue testing.}

Motivation· To systematically synthesize the current experimental data on wire arc additive manufacturing of Ti-6Al-4V, focusing on quantitative relationships between process parameters, microstructure, and mechanical properties.
