Features of the Ti-6Al-4V microstructure and phase composition formation by changing the thermal cycle during the process of direct energy deposition

Marina Gushchina, Dmytrо Anisimov, Zh.S. Shabunina, Svetlana Shalnova, O.G. Klimova-Korsmik et al. Materials Characterization 2025-06-25 10.1016/j.matchar.2025.115330
单位: Saint-Petersburg State Marine University, World-class Science Center, Russia
通讯: Marina Gushchina @ Saint-Petersburg State Marine University, World-class Science Center, Russia
相关性: 该论文研究了Ti-6Al-4V DED过程中热循环对微观结构和力学性能的影响，与用户关注的增材制造Ti-6Al-4V力学性能相关，但未涉及疲劳性能。
边界: No fatigue testing or S-N data provided; the paper focuses on microstructure evolution and strengthening mechanisms rather than cyclic/fatigue behavior.
动机· 研究热循环变化对Ti-6Al-4V DED微观结构和相组成的影响。
方法· 引入层间冷却停顿改变热循环，使用SEM、TEM、XRD分析微观结构。
结果· 增加停顿时间形成更平衡的组织，提高强度但降低塑性。
验证· 通过显微观察和力学测试验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of thermal cycle variations on the microstructure and phase composition of Ti-6Al-4V during direct energy deposition.
Method· Cooling pauses were introduced between layers to alter the thermal cycle, and microstructural analysis was performed using SEM, TEM, and XRD.
Result· Longer pauses led to a more equilibrium structure with increased alpha phase lamellae, resulting in higher strength but reduced plasticity.
Validation· Experimental validation through microscopy and mechanical testing.

High-Frequency Ultrasonic Impact Treatment Effect on Fatigue Properties of TC4 Titanium Alloy Laser Wire Filling-Welded Joints

Aiyong Cui, Jialei Zhao, Borong Shan, Haodong Liu, Weiqi Sun et al. Archives of Metallurgy and Materials 2025-06-25 10.24425/amm.2025.153455
单位: Qingdao Campus of Naval Aviat ion University, Qingdao, 266041, China
通讯: Aiyong Cui @ Qingdao Campus of Naval Aviat ion University, Qingdao, 266041, China
相关性: 该论文研究了TC4钛合金激光填丝焊接接头经超声冲击处理后的疲劳性能，属于后处理工艺对力学性能的影响，而非原始打印态性能，因此与本层方向关联度低。
边界: The paper focuses on the effect of ultrasonic impact treatment (a post-processing technique) on fatigue properties of laser-welded Ti-6Al-4V joints, not on as-built or process-state mechanical/fatigue characterization of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 提升TC4钛合金激光填丝焊接接头的疲劳性能。
方法· 对焊接接头进行高频超声冲击处理，并通过无损检测对比处理前后的组织与性能。
结果· 超声冲击后疲劳极限提高约29.4%，表面微硬度提高约12%，裂纹扩展机制改变。
验证· 通过显微硬度、静载拉伸和循环疲劳试验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the fatigue performance of laser wire-filled welded TC4 titanium alloy joints.
Method· High-frequency ultrasonic impact treatment was applied to the weld, followed by non-destructive characterization of phase, microstructure, microhardness, and mechanical properties.
Result· The fatigue limit increased by about 29.4% after UIT, with refined surface grains and altered crack propagation behavior.
Validation· Validation was performed through microhardness testing, static tensile tests, and cyclic fatigue tests.

Influence of 3D-Printed PEEK on the Tribo-Corrosion Performance of Ti6Al4V Biomedical Alloy

Dominik Jonas Federl, A. AL-Rjoub Lubricants 2025-06-25 10.3390/lubricants13070283
单位: Polymer-Tribology Group, Division of Machine Elements, Department of Engineering Sciences and Mathematics, Luleå University of Technology, Campus Porsön, 971 87 Luleå, Sweden
通讯: A. AL-Rjoub @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Coimbra, CEMMPRE, ARISE, Rua Luís Reis Santos, 3030-788 Coimbra, Por
相关性: 该论文主要研究Ti6Al4V合金与3D打印PEEK的摩擦腐蚀行为，不涉及增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学或疲劳性能。
边界: The paper does not focus on additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it studies tribo-corrosion of Ti6Al4V against PEEK, without mechanical/fatigue characterization of the Ti6Al4V itself.
动机· 研究3D打印PEEK与Ti6Al4V合金的摩擦腐蚀行为，评估其在模拟生理环境下的耐久性。
方法· 通过动电位极化和开路电位测试，分析Ti6Al4V合金与3D打印PEEK对磨时的磨损机制和电化学响应。
结果· Ti6Al4V合金具有优异耐腐蚀性，滑动条件下腐蚀性能增强，表现出混合磨损机制。
验证· 实验验证了Ti6Al4V合金在PBS溶液中的电化学稳定性和摩擦学行为。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to evaluate the tribo-corrosion behavior of Ti6Al4V alloy when sliding against 3D-printed PEEK in simulated physiological conditions.
Method· Potentiodynamic polarization and open-circuit potential measurements were used to characterize the wear mechanisms and electrochemical responses.
Result· Ti6Al4V exhibited excellent corrosion resistance, enhanced under sliding, with mixed abrasive and adhesive wear when tested against 3D-printed PEEK.
Validation· Experimental tribo-corrosion tests in PBS solution validated the electrochemical stability and wear behavior.
