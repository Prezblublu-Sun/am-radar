SHAPE VARIABILITY OF THE MEDULLARY CANAL OF THE HUMAN PROXIMAL FEMUR

Stefan Bracher, Benjamin Haas, Elhadi Sariali, Philippe K. Zysset Orthopaedic Proceedings 2025-10-22 10.1302/1358-992x.2025.9.043
单位: University of Bern, ARTORG Center for Biomedical Engineering Research, Bern, Switzerland
通讯: Stefan Bracher @ University of Bern, ARTORG Center for Biomedical Engineering Research, Bern, Switzerland
相关性: 该论文研究了股骨近端髓腔的形态变异性，为个性化假体设计提供解剖学背景，但未涉及假体设计、有限元分析或应力遮挡。
边界: This paper only characterizes proximal femoral medullary canal shape variability using PCA and does not involve implant design, FEA, stress shielding, micromotion, or any biomechanical evaluation.
动机· 了解股骨近端髓腔形态变异性以优化假体设计。
方法· 对763例CT扫描进行几何参数分析和主成分分析。
结果· 髓腔形态存在性别和年龄差异，前三个主成分解释68.4%变异。
验证· 未进行生物力学验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To characterize proximal femoral medullary canal shape variability and its relationship to age and sex for improving cementless stem design.
Method· Geometric parameters and principal component analysis were applied to CT scans of 763 femurs to quantify canal shape.
Result· Significant sex- and age-specific variations were found, with the first three PCs explaining 68.4% of total shape variance.
Validation· No biomechanical or implant-specific validation was performed.

Evaluation of the potential of FDM, MJF, and SLS printing technologies to manufacture reproducible and high-cycle fatigue resistant polymer springs

Lukas Derad, Jens Harmeling, Peter P. Pott Discover Mechanical Engineering 2025-10-22 10.1007/s44245-025-00143-1
单位: Institute of Medical Device Technology, University of Stuttgart, Pfaffenwaldring 9, 70569, Stuttgart, Germany
通讯: Lukas Derad @ Institute of Medical Device Technology, University of Stuttgart, Pfaffenwaldring 9, 70569, Stuttgart, Germany
相关性: 该论文研究了聚合物弹簧的增材制造疲劳性能，但与用户关注的生物医学植入物疲劳方向不直接相关。
边界: This paper does not involve biomedical implant design, metal AM, osseointegration, or any biological context.
动机· 评估三种3D打印技术制造聚合物弹簧的疲劳性能与可重复性
方法· 通过实验比较FDM、MJF和SLS技术打印的弹簧刚度及高周疲劳行为
结果· SLS打印的弹簧在可重复性和疲劳性能上最优，所有弹簧均通过10万次循环。
验证· 通过拉伸测试、几何测量和疲劳测试验证力学性能
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study evaluates the suitability of three additive manufacturing technologies for producing polymer springs with reproducible stiffness and high-cycle fatigue resistance.
Method· FDM, MJF, and SLS were used to fabricate three spring designs, and their mechanical anisotropy, geometric reproducibility, and fatigue life were experimentally compared.
Result· SLS-printed springs demonstrated the best combination of geometric reproducibility and fatigue performance, surviving 100,000 cycles with minimal force loss.
Validation· Validation included tensile tests for anisotropy assessment, dimensional measurements, and fatigue testing up to 100,000 cycles.
