Microstructure and Mechanical Properties of Ti-6Al-4V Fabricated by Laser Powder Bed Fusion Using Plasma-Spheroidized and Gas-Atomized Powders

Yunfei Liu, Zhenhua Hao, Rulong Ma, Yongchun Shu, Jilin He Metals and Materials International 2026-02-26 10.1007/s12540-025-02131-2
单位: School of Material Science and Engineering, Zhengzhou University, Zhengzhou , 450001, China
通讯: Zhenhua Hao @ National Key Laboratory of Special Rare Metal Materials, Zhengzhou University, Zhengzhou , 450001, China
相关性: 该论文直接研究激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V的微观结构和力学性能，完全契合L1方向重点。
边界: 该论文未涵盖疲劳寿命或疲劳裂纹萌生/扩展的定量数据，也未涉及后处理或植入物结构。
动机· 比较等离子球化与气雾化粉末对LPBF Ti-6Al-4V微观结构和力学性能的影响
方法· 采用激光粉末床熔融技术制备试样，进行显微组织和力学性能测试
结果· 等离子球化粉末制备的试样具有更优的致密度和力学性能
验证· 通过拉伸试验和硬度测量验证力学性能差异
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the influence of plasma-spheroidized versus gas-atomized powders on the microstructure and mechanical properties of Ti-6Al-4V fabricated by laser powder bed fusion.
Method· Samples were fabricated via LPBF using both powder types, followed by microstructural characterization and mechanical testing (tensile, hardness).
Result· Samples from plasma-spheroidized powder exhibited higher density and improved mechanical properties compared to those from gas-atomized powder.
Validation· Mechanical performance differences were validated through tensile tests and hardness measurements.

Creep behaviour of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V alloy using small specimen testing techniques

Mathieu Lalé, Petr Dymáček, Martin Abendroth, Arnaud Proietti, Yannick Thébault et al. Materials Science and Engineering A 2026-02-26 10.1016/j.msea.2026.150007
单位: Univ Toulouse, Toulouse INP, CNRS, CIRIMAT, 4 Allée Emile Monso, 31030 TOULOUSE, France
通讯: Mathieu Lalé @ Univ Toulouse, Toulouse INP, CNRS, CIRIMAT, 4 Allée Emile Monso, 31030 TOULOUSE, France  |  Bernard Viguier @ Univ Toulouse, Toulouse INP, CNRS, CIRIMAT, 4 Allée Emile Monso, 31030 TOULOUSE, France
相关性: 本文直接研究激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V的蠕变行为，属于增材制造Ti-6Al-4V基础力学性能范畴。
边界: This paper focuses on creep behavior rather than fatigue or tensile properties, but it still covers mechanical performance of as-built AM Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 研究增材制造Ti-6Al-4V在中等温度下的蠕变行为。
方法· 采用小型冲杆蠕变试验和单轴蠕变试验，结合微观表征和有限元模拟。
结果· 发现纳米晶通过α'马氏体碎片化旋转形成，滑动变形使应变累积。
验证· 通过实验蠕变测试和有限元模拟验证结果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the creep behavior of as-built PBF-LB/M Ti-6Al-4V at moderate temperatures using small specimen testing techniques.
Method· Small punch creep tests and uniaxial creep tests were conducted on miniature specimens, supported by microstructural characterization and finite element simulations.
Result· Nanograins formed via fragmentation and rotation of α' martensite lamellae, enabling high strain through grain boundary sliding.
Validation· Experimental creep tests and finite element modeling provided validation of the observed mechanisms.

Dielectric Barrier Discharge Plasma Jet Treatment on Additively Manufactured Biomedical Implants for Improved Surface Wettability, In Vitro Bone Mineralization, and Bacterial Decontamination

Shayan Bayki, Komal Avinash Joshi, Pushpendra Kumar, Aryan Bhardwaj, Jayesh Bellare et al. Journal of Manufacturing Science and Engineering 2026-02-26 10.1115/1.4071231
单位: Indian Institute of Technology Bombay Department of Mechanical Engineering, , ,   ,
通讯: Shayan Bayki @ Indian Institute of Technology Bombay Department of Mechanical Engineering, , ,   ,
相关性: 该论文主要关注增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的表面等离子体处理，以改善润湿性和生物相容性，未涉及力学或疲劳性能。
边界: The paper focuses on surface wettability, bone mineralization, and bacterial decontamination via plasma treatment, with no mechanical or fatigue characterization of Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 改善增材制造Ti-6Al-4V植入体表面的润湿性和生物相容性。
方法· 使用大气压介质阻挡放电等离子体射流处理样品表面。
结果· 处理2秒后接触角显著降低，骨矿化增加45%。
验证· 通过接触角测量、XPS、细胞相容性和细菌实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance surface wettability, biocompatibility, and bacterial decontamination of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V ELI implants for improved osseointegration.
Method· An atmospheric pressure dielectric barrier discharge (DBD) plasma jet was used to treat the implant surfaces.
Result· Significant water contact angle reduction after 2 s treatment and 45% increase in bone mineralization were observed.
Validation· Contact angle goniometry, XPS, cytocompatibility, and bacterial decontamination assays were performed.

Effect of sintering temperature on microstructure, grain boundary characterization and mechanical behaviour of hot-press sintered Ti-6Al-4V alloy

K Sakthivel Rajan, U Narendra Kumar, A. Raja Annamalai Materials Today Communications 2026-02-26 10.1016/j.mtcomm.2026.114922
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Vellore Institute of Technology, Vellore, 632014, Tamil Nadu, India
通讯: A. Raja Annamalai @ Centre for Innovative Manufacturing Research, Vellore Institute of Technology, Vellore, 632014, Tamil Nadu, India
相关性: 这篇论文采用热压烧结而非增材制造工艺，不属于L1方向关注的Ti-6Al-4V增材制造力学与疲劳性能范围。
边界: The paper uses hot-press sintering (a powder metallurgy technique) rather than an additive manufacturing process (LPBF/SLM/EBM/DED), so it is outside the scope of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V mechanical/fatigue characterization.
动机· 研究热压烧结温度对Ti-6Al-4V合金微观结构和力学性能的影响。
方法· 在800-1100°C下对预合金粉末进行热压烧结，并采用OM、FESEM、EBSD和拉伸测试进行表征。
结果· 900°C烧结获得最佳综合力学性能，抗拉强度达944 MPa，更高温度因晶粒粗化导致性能下降。
验证· 通过实验拉伸测试和微观结构分析验证温度影响。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the effect of sintering temperature during hot pressing on the microstructural evolution and mechanical behavior of Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· Pre-alloyed Ti-6Al-4V powder was hot-pressed at 800–1100°C under 45 MPa in vacuum, and characterized by optical microscopy, FESEM, EBSD, and tensile tests.
Result· A tensile strength of 944 MPa with good ductility was achieved at 900°C, while higher temperatures caused grain coarsening and degraded mechanical properties.
Validation· Mechanical testing and microstructural analysis validated the optimal sintering temperature.

Quantifying Stress Shielding in Dental Implants: A Comparative Finite Element Study of Titanium, CFR-PEEK, and Ceramic Materials

单位: Department of Mechanics, Mathematics and Management, Polytechnic University of Bari, 70125 Bari, Italy
通讯: Mario Ceddia @ Department of Mechanics, Mathematics and Management, Polytechnic University of Bari, 70125 Bari, Italy
相关性: 本文研究牙科植入物，而非髋关节股骨柄，与用户方向不符。
边界: The paper studies dental implants, not femoral/hip stems, and thus falls outside the specified direction of Ti-6Al-4V femoral stem design.
动机· 比较不同材料牙种植体对应力遮挡的影响
方法· 对Ti-6Al-4V、Y-TZP和CFR-PEEK种植体进行有限元分析
结果· 高刚度种植体增加嵴顶应力集中和加深种植体周围遮挡
验证· 仅有限元模拟，无实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare stress shielding effects of different materials for dental implants.
Method· Finite element analysis was performed on Ti-6Al-4V, Y-TZP, and CFR-PEEK one-piece implants.
Result· Higher-stiffness implants increased crestal stress concentration and deepened peri-implant shielding.
Validation· Only finite element simulation, no experimental validation.
