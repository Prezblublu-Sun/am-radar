A defect-based critical plane method for multiaxial high cycle fatigue life prediction in L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V alloy

Xiaofan Zhang, Jinghao Yang, Yingyu Wang, Shuai Gong International Journal of Fatigue 2025-07-27 10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2025.109199
单位: College of Aerospace Engineering, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Nanjing 210016, China
通讯: Yingyu Wang @ College of Aerospace Engineering, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Nanjing 210016, China
相关性: 该论文直接针对激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V合金的多轴高周疲劳寿命预测，完全符合L1层的力学与疲劳性能评估方向。
边界: 该论文主要聚焦于疲劳寿命预测模型，缺乏对实际零件或结构件的力学性能宏观验证。
动机· 为增材制造Ti-6Al-4V合金提供更准确的多轴高周疲劳寿命预测方法
方法· 提出基于缺陷的临界平面法，结合缺陷特征和临界平面损伤准则预测疲劳寿命
结果· 新方法能有效考虑缺陷对疲劳寿命的影响，预测精度优于传统方法
验证· 与激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V合金的多轴疲劳实验数据对比验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a more accurate multiaxial high-cycle fatigue life prediction method for additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V considering process-induced defects.
Method· A defect-based critical plane approach is proposed, incorporating defect characteristics (size, location, morphology) into a critical plane damage criterion to predict fatigue life.
Result· The proposed method effectively captures the influence of defects on fatigue life and shows improved prediction accuracy compared to conventional critical plane methods.
Validation· The model is validated against multiaxial fatigue experimental data from L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V specimens reported in the literature.

On the nature of variant selection along build direction in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V walls

Vikrant Saumitra, Avinash Gonnabattula, V. Anil Kumar, Anand K. Kanjarla Materialia 2025-07-27 10.1016/j.mtla.2025.102500
单位: Department of Metallurgical & Materials Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Madras, Chennai, India
通讯: Vikrant Saumitra @ Department of Metallurgical & Materials Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Madras, Chennai, India
相关性: 该论文专注于增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的变体选择微观机制，未提供力学或疲劳性能数据，但微观结构背景对理解各向异性有间接参考价值。
边界: No experimental mechanical or fatigue testing; focuses on crystallographic variant selection only, without reporting tensile, hardness, or fatigue results.
动机· 揭示增材制造Ti-6Al-4V墙体中变体选择沿构建方向的规律。
方法· 采用电子背散射衍射等表征技术分析不同高度位置的晶体学取向。
结果· 发现变体选择受热历史与局部冷却速率影响，形成特定织构。
验证· 通过微观组织观察与取向分析验证变体选择机制。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand the crystallographic variant selection mechanism along the build direction in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V walls.
Method· EBSD characterization and orientation analysis were performed at different heights along the build direction.
Result· Variant selection was found to be influenced by thermal history and local cooling rates, leading to specific texture gradients.
Validation· The proposed variant selection mechanism was validated through detailed microstructural observations and orientation mapping.

Machining parameters optimization in high-speed milling of titanium alloy chips

Na Zhao, Wei Du, Hao Tong, Kuo Liu Journal of Measurements in Engineering 2025-07-27 10.21595/jme.2025.24872
相关性: 该论文研究钛合金高速铣削的切屑形态和参数优化，但未涉及后处理对疲劳或力学性能的影响，因此对用户方向参考价值有限。
边界: This paper does not investigate the effect of machining post-processing on fatigue or mechanical performance of Ti-6Al-4V; it only studies chip morphology and cutting parameter optimization.
动机· 优化钛合金高速铣削参数以提高加工效率和质量。
方法· 模拟不同切削速度和温度下的切屑形状，并用蝗虫优化算法进行多目标参数优化。
结果· 大进给速度、径向切深和主轴转速可实现高性能加工。
验证· 通过仿真验证优化参数的有效性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study aims to optimize high-speed milling parameters for titanium alloy to enhance machining efficiency and surface quality.
Method· Simulation of chip shape under different cutting speeds and temperatures, combined with locust optimization algorithm for multi-objective parameter optimization.
Result· Optimal parameters identified include large feed speed, radial cutting depth, and spindle speed.
Validation· Validation was conducted through simulation-based analysis of chip morphology and optimization metrics.

In Vivo Study of Bone Growth Around Additively Manufactured Implants With Ti‐6Al‐4V and Bioactive Glass Powder Composites

Chih‐Yu Lee, Pei‐Ching Kung, Chih‐Chieh Huang, Shao‐Ju Shih, E‐Wen Huang et al. Journal of Orthopaedic Research® 2025-07-27 10.1002/jor.70037
单位: Department of Materials Science and Engineering University of Maryland College Park Maryland USA
通讯: Meng–Huang Wu @ Department of Orthopaedics, School of Medicine, College of Medicine Taipei Medical University Taipei Taiwan  |  Nien‐Ti Tsou @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University Hsinchu Taiwan
相关性: 该论文主要研究体内骨整合，而非Ti-6Al-4V的力学或疲劳性能。
边界: No mechanical or fatigue characterization of Ti-6Al-4V; focuses on in vivo osseointegration with bioactive glass composites.
动机· 研究添加生物活性玻璃对3D打印Ti-6Al-4V植入物骨整合的影响。
方法· 使用选择性激光熔化制备Ti-6Al-4V/58S BG复合植入物，进行体内动物实验。
结果· BG涂层和掺杂植入物在早期加速骨生长，但后期效果不显著。
验证· 体内动物组织学分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of bioactive glass incorporation on osseointegration of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V implants.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V/58S BG composite implants were fabricated via selective laser melting and evaluated in an animal model.
Result· Both BG-doped and BG-coated implants enhanced early bone ingrowth, but the effect became insignificant at 12 weeks.
Validation· In vivo histological analysis of bone-implant contact and bone density.

Microstructure and Mechanical Properties of the TC4 Alloy Obtained by Equal-Channel Angular Pressing in Combination with Reversible Hydrogen Alloying

И.Н. Семенова, Luiza R. Rezyapova, A. Polyakov, Yuecheng Dong, Zhonggang Sun et al. Metals 2025-07-27 10.3390/met15080839
单位: Department of Materials Science and Metal Physics, Ufa University of Science and Technology, 32 Zaki Validi St., 450076 Ufa, Russia
通讯: И.Н. Семенова @ Department of Materials Science and Metal Physics, Ufa University of Science and Technology, 32 Zaki Validi St., 450076
相关性: 该论文涉及非增材制造的TC4合金，与激光粉末床熔融等增材制造工艺无关。
边界: not additively manufactured; uses equal-channel angular pressing and reversible hydrogen alloying, not LPBF/SLM/EBM/DED processes.
动机· 研究可逆氢合金化对TC4合金ECAP变形后微观组织和力学性能的影响。
方法· 对TC4合金进行氢化处理并ECAP变形，随后真空除氢获得超细晶结构。
结果· 获得0.15μmα相超细晶，强度达1490MPa，延伸率约5%。
验证· 通过拉伸测试和微观结构表征验证了力学性能和组织变化。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of reversible hydrogen alloying on the microstructure and mechanical properties of TC4 alloy subjected to equal-channel angular pressing.
Method· Hydrogen charging and quenching followed by ECAP at 600°C for six passes, then vacuum degassing to remove hydrogen and produce an ultrafine-grained structure.
Result· An ultrafine-grained structure with 0.15 μm α-phase was achieved, resulting in a tensile strength of 1490 MPa and an elongation of about 5%.
Validation· Mechanical testing (tensile tests) and microstructure characterization (SEM, TEM) were used to validate the property and structural changes.

Development of porous customized Ti-6Al-4V cranial implant manufactured by laser beam powder bed fusion

单位: Additive Manufacturing Center for Medical Implants, Yildiz Technical University, Istanbul, Turkiye
通讯: Mirigül Altan @ Additive Manufacturing Center for Medical Implants, Yildiz Technical University, Istanbul, Turkiye
相关性: 该论文研究的是颅骨植入物而非股骨柄，与用户关注的股骨柄结构设计方向不符。
边界: The paper focuses on a cranial implant, not a femoral/hip stem, and does not address stem-specific mechanics such as stress shielding, Gruen zones, or ISO fatigue testing.
动机· 减轻颅骨植入物重量并降低刚度以避免应力遮挡
方法· 利用nTop软件设计多种点阵结构，通过有限元分析筛选最佳结构，并用PBF-LB制造

验证· 制造后进行压缩实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To reduce weight and stiffness of cranial implants to mitigate stress shielding
Method· Designed various lattice structures using nTop software, selected the best via FEA, and fabricated by PBF-LB
Result· The isotruss lattice achieved 50% weight reduction with load capacity up to 18,000 N
Validation· Compression test after fabrication
