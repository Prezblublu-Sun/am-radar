A defect-based critical plane method for multiaxial high cycle fatigue life prediction in L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V alloy

Xiaofan Zhang, Jinghao Yang, Yingyu Wang, Shuai Gong International Journal of Fatigue 2025-07-27 10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2025.109199
单位: College of Aerospace Engineering, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Nanjing 210016, China
通讯: Yingyu Wang @ College of Aerospace Engineering, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Nanjing 210016, China
相关性: 本文直接针对L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V的多轴高周疲劳寿命预测，符合Layer 1对疲劳性能的要求。
动机· 提高含缺陷L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V多轴高周疲劳寿命预测精度。
方法· 提出基于缺陷的临界平面法，结合多轴疲劳准则。
结果· 新方法能准确预测不同缺陷状态下的疲劳寿命。
验证· 通过L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V试样多轴疲劳实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve multiaxial high-cycle fatigue life prediction for L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V by incorporating defect characteristics into the critical plane approach.
Method· A defect-based critical plane model is developed, integrating defect size, shape, and orientation with multiaxial fatigue criteria.
Result· The proposed method yields accurate fatigue life predictions for L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V under various multiaxial loading conditions.
Validation· Experimental multiaxial fatigue tests on L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V specimens with controlled defect populations validate the model.

Machining parameters optimization in high-speed milling of titanium alloy chips

Na Zhao, Wei Du, Hao Tong, Kuo Liu Journal of Measurements in Engineering 2025-07-27 10.21595/jme.2025.24872
相关性: 该论文涉及钛合金高速铣削的切屑形态模拟和参数优化，但未研究后处理对疲劳或力学性能的影响。
边界: The paper focuses on chip shape simulation and machining parameter optimization, but does not evaluate the effect of these parameters on fatigue life or mechanical performance of Ti-6Al-4V after machining. No experimental mechanical testing or correlation with surface integrity or fatigue.
动机· 高速铣削钛合金时切屑形状对加工参数选择的影响机制缺乏研究。
方法· 通过模拟不同速度和温度下的切屑形状，并使用蝗虫优化算法优化多目标参数。
结果· 发现大进给速度、径向切削深度和主轴转速可实现高效加工。
验证· 仅数值模拟，无实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The influence of chip morphology on machining parameter selection during high-speed milling of titanium alloy is not well understood.
Method· Chip shapes at various speeds and temperatures are simulated, and the locust optimization algorithm is used for multi-objective parameter optimization.
Result· High feed speed, radial depth of cut, and spindle speed are identified for achieving high-performance machining.
Validation· The study relies solely on numerical simulation without experimental validation.

Microstructure and Mechanical Properties of the TC4 Alloy Obtained by Equal-Channel Angular Pressing in Combination with Reversible Hydrogen Alloying

И.Н. Семенова, Luiza R. Rezyapova, A. Polyakov, Yuecheng Dong, Zhonggang Sun et al. Metals 2025-07-27 10.3390/met15080839
单位: Department of Materials Science and Metal Physics, Ufa University of Science and Technology, 32 Zaki Validi St., 450076 Ufa, Russia
通讯: И.Н. Семенова @ Department of Materials Science and Metal Physics, Ufa University of Science and Technology, 32 Zaki Validi St., 450076
相关性: 该论文研究的是TC4合金的等通道角压及可逆氢合金化，并非增材制造，与用户关注方向无关。
边界: The paper does not involve any additive manufacturing (LPBF/SLM/EBM/DED) process; it uses ECAP, a severe plastic deformation method, which is outside the scope of AM mechanical/fatigue performance.
动机· 研究可逆氢合金化对TC4合金等通道角压后组织及力学性能的影响。
方法· 在850℃淬火引入0.3%氢形成α″-马氏体，在600℃进行6道次等通道角压，再通过真空除氢获得超细晶组织。
结果· 获得了平均α相尺寸0.15μm的超细晶结构，抗拉强度达1490MPa，延伸率约5%。
验证· 通过拉伸试验和微观组织观察验证性能提升。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of reversible hydrogen alloying on the microstructure and mechanical properties of TC4 alloy after equal-channel angular pressing.
Method· Introduction of 0.3% hydrogen followed by quenching from 850°C to form thin-plate α″-martensite, then 6 passes of ECAP at 600°C, followed by vacuum degassing to obtain an ultrafine-grained structure.
Result· An ultrafine-grained structure with average α-phase size of 0.15 μm was achieved, leading to a tensile strength of 1490 MPa and elongation of about 5%.
Validation· Mechanical testing and microstructure analysis validated the strengthening effect.
