Periprosthetic Femur Fractures in Hemiarthroplasty Correlated With Stem Type: An Analysis From the American Joint Replacement Registry

Michael J. DeRogatis, Isabella Zaniletti, Antonia F. Chen, Robert W. Gomez, Paul S. Issack et al. Journal of the American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons 2025-09-17 10.5435/jaaos-d-25-00406
单位: From the St. Luke's University Health Network, Bethlehem, PA (DeRogatis, Gomez, and Lundy), the American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons, Combined Analytics Team (Zaniletti), the University of Texas S

相关性: 该论文分析了临床登记数据中假体周围骨折风险与股骨柄设计的关系，但未涉及生物力学建模、应力遮挡或增材制造相关的植入物设计。
边界: This paper does not include biomechanical modeling, FEA, stress shielding analysis, or AM-related implant design; it is a clinical outcomes study with no mechanical testing or contact mechanics evaluation.
动机· 评估半髋置换术中股骨柄设计对假体周围骨折风险的影响。
方法· 分析美国关节置换登记中心2012-2021年56828例半髋置换数据，使用Cox模型比较不同柄型。
结果· 带领的骨水泥复合束柄骨折风险最低，无领锥形楔形柄风险最高。
验证· 通过大规模临床登记数据进行统计验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the association between femoral stem design and periprosthetic fracture risk in hip hemiarthroplasty.
Method· Analysis of American Joint Replacement Registry data from 2012-2021 for 56,828 hemiarthroplasties, using Cox proportional hazards models with inverse probability weighting.
Result· Cemented composite beam stems with a collar showed the lowest periprosthetic fracture risk, while collarless tapered wedge stems had the highest risk.
Validation· Clinical registry-based statistical validation with 56,828 patients, adjusting for confounders via inverse probability weighting.

Cement Mantle Screws in Periprosthetic Hip Fracture Fixation Near Well-Fixed Femoral Stems May Not Impact Short-Term Femoral Stem Survivorship.

James Shaw, Ruby G Patel, Peter K Sculco, Joshua Wright-Chisem, Naomi E Gadinsky et al. The Journal of bone and joint surgery. American volume 2025-09-17 10.2106/JBJS.24.00920
单位: Hospital for Special Surgery, New York Presbyterian Hospital/Weill Cornell Medical College, New York, NY.
通讯: David S Wellman @ Westchester Medical Center, New York Medical College, Valhalla, New York. [推断]
相关性: 该论文为纯临床研究，未涉及髋关节假体应力遮挡、有限元分析或增材制造。
边界: This paper is a retrospective clinical study on periprosthetic fracture fixation with no biomechanical modeling, no FEA, no implant design, no AI/ML, and no AM.
动机· 评估骨水泥鞘螺钉对股骨假体存留率的影响
方法· 回顾性分析112例假体周围骨折患者，纳入28例骨水泥鞘内螺钉固定患者
结果· 26例骨性愈合，无假体松动或骨水泥鞘骨折
验证· 临床随访33.3个月
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate whether screws placed in the cement mantle affect femoral stem survivorship in periprosthetic fractures.
Method· A retrospective review of patients treated with plate constructs and screws into the cement mantle for Vancouver Type-B1 or C fractures.
Result· 26 of 28 patients achieved union with no stem loosening or cement mantle fractures at short-term follow-up.
Validation· Clinical follow-up with radiographic assessment over a mean of 33.3 months.

Longitudinal Assessment of Impingement Risk Following Total Hip Arthroplasty Through the Direct Anterior Approach

Shahnaz Taleb, Jordan S. Broberg, J Polus, Brent A. Lanting, Matthew G. Teeter The Journal of Arthroplasty 2025-09-17 10.1016/j.arth.2025.09.017
单位: School of Biomedical Engineering, Western University, London, Ontario, Canada; Imaging Research Laboratories, Robarts Research Institute, Schulich School of Medicine & Dentistry, Western University, L
通讯: Shahnaz Taleb @ School of Biomedical Engineering, Western University, London, Ontario, Canada; Imaging Research Laboratories, Robarts Re
相关性: 该论文主要关注全髋关节置换术后撞击风险的临床评估，与用户的研究方向（人工髋关节股骨柄的增材制造与力学建模）无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not involve any implant design, additive manufacturing, finite element analysis, or biomechanical modeling of the femoral stem; it is purely a clinical impingement risk assessment.
动机· 评估直接前路全髋置换术后5年内组件撞击风险的变化。
方法· 对24名患者进行多次放射立体测量分析，使用自定义工具计算股骨柄与内衬间距离。
结果· 仰卧位距离稳定，站立位距离显著减小；小头与偏心距柄影响撞击风险。
验证· 基于临床患者数据的纵向观察研究。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the long-term risk of component-on-component impingement in total hip arthroplasty patients via the direct anterior approach over a five-year period.
Method· Radiostereometric analysis was performed at multiple time points postoperatively, and a custom computational tool computed the minimum distance between the femoral stem and polyethylene liner.
Result· Impingement risk remained stable in supine but increased in standing; smaller femoral heads and offset stems were associated with altered distances.
Validation· Clinical longitudinal study with 24 patients followed up to five years.
