Process Optimization and Effect of Post-Processing on Mechanical Properties in the Production of Ti6Al4V Alloy by Laser Metal Deposition Method

A. Ari, T. Karagöz, A. Bayram Archives of Metallurgy and Materials 2025-12-22 10.24425/amm.2025.156285
单位: Ostim Technical University, Department o f Weapon Industry Technician, Vocational School of Higher Education, Ankara, Türki̇y
通讯: A. Ari @ Ostim Technical University, Department o f Weapon Industry Technician, Vocational School of Higher Education, Ankara, Tü
相关性: 该论文直接研究Ti6Al4V合金的激光金属沉积后处理（热等静压、热处理、深冷处理）对力学性能和孔隙率的影响，与用户方向高度相关。
边界: The paper does not report fatigue data, focusing on tensile strength, ductility, and porosity reduction.
动机· 优化激光金属沉积Ti6Al4V工艺并研究后处理对力学性能的影响。
方法· 采用响应面法优化参数，并对比热等静压、热处理和深冷处理的效果。
结果· 后处理降低了强度但显著提高了塑性和减少孔隙，深冷处理进一步细化组织。
验证· 通过力学测试和孔隙率分析验证后处理效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To optimize the laser metal deposition process for Ti6Al4V and evaluate the effects of post-processing treatments on mechanical properties.
Method· Response surface methodology and ANOVA were used for parameter optimization, followed by hot isostatic pressing, heat treatment, and cryogenic treatment. Mechanical testing and porosity analysis were performed.
Result· Post-processing reduced strength but significantly improved ductility and reduced porosity; cryogenic treatment further refined the microstructure and increased elongation.
Validation· Experimental mechanical tests and porosity measurements validated the effects of each post-processing route.

Characterization of microstructure and defects in the Ti-6Al-4V alloy produced by 3D printing SLM technology

Kateřina Caldová, Andrea Školáková, Jan Pinc, Dalibor Vojtěch MANUFACTURING TECHNOLOGY 2025-12-22 10.21062/mft.2025.083
相关性: 该论文研究了热等静压对SLM成型Ti-6Al-4V合金显微组织和缺陷的影响，与后处理控制方向相关但没有提供力学性能数据。
边界: The paper does not include any mechanical testing (tensile or fatigue) to quantify the effect of post-processing on mechanical performance; only microstructure and defect characterization is presented.
动机· 表征3D打印Ti-6Al-4V合金的显微组织和缺陷，以及热等静压的影响。
方法· 使用光学显微镜、扫描电镜和显微CT进行表征。
结果· 热等静压显著减小孔隙尺寸并改变显微组织。
验证· 通过实验表征验证了微观结构变化。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To characterize the microstructure and defects of SLM-produced Ti-6Al-4V alloy and the effect of hot isostatic pressing.
Method· Optical microscopy, scanning electron microscopy, and micro-computed tomography were used for characterization.
Result· Hot isostatic pressing significantly reduces pore size and induces microstructural transformation.
Validation· Experimental characterization via microscopy and micro-CT validated the microstructural changes.

A Novel Hybrid Lubri-Cooling Approach for Machining of Ti-6Al-4V Through Combination of Pulsated Minimal Quantity Lubrication and Dry Ice Media

M. Ajmal Khan, Bing Wang, Zhanqiang Liu, Aqib Mashood Khan, B. Mouli Prasanth et al. International Journal of Precision Engineering and Manufacturing-Green Technology 2025-12-22 10.1007/s40684-025-00833-x
单位: Key Laboratory of High Efficiency and Clean Mechanical Manufacture of MOE/Shandong key Laboratory of High Performance Tools and System, Jinan, 250061, China
通讯: M. Ajmal Khan @ Key Laboratory of High Efficiency and Clean Mechanical Manufacture of MOE/Shandong key Laboratory of High Performance To
相关性: 该论文研究钛合金加工过程中的润滑冷却技术，未直接评估对疲劳或力学性能的影响。
边界: The paper focuses on machining process parameters and cooling techniques, not on the resulting mechanical performance (fatigue, tensile) of Ti-6Al-4V after post-processing.
动机· 改善钛合金加工过程的热管理和冷却效率。
方法· 结合脉冲微量润滑和干冰介质的混合润滑冷却方法。
结果· 提高了加工表面质量和刀具寿命。
验证· 通过加工实验对比不同冷却条件下的结果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve thermal management and cooling efficiency during machining of Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· A hybrid lubri-cooling approach combining pulsated minimum quantity lubrication and dry ice media.
Result· Enhanced surface quality and tool life were observed compared to conventional cooling methods.
Validation· Experimental machining tests with varying cooling conditions validated the approach.

Enhanced microstructure and toughness in narrow-gap oscillating laser-TIG hybrid welded Ti-6Al-4V plates via high-frequency pulsed current

Gang Ruan, Chen Shen, Lin Wang, Kanglong Wu, Danqi Zhang et al. Materials & Design 2025-12-22 10.1016/j.matdes.2025.115388
单位: Shanghai Changxing Ocean Laboratory, Shanghai 201913, China
通讯: Chen Shen @ Shanghai Changxing Ocean Laboratory, Shanghai 201913, China  |  Xueming Hua @ Shanghai Changxing Ocean Laboratory, Shanghai 201913, China
相关性: 该论文研究了Ti-6Al-4V的焊接工艺和力学性能，但并非增材制造，与关注方向不相关。
边界: The paper focuses on welding of Ti-6Al-4V plates, not additive manufacturing processes (LPBF/SLM/EBM/DED), and does not address fatigue performance as required by the direction.
动机· 改善厚板Ti-6Al-4V焊接接头组织与韧性。
方法· 采用窄间隙振荡激光-TIG复合焊接并施加高频脉冲电流(10 kHz)。
结果· 10 kHz脉冲电流使热影响区宽度减小26%，熔合区β晶粒细化，-40°C冲击韧性超过母材。
验证· 通过拉伸、硬度和冲击韧性测试验证力学性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the microstructure and mechanical properties of thick Ti-6Al-4V welded joints produced by hybrid welding.
Method· A narrow-gap oscillating laser-TIG hybrid welding process with high-frequency pulsed current (10 kHz) was applied to 25 mm-thick Ti-6Al-4V plates.
Result· The 10 kHz pulsed current reduced the heat-affected zone width by 26%, refined β grains, promoted columnar-to-equiaxed transition, and achieved −40°C impact toughness exceeding that of the base metal.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through tensile testing, microhardness, and Charpy impact tests.

Optimizing Dental Implant Viability with SLA and Anodizing Surface Treatments for Advanced Biological Adhesion, Enhanced Osseointegration, and Stress Resistance

Ehsan Anbarzadeh, Bijan Mohammadi, Milad Azadzaeim Journal of biomimetics, biomaterials and biomedical engineering 2025-12-22 10.4028/p-g3cyct
单位: Iran University of Science and Technology
通讯: Ehsan Anbarzadeh @ Iran University of Science and Technology
相关性: 本文聚焦于牙科植入物的表面处理以促进骨整合和细胞活性，而非AM Ti-6Al-4V的后处理对疲劳/力学性能的影响。
边界: This paper does not address mechanical fatigue or tensile performance of AM Ti-6Al-4V; it focuses on biological osseointegration and cell viability for dental implants, which falls under Layer 3 (implant structure) rather than Layer 2 (post-processing for mechanical performance).
动机· 优化牙科种植体表面处理以提高生物粘附和骨整合
方法· 结合喷砂大颗粒酸蚀(SLA)与阳极氧化处理钛基台
结果· 优化参数获得97%细胞活性，12天内增强骨整合
验证· FESEM、EDAX、MTT、润湿性及表面能分析
English summary & terms
Motivation· To optimize surface treatments on titanium dental implants for improved biological adhesion and osseointegration.
Method· Combined sandblasted large-grit acid-etched (SLA) and anodizing treatments on Ti-6Al-4V fixtures with systematic parameter variation.
Result· The optimized SLA+anodizing protocol achieved 97% cell viability and enhanced osseointegration within twelve days.
Validation· Characterization via FESEM, EDAX, MTT assay, wettability, and surface energy measurements.
