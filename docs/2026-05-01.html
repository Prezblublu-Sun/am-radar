Controlling microstructure with tailorable mesoscale spatial heterogeneity in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V via laser powder bed fusion

Sahil Dhiman, Milan Brandt, Daniel Fabijanic, Alena Siudová, VI Nithin Balaji et al. Deakin Research Online (Deakin University) 2026-05-01 10.26187/deakin.32293785
相关性: 该论文直接研究增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的微观结构调控与力学性能（强度-塑性），属于L1方向核心内容。
边界: 论文未包含疲劳性能（S-N曲线、疲劳寿命等）的测试或建模。
动机· 为了实现增材制造Ti-6Al-4V中可控的微观结构变化，通过空间调控局部热环境。
方法· 结合优化工艺参数与设计灵活性，原位形成α′马氏体（硬相）和层状α+β（软韧相）的交替带或核壳结构，并进行准原位热处理。
结果· 实现了可调的强度-塑性组合，过渡区域在变形中起关键作用。
验证· 通过力学测试和变形后微观结构分析验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To achieve controllable microstructural variations in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V by spatially tuning the local thermal environment during laser powder bed fusion.
Method· Integrated optimized process parameters with design flexibility to in-situ form alternating bands or core-shell structures of α′ martensite (hard) and lamellar α+β (soft-tough), supported by quasi-in situ heat treatments.
Result· Tunable strength-ductility combinations were achieved, with transition zones playing a critical role in localized deformation behavior.
Validation· Experimental mechanical testing and post-deformation microstructural analysis provided validation.

边界: 该论文聚焦氢脆行为而非纯力学或疲劳性能，未提供S-N曲线或疲劳寿命数据，但慢应变速率测试仍属力学表征。
