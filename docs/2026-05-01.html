Controlling microstructure with tailorable mesoscale spatial heterogeneity in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V via laser powder bed fusion

Sahil Dhiman, Milan Brandt, Daniel Fabijanic, Alena Siudová, VI Nithin Balaji et al. Deakin Research Online (Deakin University) 2026-05-01 10.26187/deakin.32293785
相关性: 该论文涉及LPBF Ti-6Al-4V的微观结构调控与力学性能，但未包含疲劳性能研究，对方向关注的核心疲劳内容具有间接参考价值。
边界: No fatigue testing or fatigue performance characterization is included; the paper focuses on microstructure control and tensile properties.
动机· 通过LPBF实现Ti-6Al-4V微观结构的空间可控变化。
方法· 利用优化的工艺参数原位形成α′马氏体与层状α+β域，调控空间取向与尺度。
结果· 获得可调的强度-延展性组合，过渡区变形行为关键。
验证· 变形后微观分析与准原位热处理验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To achieve controllable microstructural variations in Ti-6Al-4V by spatially tuning the local thermal environment during laser powder bed fusion.
Method· Integrated optimized PBF-LB/M process parameters to intentionally form α′ martensite and lamellar α+β domains with tailored spatial orientation and length scale.
Result· Tunable strength-ductility combinations were achieved, with localized deformation behavior and critical role of transition zones revealed.
Validation· Post-deformation microstructural analyses and quasi-in situ heat treatments replicating local thermal conditions validated the microstructural development pathways.

Hydrogen Embrittlement Behavior of Laser Melting Deposited Ti-6Al-4V Alloy with Different Build Orientations

Kejun Jiang, Junjun Jin, Yuxiang Cai, Zhihui Li, Sunmin Zou et al. Materials 2026-05-01 10.3390/ma19091869
单位: Key Laboratory of Advanced Technologies of Materials, Ministry of Education, School of Materials Science and Engineering, Southwest Jiaotong University, Chengdu 610031, China
通讯: Kejun Jiang @ Key Laboratory of Advanced Technologies of Materials, Ministry of Education, School of Materials Science and Engineering
相关性: 该论文研究了增材制造Ti-6Al-4V在不同构建方向下的氢脆行为，但未涉及疲劳性能，因此与方向关注点部分相关。
边界: No fatigue testing (S-N, fatigue life, crack growth) included, which is a core component of the direction's base mechanical performance evaluation.
动机· 研究激光熔化沉积Ti-6Al-4V在不同构建方向下的氢脆敏感性。
方法· 通过电化学充氢、慢应变速率拉伸和显微表征分析。
结果· 长时间充氢导致氢化物形成和性能下降，90°方向抗氢脆性能最佳。
验证· 通过慢应变速率拉伸实验验证力学性能变化。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the hydrogen embrittlement susceptibility of laser melting deposited Ti-6Al-4V alloy with different build orientations.
Method· Electrochemical hydrogen charging, slow strain rate testing, and microstructural characterization were employed.
Result· Prolonged hydrogen charging induces hydride formation and degradation of mechanical properties, with the 90° orientation showing the best embrittlement resistance.
Validation· Experimental validation via slow strain rate tensile testing on charged specimens.

Investigating the Influence of Cutting Insert Angles on Dimensional Deviation and Surface Roughness During Turning of Ti6Al4V Alloy

Djordje Vukelic, Aleksandar Milošević, Goran Šimunović, Vitalii Ivanov, Željko Santoši et al. Tehnicki vjesnik - Technical Gazette 2026-05-01 10.17559/tv-20250211002360
单位: University of Novi Sad, Faculty of Technical Sciences, Trg Dositeja Obradovica 6, 21000 Novi Sad, Serbia
通讯: Djordje Vukelic @ University of Novi Sad, Faculty of Technical Sciences, Trg Dositeja Obradovica 6, 21000 Novi Sad, Serbia
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti6Al4V合金的车削加工，但未涉及增材制造工艺，因此与本方向无关。
边界: The paper focuses on conventional machining of Ti6Al4V, not on additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V mechanical or fatigue performance.
动机· 研究刀具角度对Ti6Al4V合金车削质量的影响
方法· 采用实验设计方法，分析不同刀片角度对尺寸偏差和表面粗糙度的影响
结果· 刀片角度变化对尺寸偏差影响52.83%，对表面粗糙度影响13.16%
验证· 通过验证实验确认预测方程的准确性
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the influence of cutting insert angles on dimensional deviation and surface roughness during dry turning of Ti6Al4V alloy.
Method· A custom design of experiments was used to study the effects of rake angle, clearance angle, inclination angle, approach angle, and nose angle on output parameters.
Result· The dimensional deviation ranged from 0.053 mm to 0.081 mm and surface roughness from 1.892 mm to 2.141 mm, with the angles affecting deviation by 52.83% and roughness by 13.16%.
Validation· Confirmation experiments validated the predictive equations with low percentage errors.

β-solidifying α₂-based Ti–Al alloys for laser powder bed fusion: Synchrotron X-ray insight into phase retention and transformation

Hatem A. Soliman, Ali Ghasemi, Mohamed Elbestawi, Toshihiro Okajima, Swee Leong Sing Journal of Alloys and Compounds 2026-05-01 10.1016/j.jallcom.2026.188592
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, McMaster University, 1280 Main Street West, Hamilton, ON L8S 4L7, Canada
通讯: Hatem A. Soliman @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, McMaster University, 1280 Main Street West, Hamilton, ON L8S 4L7, Canada
相关性: 该论文研究新型Ti-Al合金的激光粉末床熔融及后热处理，并非针对Ti-6Al-4V的后处理，与用户关注方向无直接关联。
边界: The paper focuses on a novel Ti-Al alloy (not Ti-6Al-4V) and its post-build heat treatment, with no experimental data on Ti-6Al-4V post-processing effects on fatigue or mechanical performance.
动机· 利用快速凝固保留β相，开发可激光熔融的α2基Ti-Al合金。
方法· 结合Mo当量和Thermo-Calc设计成分，原位合金化制备，后进行热处理。
结果· 无裂纹近全致密件，高温拉伸强度357 MPa，总应变17.3%。
验证· 同步辐射XRD分析相变，高温拉伸验证力学性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To exploit rapid solidification in additive manufacturing to retain metastable phases in historically brittle α2-Ti3Al alloys for crack-free fabrication.
Method· A β-solidifying α2-based Ti-Al alloy was designed using Mo-equivalent and Thermo-Calc, fabricated by laser powder bed fusion with in-situ alloying, and subjected to post-build heat treatment.
Result· Crack-free near-fully dense components were achieved; hot tensile testing at 750°C showed an ultimate tensile strength of 356.9 MPa and total elongation of 17.3%.
Validation· Room-temperature and in-situ high-temperature synchrotron X-ray diffraction characterized phase retention and transformation, and hot tensile tests validated mechanical performance.
