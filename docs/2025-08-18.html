Effects of thermal cycling on visco-elastic properties of solution-treated aged and stress relief annealed Ti-6Al-4V alloy

Putti Venkata Siva Teja, Suman Pandipati, Srihari Palli, Rakesh Chandmal Sharma, Swati Mohapatra et al. Journal of the Chinese Institute of Engineers 2025-08-18 10.1080/02533839.2025.2533511
单位: Dhanekula Institute of Engineering & Technology
通讯: Srihari Palli @ Aditya Institute of Technology and Management
相关性: 该论文直接研究了两种后处理（固溶时效和应力消除退火）对Ti-6Al-4V合金粘弹性性能的影响，属于L2方向的核心内容。
边界: This paper focuses on visco-elastic properties under thermal cycling rather than traditional fatigue or tensile performance, but it still addresses post-processing effects on mechanical properties.
动机· 探究热循环对经过两种后处理的Ti-6Al-4V合金粘弹性能的影响。
方法· 对固溶时效和应力消除退火后的合金进行热循环实验，测量粘弹性参数。
结果· 热循环显著改变了两种后处理状态下的粘弹性行为，且影响程度与初始热处理相关。
验证· 通过机械测试验证了热循环前后性能变化。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how thermal cycling affects the visco-elastic properties of Ti-6Al-4V alloy that has been subjected to either solution-treated aging or stress relief annealing post-processing.
Method· Thermal cycling experiments were conducted on specimens in both post-processing states, and visco-elastic parameters were measured.
Result· Thermal cycling significantly altered the visco-elastic behavior in both conditions, with the extent of change dependent on the initial heat treatment.
Validation· Mechanical testing was performed to validate the changes in properties before and after thermal cycling.

Investigation and optimization of the laser Polishing parameters for Ti6Al4V

Van-Thien Tran, Cong-Tuan Truong, Thanh Tung Vu Discover Materials 2025-08-18 10.1007/s43939-025-00359-8
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, Hanoi, Vietnam
通讯: Van-Thien Tran @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, Hanoi, Vietnam
相关性: 该论文研究了Ti6Al4V激光抛光工艺对表面粗糙度的影响，但未直接评估机械性能，可作为后处理参数优化的参考。
边界: No mechanical testing (fatigue/tensile) was performed; the paper only evaluated surface roughness without measuring the effect on mechanical performance.
动机· 改善Ti6Al4V表面粗糙度。
方法· 实验与模拟结合，采用响应面法优化激光抛光参数。
结果· 激光功率是主要影响因素，优化参数可显著降低粗糙度。
验证· 实验验证与模拟结果一致。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the surface finish of Ti6Al4V via laser polishing.
Method· Combined experimental and simulation approach with response surface methodology to optimize laser parameters.
Result· Laser power was the dominant factor, and optimal parameters achieved highest roughness reduction.
Validation· Experimental results matched simulation predictions.

Low-Temperature Synthesis of Monetite/Dextran Hybrid Coatings with Tailored Corrosion Resistance on Ti6Al4V

Robert S. Matos, Henrique Duarte da Fonseca Filho, Michael Douglas Santos Monteiro, Nilson S. Ferreira ACS Omega 2025-08-18 10.1021/acsomega.5c05440
单位: Amazonian Materials Group
通讯: Robert S. Matos @ Amazonian Materials Group  |  Nilson S. Ferreira @ Department of Physics
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti6Al4V基体上的涂层，但未涉及增材制造、力学性能或疲劳性能。
边界: No additive manufacturing, no mechanical or fatigue testing; purely on coating synthesis and corrosion resistance.
动机· 开发低温合成方法制备兼具生物相容性和耐蚀性的涂层。
方法· 采用溶胶-凝胶辅助法在Ti6Al4V上合成磷酸氢钙/右旋糖酐杂化涂层。
结果· 4 wt%右旋糖酐涂层致密均匀，在人工唾液中耐蚀性显著提升。
验证· 通过电化学测试评估涂层的耐腐蚀性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To develop a low-temperature synthesis route for hybrid coatings that combine biocompatibility and enhanced corrosion resistance on Ti6Al4V.
Method· Monetite/dextran hybrid coatings were synthesized on Ti6Al4V substrates via a sol-gel-assisted route at ≤80 °C.
Result· The formulation with 4 wt% dextran yielded compact and homogeneous coatings, significantly improving corrosion resistance in artificial saliva.
Validation· Electrochemical evaluations including open-circuit potential and polarization resistance measurements were performed.
