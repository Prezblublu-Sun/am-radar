Bending strength and hardness comparison of titanium and cobalt–chromium alloys for dentures made by selective laser melting

Leen Audi, Ammar Almustafa Scientific Reports 2025-10-17 10.1038/s41598-025-21191-3
单位: Department of Removable Prosthodontics, Faculty of Dentistry, Damascus University, Damascus, Syria. leenaudi350@gmail.com
通讯: Leen Audi @ Department of Removable Prosthodontics, Faculty of Dentistry, Damascus University, Damascus, Syria. leenaudi350@gmail.co
相关性: 涉及Ti6Al4V选区激光熔融的弯曲强度和显微硬度实验，但与疲劳性能无关。
边界: No fatigue data, S-N curves, or defect/anisotropy analysis; focus is on dental comparative study rather than general AM mechanical characterization.
动机· 比较SLM制备的Ti6Al4V和Co-Cr合金用于义齿的弯曲强度和硬度。
方法· 进行三点弯曲试验和显微硬度测试，采用统计检验分析数据。
结果· Ti6Al4V应变较高，弹性模量和显微硬度较低，但应力无显著差异。
验证· 符合ISO 9693-1标准的体外力学测试。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to compare the bending strength and hardness of Ti6Al4V and Co-Cr alloys fabricated by selective laser melting for removable dental prostheses.
Method· Three-point bending tests following ISO 9693-1 and Micro-Vickers hardness measurements were performed, with statistical analysis using Welsh and Mann-Whitney U tests.
Result· Ti6Al4V exhibited higher strain, lower modulus of elasticity, and lower microhardness than Co-Cr, but no significant difference in stress was found.
Validation· Validation was conducted through standardized in vitro mechanical testing with ten bending specimens and six hardness specimens per alloy.

SURFACE MORPHOLOGY AND ROUGHNESS EVALUATION OF TITANIUM TREATED WITH MECHANICAL AND CHEMICAL PRE-TREATMENT METHODS

S. Sreemathi, G. Jims John Wessley Surface Review and Letters 2025-10-17 10.1142/s0218625x26500204
单位: Aerospace Engineering, Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences, Coimbatore 641114, India
通讯: S. Sreemathi @ Aerospace Engineering, Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences, Coimbatore 641114, India
相关性: 该论文仅关注Ti-6Al-4V的表面粗糙度处理，未涉及任何力学或疲劳性能，与定向评估重点不符。
边界: No mechanical or fatigue testing; the study focuses on surface roughness for fiber adhesion, not on tensile, fatigue, or any mechanical properties relevant to L1.
动机· 通过机械和化学预处理提高钛合金表面粗糙度以增强纤维粘附性。
方法· 采用喷砂、铜喷砂、单双酸蚀刻及其组合处理Ti-6Al-4V表面。
结果· 铜喷砂使粗糙度从0.27μm增至5.65μm，喷砂后双酸蚀刻实现平衡表面形貌。
验证· 使用便携式粗糙度仪和扫描电镜测量并观察表面形貌。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance fiber adhesion in metal-fiber composites by modifying the surface roughness of Ti-6Al-4V alloy through mechanical and chemical pre-treatments.
Method· Systematically evaluated sandblasting, copper blasting, single and dual acid etching, and their combinations on Ti-6Al-4V surfaces.
Result· Copper blasting achieved the highest roughness increase (21x), while sandblasting followed by dual acid etching provided a balanced morphology for bonding.
Validation· Surface roughness measured with a Mitutoyo portable tester and morphology observed by SEM.

High-Cycle Fatigue Strength Prediction Model for Ti-6Al-4V Titanium Alloy Compressor Blades Subjected to Foreign Object Damage

Wangtian Yin, Yongbao Liu, Xing He, Zegang Tian Metals 2025-10-17 10.3390/met15101150
单位: College of Power Engineering, Naval University of Engineering, Wuhan 430033, China
通讯: Wangtian Yin @ College of Power Engineering, Naval University of Engineering, Wuhan 430033, China
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的疲劳强度，但未涉及增材制造工艺，与本方向无直接关联。
边界: This paper does not address additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it studies conventionally forged blades, so it falls outside the scope of Layer 1 which requires AM-specific mechanical/fatigue characterization.
动机· 提高航空发动机叶片受外物损伤后的高周疲劳强度预测精度。
方法· 通过不同角度冲击模拟损伤，进行高周疲劳试验，并建立修正的疲劳强度预测模型。
结果· 修正模型相比Neuber和Peterson模型将10%散射带内的预测比例提高30%。
验证· 通过叶片模拟试样的高周疲劳实验验证模型准确性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the accuracy of high-cycle fatigue strength predictions for Ti-6Al-4V compressor blades damaged by foreign objects.
Method· Impact tests at various angles were conducted on blade simulation specimens, followed by high-cycle fatigue experiments, and a modified fatigue strength prediction model incorporating shear factors and tensile residual stresses was developed.
Result· The modified model increased the proportion of predictions within the 10% scatter band by 30% compared to Neuber and Peterson models.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed through high-cycle fatigue testing of impacted blade simulation specimens.

Integrating soft computing approach to optimize and predict powder mixed wire electric discharge machinability characteristics of PBLF-Ti-6Al-4V alloy

Gururaj Bolar, Rajesh Nayak, Raviraj Shetty, J P Supriya, Adithya Hegde Cogent Engineering 2025-10-17 10.1080/23311916.2025.2572292
单位: Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering, Manipal Institute of Technology, Manipal Academy of Higher Education
通讯: Raviraj Shetty @ Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering, Manipal Institute of Technology, Manipal Academy of Higher Educatio
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的粉末混合电火花线切割加工优化，而非增材制造后处理工艺，与用户方向不相关。
边界: The paper studies powder mixed wire EDM machining parameters for Ti-6Al-4V, not a post-processing treatment for additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 优化Ti-6Al-4V合金的粉末混合电火花线切割加工性能。
方法· 采用田口法设计实验，分析放电电流、脉冲时间等参数对材料去除率、表面粗糙度和显微硬度的影响。
结果· 锌粉在一克每升时获得最小线磨损；二硫化钼在一克每升时获得最高显微硬度。
验证· 通过方差分析和实验验证参数影响。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To optimize the powder mixed wire electric discharge machining (PMWEDM) performance of Ti-6Al-4V alloy by investigating the effects of process parameters and dielectric powders.
Method· Taguchi design of experiments and analysis of variance were used to evaluate the influence of discharge current, pulse on time, gap voltage, and powder concentration on material removal rate, surface roughness, and microhardness.
Result· Minimum wire weight loss of 0.0049 mg/min was achieved with zinc powder; maximum microhardness of 368 HV was achieved with molybdenum disulfide.
Validation· Experimental validation via Taguchi-optimized trials and ANOVA.
