Establishing And Advancing Qualification Strategies For Aerospace Components Produced Using LPBF

Mireles, Jorge scholarworks - UTEP (The University of Texas at El Paso) 2025-08-01 link
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的LPBF制造及热等静压后处理，对理解后处理对疲劳性能的影响有参考价值。
边界: 本文主要聚焦于认证策略而非系统性研究后处理对微观结构和性能的定量影响。
动机· 建立航空LPBF部件的认证策略，确保可重复和可靠的性能。
方法· 通过固定流程、样品中心的认证方法，结合机械测试和统计过程控制。
结果· 成功认证了Ti-6Al-4V支架，发现氧污染影响延展性，超声疲劳测试可快速筛选。
验证· 通过NADCAP实验室的拉伸、疲劳、断裂韧性等机械测试及CT检测验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To establish qualification strategies for aerospace components produced by LPBF to ensure repeatable and reliable performance.
Method· A fixed-process, sample-centric qualification methodology including machine qualification, material testing, part inspection, and statistical process control.
Result· The Ti-6Al-4V bracket was successfully qualified for flight hardware, with findings on oxygen contamination effects on ductility and the feasibility of ultrasonic fatigue testing for rapid screening.
Validation· Mechanical testing (tensile, fatigue, fracture toughness) at NADCAP-certified labs and dimensional inspection via CMM and X-ray CT.

Effect of build orientation on mechanical and physical properties of Ti-6Al-4V L-PBF additively manufactured thin structures without support

Mrinal Dwivedi, N. Arunachalam Manufacturing Letters 2025-08-01 10.1016/j.mfglet.2025.06.110
相关性: 该论文研究了Ti-6Al-4V L-PBF薄壁结构的孔隙率、残余应力和表面粗糙度随构建取向的变化，但未涉及力学性能（如拉伸、疲劳）的测试或建模。
边界: No mechanical or fatigue testing was performed; only physical properties (density, roughness, residual stress) were measured, so it does not contribute to the mechanical/fatigue characterization required for Layer 1.
动机· 探究构建取向对L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V薄壁结构物理性能的影响
方法· 制造不同角度薄壁楔形件，采用阿基米德法测密度、光学轮廓仪测粗糙度、XRD测残余应力
结果· 大构建角提高相对密度并降低残余应力，小构建角表面粗糙度更大
验证· 通过实验测量验证密度、粗糙度和残余应力的变化趋势
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand how build orientation affects porosity, residual stress, and surface roughness in unsupported thin L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V structures.
Method· Thin hollow wedges were fabricated at various build angles and characterized using Archimedes principle for density, 3D optical profilometry for roughness, and XRD for residual stress.
Result· Increasing build angle increases relative density and decreases residual stress, while lower angles yield higher surface roughness.
Validation· Experimental measurements of density, roughness, and residual stress validated the observed trends.

Digital twin-enabled surface quality prediction and optimization in dry turning of Ti6Al4V using ANFIS and genetic algorithm

Sumesh C. S, Ajith Ramesh Journal of King Saud University - Engineering Sciences 2025-08-01 10.1007/s44444-025-00030-w
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Amrita School of Engineering, Coimbatore, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, India
通讯: Sumesh C. S @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Amrita School of Engineering, Coimbatore, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, India
相关性: 该论文关注的是常规车削加工中的表面质量预测，而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的后处理工艺，与方向无关。
边界: Does not involve additive manufacturing or any post-processing treatment (heat treatment, HIP, shot peening) for AM Ti-6Al-4V; it is about conventional turning process optimization.
动机· 解决钛合金车削加工中表面质量与刀具磨损的矛盾。
方法· 结合实验设计、ANFIS建模与遗传算法优化车削参数。
结果· 最优参数下表面粗糙度预测值达0.3392μm，模型预测准确。
验证· 通过额外12组实验验证ANFIS模型精度。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To address the challenge of achieving high surface quality while minimizing tool wear in machining titanium alloys.
Method· An integrated framework combining design of experiments, ANFIS modeling, and genetic algorithm optimization was developed for dry turning of Ti-6Al-4V.
Result· Optimal machining parameters (spindle speed 660.10 rpm, feed rate 30 mm/min, depth of cut 0.3 mm) yielded a predicted surface roughness of 0.3392 μm with high model accuracy.
Validation· The ANFIS model was validated with 12 additional experiments, showing RMSE values from 0.015 to 0.038 μm for surface roughness.
