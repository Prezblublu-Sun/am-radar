Enhancing ductility of laser powder bed fusion Ti-6Al-4V alloys by molybdenum addition: A study on microstructure and deformation mechanisms

English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the ductility of laser powder bed fusion Ti-6Al-4V alloys through molybdenum addition.
Method· Microstructure characterization using SEM/EBSD and tensile testing to evaluate mechanical properties.
Result· Molybdenum addition leads to a significant improvement in ductility while maintaining strength.
Validation· Experimental validation through microstructural analysis and mechanical testing.

Effect of build orientation on mechanical and physical properties of Ti-6Al-4V L-PBF additively manufactured thin structures without support

Mrinal Dwivedi, N. Arunachalam Manufacturing Letters 2025-08-01 10.1016/j.mfglet.2025.06.110
相关性: 本文研究了Ti-6Al-4V LPBF薄壁结构的构建取向对密度、粗糙度和残余应力的影响，但未涉及力学性能或疲劳测试。
边界: No tensile, yield, or fatigue data; focused on density and residual stress without direct mechanical property characterization.
动机· 探究构建取向对Ti-6Al-4V LPBF薄壁结构孔隙和残余应力的影响
方法· 制备不同角度的空心楔形件，采用阿基米德法、光学轮廓仪和XRD表征
结果· 构建角增大使相对密度升高、残余应力降低，低角度时粗糙度更高
验证· 通过阿基米德法、光学轮廓仪和XRD进行实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how build orientation affects porosity and residual stress in thin-walled Ti-6Al-4V LPBF structures.
Method· Thin hollow wedges were printed at various angles and characterized using Archimedes principle, optical profilometry, and XRD.
Result· Higher build angles increased relative density and decreased residual stress, while lower angles gave higher surface roughness.
Validation· Validation by Archimedes density measurements, 3D optical surface profiling, and XRD residual stress analysis.

Establishing And Advancing Qualification Strategies For Aerospace Components Produced Using LPBF

Mireles, Jorge scholarworks - UTEP (The University of Texas at El Paso) 2025-08-01 link
相关性: 该论文主要关注航空航天Ti-6Al-4V构件的LPBF认证方法，虽涉及热等静压等后处理，但并非系统研究后处理对疲劳/力学性能的影响。
边界: The paper does not systematically study the effect of a specific post-processing treatment on fatigue/mechanical performance; it is a qualification methodology study with incidental post-processing observations.
动机· 建立航空航天LPBF Ti-6Al-4V构件的认证策略。
方法· 采用试样中心方法进行机器、材料、零件及批量认证，包含拉伸、疲劳等测试。
结果· 成功认证支架，发现气流和氧污染影响，开发了快速疲劳测试方法。
验证· 通过NADCAP实验室力学测试和过程控制统计验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To establish qualification strategies that ensure repeatable, reliable, and certifiable performance for LPBF aerospace components.
Method· A sample-centric qualification methodology including machine/parameter, material/process, part, and recurring production testing, with mechanical tests (tensile, fatigue, fracture toughness) and process control.
Result· Successful qualification of an aerospace Ti-6Al-4V bracket; identification of gas flow and oxygen contamination effects on mechanical properties; demonstration of ultrasonic fatigue testing as a rapid screening tool.
Validation· Mechanical testing at NADCAP-accredited laboratories and statistical process control metrics (Cp, Cpk, Pp, Ppk).

单位: Univ. of Miami (United States)
通讯: Russell Roberts @ Univ. of Miami (United States)
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的后处理（应力消除退火和抛光），但完全聚焦于X射线镜的光学性能，未涉及任何力学性能（疲劳、拉伸等），且未使用增材制造工艺。

English summary & terms
Motivation· To fabricate ultra-flat, low surface roughness titanium substrates for high-reflectivity x-ray mirrors.
Method· Commercial Ti-6Al-4V sheets are flattened via restrained stress relief annealing and polished using chemical mechanical polishing with an environmentally friendly slurry.
Result· Surface roughness below 1 nm was achieved, and preliminary reflectivity tests showed promising results for soft x-ray mirrors.
Validation· Validation was performed through reflectivity tests on the polished surfaces.

Digital twin-enabled surface quality prediction and optimization in dry turning of Ti6Al4V using ANFIS and genetic algorithm

Sumesh C. S, Ajith Ramesh Journal of King Saud University - Engineering Sciences 2025-08-01 10.1007/s44444-025-00030-w
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Amrita School of Engineering, Coimbatore, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, India
通讯: Sumesh C. S @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Amrita School of Engineering, Coimbatore, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, India
相关性: 本文涉及Ti-6Al-4V的干式车削表面质量预测，未涉及增材制造及机械性能。
边界: This paper does not study additive-manufactured Ti-6Al-4V, does not report fatigue or mechanical performance, and does not evaluate any post-processing effect on microstructure or mechanical properties.
动机· 提高干式车削Ti-6Al-4V的表面质量并减少刀具磨损。
方法· 结合实验设计、ANFIS建模和遗传算法优化。
结果· 最优参数下表面粗糙度预测值为0.3392 µm。
验证· 通过额外12次实验验证ANFIS模型的预测精度。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To predict and minimize surface roughness during dry turning of Ti-6Al-4V for improved quality control.
Method· Experimental design with RSM, ANFIS modeling, and genetic algorithm optimization.
Result· Optimal machining parameters achieved predicted surface roughness of 0.3392 µm.
Validation· Additional 12 experiments validated ANFIS model with low RMSE.
