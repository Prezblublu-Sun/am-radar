Modular Femoral Stem for Hartofilakidis Type C Hip Dysplasia: Is It Necessary for 1-mm Increment of Distal Stem Diameter?

Jiafeng Yi, Hongbin Xie, Yubo Liu, Yijian Huang, Wei Chai et al. Orthopaedic surgery 2025-08-01 10.1111/os.70092
单位: School of Medicine, Nankai University, Tianjin, China.; Senior Department of Orthopedics, The Fourth Medical Center of PLA General Hospital, Beijing, China.; National Clinical Research Center for Orth
通讯: Xiangpeng Kong @ Senior Department of Orthopedics, The Fourth Medical Center of PLA General Hospital, Beijing, China.; National Clinical [推断]
相关性: 该论文为临床回顾性研究，未涉及应力遮挡、微动、骨整合或增材制造等用户研究方向的核心内容。
边界: This paper is a clinical outcome study on S-ROM stem fracture rates, lacking any quantification of stress shielding, micromotion, primary stability, or bone-implant interface mechanics, and does not address additive manufacturing or FE modeling.
动机· 评估S-ROM假体2mm直径增量是否增加术中骨折风险
方法· 回顾性纳入95例Hartofilakidis C型DDH患者，比较9mm与11mm组临床结局
结果· 2mm增量组骨折率近5倍于1mm增量组，1mm增量更安全
验证· 基于临床回顾性数据的统计分析
English summary & terms
Motivation· To determine whether the 2-mm incremental increase in distal stem diameter of the S-ROM prosthesis elevates the risk of intraoperative femoral fractures in high-riding DDH patients.
Method· A retrospective comparative study of 95 DDH patients (109 hips) receiving 9 mm or 11 mm S-ROM stems, analyzing intraoperative fracture rates, medullary diameter, and canal filling ratio.
Result· The 11 mm group exhibited a significantly higher intraoperative fracture rate (16.3% vs 3.3%), indicating that a 2-mm increment increases fracture risk and a 1-mm increment is advisable.
Validation· Clinical retrospective cohort with statistical hypothesis testing (t-test, chi-square, logistic regression).
