Features of Manufacturing Parts Using Additive Technologies from Metal Alloys. An Analytical Review

Oles Sonets, Borys Vorontsov Mechanics and Advanced Technologies 2025-06-26 10.20535/2521-1943.2025.9.2(105).326824
单位: National Technical University of Ukraine “Igor Sikorsky Kyiv Polytechnic Institute”
通讯: Oles Sonets @ National Technical University of Ukraine “Igor Sikorsky Kyiv Polytechnic Institute”
相关性: 此综述涉及增材制造Ti-6Al-4V零件的机械加工，但未定量研究后处理对疲劳/力学性能的影响。
边界: This is a review that discusses machinability of AM Ti-6Al-4V but does not provide experimental mechanical data or a specific post-processing effect on fatigue/tensile performance.
动机· 综述增材制造金属零件加工现状，指出Ti6Al4V加工研究不足。
方法· 文献综述，分类金属合金与3D打印技术，分析层取向和微观结构对加工的影响。
结果· 强调后加工不可替代，需研究Ti6Al4V特定打印方法的铣削工艺。
验证· 无实验验证，仅文献总结。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This review surveys current research on machining of additively manufactured metal parts and highlights the need for studies on Ti6Al4V.
Method· A classification of metal alloys and printing technologies is provided, and the influence of layer orientation and microstructure anisotropy on machinability is analyzed.
Result· It concludes that finish machining cannot be eliminated and that milling of Ti6Al4V parts produced by specific methods requires further investigation.
Validation· No experimental validation; purely a literature review.

Microstructure and Mechanical Properties of Functionally Graded Materials on a Ti-6Al-4V Titanium Alloy by Laser Cladding

Lanyi Liu, Xiaoyang Huang, Guocheng Wang, Xiaoyong Zhang, Kechao Zhou et al. Materials 2025-06-26 10.3390/ma18133032
单位: State Key Laboratory of Powder Metallurgy, Central South University, Changsha 410083, China
通讯: Bingfeng Wang @ School of Materials Science and Engineering, Central South University, Changsha 410083, China
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V表面功能梯度熔覆层的力学性能，但未研究基体材料的疲劳性能或增材制造状态下的力学行为。
边界: No fatigue characterization or as-built AM mechanical properties; focuses on surface coating via laser cladding rather than bulk Ti-6Al-4V mechanical/fatigue performance.
动机· 提高Ti-6Al-4V表面硬度和耐磨性
方法· 激光熔覆制备功能梯度材料并表征微观结构与力学性能
结果· 熔覆层硬度、耐磨性和冲击韧性显著优于基体
验证· 通过纳米压痕、磨损试验和动态压缩试验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the surface hardness and wear resistance of Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· Functionally graded materials were fabricated on Ti-6Al-4V by laser cladding, and microstructure and mechanical properties were investigated.
Result· The FGM cladding layer exhibited superior wear resistance, dynamic compressive strength (1701.6 MPa), and impact absorption energy (189.6 MJ/m³) compared to the base alloy.
Validation· Validated through nanoindentation, wear tests, dynamic compression, and impact testing.

Systematic studies on structural instability in microscopic polycrystalline Ti-6Al-4V alloys subject to annealing via molecular dynamics

Taeil Yi Journal of Mechanical Science and Technology 2025-06-26 10.1007/s12206-025-2301-z
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Kyungnam University, Changwon, 51767, Korea
通讯: Taeil Yi @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Kyungnam University, Changwon, 51767, Korea
相关性: 该论文通过分子动力学研究退火对Ti-6Al-4V显微组织不稳定性，但未直接涉及力学性能，与后处理控制方向关联较弱。
边界: No experimental or modeling data on mechanical performance (fatigue, tensile) after annealing; focuses only on atomic-scale structural instability without linking to macroscopic properties.
动机· 探究退火过程中Ti-6Al-4V多晶合金的结构不稳定性机理
方法· 采用分子动力学模拟研究退火对微观组织的影响
结果· 揭示了退火条件下原子尺度结构不稳定性演化规律
验证· 无实验验证，纯分子动力学模拟
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the mechanisms of structural instability in Ti-6Al-4V polycrystalline alloys during annealing.
Method· Molecular dynamics simulations were employed to study microstructural evolution under annealing conditions.
Result· The simulations revealed atomic-scale structural instability evolution during annealing.
Validation· No experimental validation; purely computational molecular dynamics study.
