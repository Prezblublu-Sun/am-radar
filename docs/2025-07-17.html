The Relationship between Fatigue Life and the Microstructure and Mesostructure of Additively Manufactured Materials

Emiel Amsterdam, Ali Chabok NATO Journal of Science and Technology 2025-07-17 10.14339/sto-avt-384-5
单位: Nationaal Lucht- en Ruimtevaartlaboratorium
通讯: Emiel Amsterdam @ Nationaal Lucht- en Ruimtevaartlaboratorium
相关性: 该论文直接研究了增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的疲劳寿命与微观/介观结构的关系，符合L1方向要求。
边界: This paper also includes AlSi10Mg and does not cover other mechanical properties beyond fatigue, but it remains directly relevant to Ti-6Al-4V fatigue.
动机· 理解增材制造Ti-6Al-4V微观/介观结构与疲劳寿命的关系
方法· 引入基于断裂力学的现象学模型并结合多路径试样实验
结果· 多路径结构损伤容限高但疲劳寿命降低
验证· 采用LPBF AlSi10Mg和Ti-6Al-4V试样进行高周疲劳测试验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand the relationship between microstructure/mesostructure and fatigue life of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· A fracture-mechanics-informed phenomenological model was introduced and validated with experiments on stretch-dominated multi-load-path specimens.
Result· Multi-load-path structures exhibit higher damage tolerance but lower overall fatigue life.
Validation· High-cycle fatigue tests on LPBF AlSi10Mg and Ti-6Al-4V specimens in as-built condition.

Life Prediction Model for High-Cycle and Very-High-Cycle Fatigue of Ti-6Al-4V Titanium Alloy Under Symmetrical Loading

Xi Fu, Lina Zhang, Wenzhao Yang, Zhaoming Yin, Jiakang Zhou et al. Materials 2025-07-17 10.3390/ma18143354
单位: School of Machinery and Automation, Weifang University, Weifang 261061, China
通讯: Xi Fu @ School of Machinery and Automation, Weifang University, Weifang 261061, China
相关性: 该论文直接针对Ti-6Al-4V合金的高周和超高周疲劳寿命预测，与方向焦点完全吻合。
边界: The paper focuses on fatigue life prediction models with limited experimental data; it does not provide new mechanical property data beyond model fitting.
动机· 传统疲劳极限的无限寿命设计在超过10^8循环时不再安全，需要更准确的疲劳寿命预测模型。
方法· 通过对称加载高周和超高周疲劳试验，引入内应力参数建立非线性累积损伤寿命预测模型。
结果· 建立的模型能良好拟合试验数据，经参数优化后平均预测误差从59%降至38%。
验证· 通过疲劳试验数据验证模型精度，并进行参数敏感性分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Infinite life design based on traditional fatigue limit is unsafe for components requiring over 10^8 cycles, necessitating improved fatigue life prediction models for Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· A nonlinear cumulative damage life prediction model was developed by conducting high-cycle and very-high-cycle fatigue tests under symmetrical loading and introducing an internal stress parameter to describe plastic strain energy evolution.
Result· The proposed models fit the experimental data well, and after parameter optimization via sensitivity analysis, the average prediction error decreased from 59% to 38%.
Validation· Model accuracy was validated against fatigue test data, and parameter sensitivity analysis was performed.
