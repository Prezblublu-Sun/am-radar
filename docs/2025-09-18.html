A cellular automata-based crystal plasticity analysis of slip activity in additive manufactured Ti-6Al-4V during dwell fatigue

Hanqing Liu, Masanori Kitamura, Fabien Briffod, Takayuki Shiraiwa Journal of Alloys and Compounds 2025-09-18 10.1016/j.jallcom.2025.183867
单位: Department of Materials Engineering, The University of Tokyo, 7-3-1 Hongo, Bunkyo-ku, Tokyo 113-8656, Japan
通讯: Takayuki Shiraiwa @ Department of Materials Engineering, The University of Tokyo, 7-3-1 Hongo, Bunkyo-ku, Tokyo 113-8656, Japan
相关性: 该论文专注于增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的驻留疲劳行为建模，与用户关注的力学和疲劳性能直接相关。
边界: The paper is purely computational and does not provide new experimental fatigue data; the simulation is not validated against experimental fatigue results.
动机· 揭示增材制造Ti-6Al-4V在驻留疲劳下的滑移行为和疲劳机制。
方法· 结合元胞自动机和晶体塑性有限元方法，并引入α变体选择准则。
结果· 模型表明累积滑移导致材料硬化，且载荷转移与滑移活动相关。
验证· 通过增材制造试样的拉伸实验结果标定晶体塑性参数。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand slip behaviors and fatigue mechanisms of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V under dwell fatigue conditions.
Method· A cellular automata-based crystal plasticity finite element model with Burgers orientation relationship for α-variant selection.
Result· The model reveals material hardening from accumulated slip activations and a consistent relationship between load shedding and slip activity.
Validation· Crystal plasticity parameters were calibrated against tensile test results from additively manufactured specimens.

Microstructure evolution and corrosion behavior of Ti-6Al-4V alloy induced by laser shock peening without coating

Longlong Zhou, Weiling Guo, Gengchao He, Haidou Wang, Jun Zhou et al. Journal of Materials Research and Technology 2025-09-18 10.1016/j.jmrt.2025.09.147
单位: National Key Lab for Remanufacturing, Army Academy of Armored Forces, Beijing, 100072, China
通讯: Zhenbing Cai @ Tribology Research Institute, Key Lab of Advanced Technologies of Materials, Southwest Jiaotong University, Chengdu, 610  |  Zhiguo Xing @ National Key Lab for Remanufacturing, Army Academy of Armored Forces, Beijing, 100072, China
相关性: 该论文研究了无涂层激光冲击强化对Ti-6Al-4V合金的耐腐蚀性能，但未涉及疲劳性能，因此与用户关注的后处理对疲劳/力学性能的影响关联度有限。
边界: The paper concentrates on corrosion resistance rather than fatigue performance, and does not present any fatigue test data or discuss the effect of LSPwC on fatigue life.
动机· 提升Ti-6Al-4V在海洋环境中的耐腐蚀性能
方法· 采用无涂层激光冲击强化（LSPwC）处理合金表面
结果· LSPwC提高了硬度、残余压应力和接触角，细化了晶粒，增强了耐腐蚀性
验证· 通过电化学测试和微观结构分析验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the corrosion resistance of Ti-6Al-4V alloy in marine environments.
Method· Laser shock peening without coating (LSPwC) was applied to modify the surface properties of the alloy.
Result· LSPwC increased microhardness by 30%, residual compressive stress from -120 to -615 MPa, contact angle above 100°, refined grain size, and significantly enhanced corrosion resistance.
Validation· Electrochemical tests (impedance, polarization) and microstructural characterization (SEM, XRD, EBSD) were performed.

Data-Driven Design and Additive Manufacturing of Patient-Specific Lattice Titanium Scaffolds for Mandibular Bone Reconstruction

Nail Beisekenov, Bagdat Azamatov, Marzhan Anuarbekovna Sadenova, Dmitriy Dogadkin, Daniyar Kaliyev et al. Journal of Functional Biomaterials 2025-09-18 10.3390/jfb16090350
单位: Department of Ecology and Conservation Biology, Texas A&M University, College Station, TX 77843, USA
通讯: Marzhan Anuarbekovna Sadenova @ Center of Excellence “VERITAS”, D. Serikbayev East Kazakhstan Technical University, Ust-Kamenogorsk 070004, Kazakhstan
相关性: 该论文研究的是下颌骨支架而非股骨柄，与用户关注的Ti-6Al-4V股骨假体结构设计方向不符。
边界: This paper studies a mandibular bone scaffold, not a femoral/hip stem, so it does not meet the required anatomical application.
动机· 针对下颌骨节段缺损重建需求，设计并制造患者特异性多孔钛支架。
方法· 结合CT数据、有限元模拟、人工神经网络和贝叶斯网络进行数据驱动优化。
结果· 优化后的支架孔隙率55-65%，在600N咬合力下峰值应力225MPa，微动<150μm。
验证· 通过机械测试验证有限元预测精度达98%。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To address the need for patient-specific scaffolds for segmental mandibular bone defects, combining mechanical safety and biological functionality.
Method· A cyber-physical loop integrating CT-derived geometry, high-fidelity FEM, an artificial neural network (ANN) with millisecond speed, and a Bayesian network (BN) for uncertainty quantification.
Result· The optimized scaffold with 55-65% porosity reduced titanium use by 15%, achieved a peak von Mises stress of 225 MPa and micromotion <150 µm under a 600 N bite load.
Validation· Mechanical testing confirmed FEM predictions with 98% accuracy and ANN reproduced outputs with 94% fidelity.
