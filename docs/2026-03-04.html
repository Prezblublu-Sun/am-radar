Design of bionic porous structure of bone scaffolds and analysis of fatigue and permeability characteristics

单位: School of Intelligent Manufacturing, Nanjing Vocational College of Information Technology
通讯: Long Chao @ School of Intelligent Manufacturing, Nanjing Vocational College of Information Technology
相关性: 该论文涉及LPBF Ti-6Al-4V支架的疲劳性能测试，与L1方向直接相关。
边界: This paper focuses on bone scaffold design rather than generic Ti-6Al-4V mechanical/fatigue properties.
动机· 骨缺损修复需要人工支架，但现有支架力学与渗透性不足。
方法· 基于Voronoi图设计多孔结构，通过LPBF制造Ti-6Al-4V支架，并进行静力仿真、疲劳测试和流体仿真。
结果· 疲劳性能满足天然骨植入标准，渗透性与天然骨接近，有助于细胞增殖。
验证· 疲劳测试和体外渗透实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Bone defects require artificial scaffolds with adequate mechanical and permeability properties.
Method· A Voronoi-based bionic porous structure was designed, fabricated by LPBF with Ti-6Al-4V, and evaluated via static simulation (ABAQUS), fatigue tests, fluid simulation (COMSOL), and in vitro experiments.
Result· Fatigue performance met natural bone implant criteria, and permeability closely matched natural bone, promoting cell proliferation.
Validation· Validation through mechanical fatigue testing and in vitro permeability experiments.

相关性: 该论文主要关注仿生股骨假体设计，属于L3层，而非L1层的基础力学与疲劳性能研究。
边界: The paper's primary focus is on designing a bioinspired femoral prosthesis, and it includes heat treatment, which places it in Layer 2/3 rather than Layer 1's scope of fundamental mechanical/fatigue characterization of as-built or process-state Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 为设计仿生股骨假体，比较传统与增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的性能。
方法· 对SLM制备的Ti-6Al-4V进行热处理，分析微观结构、力学、电化学和生物学性能。

验证· 通过实验测试微观结构和力学性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To design a bioinspired femoral prosthesis by comparing conventionally manufactured and additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V samples were fabricated by SLM with and without heat treatment, and characterized for microstructure, mechanical, electrochemical, and biological properties.
Result· Heat treatment transformed the martensitic α' to lamellar α+β, improving ductility, wettability, and antibacterial activity.
Validation· Experimental microstructural and mechanical characterization were performed.

ELECTRON BEAM WELDING OF TITANIUM AND ALUMINUM ALLOYS WITH A VANADIUM FILLER

G Kotlarski, Darina Kaisheva, Dimitar Dechev, Nikolay Ivanov, Maria Ormanova et al. Journal of Chemical Technology and Metallurgy 2026-03-04 10.59957/jctm.v61.i2.2026.18
单位: Institute of Electronics, Bulgarian Academy of Sciences; Center of competence “Smart mechatronic, eco-and energy-saving systems and technologies”
通讯: G Kotlarski @ Institute of Electronics, Bulgarian Academy of Sciences; Center of competence “Smart mechatronic, eco-and energy-saving
相关性: 该论文研究的是电子束焊接钛合金和铝合金，而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学性能，与本方向无关。
边界: This paper is about electron beam welding of dissimilar metals (Ti6Al4V and Al6082T6) with a V filler, not about additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V mechanical or fatigue performance.
动机· 研究钒填充层对电子束焊接钛合金和铝合金接头微观结构和力学性能的影响。
方法· 使用电子束焊接Ti6Al4V和Al6082T6板材，并引入钒作为中间层，通过XRD、SEM、EDS分析微观结构，测量显微硬度和拉伸性能。
结果· 钒中间层改善了焊接区Ti-Al金属间化合物的分布，拉伸强度从45 MPa提高到71 MPa，显微硬度变化不大。
验证· 实验验证：通过显微硬度和拉伸试验评价力学性能，并与无填充层样品对比。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the effect of a vanadium interlayer on the microstructure and mechanical properties of electron beam welded Ti6Al4V and Al6082T6 joints.
Method· Ti6Al4V and Al6082T6 plates were joined by electron beam welding with a V filler; the weld seam microstructure was characterized by XRD, SEM, EDS, and mechanical properties were assessed via microhardness and tensile tests.
Result· Adding a V interlayer improved the distribution of Ti-Al intermetallic phases in the fusion zone, increasing ultimate tensile strength from 45 MPa to 71 MPa, while microhardness remained similar.
Validation· The experimental approach involved comparative testing of samples with and without V filler, including microhardness and tensile measurements.
