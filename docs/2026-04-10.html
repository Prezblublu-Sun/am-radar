Result· The chessboard strategy yielded improved tensile strength and ductility compared to the stripe strategy.
Validation· Mechanical testing including tensile and hardness measurements was performed.

Effect of annealing temperature on microstructure, texture and tensile properties of TC4 titanium alloy wire

单位: Xinjiang Xiangrun New Materials Technology Co., Ltd
通讯: Zhang Mingyu @ Xinjiang Xiangrun New Materials Technology Co., Ltd
相关性: 论文研究了TC4钛合金丝材的退火温度对组织和拉伸性能的影响，但未涉及增材制造工艺，与AM Ti-6Al-4V后处理控制方向关联较弱。
边界: The paper uses wire material without any mention of additive manufacturing, and it only reports tensile properties, not fatigue performance.
动机· 研究退火温度对TC4钛合金丝材组织与拉伸性能的影响
方法· 设置920-980°C四个退火温度，通过显微组织、相分析、织构及拉伸测试表征性能
结果· 980°C退火获得最高强度（抗拉1210MPa），但延伸率仅5%；低温退火塑性更好
验证· 通过拉伸测试和断口SEM观察验证力学性能变化
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of annealing temperature on the microstructure, texture, and tensile properties of TC4 titanium alloy wire.
Method· Four annealing temperatures (920, 940, 960, 980 °C) were applied, and the microstructure, phase composition, texture, and tensile properties were characterized.
Result· Annealing at 980 °C produced the highest strength (UTS 1210 MPa, YS 1110 MPa) but only 5% elongation, while lower temperatures maintained better plasticity.
Validation· Mechanical validation was performed via tensile testing and fracture surface SEM analysis.

English summary & terms

Physics-Informed Symbolic Regression Ensemble (PISRE) for surface roughness prediction in Ti-6Al-4V dry turning

Natália Vilas Boas Pappi Maciel, Alex Fernandes de Souza, Paulo Henrique da Silva Campos, Antônio Carlos Zambroni de Souza, Filipe Alves Neto Verri et al. The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology 2026-04-10 10.1007/s00170-026-17994-x
单位: Institute of Industrial Engineering and Management, Federal University of Itajubá, 1303 BPS Avenue, Itajubá, 37500-903, Brazil
通讯: Natália Vilas Boas Pappi Maciel @ Institute of Industrial Engineering and Management, Federal University of Itajubá, 1303 BPS Avenue, Itajubá, 37500-903,
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V车削后表面粗糙度预测，但未研究后处理对疲劳或力学性能的影响，与方向核心问题关系松散。
边界: The paper does not investigate the effect of post-processing on fatigue or mechanical performance; it only predicts surface roughness after dry turning.
动机· 提高少量实验数据下Ti-6Al-4V车削表面粗糙度预测精度。
方法· 提出物理信息符号回归集成模型(PISRE)，融合物理层和不确定性加权机制。
结果· PISRE的RMSE为0.443 µm，R²=0.915，比XGBoost和随机森林分别降低32.6%和38.1%。
验证· 采用中心复合设计实验和留一交叉验证模型。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve surface roughness prediction accuracy for Ti-6Al-4V turning under small experimental datasets.
Method· A Physics-Informed Symbolic Regression Ensemble (PISRE) integrating a physics layer, multiple symbolic branches, and uncertainty-weighted confidence is proposed.
Result· PISRE achieves RMSE=0.443 µm and R²=0.915, outperforming XGBoost and Random Forest by 32.6% and 38.1% in RMSE respectively.
Validation· Model validation is performed using a Central Composite Design experiment with 19 runs and Leave-One-Out cross-validation.
