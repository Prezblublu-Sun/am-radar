Structure-Property Relationships in Cold-Sprayed Ti-6Al-4V and Ti-6Al-4V/SiC Multilayer Deposits: Influence of Ceramic Reinforcement

Jindřich Viliš, Lukáš Řehořek, Marek Doubrava, Jiří Procházka, Martin Klimeš et al. Journal of Thermal Spray Technology 2026-06-29 10.1007/s11666-026-02275-6
单位: Faculty of Military Technology, Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Defence, Kounicova 65, 602 00, Brno, Czech Republic
通讯: Jindřich Viliš @ Faculty of Military Technology, Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Defence, Kounicova 65, 602 00, Brno,
相关性: 该论文直接研究冷喷涂Ti-6Al-4V的力学性能（硬度、弯曲强度），属于L1方向覆盖的增材制造Ti-6Al-4V机械性能表征。
边界: No fatigue testing; only flexural and hardness characterization without S-N data or fatigue crack initiation/growth.
动机· 研究陶瓷增强相对冷喷涂Ti-6Al-4V沉积层微观结构和力学性能的影响。
方法· 制备Ti-6Al-4V和Ti-6Al-4V/SiC多层沉积层，进行显微组织、硬度和弯曲性能测试。
结果· SiC增强提高了局部硬度但降低了弯曲强度和刚度，并增加了结构异质性。
验证· 通过扫描电镜、硬度测试和三点弯曲实验进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates how ceramic reinforcement affects the microstructure and mechanical properties of cold-sprayed Ti-6Al-4V deposits.
Method· Multilayer Ti-6Al-4V and Ti-6Al-4V/SiC deposits on steel substrate with nickel interlayer were prepared and characterized by microscopy, hardness, and flexural tests on freestanding specimens.
Result· Incorporation of SiC increased local hardness but reduced overall flexural strength and stiffness, increasing structural heterogeneity.
Validation· Validation was performed via SEM, microhardness mapping, and three-point bending with fractographic analysis.

Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing for Lightweight Material Applications: A Systematic Literature Review

Agus Widyianto, Heri Wibowo, Riswan Dwi Djatmiko, Atik Setyani Journal of Automotive and Mechanical Applied Technology 2026-06-29 10.21831/jamat.v3i1.3332
单位: Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta, Indonesia
相关性: 该综述覆盖了WAAM工艺参数及缺陷，但未聚焦Ti-6Al-4V的后处理对疲劳/力学性能的影响。
边界: This is a broad systematic review covering WAAM for multiple lightweight alloys, and it does not specifically investigate the effect of any post-processing treatment on Ti-6Al-4V's fatigue or mechanical performance; post-processing is only mentioned as a future direction.
动机· 系统评估WAAM在轻质合金（铝、钛、镁）中的研究进展
方法· 遵循PRISMA框架，对1052篇文献进行筛选和证据综合
结果· 揭示了工艺参数对微观组织和缺陷的控制作用，但未聚焦后处理
验证· 文献综述，无实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· This systematic review evaluates recent progress in wire arc additive manufacturing for lightweight metal alloys (aluminum, titanium, magnesium).
Method· Following the PRISMA 2020 framework, the authors screened 747 records and synthesized evidence from 265 studies on process parameters, microstructure, and mechanical properties.
Result· The synthesis shows that heat input, wire feed speed, and deposition strategy control bead geometry, grain morphology, and porosity, but post-processing effects on fatigue require further study.
Validation· No experimental validation; this is a literature review.

Result· The audio and magnetic pulses are synchronized with a phase error below 10 nanoseconds.
Validation· Validation was performed using a 4-channel oscilloscope to measure the delay between audio and magnetic signals.

Regulation of microstructure and mechanical properties of powder metallurgy Ti-6Al-4V-2Mo-2Fe alloy by solution treatment

Zhenhong Du, Du Linyan, Fang Yang, Shangxing Qiu, Yuping Dong et al. Journal of Alloys and Compounds 2026-06-29 10.1016/j.jallcom.2026.189495
单位: Institute for Advanced Materials and Technology, Shunde Innovation School, University of Science and Technology Beijing, Beijing 100083, China
通讯: Fang Yang @ Institute for Advanced Materials and Technology, Shunde Innovation School, University of Science and Technology Beijing,  |  C T Chen @ Institute for Advanced Materials and Technology, Shunde Innovation School, University of Science and Technology Beijing,  |  Xinhua Liu @ Institute for Advanced Materials and Technology, Shunde Innovation School, University of Science and Technology Beijing,
相关性: 该论文研究粉末冶金Ti-6Al-4V-2Mo-2Fe合金的固溶处理，而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V，不属于关注方向。
边界: The paper addresses powder metallurgy, not additive manufacturing (AM), and uses a modified Ti-6Al-4V-2Mo-2Fe alloy instead of standard Ti-6Al-4V, so it does not fall under the AM post-processing direction.
动机· 研究固溶处理对粉末冶金Ti-6Al-4V-2Mo-2Fe合金微观结构和力学性能的影响。
方法· 通过不同固溶温度和时间处理合金，并进行显微组织和力学测试。
结果· 固溶处理优化了合金的相组成和力学性能。
验证· 实验验证，包括显微观察和拉伸测试。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of solution treatment on the microstructure and mechanical properties of powder metallurgy Ti-6Al-4V-2Mo-2Fe alloy.
Method· The alloy was subjected to solution treatments at various temperatures and times, followed by microstructural characterization and mechanical testing.
Result· Solution treatment optimized the phase composition and improved the mechanical properties of the alloy.
Validation· Experimental validation through microstructural observation and tensile testing.
