Validation· Model predictions were validated against experimental fatigue data from tested specimens.

A comprehensive framework to study the influence of beam shaping in laser metal fusion processes

Marida Pontrandolfi, Linda Squillaci, Jonas Olsson, Pradip Aryal, Robert Pederson et al. Materials & Design 2025-09-22 10.1016/j.matdes.2025.114805
相关性: 该论文聚焦于Ti-6Al-4V的光束整形工艺，未涉及力学或疲劳性能，对力学性能研究者的直接参考价值有限。
边界: No mechanical or fatigue characterization; focuses solely on melt-pool dynamics, temperature, and microstructure under beam shaping.
动机· 光束整形技术有望改善激光金属熔融质量，但对其熔池动力学的系统研究尚缺。
方法· 结合CFD模拟、原位温度测量和金相分析，研究环形光束轮廓对Ti-6Al-4V熔池的影响。
结果· 环形模式降低峰值温度35%，将深窄熔池变为浅宽熔池，抑制了驼峰缺陷。
验证· CFD预测与高温计测量和金相截面验证，温度误差在5%以内。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Beam shaping promises to improve quality and robustness in Laser Metal Fusion, but systematic investigations of its effects on melt-pool dynamics are lacking.
Method· An integrated approach combining Computational Fluid Dynamics, in-situ pyrometry, and metallographic analysis was used to study programmable ring beam profiles on Ti-6Al-4V bead-on-plate tracks.
Result· Ring modes lowered peak temperature by up to 35%, transforming deep-narrow pools into shallow-wide ones and suppressing humping at high line energies.
Validation· CFD predictions were validated against in-situ pyrometer measurements within 5% error and metallographic cross-sections.

Compressive and energy absorption characteristics of directional graded Ti6Al4V Split-P lattices for biomedical applications

Uday Kumar Jonnala, Ravi Kumar Yennam, Ian Gibson Rapid Prototyping Journal 2025-09-22 10.1108/rpj-03-2025-0091
单位: National Institute of Technology Warangal Department of Mechanical Engineering, , Warangal,
通讯: Uday Kumar Jonnala @ National Institute of Technology Warangal Department of Mechanical Engineering, , Warangal,
相关性: 该论文设计了不同梯度方向的Ti6Al4V Split-P点阵结构并测试其力学性能，为髋关节假体柄的应力屏蔽设计提供了可借鉴的材料数据，但未直接涉及股骨柄结构。
边界: This paper focuses on graded lattice unit cells rather than a complete femoral stem design; it does not include stem-level structural analysis, ISO fatigue testing, or implant-specific biomechanical assessment.
动机· 研究梯度方向对Ti6Al4V Split-P点阵力学性能与能量吸收的影响，以优化医用植入体设计。
方法· 通过L-PBF制备不同梯度方向（1D/2D/3D）的Split-P点阵，进行准静态压缩试验与均匀化分析。
结果· 1D梯度点阵弹性模量4.97 GPa接近松质骨，3D梯度点阵能量吸收达75.85 MJ/m³。
验证· 通过压缩实验测试力学性能，并用幂律模型预测不同梯度方向的性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of grading direction on the mechanical and energy absorption properties of Ti6Al4V Split-P lattices for biomedical implant applications.
Method· Graded Split-P lattices with one-, two-, and three-directional grading were designed and fabricated by laser powder bed fusion, then tested under quasi-static compression with homogenization analysis.
Result· The 1-D graded lattice achieved an elastic modulus of 4.97 GPa, close to cancellous bone, while the 3-D graded lattice showed the highest energy absorption of 75.85 MJ/m³.
Validation· Mechanical properties were validated through compression testing and power-law models were developed to predict behavior of graded lattices.

Defect analysis by computed tomography in metallic materials: Optimisation, uncertainty quantification and classification

Emanuele Avoledo, Marco Petruzzi, Marco Pelegatti, Alessandro Tognan, Francesco De Bona et al. Precision Engineering 2025-09-22 10.1016/j.precisioneng.2025.09.008
单位: Polytechnic Department of Engineering and Architecture, University of Udine, Via delle Scienze 206, Udine, 33100, Italy
通讯: Emanuele Avoledo @ Polytechnic Department of Engineering and Architecture, University of Udine, Via delle Scienze 206, Udine, 33100, Italy
相关性: 该论文关注CT扫描参数优化而非Ti-6Al-4V的后处理工艺（如热处理、表面强化等），与研究方向不符。
边界: The paper deals with CT post-processing parameter optimization, not with post-processing treatments (heat treatment, HIP, shot peening, etc.) of Ti-6Al-4V parts; no mechanical performance data from post-processed specimens is reported.
动机· 优化X射线CT参数以提高增材制造钛合金中缺陷检测的准确性。
方法· 迭代比较CT与断口测量，确定最佳灰度阈值，并传播不确定性进行缺陷分类。
结果· 优化后的灰度阈值比商业算法高134%，缺陷分类精度达99%。
验证· 通过11个电子束熔融Ti-6Al-4V样品的断口测量验证CT优化结果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To optimize X-ray CT post-processing parameters for accurate defect detection in additively manufactured metallic materials.
Method· Iterative comparison between CT-based defect measurements and fractography data to identify optimal gray threshold, followed by uncertainty propagation and unsupervised classification.
Result· The optimized gray threshold was 134% higher than a commercial algorithm, reducing systematic uncertainty, and defect classification accuracy reached 99%.
Validation· Validation via fractography measurements on 11 electron-beam-melted Ti-6Al-4V dog-bone specimens.
