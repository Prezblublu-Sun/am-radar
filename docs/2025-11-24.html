An Analytical Framework for Correlation Between Laser Powder Bed Fusion Factors and Porosity-Induced Fracture Strength and Plasticity

Reza Teimouri, Andrzej Stwora, Maziar Khademi International Journal of Precision Engineering and Manufacturing-Green Technology 2025-11-24 10.1007/s40684-025-00813-1
单位: Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, Cracow University of Technology, Cracow, Poland
通讯: Reza Teimouri @ Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, Cracow University of Technology, Cracow, Poland
相关性: 本文主要针对不锈钢316L开发了LPBF工艺-孔隙-力学性能的集成模型，仅用文献数据验证了Ti6Al4V的预测，未提供Ti6Al4V的新实验或专门建模结果。
边界: The paper's primary material is stainless steel 316L, with Ti6Al4V only used for comparison using literature data; it does not present new experimental or dedicated modeling for Ti6Al4V LPBF mechanical/fatigue performance.
动机· 建立LPBF工艺参数与孔隙率及力学性能之间的关联模型
方法· 集成多物理场模型与GTN损伤模型预测应力-应变曲线
结果· 模型对多种材料（316L、Ti6Al4V等）的孔隙率、UTS和延伸率预测精度良好
验证· 通过316L的微CT和拉伸实验及文献数据验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To establish a correlation between LPBF process factors, porosity level, and resulting mechanical properties for additive manufactured parts.
Method· An integrated multi-physics analytical model combining melt pool and vapor depression mechanics with the Gurson-Tvergaard-Needleman (GTN) continuum damage model was developed to predict stress-strain behavior from process parameters.
Result· The model successfully predicted porosity, ultimate tensile strength, and elongation for multiple materials (316L, Ti6Al4V, Inconel 718, AlSi10Mg) with relative root mean square error ranging from 3% to 30%.
Validation· The model was validated against micro-CT and tensile tests for 316L samples and compared with literature data for Ti6Al4V and other alloys.

Frictional behavior between bone and additively manufactured Ti6Al4V implants is affected by bone density and surface texture under varying loads

Ali Abedi, Farzam Farahmand, Mohammadjavad Salmanimehrjardi, H. Khonsari Scientific Reports 2025-11-24 10.1038/s41598-025-25586-0
单位: Mechanical Engineering Department, Sharif University of Technology, Tehran, Iran
通讯: Ali Abedi @ Mechanical Engineering Department, Sharif University of Technology, Tehran, Iran
相关性: 该论文主要关注骨与增材制造Ti6Al4V植入物界面的摩擦行为，而非Ti-6Al-4V的基础力学或疲劳性能，因此对L1方向的相关性较低。
边界: This paper focuses on interfacial friction between bone and implant rather than on the mechanical or fatigue properties of Ti-6Al-4V itself (e.g., tensile strength, S-N curves, crack initiation).
动机· 骨-植入物界面摩擦系数数据缺乏，尤其是增材制造表面形态与骨质量组合。
方法· 使用往复摩擦试验测量不同骨密度和SLM Ti6Al4V表面纹理在三种法向应力下的摩擦系数。
结果· 摩擦系数范围为0.37-0.95，受表面粗糙度、骨密度和法向应力的显著影响。
验证· 通过人松质骨样品和SLM制备的Ti6Al4V试件进行实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Limited data exists on coefficients of friction for combinations of bone quality and additively manufactured implant surface textures, which are critical for initial stability.
Method· Reciprocal friction tests were conducted with human cancellous bone of three bone mineral density levels and four SLM-produced Ti6Al4V surface textures under three normal stresses.
Result· Coefficients of friction ranged from 0.37 to 0.95, significantly influenced by surface roughness (positive), bone density (negative), and normal stress (negative), with no significant interaction effects.
Validation· Validation was performed through experimental friction measurements using actual human bone specimens and additively manufactured Ti6Al4V samples.

Effect of Copper Addition to Titanium Alloy Prepared By Additive Manufacturing for Biomedical Applications

Ingrid Milošev, Denis Sačer, Barbara Kapun, Chenyang Xie, Ana Kraš et al. ECS Meeting Abstracts 2025-11-24 10.1149/ma2025-02151156mtgabs
单位: Jozef Stefan Institute
通讯: Ingrid Milošev @ Jozef Stefan Institute
相关性: 该论文研究了添加铜的Ti-6Al-4V合金的抗菌性和腐蚀性能，但未涉及任何力学或疲劳性能，因此与本方向的核心关注点不相关。
边界: The paper focuses on antibacterial properties, microstructure, and corrosion resistance of Cu-added Ti-6Al-4V, with no mechanical or fatigue characterization.
动机· 开发具有抗菌性能且不损害生物相容性的Ti-6Al-4V合金。
方法· 通过激光直接能量沉积制备含铜Ti-6Al-4V合金，并进行微观结构和电化学表征。
结果· 添加铜未影响合金在模拟生理溶液中的钝化行为，且无局部腐蚀。
验证· 通过在模拟生理溶液中的电化学测试和长期浸泡实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to develop a Ti-6Al-4V alloy with inherent antibacterial properties by adding copper without compromising other biomaterial qualities.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V-xCu alloys were fabricated by laser-based direct energy deposition (DED-LB) and characterized using SEM/EDS, XRD, XPS, and electrochemical tests.
Result· Copper addition did not degrade the passivation behavior or corrosion resistance in simulated physiological solutions, and the alloys showed no localized corrosion.
Validation· Validation was performed through electrochemical tests, long-term immersion, and osseointegration tests in simulated body fluids.

Effect of Cryo-Rolling on the Microstructural Evolution and Mechanical Properties of Ti-6Al-4V Alloy

Vaibhav Gaur, Pargam Vashishtha, Biraj Kumar Sahoo, Ravi Kumar Bandari, Raj Das Materials 2025-11-24 10.3390/ma18235296
单位: Academy of Scientific and Innovative Research (AcSIR), Ghaziabad 201002, India
通讯: Vaibhav Gaur @ Academy of Scientific and Innovative Research (AcSIR), Ghaziabad 201002, India  |  Ravi Kumar Bandari @ Academy of Scientific and Innovative Research (AcSIR), Ghaziabad 201002, India
相关性: 该论文研究的是传统加工Ti-6Al-4V的低温轧制，而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V，因此与用户关注的后处理方向不直接相关。
边界: This paper studies cryo-rolling on conventionally processed Ti-6Al-4V, not additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V, thus it does not address the specific post-processing behavior of AM material.
动机· 探索低温轧制对Ti-6Al-4V微观结构和力学性能的影响。
方法· 对Ti-6Al-4V进行不同程度的低温轧制变形，部分样品随后加热至900°C，分析微观结构和力学性能。
结果· 低温轧制使晶粒尺寸减小45%，位错密度增加160%，屈服强度和抗拉强度分别提高19%和23%，硬度提高10%，而延性基本保持不变。
验证· 通过X射线衍射、微观组织观察和拉伸/硬度测试验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of cryo-rolling on the microstructural evolution and mechanical properties of Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· Ti-6Al-4V samples were subjected to various degrees of cryo-rolling deformation, with selected samples subsequently heated at 900°C; microstructural and mechanical characterizations were performed.
Result· Cryo-rolling reduced grain and crystallite sizes by 45% and 29% respectively, increased dislocation density by 160%, and raised yield strength, tensile strength, and hardness by 19%, 23%, and 10% while maintaining ductility.
Validation· Validation was done via X-ray diffraction, microstructural analysis, and tensile/hardness testing.

边界: The paper focuses on machining energy consumption and carbon emission, not on post-processing effects on fatigue or mechanical performance of AM Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 研究Ti6Al4V合金面铣加工的能耗与碳排放规律

验证· 实验验证了能耗与碳排放模型的准确性
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the energy consumption and carbon emission characteristics of face milling of Ti6Al4V alloy.
Method· Experimental measurements and modeling were employed to analyze the influence of machining parameters on energy and emissions.
Result· Quantitative relationships between machining parameters, energy consumption, and carbon emissions were established.
Validation· The energy and emission models were validated through experimental data.
