Geometric deviation and compressive failure of Ti-6Al-4V alloy honeycomb structures printed by laser powder bed fusion examined by X-ray microcomputed tomography

Md. Bengir Ahmed Shuvho, Sophie Rapagna, Agatha Labrinidis, Aditya Khanna, Andrei Kotousov et al. Progress in Additive Manufacturing 2026-02-16 10.1007/s40964-026-01542-w
单位: School of Electrical and Mechanical Engineering, The University of Adelaide, Adelaide, SA, 5005, Australia
通讯: Md. Bengir Ahmed Shuvho @ School of Electrical and Mechanical Engineering, The University of Adelaide, Adelaide, SA, 5005, Australia
相关性: 该论文研究了激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V蜂窝结构的压缩力学性能及打印缺陷，与用户的力学与疲劳方向直接相关但侧重压缩而非疲劳。
边界: This paper focuses on compressive behavior of honeycomb structures rather than fatigue life, tensile properties, or standard mechanical characterization of bulk Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 研究打印缺陷和方向对Ti-6Al-4V蜂窝结构压缩性能的影响。
方法· 使用LPBF打印蜂窝圆柱，进行微CT扫描和压缩试验。
结果· 垂直打印的几何精度更高，蜂窝结构和方向显著影响名义模量，压缩失效为剪切破坏。
验证· 通过微CT和压缩实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the influence of printing defects, honeycomb geometries, and orientations on the compressive mechanical properties of LPBF Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Honeycomb cylinders were fabricated by LPBF, examined by micro-CT, and subjected to compression testing with post-failure scanning.
Result· Vertically printed samples showed higher geometric accuracy; both orientations exhibited compression-induced shear failure; geometry and orientation significantly affected modulus.
Validation· Experimental compression tests and micro-CT imaging were used.

Plasma-Enhanced Graphene Coatings on Ti-6Al-4V: Insights from Non-Destructive Characterization

Victor J. Sagrero, Fnu Gorky, Vashanti Storr, Fernando M. de de Oliveira, Hector Carreon et al. Materials 2026-02-16 10.3390/ma19040774
单位: Instituto de Investigación en Metalurgia y Materiales, Universidad Michoacana de San Nicolás de Hidalgo, Morelia 58030, Michoacán, Mexico
通讯: Maria L. Carreon @ Ralph E. Martin Department of Chemical Engineering, University of Arkansas, Fayetteville, AR 72701, USA
相关性: 该论文研究Ti-6Al-4V基底上的石墨烯涂层，未涉及增材制造或力学/疲劳性能，与用户方向无关。
边界: This paper does not address additive manufacturing, mechanical characterization (tensile, fatigue, hardness), or any build-process variables; it focuses solely on coating deposition and non-destructive surface characterization of wrought Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 通过PECVD在Ti-6Al-4V上沉积石墨烯涂层以改善表面性能。
方法· 采用PECVD技术沉积石墨烯，利用拉曼光谱、热电势和涡流进行非破坏性表征。
结果· 获得了均匀微米级石墨烯涂层，热电性能呈金属行为，表面粗糙度轻微增加。
验证· 通过拉曼光谱验证石墨烯存在，SEM测量涂层厚度约1微米。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance surface properties of Ti-6Al-4V by depositing graphene coatings via PECVD.
Method· Graphene coatings were deposited using PECVD and characterized non-destructively by Raman spectroscopy, thermoelectric potential, eddy current, and surface roughness measurements.
Result· Uniform micrometric graphene coatings were obtained, exhibiting metallic Seebeck behavior and slight increase in surface roughness.
Validation· Raman spectroscopy confirmed graphene presence, and SEM measured coating thickness around 1 μm.

Analysis of the influence of geometric parameters of laser-produced texturing on carbide tools in Ti6Al4V turning

Felipe Chagas Rodrigues de Souza, Fábio Rüstow de Paula, Álisson Rocha Machado, Wagner de Rossi The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology 2026-02-16 10.1007/s00170-026-17449-3
单位: Federal University of Uberlandia, Avenue João Naves de Ávila, MG, 38400-902, Uberlândia, Brazil
通讯: Felipe Chagas Rodrigues de Souza @ Federal University of Uberlandia, Avenue João Naves de Ávila, MG, 38400-902, Uberlândia, Brazil
相关性: 该论文研究刀具纹理对加工Ti6Al4V的影响，未涉及Ti-6Al-4V增材制造件的后处理工艺。
边界: This paper does not study any post-processing treatment of Ti-6Al-4V; it investigates laser texturing of cutting tools for machining Ti-6Al-4V, which is about tool modification, not material post-processing.
动机· 探索不同沟槽纹理几何参数对车削Ti6Al4V的碳化物刀具性能的影响。
方法· 使用飞秒激光在刀具表面制备无缺陷纹理，并测试不同参数下的切削力。
结果· 纹理方向和距离切削刃的距离是影响最大的参数，而纹理尺寸影响较小。
验证· 通过车削实验测量切削力验证纹理效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how different groove texture geometries on carbide cutting tools affect the turning performance of Ti6Al4V titanium alloy.
Method· Femtosecond laser was used to produce defect-free textures on tool surfaces, and cutting forces were measured under various texture parameters.
Result· Texture direction and distance from the cutting edge were the most influential parameters, while texture dimensions had lesser impact.
Validation· Machining experiments with force measurement validated the effects of textures.
