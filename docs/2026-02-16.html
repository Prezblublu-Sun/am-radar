Geometric deviation and compressive failure of Ti-6Al-4V alloy honeycomb structures printed by laser powder bed fusion examined by X-ray microcomputed tomography

Md. Bengir Ahmed Shuvho, Sophie Rapagna, Agatha Labrinidis, Aditya Khanna, Andrei Kotousov et al. Progress in Additive Manufacturing 2026-02-16 10.1007/s40964-026-01542-w
单位: School of Electrical and Mechanical Engineering, The University of Adelaide, Adelaide, SA, 5005, Australia
通讯: Md. Bengir Ahmed Shuvho @ School of Electrical and Mechanical Engineering, The University of Adelaide, Adelaide, SA, 5005, Australia
相关性: 该论文直接研究激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V蜂窝结构的几何偏差和压缩失效，符合L1方向对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V力学性能的要求。
边界: Paper focuses on compressive behavior of honeycomb structures, not on tensile/fatigue properties typical of the direction, but still within scope for Ti-6Al-4V AM mechanical characterization.
动机· 研究打印缺陷和几何形状对LPBF Ti-6Al-4V蜂窝结构压缩性能的影响。
方法· 采用微CT分析打印质量，并进行压缩试验及断后扫描。
结果· 垂直打印结构几何精度更高，蜂窝设计和打印方向显著影响名义模量，压缩失效模式为剪切破坏。
验证· 通过微CT和压缩试验验证了打印缺陷与力学性能的关系。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the influence of printing defects, honeycomb geometries, and printing orientations on the compressive mechanical properties of LPBF Ti-6Al-4V cylinders.
Method· X-ray microcomputed tomography was used to assess geometric formation accuracy and defects, followed by compression testing and post-fracture scanning.
Result· Vertically printed honeycomb cylinders exhibited higher geometric accuracy; both honeycomb design and orientation significantly affected nominal modulus, with shear failure observed in all cases.
Validation· Experimental validation via micro-CT characterization and compression mechanical testing.

Effect of melt pool interference modes on mesostructure and microstructure of laser powder bed fusion fabricated Ti-6Al-4V alloy

Zhuohan Cao, Yi Liu, Jingyuan Shen, Zi Li, Yupei Tian et al. Materials Science and Engineering A 2026-02-16 10.1016/j.msea.2026.149944
单位: Key Laboratory for Advanced Materials Processing Technology, Ministry of Education, Beijing 100084, China
通讯: Xiaopeng Li @ School of Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering, University of New South Wales (UNSW Sydney), Sydney, NSW 2052, Austr
相关性: 该论文直接研究了激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V的熔池干扰模式对微观结构和力学性能的影响，符合基础层方向。
边界: The paper does not include fatigue testing, focusing instead on tensile properties and defect formation.
动机· 研究熔池干扰模式对LPBF Ti-6Al-4V组织和性能的影响。
方法· 采用数值模拟与机器学习分析相结合的方法。
结果· 连续熔池干扰形成均匀热环境，改善力学性能；间歇干扰导致缺陷和组织粗化。
验证· 通过力学性能测试和相对密度比较验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand how different melt pool interference modes affect the thermal history, microstructure, and resulting mechanical properties in LPBF-fabricated Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· A combined numerical simulation and machine learning approach was used to analyze local thermal profiles and microstructure evolution under continuous and intermittent melt pool interference.
Result· Intermittent interference caused heat accumulation, lack-of-fusion defects, high residual stress, and microstructural coarsening leading to degraded mechanical properties, while continuous interference promoted a uniform thermal environment and improved properties.
Validation· Mechanical properties (strength, ductility) and relative density were measured to validate the detrimental effects of intermittent interference.

Plasma-Enhanced Graphene Coatings on Ti-6Al-4V: Insights from Non-Destructive Characterization

Victor J. Sagrero, Fnu Gorky, Vashanti Storr, Fernando M. de de Oliveira, Hector Carreon et al. Materials 2026-02-16 10.3390/ma19040774
单位: Instituto de Investigación en Metalurgia y Materiales, Universidad Michoacana de San Nicolás de Hidalgo, Morelia 58030, Michoacán, Mexico
通讯: Maria L. Carreon @ Ralph E. Martin Department of Chemical Engineering, University of Arkansas, Fayetteville, AR 72701, USA
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V基体上的石墨烯涂层，但未涉及增材制造或力学/疲劳性能，与用户关注方向无关。
边界: No additive manufacturing content; focuses on surface coating and sensing, not on mechanical or fatigue performance of AM Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 通过石墨烯涂层改善Ti-6Al-4V表面性能以用于传感应用。
方法· 采用PECVD在Ti-6Al-4V上沉积石墨烯，并用Raman、热电势、涡流等非破坏性方法表征。
结果· 获得了均匀的微米级石墨烯涂层，表现出金属性Seebeck行为，对基底厚度敏感。
验证· 通过Raman光谱、SEM和热电测量验证涂层质量和性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the surface properties of Ti-6Al-4V for implantable sensor applications by depositing graphene coatings.
Method· Graphene coatings were deposited on Ti-6Al-4V ELI substrates via PECVD and characterized by Raman spectroscopy, thermoelectric potential, eddy current, and SEM.
Result· Uniform micrometer-thick graphene coatings with metallic-like Seebeck coefficients and sensitivity to substrate thickness were obtained.
Validation· Experimental characterization using non-destructive techniques and SEM confirmed coating uniformity and thermoelectric behavior.

Analysis of the influence of geometric parameters of laser-produced texturing on carbide tools in Ti6Al4V turning

Felipe Chagas Rodrigues de Souza, Fábio Rüstow de Paula, Álisson Rocha Machado, Wagner de Rossi The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology 2026-02-16 10.1007/s00170-026-17449-3
单位: Federal University of Uberlandia, Avenue João Naves de Ávila, MG, 38400-902, Uberlândia, Brazil
通讯: Felipe Chagas Rodrigues de Souza @ Federal University of Uberlandia, Avenue João Naves de Ávila, MG, 38400-902, Uberlândia, Brazil
相关性: 该论文研究的是车刀纹理几何参数对钛合金车削性能的影响，而非Ti-6Al-4V增材制造后的后处理工艺。
边界: The paper does not study any post-processing treatment applied to additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; instead it investigates laser texturing on carbide cutting tools for turning Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 探索不同沟槽纹理几何参数对硬质合金刀具车削Ti6Al4V性能的影响。
方法· 使用飞秒激光在刀具上制备不同深度、宽度、间距、方向和距刃距的纹理，并测试车削力。
结果· 纹理方向和距刃距是影响切削力的主要参数，纹理尺寸影响较小。
验证· 通过车削实验测量切削力验证纹理效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to understand how geometric parameters of groove textures on carbide cutting tools influence machining performance during turning of Ti6Al4V titanium alloy.
Method· Femtosecond laser was used to produce defect-free textures with varying depth, width, spacing, direction, and distance from the cutting edge on carbide tools, followed by turning tests.
Result· Texture direction and distance from the cutting edge were the most influential parameters on machining forces, while texture dimensions had a lesser impact.
Validation· Experimental turning tests measured machining forces to validate the influence of texture parameters.
