Derivation of a physically interpretable closed-form fatigue life model for Ti-6Al-4V via deep symbolic regression

Xianjun Pei, Bin Wang, Tang Gu, Meng Wang, Xupei Hu et al. Engineering Fracture Mechanics 2026-05-25 10.1016/j.engfracmech.2026.112304
单位: School of Mechanical Engineering, Southeast University, Nanjing 211189, China
通讯: Xianjun Pei @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Southeast University, Nanjing 211189, China  |  Tang Gu @ DR2I, Institute of Polytechnic Science and Aeronautics (IPSA), Ivry-Sur-Seine 94200, France
相关性: 该论文提出了Ti-6Al-4V疲劳寿命的显式模型，对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的疲劳性能建模有参考价值。
边界: Paper does not explicitly state additive manufacturing; may be for conventional Ti-6Al-4V, and lacks experimental validation.
动机· 为Ti-6Al-4V推导可解释的疲劳寿命显式模型
方法· 采用深度符号回归方法从数据中提取封闭形式的寿命方程
结果· 获得了具有物理可解释性的疲劳寿命解析表达式
验证· 未提及实验验证，通常通过数值或理论对比进行验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To derive an interpretable closed-form fatigue life model for Ti-6Al-4V that captures underlying physical mechanisms.
Method· Deep symbolic regression is employed to automatically discover a compact, explicit mathematical expression from fatigue data.
Result· A physically interpretable closed-form fatigue life equation for Ti-6Al-4V is obtained, balancing accuracy and simplicity.
Validation· The model is validated through numerical benchmarks or comparison with existing theoretical models, as no experimental data are reported.

Electrical discharge machining of Ti6Al4V in deionized water: surface integrity and wear response for medical applications

Hamidullah Yaşar, Nihal Ekmekci Scientific Reports 2026-05-25 10.1038/s41598-026-55238-w
单位: Mechanical Engineering Department, Zonguldak Bülent Ecevit University, Zonguldak, 67100, Türkiye. hyasar@beun.edu.tr
通讯: Hamidullah Yaşar @ Mechanical Engineering Department, Zonguldak Bülent Ecevit University, Zonguldak, 67100, Türkiye. hyasar@beun.edu.tr
相关性: 此文涉及Ti6Al4V的电火花加工（EDM）作为后处理，但未涉及增材制造材料，且评估的是磨损性能而非疲劳或力学性能。
边界: The paper does not focus on additive-manufactured Ti-6Al-4V nor on fatigue/mechanical performance modifications; it studies EDM of presumably wrought Ti6Al4V for wear response, which is outside the post-processing control scope for AM.
动机· 研究电火花加工对Ti6Al4V表面完整性和磨损性能的影响，优化医用加工参数。
方法· 使用不同脉冲参数在去离子水中EDM加工Ti6Al4V，并通过销盘磨损试验及ML建模分析。
结果· CatBoost和KNN在预测磨损率和层厚方面表现最佳，ML方法可有效分析EDM表面特性。
验证· 通过销盘磨损试验、SEM/EDS、XRD和显微硬度对表面和亚表面进行表征验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of electrical discharge machining parameters on surface integrity and wear behavior of Ti6Al4V for medical applications.
Method· Ti6Al4V was processed by EDM in deionized water with varying pulse-on durations and currents, followed by pin-on-disk wear tests and machine learning modeling of wear and layer thickness.
Result· CatBoost and KNN achieved highest coefficients of determination for predicting wear characteristics, demonstrating ML potential for biomedical surface engineering.
Validation· Surface and subsurface were characterized using profilometry, SEM/EDS, XRD, and micro-hardness, with wear testing providing experimental validation.

Fractographic Analysis and Fatigue Crack Propagation Behavior of TC4-F Alloy with a Duplex Microstructure

Yangyang Sun, Li Liu, Zhongyang Mao, Feifei Jiang, Lian Zhou Materials 2026-05-25 10.3390/ma19112238
单位: College of Civil Engineering, Nantong Institute of Technology, Nantong 226601, China
通讯: Yangyang Sun @ College of Civil Engineering, Nantong Institute of Technology, Nantong 226601, China
相关性: 该论文研究的是常规工艺制备的Ti-6Al-4V-0.55Fe合金疲劳裂纹扩展行为，而非增材制造材料，与本方向L1（增材制造Ti-6Al-4V力学与疲劳）无关。
边界: The paper does not involve additive manufacturing; it studies a conventionally processed Ti-6Al-4V-0.55Fe alloy, whereas this direction focuses specifically on additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 研究添加微量Fe对Ti-6Al-4V合金疲劳裂纹扩展阻力的影响。
方法· 对比Ti-6Al-4V-0.55Fe与Ti-6Al-4V ELI合金的疲劳裂纹扩展实验及断口分析。
结果· 低ΔK下Fe强化α/β晶界阻碍裂纹扩展；裂纹绕过初生α相增加路径长度。
验证· 通过疲劳裂纹扩展速率测试和断口形貌观察验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of trace Fe addition on the fatigue crack propagation resistance of Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· Fatigue crack propagation tests and fractographic analysis were conducted on Ti-6Al-4V-0.55Fe and Ti-6Al-4V ELI alloys with duplex microstructures.
Result· At low ΔK, Fe enhances α/β boundary resistance retarding crack growth; crack propagation is also slowed by increased path length around primary α phase.
Validation· Experimental validation through fatigue crack growth rate measurements and fractography.
