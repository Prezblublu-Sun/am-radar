Hydroxyapatite/Graphene Composite Anticorrosion Coating on Ti-6Al-4V: Fabrications and Trend

Zufar Maulana, Falihah Balqis, Muhammad Irsyad, Alhadi Kasyfi, Ekavianty Prajatelistia et al. ETHOS (Jurnal Penelitian dan Pengabdian) 2025-07-11 10.29313/ethos.v13i2.3814
单位: Bandung Institute of Technology
通讯: Zufar Maulana @ Bandung Institute of Technology
相关性: 该论文讨论Ti-6Al-4V的涂层，而非股骨柄的结构设计或力学性能。
边界: Paper does not address femoral stem structural design, lattice architecture, stress shielding, fatigue testing, or any mechanical/biomechanical analysis of stems; it is a review on anticorrosion coatings.
动机· 提高Ti-6Al-4V植入物耐腐蚀性

验证· 无实验验证，纯综述
English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve the corrosion resistance of Ti-6Al-4V orthopedic implants through coatings.
Method· This work reviews trends, fabrication techniques, and future perspectives of hydroxyapatite/graphene composite coatings on Ti-6Al-4V.
Result· A summary of current trends and future directions for hydroxyapatite/graphene coatings is provided.
Validation· No experimental validation; the paper is a literature review.

Effect of Post-Weld Heat Treatment on Residual Stress and Fatigue Crack Propagation Behavior in Linear Friction Welded Ti-6Al-4V Alloy

Sungkyoung Lee, Hyunsung Choi, Yunji Cho, Min Jae Baek, Hyeonil Park et al. Materials 2025-07-11 10.3390/ma18143285
单位: Aerospace Materials Center, Korea Institute of Materials Science, 797 Changwondaero, Changwon 51508, Republic of Korea
通讯: Namhyun Kang @ Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Pusan National University, Busan 46241, Republic of Korea  |  Dong-Jun Lee @ Aerospace Materials Center, Korea Institute of Materials Science, 797 Changwondaero, Changwon 51508, Republic of Korea
相关性: 该论文涉及线性摩擦焊而非增材制造，因此与方向不直接相关。
边界: This paper studies welded joints, not additive manufactured components; therefore, it does not address post-processing of AM Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 研究PWHT对线性摩擦焊Ti-6Al-4V接头残余应力和疲劳裂纹扩展的影响。
方法· 采用SEM、EBSD、硬度测试、DIC拉伸和轮廓法测量残余应力。
结果· PWHT有效降低了残余应力，使疲劳裂纹均匀扩展，速率与母材相当。
验证· 通过轮廓法测量残余应力，并与疲劳试验结果对比验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how post-weld heat treatment affects residual stress and fatigue crack propagation in linear friction welded Ti-6Al-4V joints.
Method· Microstructural characterization (SEM, EBSD), mechanical testing (hardness, DIC tensile), and residual stress measurement (contour method) were conducted.
Result· Post-weld heat treatment at 650°C and 750°C significantly reduced residual stresses, leading to uniform fatigue crack growth with rates similar to base metal.
Validation· Experimental validation through residual stress contour method and fatigue crack growth rate comparison.
