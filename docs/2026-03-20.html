Factors controlling the residual stresses in laser powder bed fusion fabricated AlSi10Mg and Ti6Al4V

M. Rais A. Taufiq, Peidong He, Qian Liu, Zhuohan Cao, Bernd Gludovatz et al. Progress in Additive Manufacturing 2026-03-20 10.1007/s40964-026-01574-2
单位: Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering, Universitas Gadjah Mada, Yogyakarta, Indonesia
通讯: M. Rais A. Taufiq @ Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering, Universitas Gadjah Mada, Yogyakarta, Indonesia
相关性: 本文研究了激光粉末床熔融Ti6Al4V的残余应力与工艺参数的关系，属于L1方向的核心内容。
边界: 本文重点在于残余应力控制而非直接的力学性能（如拉伸、疲劳）表征，且同时研究了AlSi10Mg。
动机· 激光粉末床熔融中残余应力影响零件精度和性能，需明确工艺参数的影响。
方法· 采用桥曲率法测量AlSi10Mg和Ti6Al4V的残余应力，分析激光功率、扫描速度和基板预热的影响。
结果· Ti6Al4V残余应力高于AlSi10Mg，仅基板预热显著影响Ti6Al4V的残余应力，归因于马氏体相变冷却速率。
验证· 通过桥曲率法实验测量样品平面度偏差作为残余应力指标。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Residual stresses in LPBF parts cause distortion and reduce mechanical properties, motivating a systematic study of how process parameters control them.
Method· The bridge curvature method was used to measure residual stresses in AlSi10Mg and Ti6Al4V samples fabricated with varying laser power, scan speed, and build plate preheating.
Result· Only build plate preheating significantly affected residual stresses in Ti6Al4V, attributed to its influence on cooling rates during martensitic transformation; AlSi10Mg showed strong correlation with all parameters.
Validation· Experimental measurement of total flatness deviation (FLTt) via bridge curvature method served as a relative residual stress indicator.

Multi-objective process parameter optimization using a hybrid reinforcement/machine learning model in metal additive manufacturing

Arash Seifoddini, Arefeh Azad, Mohammad Hossein Mosallanejad, Abdollah Saboori Advances in Manufacturing 2026-03-20 10.1007/s40436-026-00596-x
单位: Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Polytechnic University of Turin, 10129, Torino, Italy
通讯: Abdollah Saboori @ Department of Management and Production Engineering, Polytechnic University of Turin, 10129, Torino, Italy
相关性: 该论文主要研究Ti-6Al-4V的LPBF工艺参数优化，而非力学或疲劳性能，仅间接相关。
边界: No experimental mechanical or fatigue characterization of Ti-6Al-4V; focuses on melt pool prediction and defect control via machine learning optimization.
动机· 为优化LPBF工艺参数以控制熔池缺陷，首次提出机器学习与强化学习混合框架。
方法· 从文献提取数据训练高斯过程回归模型，结合Q学习强化学习智能体推导最优参数。
结果· GPR模型预测精度高（R²=96.05%），RL方法实现目标熔池深度并最小化归一化焓。
验证· 使用文献实验数据验证预测精度，未进行新实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To optimize L-PBF process parameters for controlling melt pool characteristics and defects, a hybrid machine learning and reinforcement learning framework is proposed for the first time.
Method· Experimental data from the literature were used to train Gaussian process regression (GPR) models, which were then integrated with a Q-learning agent to derive optimal process parameters.
Result· The GPR model achieved high prediction accuracy (RMSE 34 µm, R² 96.05%) for melt pool depth, and the RL framework successfully minimized the normalized enthalpy to reduce keyhole defect risk.
Validation· Validation was performed using literature experimental data to assess model accuracy; no new experimental validation was conducted.

Mechanical Properties of 3D Printed Porous Ti-6Al-4V Alloy for Biomedical Applications

Markéta Straková, Jiří Kubásek, Dalibor Vojtěch MANUFACTURING TECHNOLOGY 2026-03-20 10.21062/mft.2026.005
相关性: 该文研究了Ti-6Al-4V多孔结构的力学性能，虽未直接涉及股骨柄设计，但TPMS结构特性对植入物设计有参考价值。
边界: The paper does not focus on a femoral/hip stem design, stress shielding, Gruen zones, or ISO fatigue testing of stems; it only examines generic TPMS lattice samples under compressive and bending loads.
动机· 为生物医学应用优化多孔Ti-6Al-4V合金的力学性能。
方法· 通过增材制造制备Diamond和Gyroid结构的样品，进行压缩和弯曲测试。
结果· Diamond结构刚度和强度更高，Gyroid结构更各向同性且柔性更好。
验证· 通过实验力学测试验证不同孔隙结构的性能差异。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study aims to optimize the mechanical properties of porous Ti-6Al-4V structures for biomedical applications by comparing TPMS architectures.
Method· Additively manufactured Diamond and Gyroid TPMS samples were tested under compressive and bending loads to evaluate their mechanical behavior.
Result· Diamond structures exhibited higher stiffness and strength, while Gyroid structures provided a more isotropic and flexible response.
Validation· The mechanical behavior was validated through experimental compression and bending tests on fabricated specimens.

Mechanical Properties of High-Entropy Coatings of the (TiZrVCrAl)N System of Different Architectures Deposited by the Arc-PVD Method on the Surface of Ti-6Al-4V Alloy

Yana N. Savina, R. R. Valiev, R. R. Valiev, Stanislav Ovchinnikov, A. Yu. Nazarov et al. Metals 2026-03-20 10.3390/met16030350
单位: Institute of Physics of Advanced Materials, Ufa University of Science and Technology, Ufa 450076, Russia
通讯: R. R. Valiev @ Herbert Gleiter International Institute, Liaoning Academy of Materials, Shenyang 110167, China  |  Prof. Ruslan Z. Valiev @ Herbert Gleiter International Institute, Liaoning Academy of Materials, Shenyang 110167, China
相关性: 本文涉及Ti-6Al-4V基体上的涂层性能，但未涉及增材制造工艺，因此与方向无关。
边界: The paper does not study additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it investigates coatings deposited on conventionally processed Ti-6Al-4V, which is outside the scope of the L1 direction.
动机· 研究在Ti-6Al-4V基体上沉积高熵涂层以提升机械性能。
方法· 采用电弧物理气相沉积制备不同结构涂层并进行纳米压痕和划痕测试。
结果· 多层涂层性能优于单层，超细晶基体上效果更佳。
验证· 纳米压痕和划痕实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the mechanical properties of high-entropy coatings deposited on Ti-6Al-4V substrates with different microstructures.
Method· Arc-PVD was used to deposit monolayer and multilayer (TiZrVCrAl)N coatings, and mechanical performance was evaluated via nanoindentation and scratch tests.
Result· Multilayer coatings, especially the 720-layer (TiZrVCrAl)N/TiN on ultrafine-grained Ti-6Al-4V, exhibited superior H/E and H3/E2 ratios.
Validation· Experimental nanoindentation and scratch testing.
