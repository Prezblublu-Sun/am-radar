High-Throughput Exploration of Refractory High-Entropy Alloys for Strength and Plasticity

Stephen A. Giles, Hugh Shortt, Peter K. Liaw, Debasis Sengupta arXiv preprint 2025-11-20 link
相关性: 该论文使用深度学习筛选难熔高熵合金的力学性能，但未涉及增材制造工艺，与用户研究方向的AM力学性能无直接关联。
边界: No additive manufacturing context; the study focuses on alloy composition design and bulk mechanical testing, not on AM-specific process-structure-property relationships.
动机· 难熔高熵合金成分空间大，需兼顾高温强度和室温塑性。
方法· 利用深度学习模型预测超10万种RHEA的高温屈服强度和室温塑性。
结果· 筛选出多个候选合金，实验验证其综合性能超过已有报道。
验证· 通过实验合成并测试候选合金的力学性能进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Refractory high-entropy alloys have vast composition spaces and require simultaneous high-temperature strength and room-temperature plasticity.
Method· Deep learning models were used to predict compressive yield strength at 1000°C and room-temperature plasticity for over 100,000 RHEA compositions.
Result· Multiple candidate alloys with exceptional strength-plasticity balance were identified and experimentally confirmed to outperform prior RHEAs.
Validation· Experimental synthesis and mechanical testing (compression) of selected candidates validated the predictions.

Skyrmionium meta-matter: a topologically heterogeneous magnetic crystal with emergent hybrid dynamics

Andrey O. Leonov, Kaito Nakamura arXiv preprint 2025-11-20 link
相关性: 该论文研究磁性拓扑结构，与生物打印、髋关节植入物、有限元代理或生物医学增材制造无关。
边界: This paper studies topologically heterogeneous magnetic crystals for spintronic applications, with no connection to biomechanics, implant design, FEA surrogate modeling, or biomedical additive manufacturing.
动机· 探索新型磁性拓扑结构及其混合动力学
方法· 通过微磁学模拟构建天空离子-斯格明子混合晶格
结果· 发现变形辅助旋转和轨道模式等独特混合激发
验证· 数值模拟验证吸收谱和自旋动力学
English summary & terms
Motivation· To introduce and characterize a new class of topological magnetic crystals with hybrid dynamics beyond conventional skyrmion lattices.
Method· Systematically investigated skyrmionium-skyrmion mixed lattices via micromagnetic simulations varying composition and lattice spacing.
Result· Identified deformation-assisted rotations and orbital modes unique to these mixed crystals, with mode frequencies spanning sub-GHz to above 10 GHz.
Validation· Validated through numerical simulations of spin dynamics and absorption spectra under AC magnetic fields.
