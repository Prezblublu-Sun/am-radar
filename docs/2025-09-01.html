Simulating the Frequency and Dwell Effects on Fatigue Life Using an Evolutionary Damage Model

D. S. Petukhov, I. E. Keller Mechanics of Solids 2025-09-01 10.1134/s0025654425605488
单位: Institute of Continuous Media Mechanics UB RAS, 614013, Perm, Russia
通讯: D. S. Petukhov @ Institute of Continuous Media Mechanics UB RAS, 614013, Perm, Russia
相关性: 该论文提出了考虑加载频率和保载效应的疲劳损伤模型，并针对Ti-6Al-4V进行了实验验证，属于L1层核心研究。
边界: The paper is primarily a modeling study, but it includes experimental validation and covers frequency/dwell effects on fatigue, which is within scope; no missing aspects for the L1 direction.
动机· 模拟加载频率和保载周期对疲劳寿命的影响
方法· 建立与塑性应变无关、依赖于应力时间历程的演化疲劳损伤模型
结果· 模型能准确描述不同频率和保载下的疲劳数据并能预测更广范围载荷条件
验证· 通过不同频率和保载周期的疲劳试验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To simulate the influence of loading frequency and dwell periods on the fatigue life of Ti-6Al-4V alloy.
Method· An evolutionary fatigue damage model was developed as a functional of stress variation over time, generalized to account for rheonomic effects.
Result· The model correctly describes experimental fatigue data under various frequencies and dwell times and predicts fatigue life for a wider range of loading conditions.
Validation· The model was validated using fatigue tests with varied frequencies and dwell periods on VT6 (Ti-6Al-4V) specimens.

Low cycle fatigue behavior of the beta-type titanium cellular structures fabricated by electron beam melting

单位: School of Electrical Engineering, Shenyang University of Technology, Shenyang, 110870, Liaoning, China
通讯: Chao Li @ Department of Spine Surgery, Shengjing Hospital of China Medical University, Shenyang, 110004, China  |  S.J. Li @ Institute of Metal Research, Chinese Academy of Sciences, 72 Wenhua Road, Shenyang, 110016, China
相关性: 该论文的基材是β型钛合金，并非Ti-6Al-4V，因此与方向要求不符。
边界: The paper does not study Ti-6Al-4V; it investigates a β-type titanium alloy. The only mention of Ti-6Al-4V is as a comparative reference, not as the primary material.
动机· 研究β型钛多孔结构作为植入物的低周疲劳性能。
方法· 用电子束熔融制备两种晶胞构型，进行压缩和应变控制低周疲劳测试。
结果· 菱形十二面体结构比拓扑优化结构具有更好的应变控制低周疲劳性能。
验证· 通过实验对比不同构型和材料（含Ti-6Al-4V参考）的疲劳数据。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand the low-cycle fatigue behavior of beta-type titanium cellular structures for safe long-term implant applications.
Method· Cellular structures with rhombic dodecahedron and topology-optimized unit cells were fabricated by EBM and tested under compressive and strain-controlled LCF conditions.
Result· The rhombic dodecahedron structure showed lower yield strength but superior strain-controlled LCF performance compared to the topology-optimized structure.
Validation· Experimental comparison of LCF properties between two geometries and with Ti-6Al-4V reference under identical EBM conditions.

In silico testing of a multimaterial scaffold for mandibular reconstruction

Pedro Rebolo, Vincenzo Orassi, Bruno Areias, Sara Checa, Nilza Ramião et al. Frontiers in Bioengineering and Biotechnology 2025-09-01 10.3389/fbioe.2025.1535756
单位: INEGI - Institute of Science and Innovation in Mechanical and Industrial Engineering, Porto, Portugal
通讯: Pedro Rebolo @ INEGI - Institute of Science and Innovation in Mechanical and Industrial Engineering, Porto, Portugal
相关性: 论文主要关注下颌骨重建的多材料支架设计，而非增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学性能或疲劳特性。
边界: The paper does not present mechanical or fatigue characterization of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; it is a biomechanical simulation study on scaffold design for mandibular reconstruction, using Ti-6Al-4V only as a fixation plate material.
动机· 探究支架设计对下颌骨重建生物力学环境的影响。
方法· 建立下颌骨有限元模型，模拟不同固定材料和支架结构的力学响应。
结果· 多材料支架比纯水凝胶支架产生更高应变，可能更利于骨愈合。
验证· 有限元仿真，未涉及实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· The study aims to understand how scaffold design and material distribution affect biomechanical conditions in mandibular reconstruction after segmental resection.
Method· A 3D finite element model of the mandible was developed to simulate a large bone defect bridged by a scaffold, supported by a titanium mesh, and stabilized with a fixation plate made of either Ti-6Al-4V or PLA, testing three scaffold architectures and two material distributions.
Result· PLA fixation plates induced higher strains in the healing region than titanium plates; scaffold architecture had little effect, but multimaterial scaffolds (hydrogel/ceramic) produced higher strains than pure hydrogel scaffolds, potentially promoting bone healing.
Validation· The study is computational only, with no experimental validation of the model or materials.

单位: Universitas Dirgantara Marsekal Suryadarma, Indonesia
通讯: Budi Aji Warsiyanto @ Universitas Dirgantara Marsekal Suryadarma, Indonesia

边界: This paper does not cover femoral stem design, orthopaedic implants, or any biomedical mechanical performance; it focuses on bird strike resistance of helicopter engine cowling using lattice structures.
动机· 评估晶格结构用于直升机发动机整流罩抗鸟撞的防护效果。

English summary & terms

Method· Nonlinear dynamic finite element simulations coupled with Taguchi optimization to identify optimal lattice topology, material, and relative density for maximum specific energy absorption.
Result· Body-centered cubic Z (BCCZ) lattice made of Ti-6Al-4V with 30% relative density provided the best impact energy absorption.
Validation· The study relied solely on computational simulations without experimental validation.
