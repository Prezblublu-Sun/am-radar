Ancient Principles, Modern Impact: Increased Femoral Offset Demonstrates Decreased Load to Failure in Total Hip Arthroplasty.

William F Sherman, Jackson P Tate, Nolan M Reinhart, Justin Kim, Andrew M Gabig et al. Journal of the American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons. Global research & reviews 2025-09-01 10.5435/JAAOSGlobal-D-25-00255
单位: From the Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Tulane University School of Medicine, New Orleans, LA (Dr. Sherman, Dr. Tate, Dr. Reinhart, Dr. Gabig, Verma, and Dr. Patel), and the Carle Illinois College
相关性: 该论文涉及股骨柄生物力学评估，但未涉及增材制造技术。
边界: This paper does not address additive manufacturing of femoral stems or design optimization for AM.
动机· 研究股骨距偏移对全髋关节置换术后股骨骨折风险的影响。
方法· 对两种不同颈干角的人工股骨柄进行力学测试和有限元分析。
结果· 高偏移柄的失效载荷显著低于标准偏移柄。
验证· 使用骨质疏松复合股骨模型进行轴向力学测试。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of femoral offset on load to failure in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Mechanical testing and finite element analysis were performed on two femoral stems with different neck-shaft angles.
Result· Stems with higher offset exhibited significantly lower ultimate load to failure.
Validation· Mechanical testing on osteoporotic composite femurs under axial loading.

单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Rush University Medical Center, Chicago, Illinois.
通讯: Tad L Gerlinger @ Department of Orthopaedic Surgery, Rush University Medical Center, Chicago, Illinois. [推断]
相关性: 该论文为临床回顾性研究，关注股骨假体周围骨折后的翻修结果，不涉及任何生物力学建模、有限元分析或增材制造设计。
边界: No biomechanics modeling, FEA, implant design, or AM methods; purely clinical outcomes without mechanical characterization or computational simulation.
动机· 评估急性股骨假体周围骨折后翻修术的并发症和再手术风险
方法· 回顾性分析49例初次THA后8周内因PFF行髋臼股骨翻修的患者
结果· 术后并发症发生率20.4%，再手术率18.4%，假体周围感染占10.2%

English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate outcomes and complication rates of isolated femoral revision after acute periprosthetic femoral fracture following primary total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective chart review of 49 patients who underwent revision THA within 8 weeks of primary THA, with mean follow-up of 5.5 years.
Result· Postoperative complications occurred in 20.4% of patients, with 18.4% requiring additional revision surgery, and periprosthetic joint infection in 10.2%.
Validation· Clinical follow-up data from an institutional database over 2004-2022.

方法· 回顾性病例报告，描述保留近端股骨柄、取出远端柄并联合钢板固定与异体骨板植骨的手术过程。

Result· The proximal stem preservation strategy minimized surgical morbidity and supported fracture healing, offering a less invasive option for complex APFFs.
Validation· Single case report without experimental or numerical validation.
