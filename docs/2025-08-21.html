Ultrastrong and ductile CoNiMoAl medium-entropy alloys enabled by L12 nanoprecipitate-induced multiple deformation mechanisms

Min Young Sung, Tae Jin Jang, Sang Yoon Song, Gunjick Lee, KenHee Ryou et al. arXiv preprint 2025-08-21 10.1016/j.jmst.2024.11.026
相关性: 该论文研究传统铸造合金的力学性能，与增材制造力学性能方向无关。
边界: This paper does not involve additive manufacturing, no AM processing, no AM-specific microstructures or defects.
动机· 探索新型CoNiMoAl中熵合金中L12析出相的强化机制。
方法· 通过改变Al含量设计合金，结合DFT计算和TEM分析变形行为。
结果· (CoNi)81Mo12Al7合金实现1086 MPa屈服强度、1520 MPa抗拉强度和35%塑性。
验证· 通过拉伸测试、TEM和DFT计算验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To uncover the strengthening mechanisms in novel Co-Ni-Mo-Al medium-entropy alloys with L12 precipitates by systematically varying Al content.
Method· Alloy design with varying Al content, combined with DFT calculations of generalized stacking fault energy and TEM dislocation analysis.
Result· The (CoNi)81Mo12Al7 alloy achieves yield strength of 1086 MPa, tensile strength of 1520 MPa, and ductility of 35%, with multiple deformation modes including stacking faults and nanotwins.
Validation· Mechanical tensile testing, TEM characterization, and DFT calculations.

Strong lead-free bioinspired piezoceramics for durable energy transducers

Ruxue Yang, Temesgen Tadeyos Zate, Peiren Wang, Soumyajit Mojumder, Elo Overgaard Mogensen et al. arXiv preprint 2025-08-21 link
相关性: 该论文研究仿生压电陶瓷的力学性能，与增材制造（AM）的力学性能研究方向无关。
边界: This paper does not involve additive manufacturing (AM) processes or AM mechanical properties; it focuses on colloidal processing of ceramics.
动机· 提高无铅压电陶瓷的力学耐久性而不牺牲压电性能。
方法· 采用可扩展的胶体工艺制备砖泥结构的仿生BNT陶瓷。
结果· 弯曲强度和断裂韧性提升2-3倍和1.6-2倍，抗疲劳性提升10-15倍。
验证· 通过Raman光谱、XRD和悬臂梁换能器弯曲模式验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the mechanical durability of lead-free piezoceramics without compromising piezoelectric performance.
Method· Bioinspired brick-and-mortar microstructure in BNT ceramics was fabricated via a scalable colloidal process.
Result· Flexural strength and fracture toughness were improved 2- to 3-fold and 1.6- to 2-fold, respectively, with 10- to 15-fold enhanced fatigue resistance.
Validation· Anisotropic residual stress fields were confirmed by Raman spectroscopy and XRD, and electromechanical fatigue resistance was demonstrated in cantilever beam transducers.
