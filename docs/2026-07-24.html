Microstructural gradients and associated mechanical and fatigue behavior in DED Ti-6Al-4V across the build height

Andrea Školáková, Jan Pinc, Hana Lesáková, Ondřej Kovářík, Karel Tesař et al. Materials Science in Additive Manufacturing 2026-07-24 10.36922/msam026210047
相关性: 该论文研究了DED成形Ti-6Al-4V的微观结构和疲劳性能，但未涉及任何后处理工艺，属于第一层（as-built）研究。
边界: The paper studies as-built DED Ti-6Al-4V without applying any post-processing treatment, so it does not address the effect of post-processing on mechanical/fatigue performance.
动机· 理解DED Ti-6Al-4V构件沿高度的微观结构梯度及其对力学和疲劳性能的影响。
方法· 通过EBSD、TEM和力学测试系统表征不同高度的微观结构及拉伸/疲劳行为。
结果· 组织为马氏体α'，孔隙垂直于构建方向，疲劳裂纹源于孔隙但扩展受马氏体控制，延性低。
验证· 通过拉伸和疲劳试验验证微观结构-性能关系。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand how process-induced microstructural gradients and porosity across build height affect the mechanical and fatigue performance of DED Ti-6Al-4V components.
Method· Systematic characterization of microstructure via EBSD, TEM, and mechanical testing (tensile and fatigue) at different build heights.
Result· The alloy exhibits a martensitic α' microstructure with no significant texture, porosity oriented perpendicular to build direction, and fatigue crack initiation at pores, while crack propagation is governed by the martensitic phase.
Validation· Experimental validation through mechanical testing including tensile and fatigue tests.
