Comparison of traditional and robotic size prediction methods and stem positioning outcomes following prediction discrepancies in hip arthroplasty.

Wenzhe Wang, Fan Chen, Zian Zhang, Chaoqun Yu, Chang Liu et al. Journal of robotic surgery 2025-07-21 10.1007/s11701-025-02594-w
单位: Department of Joint Surgery, The Affiliated Hospital of Qingdao University, Qingdao, 266000, Shandong, China.
通讯: Haining Zhang @ Department of Joint Surgery, The Affiliated Hospital of Qingdao University, Qingdao, 266000, Shandong, China. hainingzha
相关性: 该论文探讨了机器人辅助全髋关节置换术中的假体尺寸预测和柄位置，但未涉及应力遮挡、微动、骨整合或任何生物力学或FEA建模。
边界: This paper is a clinical retrospective study on robotic vs. traditional size prediction in THA, with no implant mechanics (stress shielding, micromotion), no FEA, no AI/ML model, and no AM/additive manufacturing. It does not address any of the user's core directions.
动机· 比较传统与机器人两种假体尺寸预测方法在THA中的准确性差异。
方法· 回顾性分析RATHA患者数据，按计划与实际尺寸匹配情况分组并比较柄位置。
结果· 机器人系统预测更准确，使用较小柄导致冠状面成角增大和填充比降低。
验证· 通过术后影像学测量对比各组柄位置参数。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study compares the accuracy of traditional 2D and robotic MAKO methods for predicting prosthesis size in total hip arthroplasty.
Method· A retrospective analysis was conducted on patients who underwent robotic-assisted THA, categorizing cases into matched, reduced, enlarged, and deviated groups based on plan-actual size consistency, and postoperative stem positioning was compared.
Result· The robotic system showed higher prediction accuracy for both cup and stem, and using a smaller stem than planned was associated with larger coronal plane angulation and reduced canal fill ratio.
Validation· Validation was performed via retrospective clinical data and postoperative imaging measurements.

Autonomous Multi-objective Alloy Design through Simulation-guided Optimization

Penghui Yang, Chendong Zhao, Bijun Tang, Zhonghan Zhang, Xinrun Wang et al. arXiv preprint 2025-07-21 link
相关性: 该论文专注于合金设计，不涉及增材制造机械性能评估。
边界: Paper does not address additive manufacturing processes, mechanical properties of AM parts, or any biomedical implant context.
动机· 加速合金发现，解决多目标优化和实验成本问题。
方法· 提出AutoMAT框架，融合大语言模型、CALPHAD模拟和AI优化。
结果· 发现钛合金密度降低8.1%、强度提升13.0%，高熵合金强度提升28.2%。
验证· 通过实验合成和力学测试验证合金性能。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Accelerate alloy discovery by addressing vast compositional spaces and high experimental costs.
Method· Proposes AutoMAT, a hierarchical framework integrating large language models, automated CALPHAD simulations, residual-learning correction, and AI-guided optimization.
Result· Identifies a titanium alloy with 8.1% lower density and 13.0% higher strength than Ti-185, and a high-entropy alloy with 28.2% higher yield strength.
Validation· Experimental synthesis and mechanical testing confirm the predicted alloy properties.

Local texture of three-stage CVD SiC fibre by precession electron diffraction (PED) and XRD

B. Huang, Y. Q. Yang, M. H. Li, Y. X. Chen, X. Luo et al. arXiv preprint 2025-07-21 10.1179/1743284713Y.0000000468
相关性: 该论文研究的是CVD SiC纤维的织构，与增材制造和生物医学植入物方向无关。
边界: The paper studies texture in CVD SiC fibres, with no additive manufacturing context, biomedical application, or mechanical property validation relevant to the user's directions.
动机· 研究CVD SiC纤维的局部织构以理解其力学性能。
方法· 使用XRD和PED技术表征纤维不同沉积阶段的织构。
结果· 发现第二、三沉积阶段存在(110)<111>和(110)<115>织构，且第三阶段晶粒更细。
验证· XRD和PED结果相互验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the local texture of CVD SiC fibres for understanding their anisotropic mechanical properties.
Method· X-ray diffraction (XRD) and precession electron diffraction (PED) in TEM were used to characterize texture across different deposition stages.
Result· Two types of texture, (110)<111> and (110)<115>, were identified in the second and third stages, with finer grain size at the third stage due to lower temperature.
Validation· Agreement between XRD and PED results cross-validated the texture analysis.
