Critical geometrical constraints and buckling phenomena in Ti6Al4V ultra-thin walls printed by laser-based powder bed fusion

Arif Hussain, Junghoon Lee, Jae Hee Kim, Hae Ju Park, Young-Sam Kwon et al. Progress in Additive Manufacturing 2025-07-29 10.1007/s40964-025-01268-1
单位: Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH), Pohang, Korea, Republic of
通讯: Arif Hussain @ Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH), Pohang, Korea, Republic of
相关性: 论文涉及Ti6Al4V薄壁的屈曲和残余应力，但无机械/疲劳性能数据，与Layer 1方向不直接相关。
边界: The paper does not include any mechanical/fatigue performance data (e.g., tensile strength, fatigue life, S-N curves) for Ti-6Al-4V, which is the core requirement of the Layer 1 direction.
动机· 研究激光粉末床熔融中Ti6Al4V超薄壁的屈曲机制与几何约束。
方法· 实验打印不同厚度薄壁并观察屈曲，结合残余应力分析。
结果· 发现临界屈曲高度与壁厚之比恒定约31.5，并建立经验关系。
验证· 通过实验测量薄壁屈曲高度和残余应力验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the buckling mechanisms of free-form thin walls in PBF-LB/M of Ti6Al4V and identify geometric constraints limiting build height.
Method· Dense sub-100 µm Ti6Al4V thin walls were printed at various thicknesses and observed for buckling, with residual stress evolution characterized.
Result· A consistent critical buckling height-to-thickness ratio of ~31.5 was found for sub-150 µm walls, and an empirical correlation linking critical height to peak residual stress and thickness was established.
Validation· Experimental observation of buckling heights and residual stress measurements validated the empirical model.

Additive Manufacturing of Orthopaedic Implants: Advances in Titanium Alloys, Bioresorbable Metals, and Porous Structures for Clinical Applications

Moyosore Fakoya, Samuel O. Afolabi, Bukola Adesanmi Current Journal of Applied Science and Technology 2025-07-29 10.9734/cjast/2025/v44i74582
相关性: 该综述覆盖钛合金骨植入物增材制造，但缺乏对股骨柄结构设计、力学性能及验证的专门分析。
边界: This is a broad review on AM orthopaedic implants, not specifically addressing femoral stem structural design, lattice architectures, stress shielding, or stem fatigue/strength with experimental validation.
动机· 传统方法难以制造个性化骨植入物，需综述增材制造进展。
方法· 系统检索2020-2025年文献，分析钛合金、生物可吸收金属及多孔结构。
结果· Ti-6Al-4V仍最常用，多孔结构促进骨整合，临床案例显示积极效果。
验证· 文献综述，未提供新实验或计算验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To review recent advances in additive manufacturing of orthopaedic implants, focusing on titanium alloys, bioresorbable metals, and porous structures for clinical applications.
Method· A systematic literature search covering 2020-2025 was conducted across multiple databases to synthesize findings on materials, fabrication techniques, and clinical outcomes.
Result· Ti-6Al-4V remains the most studied alloy; porous structures enhance osseointegration; case studies in hip, knee, and spine show positive surgical outcomes.
Validation· No original experimental or computational validation; this is a review paper.

Novel Bayesian Statistical Analysis on Acoustic Emission Data during Dwell-fatigue of Ti-6Al-4V Alloys

Haoyu Hu, Fabien Briffod, Takayuki Shiraiwa, Manabu Enoki ISIJ International 2025-07-29 10.2355/isijinternational.isijint-2025-110
单位: Department of Materials Engineering, The University of Tokyo
通讯: Haoyu Hu @ Department of Materials Engineering, The University of Tokyo
相关性: 论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V合金的热处理微观结构，但未针对增材制造材料，且主要关注贝叶斯统计方法而非后处理对疲劳性能的影响。
边界: Not about additive-manufactured Ti-6Al-4V; no comparison of post-processing effects on mechanical performance; core contribution is a statistical method, not post-processing control.
动机· 揭示Ti-6Al-4V合金保载疲劳的阶段性应变累积机制
方法· 对三种不同热处理微观结构的试样进行保载疲劳实验，结合声发射监测与贝叶斯统计分析
结果· 贝叶斯方法成功检测到应变累积曲线的阶段转变点，并利用声发射参数预测了应变行为
验证· 通过实验声发射数据与应变曲线的对比验证了检测方法的有效性
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand the three-stage strain accumulation behavior during dwell-fatigue of Ti-6Al-4V alloys with different microstructures.
Method· Uniaxial dwell-fatigue tests with 120-second peak stress hold were performed on Ti-6Al-4V specimens having equiaxed, bimodal, and lamellar microstructures obtained by heat treatment, while recording acoustic emission signals and applying Bayesian Markov chain Monte Carlo methods to detect transition points and select strain accumulation models.
Result· The Bayesian analysis identified two change-points correlating with the primary-secondary-tertiary stages, and acoustic emission parameters, especially average frequency, enabled the prediction of strain accumulation behavior.
Validation· The detection of stage transitions was validated by direct comparison with the strain accumulation curves from experiments.

Effect of Heat Treatments and Related Microstructural Modifications on High-Cycle Fatigue Behavior of Powder Bed Fusion–Laser Beam-Fabricated Ti-6Al-2Sn-4Zr-6Mo Alloy

Gianluca Pirro, Alessandro Morri, Alessandra Martucci, Mariangela Lombardi, Lorella Ceschini Metals 2025-07-29 10.3390/met15080849
单位: Department of Industrial Engineering (DIN), Alma Mater Studiorum—University of Bologna, Viale del Risorgimento 4, 40136 Bologna, Italy
通讯: Gianluca Pirro @ Department of Industrial Engineering (DIN), Alma Mater Studiorum—University of Bologna, Viale del Risorgimento 4, 40136
相关性: 该论文研究的是Ti6246合金而非Ti-6Al-4V，与用户方向要求的材料不符。
边界: The paper focuses on Ti-6Al-2Sn-4Zr-6Mo (Ti6246), not Ti-6Al-4V, thus violating the material requirement for this direction.
动机· 研究热处理对PBF-LB制备的Ti6246合金高周疲劳行为的影响
方法· 采用α-β退火和固溶时效两种热处理，通过旋转弯曲疲劳测试分析
结果· STA825处理获得更细的微观结构，疲劳强度比AN875提高25%
验证· 通过疲劳实验和Kitagawa-Takahashi模型验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how post-processing heat treatments affect the high-cycle fatigue behavior of PBF-LB Ti6246 alloy.
Method· Two heat treatments (α-β annealing at 875°C and solution treatment at 825°C followed by aging at 500°C) were applied, and rotating bending fatigue tests were conducted.
Result· The STA825 condition exhibited 25% higher fatigue resistance than AN875 due to finer bilamellar microstructure.
Validation· Fatigue experiments and the Kitagawa-Takahashi approach with the El-Haddad model were used for validation.
