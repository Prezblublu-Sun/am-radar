Multiaxial fatigue behavior and modeling for additive manufactured Ti-6Al-4V: The effect of surface texture

Seung-Jong Lee, Reza Molaei, Patricio E. Carrion, Shuai Shao, Nima Shamsaei International Journal of Fatigue 2025-07-29 10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2025.109205
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Auburn University, Auburn, AL 36849, USA
通讯: Nima Shamsaei @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Auburn University, Auburn, AL 36849, USA
相关性: 本论文直接研究了增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的多轴疲劳行为及表面纹理影响，高度相关。
边界: This paper fully covers the L1 direction focus; it does not require post-processing or implant-specific aspects.
动机· 探究表面纹理对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V多轴疲劳行为的影响。
方法· 通过多轴疲劳实验并结合建模分析表面纹理效应。
结果· 发现表面纹理显著影响多轴疲劳寿命，模型可有效预测。
验证· 采用多轴疲劳实验数据验证模型准确性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of surface texture on the multiaxial fatigue behavior of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Multiaxial fatigue experiments were conducted and a modeling framework incorporating surface texture effects was developed.
Result· Surface texture was found to significantly influence multiaxial fatigue life, and the proposed model accurately predicted the observed behavior.
Validation· The model was validated against experimental multiaxial fatigue test data.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate buckling mechanisms of ultra-thin Ti6Al4V walls fabricated by laser powder bed fusion.
Method· Experimental printing of sub-100 µm walls and analysis of residual stress and buckling.
Result· A consistent aspect ratio of 31.5 for critical buckling height to wall thickness was found.
Validation· Mechanical testing and residual stress measurement.

Novel Bayesian Statistical Analysis on Acoustic Emission Data during Dwell-fatigue of Ti-6Al-4V Alloys

Haoyu Hu, Fabien Briffod, Takayuki Shiraiwa, Manabu Enoki ISIJ International 2025-07-29 10.2355/isijinternational.isijint-2025-110
单位: Department of Materials Engineering, The University of Tokyo
通讯: Haoyu Hu @ Department of Materials Engineering, The University of Tokyo
相关性: 该论文研究了不同热处理工艺对Ti-6Al-4V合金保载疲劳过程中声发射数据的贝叶斯统计分析，其方法可应用于评估后处理对力学性能的影响，但非直接关注后处理效果。
边界: The paper does not directly quantify the effect of post-processing on fatigue life or mechanical performance; instead, it focuses on a novel statistical method for acoustic emission data analysis during dwell-fatigue.
动机· 利用声发射数据贝叶斯统计分析监测Ti-6Al-4V保载疲劳阶段转变
方法· 对三种显微组织的Ti-6Al-4V试样进行保载疲劳实验，采用MCMC方法检测声发射数据拐点
结果· 检测到两个拐点与应变累积曲线三阶段转变强相关，平均频率可监测保载疲劳起始
验证· 通过保载疲劳实验和声发射测量验证贝叶斯方法有效性
English summary & terms
Motivation· To monitor stage transitions in dwell-fatigue of Ti-6Al-4V alloys using Bayesian statistical analysis of acoustic emission data.
Method· Uniaxial dwell-fatigue tests with 120-second peak stress hold were conducted on Ti-6Al-4V specimens with equiaxed, bimodal, and lamellar microstructures, and Markov chain Monte Carlo simulation was applied to detect change-points in acoustic emission signals.
Result· Two detected change-points correlated strongly with the three-stage strain accumulation curve, and average frequency was found to be critical for monitoring the onset of dwell-fatigue.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed through mechanical dwell-fatigue testing with concurrent acoustic emission measurements.

相关性: 该综述广泛涵盖骨科植入物增材制造，但对Ti-6Al-4V股骨柄的结构设计及力学性能缺乏针对性。
边界: The paper is a general review covering multiple implant types and materials, with no specific focus on Ti-6Al-4V femoral stem structural design, stress shielding, or mechanical testing.
动机· 综述增材制造在骨科植入物中的进展，集中于钛合金、生物可吸收金属及多孔结构。
方法· 系统文献分析，涵盖2020-2025年文献，总结SLM、EBM等技术。
结果· Ti-6Al-4V是最广泛研究的合金，多孔结构可提升骨整合，但临床推广需进一步验证。
验证· 无实验验证，为文献综述。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This review surveys recent advances in additive manufacturing for orthopaedic implants, focusing on titanium alloys, bioresorbable metals, and porous structures for clinical applications.
Method· A systematic literature analysis of studies published between 2020 and 2025, covering techniques such as selective laser melting and electron beam melting.
Result· Ti-6Al-4V remains the most extensively studied alloy, and porous structures fabricated by AM improve osseointegration, but widespread clinical adoption requires further in vivo and long-term data.
Validation· No experimental validation; the paper is a literature review without original experimental or computational data.

Effect of Heat Treatments and Related Microstructural Modifications on High-Cycle Fatigue Behavior of Powder Bed Fusion–Laser Beam-Fabricated Ti-6Al-2Sn-4Zr-6Mo Alloy

Gianluca Pirro, Alessandro Morri, Alessandra Martucci, Mariangela Lombardi, Lorella Ceschini Metals 2025-07-29 10.3390/met15080849
单位: Department of Industrial Engineering (DIN), Alma Mater Studiorum—University of Bologna, Viale del Risorgimento 4, 40136 Bologna, Italy
通讯: Gianluca Pirro @ Department of Industrial Engineering (DIN), Alma Mater Studiorum—University of Bologna, Viale del Risorgimento 4, 40136
相关性: 论文使用Ti-6Al-2Sn-4Zr-6Mo合金，而非Ti-6Al-4V，与L2方向要求不符。
边界: The paper investigates Ti-6Al-2Sn-4Zr-6Mo alloy, not Ti-6Al-4V, thus does not meet the mandatory requirement of the focus direction.
动机· 研究Ti6246合金不同热处理后的疲劳行为差异。
方法· 对比α-β退火和固溶时效两种热处理对显微组织和疲劳性能的影响。
结果· STA825处理比AN875疲劳强度提高25%，归因于更细的层状显微组织。
验证· 旋转弯曲疲劳实验和Kitagawa-Takahashi模型分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study aims to understand how different heat treatments influence the high-cycle fatigue behavior and failure mechanisms of Ti-6Al-2Sn-4Zr-6Mo alloy produced by powder bed fusion.
Method· Two post-processing heat treatments were applied: α-β annealing at 875°C (AN875) and solution treatment at 825°C followed by aging at 500°C (STA825), and the resulting microstructures and fatigue properties were characterized.
Result· The STA825 condition exhibited 25% higher fatigue resistance than AN875, attributed to a finer bilamellar microstructure with thinner primary α lamellae and more homogeneous secondary α lamellae distribution.
Validation· Rotating bending fatigue tests were performed and the Kitagawa-Takahashi approach was used to analyze defect sensitivity and crack propagation resistance.
