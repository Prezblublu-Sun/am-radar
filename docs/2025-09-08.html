Hybrid auxetics: Postponing failure with elastomer infiltration of Ti-6Al-4V lattices

Frédéric Albertini, Justin Dirrenberger, Cyrille Sollogoub, Andrey Molotnikov, Jérôme Adrien et al. International Journal of Mechanical Sciences 2025-09-08 10.1016/j.ijmecsci.2025.110704
单位: PIMM, Arts et Métiers, Cnam, CNRS UMR 8006, 151 bd de l’Hôpital, 75013 Paris, France
通讯: Justin Dirrenberger @ Institut universitaire de France (IUF), Paris, France
相关性: 该论文直接研究激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V点阵结构的力学性能，包括弹性体浸润后的失效延迟和能量吸收，符合L1方向要求。
边界: This paper focuses on compressive behavior of auxetic lattice structures with elastomer infiltration, rather than standard tensile/fatigue characterization of bulk Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 利用弹性体浸润延迟金属点阵结构的失效
方法· 设计可打印的六边形反手性单元，激光粉末床熔融制备Ti-6Al-4V点阵，聚氨酯浸润后进行原位压缩显微CT实验
结果· 混合点阵延迟了支柱级失效和剪切带形成，缓解了节点开裂，在大变形下保持承载能力
验证· 原位压缩实验结合显微断层成像验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To postpone failure in additively manufactured metal lattice structures by infiltrating a soft elastomeric phase for enhanced integrity and stress redistribution.
Method· A novel hexaround auxetic unit-cell was designed for printability via laser powder-bed fusion of Ti-6Al-4V; lattices were infiltrated with compliant polyurethane and tested with in-situ microtomography under compression.
Result· Hybrid lattices exhibited delayed strut-level failure, mitigated nodal cracking, and more controlled collapse, maintaining load-bearing capacity at large deformations.
Validation· In-situ compression experiments coupled with microtomography provided direct evidence of shear-band deferral and crack mitigation.

Influence of Subsurface Defects and Material Anisotropy on the Fretting Fatigue Response of AMed Ti-6Al-4V

Glodek, Grzegorz, Gothivarekar, Sanjay, Talemi, Reza Lirias (KU Leuven) 2025-09-08 link
相关性: 该论文直接研究增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的微动疲劳性能，涉及缺陷和材料各向异性，与L1层方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not provide tensile or standard S-N fatigue data, focusing instead on fretting fatigue which is a specific contact-driven failure mode.
动机· 研究增材制造Ti-6Al-4V中亚表面缺陷和材料各向异性对微动疲劳行为的影响。
方法· 通过微动疲劳实验和有限元建模分析缺陷和织构的耦合作用。
结果· 发现亚表面缺陷显著降低微动疲劳寿命，各向异性影响裂纹起始位置。
验证· 基于实验的微动疲劳测试验证模型预测。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how subsurface defects and material anisotropy influence the fretting fatigue performance of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Fretting fatigue experiments combined with finite element modeling were conducted to analyze the coupled effects of defects and crystallographic texture.
Result· Subsurface defects significantly reduce fretting fatigue life, while anisotropy governs crack initiation sites.
Validation· Experimental fretting fatigue tests validated the model predictions.

Fretting Fatigue Behaviour of Laser Powder Bed Fusion- Produced Ti-6Al-4V: Experimental and Numerical Analysis

相关性: 该论文直接研究激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V的微动疲劳行为，包含实验与数值分析，完全符合L1方向要求。
边界: Focuses on fretting fatigue rather than plain axial fatigue, so contact-related fatigue behavior is emphasized.
动机· 揭示LPBF Ti-6Al-4V在接触条件下的微动疲劳行为。
方法· 开展微动疲劳实验并建立有限元模型进行分析。
结果· 获得了微动疲劳寿命和损伤机制的特征。
验证· 实验与数值结果对比验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the fretting fatigue behavior of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V produced by laser powder bed fusion.
Method· Fretting fatigue experiments were conducted combined with finite element simulations to analyze the stress and damage evolution.
Result· The fretting fatigue life and underlying damage mechanisms were characterized and compared between experimental and numerical results.
Validation· Validation was performed by directly comparing experimental fatigue life data with numerical predictions.

Mechanical and Corrosion Properties of Ultrafine-Grained TC4-0.55Fe Alloy Processed by Equal-Channel Angular Pressing

Yumeng Guo, Lu Yu, Miaoxia He, Yu Wang, Yuecheng Dong et al. Crystals 2025-09-08 10.3390/cryst15090795
单位: College of Materials Science and Engineering, Tech Institute for Advanced Materials, Nanjing Tech University, Nanjing 211816, China
通讯: Yuecheng Dong @ College of Materials Science and Engineering, Tech Institute for Advanced Materials, Nanjing Tech University, Nanjing 21
相关性: 该论文研究的是非增材制造的TC4-0.55Fe合金通过ECAP获得的力学性能，与增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的机械性能与疲劳方向无关。
边界: This paper does not involve additive manufacturing (LPBF/SLM/EBM/DED) and instead uses severe plastic deformation (ECAP) on a Fe-modified Ti-6Al-4V alloy, so it provides no data on as-built or process-state AM Ti-6Al-4V mechanical/fatigue performance.
动机· 研究多道次ECAP对TC4-0.55Fe合金力学和腐蚀性能的影响。
方法· 通过室温拉伸、电化学实验、SEM和EBSD表征分析ECAP处理效果。
结果· 四道次ECAP后屈服强度提升至995 MPa，伸长率略降至12%，腐蚀性能显著改善。
验证· 通过拉伸和腐蚀实验验证性能变化。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This work aims to investigate the effect of multi-pass equal-channel angular pressing on the mechanical and corrosion properties of TC4-0.55Fe alloy.
Method· Room-temperature tensile tests, electrochemical experiments, SEM, and EBSD characterization were employed to evaluate the processed alloy.
Result· After four ECAP passes, yield strength and ultimate tensile strength increased to 995 MPa and 1022 MPa, respectively, with a slight decrease in elongation to 12.0%, while corrosion resistance was significantly enhanced.
Validation· The results were validated through mechanical testing and electrochemical corrosion measurements.

Surface Modification of Ti-6Al-4V with Surface Roughness, Wettability and Wear Analysis Using Titanium Carbonitride Powder-Mixed Electric Discharge Process

N. V. Prajina, Jose Mathew, Basil Kuriachen Journal of Materials Engineering and Performance 2025-09-08 10.1007/s11665-025-11998-6
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, LBS College of Engineering, Kasaragod, 671542, India
通讯: N. V. Prajina @ Department of Mechanical Engineering, LBS College of Engineering, Kasaragod, 671542, India
相关性: 该论文采用非增材制造的放电加工表面改性方法，与增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学和疲劳性能无关。
边界: The paper studies surface modification via electric discharge machining, not additive manufacturing, and does not address mechanical or fatigue properties of as-built or process-state AM Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 通过粉末混合电火花加工改善Ti-6Al-4V的表面性能。
方法· 使用钛碳氮化物粉末混合电火花加工对Ti-6Al-4V进行表面改性。
结果· 表面粗糙度、润湿性和耐磨性得到改善。
验证· 通过实验测量表面粗糙度、接触角和磨损率进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To enhance the surface properties of Ti-6Al-4V through powder-mixed electric discharge machining.
Method· Titanium carbonitride powder was mixed into the dielectric fluid during electric discharge machining of Ti-6Al-4V.
Result· Improved surface roughness, wettability, and wear resistance were achieved.
Validation· Experimental characterization of surface roughness, contact angle, and wear rate.
