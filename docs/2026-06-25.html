Effect of in-situ heat treatment on flow stress behavior of additive manufacturing of Ti–6Al–4V alloy under quasi-static and dynamic deformation: a comparative study through experimental and modelling through J–C model and ANN approach

Pai Chen Lin, H. H. Liu, Pei-Cheng Wu, Zhi-Hong Zhang, Chi-Wei Wang et al. Progress in Additive Manufacturing 2026-06-25 10.1007/s40964-026-01780-y
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering & Advanced Institute of Manufacturing with High-tech Innovations, National Chung Cheng University, Chiayi, Taiwan
通讯: Pai Chen Lin @ Department of Mechanical Engineering & Advanced Institute of Manufacturing with High-tech Innovations, National Chung Ch
相关性: 该论文研究了原位热处理对Ti-6Al-4V合金流动应力行为的影响，虽然未直接涉及疲劳，但提供了后处理控制的相关数据。
边界: The paper does not directly address post-processing fatigue performance; it studies in-situ heat treatment during fabrication and focuses on flow stress rather than fatigue life.
动机· 为消除传统热处理方法，降低设备、时间和能耗成本。
方法· 通过单次扫描和原位热处理（双扫描）制备试样，进行准静态和动态拉伸试验，并建立改进的J-C模型和ANN预测模型。
结果· ANN模型与改进J-C模型预测的流动应力值吻合良好。
验证· 通过场发射扫描电镜和电子背散射衍射分析微观结构，并通过不同应变率下的拉伸实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To eliminate the need for post-build heat treatment and reduce costs by applying in-situ heat treatment during SLM of Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Single-scan and in-situ heat-treated (double-scan) samples were fabricated, and tensile tests at quasi-static and dynamic strain rates were performed; modified Johnson-Cook and ANN models were developed.
Result· The ANN model showed comparable prediction accuracy to the modified Johnson-Cook model for flow stress.
Validation· Microstructural analysis via FESEM/EBSD and experimental tensile testing at various strain rates validated the models.

Effect of laser shock peening parameters on electrochemical and tribocorrosion behaviour of Ti-6Al-4V alloy in simulated body fluid

Dileep Madapana, Harikrishnan Ramadas, Ashish Kumar Nath, Jyotsna Dutta Majumdar Optics & Laser Technology 2026-06-25 10.1016/j.optlastec.2026.115761
单位: Department of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, Kharagpur, West Bengal 721302, India
通讯: Jyotsna Dutta Majumdar @ Department of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, Kharagpur, West Bengal
相关性: 虽然采用了激光冲击强化（LSP）后处理，但关注的是电化学和摩擦腐蚀行为，而非疲劳或力学性能。
边界: The paper investigates laser shock peening but focuses on electrochemical and tribocorrosion properties, not fatigue or mechanical performance as required by the direction focus.
动机· 探究激光冲击强化参数对Ti-6Al-4V在模拟体液中电化学和摩擦腐蚀行为的影响
方法· 采用不同参数的激光冲击强化处理，随后进行电化学和摩擦腐蚀测试
结果· 激光冲击强化提高了耐腐蚀性并减少了磨损-腐蚀协同作用
验证· 通过实验测试验证电化学和摩擦腐蚀性能变化
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how laser shock peening parameters influence the electrochemical and tribocorrosion behavior of Ti-6Al-4V in simulated body fluid.
Method· The alloy was subjected to laser shock peening with varying parameters, followed by electrochemical and tribocorrosion tests.
Result· Laser shock peening improved corrosion resistance and reduced wear-corrosion synergy in simulated body fluid.
Validation· Validation was performed through experimental electrochemical and tribocorrosion measurements.

Rare-Earth modified biodegradable Zn alloys with high strength and enhanced osteogenesis

J Shi, Mingyu Qu, Chaoyang Sun, S Y Li, Jialian Xu et al. Nature Communications 2026-06-25 10.1038/s41467-026-74069-x
单位: School of Materials Science and Engineering Peking University, Beijing, PR China
通讯: Xinhua Qu @ Department of Bone and Joint Surgery, Department of Orthopedics, Renji Hospital, Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of  |  Hongtao Yang @ School of Engineering Medicine, Beihang University, Beijing, PR China  |  Yufeng Zheng @ School of Materials Science and Engineering Peking University, Beijing, PR China
相关性: 本文研究的是可降解Zn合金而非Ti-6Al-4V股骨柄设计，与用户方向无关。
边界: This paper focuses on biodegradable Zn alloys, not on Ti-6Al-4V femoral stem design, additive manufacturing, or any structural/mechanical analysis relevant to orthopedic implant stems.
动机· 开发兼具高强度和促骨再生能力的可降解Zn合金
方法· 向Zn-Li-Mn体系添加微量稀土元素形成REZn11-13相，构建双峰晶粒组织
结果· 合金抗拉强度达772 MPa，弯曲强度1207 MPa，骨再生能力为钛合金的两倍
验证· 通过动物实验验证骨再生效果，并与钛合金植入物对比
English summary & terms
Motivation· Developing biodegradable Zn alloys that combine high strength with enhanced osteogenesis for orthopedic implants.
Method· Trace rare-earth elements were added to a Zn-Li-Mn system to form REZn11-13 intermetallic phases, creating a bimodal grain structure.
Result· The alloy achieved a tensile strength of 772 MPa and bending strength of 1207 MPa, with twofold greater bone regeneration compared to titanium alloy implants.
Validation· In vivo animal experiments were performed to evaluate bone regeneration, with comparison to titanium alloy implants.
