Comparative study on the microstructure and mechanical properties of LPBF Ti-6Al-4V fabricated from PREP and EIGA powders

单位: Institute for Advanced Materials and Technology, University of Science and Technology Beijing, Beijing 100083, China
通讯: Xuepeng Ren @ Institute for Advanced Materials and Technology, University of Science and Technology Beijing, Beijing 100083, China  |  Jialong Chen @ College of Materials Science and Engineering, Beijing University of Technology, Beijing 100124, China  |  Shubin Ren @ Institute for Advanced Materials and Technology, University of Science and Technology Beijing, Beijing 100083, China  |  Jianhao Chen @ College of Materials Science and Engineering, Beijing University of Technology, Beijing 100124, China
相关性: 该论文直接研究LPBF Ti-6Al-4V的微观结构和力学性能，符合方向要求。
边界: No fatigue testing or fatigue life data; only static mechanical properties.
动机· 比较PREP和EIGA粉末对LPBF Ti-6Al-4V微观结构和力学性能的影响

验证· 通过拉伸、硬度等力学试验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To comparatively study the effect of powder source (PREP vs EIGA) on microstructure and mechanical properties of LPBF Ti-6Al-4V
Method· LPBF fabrication using PREP and EIGA powders followed by microstructural characterization and mechanical testing
Result· Differences in microstructure and mechanical properties (e.g., strength, ductility) are observed between the two powder conditions
Validation· Experimental mechanical testing including tensile and hardness tests

Exploiting Adiabatic Softening for Defect-Free Hot Forging of Ti-6Al-4V Femoral Stems

Víctor Tuninetti, Josué Castro, Rodrigo Valle, César Garrido, Ángelo Oñate Journal of Functional Biomaterials 2026-06-12 10.3390/jfb17060292
单位: Department of Mechanical Engineering, Universidad de La Frontera, Temuco 4811230, Chile
相关性: 该论文涉及Ti-6Al-4V热锻造，而非增材制造，因此与用户研究方向无关。
边界: This paper concerns conventional hot forging of Ti-6Al-4V femoral stems, not additive manufacturing (LPBF/SLM/EBM/DED) of Ti-6Al-4V, so it does not meet the directional requirement for L1.
动机· 研究热锻过程中应变率和温度对Ti-6Al-4V股骨柄延性损伤的影响。
方法· 建立有限元框架，结合Johnson-Cook损伤准则，全因子分析不同工艺参数。
结果· 高冲压速度抑制损伤，低速度导致局部缺陷。
验证· 通过损伤准则验证确定安全工艺窗口。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To quantify the coupled influence of strain rate and temperature on ductile damage evolution during hot forging of Ti-6Al-4V femoral stems.
Method· A finite element framework incorporating strain rate- and temperature-dependent plasticity and the Johnson-Cook damage criterion was developed and analyzed via full factorial design.
Result· Increasing ram velocity enhances strain-rate hardening and reduces damage, while temperature has a secondary effect.
Validation· Damage criterion validation confirmed safe operating windows.

单位: Northwest Institute for Non-Ferrous Metal Research, Xi’an 710016, China
通讯: Bao Qu @ Western Superconducting Materials Technology Co., Ltd., Xi’an 710018, China  |  Xianghong Liu @ Northwest Institute for Non-Ferrous Metal Research, Xi’an 710016, China
相关性: 该论文研究了非Ti-6Al-4V合金的热处理，与用户关注的方向材料不符。
边界: The paper uses a Ti-Al-Mo-V-Cr-Sn-Zr alloy (Ti62F), not Ti-6Al-4V, and involves wrought bar processing, not additive manufacturing post-processing.
动机· 实现钛合金强度、塑性和韧性的协同提升
方法· 双退火热处理调控Ti62F合金显微组织
结果· 优化工艺获得强度-韧性平衡优于TA15、TC4等合金
验证· 通过拉伸和冲击实验验证力学性能
English summary & terms
Motivation· To achieve a favorable synergy of strength, ductility, and toughness in titanium alloys for engineering applications.
Method· Systematic investigation of double-annealing heat treatment on microstructure and mechanical properties of a novel Ti62F titanium alloy bar.
Result· Optimized schedule (880°C/2h/AC + 650°C/4h/AC) yields superior strength-toughness balance compared to conventional medium-strength titanium alloys.
Validation· Mechanical testing including tensile and impact tests, along with microstructural characterization.
