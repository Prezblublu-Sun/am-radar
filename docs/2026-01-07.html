Investigating the Impact of Bio-Inspired Structural Design on the Fatigue Life of Lightweight Metallic Alloys

Hyginus C.O. Unegbu, Danjuma Yawas The Journal of The Institution of Engineers Malaysia 2026-01-07 10.54552/v86i4.290
相关性: 该论文直接研究了增材制造Ti-6Al-4V仿生结构的疲劳性能，与用户方向完全契合。
边界: The paper examines systemic design-level fatigue behavior rather than material-level microstructure-property relationships.
动机· 探索仿生结构设计对轻质金属合金疲劳寿命的影响。
方法· 增材制造三种仿生几何结构（蜂窝、小梁、晶格）并进行疲劳测试。
结果· 仿生结构显著提升疲劳寿命，蜂窝结构提升最多达三倍。
验证· 通过S-N曲线、威布尔分布和断口分析验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of bio-inspired structural designs on the fatigue life of lightweight metallic alloys fabricated by additive manufacturing.
Method· Three bio-inspired geometries (honeycomb, trabecular, cellular lattice) were fabricated from Ti-6Al-4V and AA7075 using AM and tested under cyclic loading.
Result· Bio-inspired designs improved fatigue life by up to threefold for Ti-6Al-4V, with honeycomb structures showing the greatest enhancement.
Validation· Experimental fatigue testing with S-N curves, Weibull distribution analysis, and fractographic examination.

Effect of heat treatment on the microhardness of Ti-6Al-4V alloy fabricated by laser powder bed fusion and its prediction using machine learning

Jateen Shirodkar, Shruti Maheshwari, Vijaykumar Jatti, Vikas Gulia Springer Link (Chiba Institute of Technology) 2026-01-07 10.1051/epjconf/202634501060/pdf
相关性: 该论文直接研究退火热处理对LPBF制备的Ti-6Al-4V显微硬度的影响，并涉及机器学习预测，符合后处理对力学性能调控的方向。
边界: Does not include fatigue or tensile testing, limiting its coverage of mechanical performance to microhardness only.
动机· 探索热处后处理对LPBF Ti-6Al-4V显微硬度的影响，并利用机器学习预测。
方法· 采用田口实验设计对不同激光参数样品进行退火，测量维氏硬度，并用随机森林和CatBoost建模。
结果· 退火后硬度最高提升20.9%（543 HV），CatBoost预测准确度最高（R²=0.93）。
验证· 通过维氏硬度实测验证模型预测效果。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study investigates the effect of post-processing heat treatment on the microhardness of laser powder bed fused Ti-6Al-4V and explores machine learning for its prediction.
Method· A Taguchi orthogonal array was used to reduce experimental runs; specimens were annealed and measured by Vickers hardness, and Random Forest and CatBoost models were trained on as-built and heat-treated data.
Result· The highest hardness of 543 HV (20.9% increase) was achieved after annealing, with CatBoost yielding the best prediction accuracy (R²=0.93).
Validation· Experimental Vickers hardness measurements validated the machine learning predictions.

Effect of rear impact on the instrumented cervical spine: a finite element study

Balaji Harinathan, Davidson Jebaseelan, Narayan Yoganandan Frontiers in Bioengineering and Biotechnology 2026-01-07 10.3389/fbioe.2025.1681077
单位: Department of Neurosurgery, Medical College of Wisconsin, Milwaukee, WI, United States
通讯: Davidson Jebaseelan @ School of Mechanical Engineering, Vellore Institute of Technology, Chennai, India
相关性: 该论文涉及颈椎植入物的生物力学分析，仅提及Ti-6Al-4V的屈服强度，与增材制造Ti-6Al-4V的力学和疲劳性能无关。
边界: The paper does not concern additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V nor investigate its mechanical or fatigue performance; it is a finite element biomechanical study of cervical spine implants with only a passing mention of Ti-6Al-4V yield strength.
动机· 探究后碰撞下颈椎内固定系统的生物力学响应。
方法· 使用带主动肌肉的有限元模型模拟三种手术方式。
结果· PCLF降低脊髓应力但增加植入物应力，LP保留运动但增加韧带应变。
验证· 基于验证的有限元模型进行仿真分析。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To understand the biomechanical response of instrumented cervical spines under rear-impact loading conditions.
Method· A validated finite element model of the head-neck complex with active musculature and spinal cord was used to simulate ACDF, PCLF, and laminoplasty constructs.
Result· PCLF reduced spinal cord stress and strain at the index level but increased implant stress above Ti-6Al-4V yield strength at most levels; LP preserved motion at the cost of increased facet capsular ligament strain.
Validation· The study employed a previously validated finite element model of the cervical spine with active musculature.
