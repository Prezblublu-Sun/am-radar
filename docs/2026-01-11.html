Bgolearn: a Unified Bayesian Optimization Framework for Accelerating Materials Discovery

Bin Cao, Jie Xiong, Jiaxuan Ma, Yuan Tian, Yirui Hu et al. arXiv preprint 2026-01-11 link
相关性: 该论文介绍通用的贝叶斯优化框架，未涉及增材制造力学性能或生物医学植入物验证。
边界: This paper presents a Bayesian optimization framework for general materials discovery, without any focus on additive manufacturing, mechanical testing, or biomedical implant applications.
动机· 加速材料发现，降低实验成本
方法· 开发统一贝叶斯优化框架Bgolearn，集成多种代理模型和采集函数
结果· 相比随机搜索等方法减少40-60%实验次数
验证· 通过基准测试和三个材料发现案例验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· Efficient exploration of compositional and processing spaces is essential for accelerated materials discovery, but Bayesian optimization adoption is hindered by complexity.
Method· We present Bgolearn, a Python framework offering intuitive interfaces, multiple surrogate models, acquisition functions, and bootstrap-based uncertainty quantification for single and multi-objective optimization.
Result· Bgolearn reduces required experiments by 40-60% compared with traditional search methods while maintaining solution quality.
Validation· Validation includes benchmark studies and case studies on triply periodic minimal surface structures, high-entropy alloys, and high-strength steels.
