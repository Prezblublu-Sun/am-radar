Novel high cycle vibration-based fatigue test for pure shear loading

Andrea Varela de Albuquerque Dupke, Étienne Pessard, David Mellé, Annie Ross, Myriam Brochu International Journal of Fatigue 2025-08-05 10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2025.109180
单位: Arts et Metiers Institute of Technology, LAMPA, HESAM Université, Angers, France
通讯: Andrea Varela de Albuquerque Dupke @ Arts et Metiers Institute of Technology, LAMPA, HESAM Université, Angers, France
相关性: 本文提出了一种新型高频振动疲劳测试方法，用于评估激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V合金在纯剪切载荷下的疲劳性能，与标准扭转试验结果一致。
边界: This paper concentrates solely on pure shear fatigue and does not cover tensile/compressive or multiaxial fatigue behavior that is also common in structural applications.
动机· 传统疲劳测试成本高、周期长，需开发高效方法。
方法· 设计了共振纯剪切试样，在700Hz下进行振动疲劳试验，并用应变花验证应力状态。
结果· 获得的疲劳强度与标准扭转试验一致，裂纹起裂于典型增材制造缺陷处。
验证· 通过应变花测量和断口分析验证了纯剪切状态及失效机制。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To reduce the high cost and long duration of conventional fatigue tests for multiaxial stresses.
Method· A resonance-based fatigue specimen was designed to achieve pure shear loading, validated by a strain rosette, and tested at 700 Hz using an electrodynamic shaker.
Result· The fatigue strengths of L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V with as-built surfaces matched standard torsion tests at 5 Hz, with all failures initiating at process-related discontinuities.
Validation· Stress state validation via strain measurement and fractography confirmed pure shear conditions and failure mechanisms.

Establishing Rational Processing Parameters for Dry Finish-Milling of SLM Ti6Al4V over Metal Removal Rate and Tool Wear

С. В. Панин, А. В. Филиппов, Mengxu Qi, Zihao Ding, Qingrong Zhang et al. Construction Materials 2025-08-05 10.3390/constrmater5030053
单位: Laboratory of Mechanics of Polymer Composite Materials, Institute of Strength Physics and Materials Science of Siberian Branch of Russian Academy of Sciences, 634055 Tomsk, Russia
通讯: С. В. Панин @ Laboratory of Mechanics of Polymer Composite Materials, Institute of Strength Physics and Materials Science of Siberian
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM Ti6Al4V的干式精铣后处理工艺参数，但未涉及疲劳或力学性能，仅关注表面粗糙度和刀具磨损。
边界: The paper investigates machining parameters for dry finish-milling of SLM Ti6Al4V but does not evaluate mechanical performance (fatigue, tensile) or microstructure effects relevant to post-processing control.
动机· 研究干式精铣对SLM Ti6Al4V后处理的适用性，追求环保和成本效益。
方法· 改变径向切深和进给量，分析刀具磨损机制和表面粗糙度。
结果· 确定了优化铣削参数，主要磨损机制为积屑瘤和磨粒磨损。
验证· 通过多模式实验测量表面粗糙度和刀具磨损验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To establish rational dry finish-milling parameters for post-processing of SLM Ti6Al4V, focusing on environmental benefits and cost reduction.
Method· Varying radial depth of cut and feed rate at constant axial depth and cutting speed to analyze tool wear patterns and surface roughness.
Result· Identified near-optimal milling parameters and characterized built-up edge and abrasive wear mechanisms.
Validation· Experimental measurement of surface roughness and tool wear across multiple machining modes.

Evaluation of the Stress-Shielding Effect of a PEEK Knee Prosthesis. A Finite Element Study

Mario Ceddia, Arcangelo Morizio, Giuseppe Solarino, Bartolomeo Trentadue Osteology 2025-08-05 10.3390/osteology5030024
单位: Department of Mechanics, Mathematics and Management, Polytechnic of Bari University, 70125 Bari, Italy
通讯: Mario Ceddia @ Department of Mechanics, Mathematics and Management, Polytechnic of Bari University, 70125 Bari, Italy
相关性: 该论文研究的是PEEK膝关节假体与钛合金钛6铝4V的应力遮挡比较，并非针对钛6铝4V股骨柄的增材制造设计，因此与方向无关。
边界: The paper focuses on a knee prosthesis, not a femoral hip stem; it does not address additive manufacturing, stem structural design, lattice architectures, or fatigue testing for Ti-6Al-4V femoral stems.
动机· 比较PEEK和钛合金膝关节假体的应力遮挡效应
方法· 有限元分析评估胫骨近端应力分布和应力遮挡因子
结果· PEEK假体应力分布更均匀，应力遮挡更小
验证· 仅有限元模拟，无实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To compare stress-shielding effects of PEEK versus titanium alloy Ti6Al4V knee prostheses.
Method· Finite element analysis evaluated von Mises stress and stress-shielding factor at seven critical points on an osteoporotic tibial bone model under physiological loads.
Result· The PEEK prosthesis exhibited more uniform stress transmission with values within the optimal range and minimal stress shielding, unlike the titanium implant.
Validation· Finite element simulation without experimental validation.
