Novel high cycle vibration-based fatigue test for pure shear loading

Andrea Varela de Albuquerque Dupke, Étienne Pessard, David Mellé, Annie Ross, Myriam Brochu International Journal of Fatigue 2025-08-05 10.1016/j.ijfatigue.2025.109180
单位: Arts et Metiers Institute of Technology, LAMPA, HESAM Université, Angers, France
通讯: Andrea Varela de Albuquerque Dupke @ Arts et Metiers Institute of Technology, LAMPA, HESAM Université, Angers, France
相关性: 该论文针对激光粉末床熔融Ti-6Al-4V的纯剪切疲劳行为提出了高效振动测试方法，直接符合L1层对增材制造Ti-6Al-4V力学与疲劳性能的研究方向。
边界: The paper focuses on developing a novel test method rather than systematically investigating anisotropy, layer thickness, or porosity effects, but it still provides fatigue data for as-built L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 传统纯剪切疲劳测试耗时且成本高昂，亟需高效方法获取代表性数据。
方法· 开发基于电振动台的纯剪切共振疲劳测试方法，采用平板试样并验证应力状态。
结果· 在700Hz下获得Ti-6Al-4V L-PBF的疲劳强度，与5Hz标准扭转试验结果一致，失效均源于典型AM缺陷。
验证· 通过应变花验证应力状态，并结合断口分析确认失效机制。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Conventional pure shear fatigue tests are time-consuming and expensive, motivating the development of a faster, reliable method.
Method· A novel vibration-based fatigue test for pure shear loading was designed using plate specimens, validated with strain rosettes, and applied at 700 Hz on an electrodynamic shaker.
Result· Fatigue strengths of as-built L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V obtained at 700 Hz matched standard torsion test results at 5 Hz, with all failures initiating at lack of fusion or surface roughness features.
Validation· Experimental validation included strain rosette measurements and fractographic analysis confirming edge-free crack initiation in the high-stress zone.

单位: Mechanical and Electrical Engineering Institute, Shenyang Aerospace University, Shenyang 110136, China
通讯: Guang Yang @ Mechanical and Electrical Engineering Institute, Shenyang Aerospace University, Shenyang 110136, China

边界: This paper focuses on a post-processing surface finishing treatment (ultrasonic-assisted magnetic abrasive finishing) rather than the as-built mechanical or fatigue characterization of Ti-6Al-4V required by Layer 1.

结果· 超声辅助磁力研磨改善了表面形貌并减少了表面缺陷
验证· 通过扫描电镜和粗糙度测量进行实验验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate the effect of ultrasonic-assisted magnetic abrasive finishing on the surface morphology and defects of SLM-produced TC4 alloy.
Method· Ultrasonic-assisted magnetic abrasive finishing was applied to SLM TC4 samples, and surface morphology and defects were characterized.
Result· Ultrasonic-assisted magnetic abrasive finishing improved surface morphology and reduced surface defects.
Validation· Experimental validation was performed through SEM imaging and surface roughness measurements.

Establishing Rational Processing Parameters for Dry Finish-Milling of SLM Ti6Al4V over Metal Removal Rate and Tool Wear

С. В. Панин, А. В. Филиппов, Mengxu Qi, Zihao Ding, Qingrong Zhang et al. Construction Materials 2025-08-05 10.3390/constrmater5030053
单位: Laboratory of Mechanics of Polymer Composite Materials, Institute of Strength Physics and Materials Science of Siberian Branch of Russian Academy of Sciences, 634055 Tomsk, Russia
通讯: С. В. Панин @ Laboratory of Mechanics of Polymer Composite Materials, Institute of Strength Physics and Materials Science of Siberian
相关性: 该论文研究了SLM Ti6Al4V后处理中的干式精铣削参数优化，但未评估对疲劳或力学性能的影响，因此与用户关注的后处理对力学性能影响方向关联较弱。
边界: This paper investigates milling parameters for surface finish and tool wear, but does not provide experimental data on the effect of post-processing on tensile/fatigue properties or microstructure evolution, which is the core requirement for this direction.
动机· 为了环境友好，研究干式精铣削SLM Ti6Al4V的合理参数。
方法· 通过改变径向切深和进给量进行铣削实验，观察刀具磨损和表面粗糙度。
结果· 确定了接近合理的铣削参数（Vc=60 m/min等），并分析了刀具磨损机理。
验证· 通过实验测量刀具磨损和表面粗糙度验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To establish rational dry finish-milling parameters for post-processed SLM Ti6Al4V while considering tool wear and surface roughness.
Method· Milling experiments with varied radial depth of cut and feed rate at constant axial depth and cutting speed, examining tool wear patterns and surface roughness.
Result· Identified near-optimal parameters (Vc=60 m/min, Vf=400 mm/min, ap=4 mm, ae=0.4 mm) and characterized wear mechanisms (built-up edge, abrasive wear).
Validation· Experimental measurements of tool wear and surface roughness.

Evaluation of the Stress-Shielding Effect of a PEEK Knee Prosthesis. A Finite Element Study

Mario Ceddia, Arcangelo Morizio, Giuseppe Solarino, Bartolomeo Trentadue Osteology 2025-08-05 10.3390/osteology5030024
单位: Department of Mechanics, Mathematics and Management, Polytechnic of Bari University, 70125 Bari, Italy
通讯: Mario Ceddia @ Department of Mechanics, Mathematics and Management, Polytechnic of Bari University, 70125 Bari, Italy
相关性: 该论文研究的是PEEK膝关节假体而非Ti-6Al-4V股骨柄，与用户方向不匹配。
边界: The paper studies a knee prosthesis made of PEEK, not a Ti-6Al-4V femoral stem for hip arthroplasty, and does not involve additive manufacturing.
动机· 评估PEEK膝关节假体与钛合金假体的应力遮挡效应。
方法· 使用有限元分析比较PEEK和Ti6Al4V假体在骨质疏松胫骨模型上的应力分布。
结果· PEEK假体应力传递更均匀，应力遮挡因子接近零，优于钛合金。
验证· 仅有限元仿真，无实验验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the stress-shielding effect of a PEEK knee prosthesis compared to a conventional titanium implant in total knee arthroplasty.
Method· Finite element analysis was used to compute stress distribution and stress-shielding factor at seven critical points on the proximal tibia under physiological loading.
Result· The PEEK prosthesis showed more uniform stress transmission and minimal stress shielding, while titanium implants exhibited significant stress shielding.
Validation· The study relied solely on finite element simulation without experimental or clinical validation.
