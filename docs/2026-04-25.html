Ultra-High Dynamic Strength of Additively Manufactured GRX-810 Under Coupled Conditions of High Strain Rate and Elevated Temperature

Naveen Dinujaya, Suhas Eswarappa Prameela arXiv preprint 2026-04-25 link
相关性: 该论文直接研究增材制造GRX-810合金在高应变率和高温下的动态力学性能，与用户关注的增材制造力学性能方向高度相关。
边界: This paper does not cover static or fatigue mechanical properties, microstructure characterization, or process-property relationships for biomedical implant applications.
动机· 揭示增材制造ODS-MPEA在高应变率和高温下的动态强度机制。
方法· 采用分离式霍普金森压杆测试动态压缩性能。
结果· ODS变体动态强度达准静态2.79倍，但高温下发生热软化。
验证· 通过实验动态力学测试验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· Investigate the dynamic strength of additively manufactured oxide-dispersion-strengthened multi-principal element alloys under coupled high strain rate and elevated temperature conditions.
Method· Split-Hopkinson pressure bar experiments were conducted to characterize the dynamic compressive response.
Result· The ODS variant exhibited a dynamic strength 2.79 times its quasi-static strength at ambient temperature, but thermal softening occurred at elevated temperatures.
Validation· Experimental mechanical testing at high strain rates and elevated temperatures validated the findings.

Comparison of Cemented and Cementless Hemiarthroplasty Outcomes: A Retrospective Analysis of Mortality, Complications, and Subsidence

Murat Taşçı, Furkan Uçar, Elif Reyyan Çadırcıbaşı Düzce Tıp Fakültesi Dergisi 2026-04-25 10.18678/dtfd.1810569
单位: Ümraniye Training and Research Hospital, Department of Orthopaedics and Traumatology
通讯: Murat Taşçı @ Ümraniye Training and Research Hospital, Department of Orthopaedics and Traumatology
相关性: 该论文为临床回顾性研究，未涉及任何生物力学建模、有限元分析或植入物设计，与用户研究方向无关。
边界: This paper is a clinical retrospective study of hemiarthroplasty outcomes with no biomechanical modeling, FEA, stress shielding quantification, or implant design analysis.
动机· 比较骨水泥与非骨水泥半髋关节置换的临床和影像学结果
方法· 回顾性分析364例股骨颈骨折半髋关节置换患者的数据
结果· 两组在死亡率、并发症和假体下沉方面无显著差异，术前等待时间影响早期死亡率
验证· 基于医院记录的临床数据统计验证
English summary & terms
Motivation· This study compares clinical and radiological outcomes between cemented and cementless hemiarthroplasty for femoral neck fractures.
Method· A retrospective analysis was conducted on 364 patients who underwent hemiarthroplasty, collecting demographic, perioperative, and postoperative data.
Result· No significant differences were found in mortality, implant-related complications, or stem subsidence between the two groups; preoperative waiting time was strongly associated with one-year mortality.
Validation· Validation was based on statistical analysis of clinical records from hospital archives.

Weak Polar Optical Phonon Scattering Decouples Electron and Phonon Transport in Layered Thermoelectric Materials

Zhonghao Xia, Michele Reticcioli, Yateng Wang, Yali Yang, Alessandro Stroppa et al. arXiv preprint 2026-04-25 link
相关性: 该论文研究热电材料中的电子和声子输运，与用户的研究方向（生物打印、髋关节植入物、有限元代理、增材制造生物力学）完全无关。
边界: This paper focuses on thermoelectric materials and does not address any biomedical context, implant mechanics, mechanical testing, or additive manufacturing for biomedical applications.
动机· 寻找高效热电材料以解耦电子和声子输运。
方法· 高通量密度泛函理论筛选236种层状半导体。
结果· 发现GaGe2Te具有高电导率和超低热导率。
验证· 理论计算验证，无实验。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To identify high-performance thermoelectric materials by decoupling electron and phonon transport in layered compounds.
Method· High-throughput density functional theory screening of 236 layered semiconductors for low effective mass and weak polar optical phonon scattering.
Result· GaGe2Te exhibits exceptionally high cross-plane electrical conductivity and power factor along with ultralow lattice thermal conductivity.
Validation· Validation is performed through first-principles calculations without experimental confirmation.
