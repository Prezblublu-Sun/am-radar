Computational Design of Ductile Additively Manufactured Tungsten-Based Refractory Alloys

Kareem Abdelmaqsoud, Daniel Sinclair, Venkata Satya Surya Amaranth Karra, S. Mohadeseh Taheri-Mousavi, Michael Widom et al. arXiv preprint 2026-01-16 link
相关性: 该论文直接研究增材制造钨合金的力学性能，通过计算和实验结合的方法优化延展性，与您关注的AM力学性能方向高度相关。
边界: This paper focuses on refractory tungsten alloys for nuclear fusion, not on biomedical implants or bone mechanics, and does not address stress shielding, osseointegration, or implant stability.
动机· 克服钨在增材制造中的脆性和低可打印性，设计高延展性成分。
方法· 结合DFT计算的Pugh比和机器学习势加速筛选W-Ta-Nb合金的延展性。
结果· 预测的高Pugh比合金W20Ta70Nb10和W30Ta60Nb10在实验中无微裂纹。
验证· 通过增材制造合金的裂纹分数实验验证Pugh比与延展性的相关性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To overcome the inherent brittleness and poor printability of tungsten in additive manufacturing by designing alloys with improved ductility.
Method· A computational approach combining density functional theory calculations of the Pugh ratio with machine learning interatomic potentials to rapidly screen alloy compositions in the W-Ta-Nb system.
Result· Alloys with the highest predicted Pugh ratios, W20Ta70Nb10 and W30Ta60Nb10, exhibited no intergranular microcracking in experimental validation.
Validation· Experimental validation via crack fraction analysis in additively manufactured alloys confirmed a strong correlation between the computed Pugh ratio and ductility.

Low Cycle Fatigue Response of Additive Manufactured Advanced Structural Alloys: Insights into High Performance Alloy Fatigue Life

N.C. Santhi Srinivas, Kausik Chattopadhyay, Shreyasi Vasu, Kumar R. Pavan, Jaydeep Vishwakarma Materials science forum 2026-01-16 10.4028/p-zpm7ep
单位: Indian Institute of Technology (Banaras Hindu University)
通讯: N.C. Santhi Srinivas @ Indian Institute of Technology (Banaras Hindu University)
相关性: 该论文直接研究了增材制造合金的低周疲劳性能，与用户关注的增材制造力学性能密切相关。
边界: This paper does not address biomedical implant applications or include biocompatibility, osseointegration, or in vivo testing.
动机· 研究增材制造合金在实际服役环境中的疲劳行为，确保其安全应用。
方法· 通过低周疲劳试验和扫描电镜断口分析，评估不同应变幅、热处理和构建方向对疲劳寿命的影响。
结果· 构建方向和应变幅显著影响疲劳寿命，层间缺陷降低疲劳寿命，超声喷丸可改善性能。
验证· 使用标准疲劳试验和SEM断口表征进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To evaluate the low cycle fatigue behavior of additively manufactured maraging steel and Inconel 625 for high performance applications.
Method· LCF tests at varying strain amplitudes, heat treatments, and build orientations, with fractographic analysis via SEM.
Result· Build orientation and strain amplitude significantly affect fatigue life; layer defects reduce life, while ultrasonic shot peening improves it.
Validation· Mechanical fatigue testing and SEM fractography.

MARS MRI for the Diagnosis of Aseptic Stem Loosening in Cementless Total Hip Arthroplasty.

Martin Aepli, Andreas Fischer, Hannes A Rüdiger, Michael Leunig, Andrea B Rosskopf et al. Investigative radiology 2026-01-16 10.1097/RLI.0000000000001256
相关性: 该论文为临床诊断研究，不涉及股骨柄假体的力学建模、应力遮挡、微动或增材制造，与用户研究方向不相关。
边界: This paper is a clinical diagnostic study using MARS MRI for stem loosening detection, with no biomechanics modeling, no FEA, no implant design, and no AM or AI/ML for bioprinting.
动机· 缺乏诊断股骨柄松动的MARS MRI验证标准。
方法· 回顾性分析114例全髋关节置换术后的MARS MRI，评估骨吸收、骨髓水肿等特征。
结果· 预测模型在远端区域具有高特异性（0.970）和阳性预测值（0.944）。
验证· 以术中发现为金标准进行临床验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To validate MARS MRI criteria for diagnosing aseptic femoral stem loosening in cementless total hip arthroplasty.
Method· Retrospective study of 114 cases assessed periprosthetic bone resorption, bone marrow edema, periosteal reaction, and osteolysis in Gruen zones, with a predictive model using binomial logistic regression.
Result· The model achieved 0.708 sensitivity, 0.970 specificity, and 0.944 positive predictive value for mid-distal findings indicating loosening.
Validation· Validation against intraoperative findings at revision surgery.
