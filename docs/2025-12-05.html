Acoustic emission monitoring reveals keyhole formation and evolution

Louis Galiègue, Fabien Briffod, Kaita Ito, Hiroshige Masuo, Makoto Watanabe et al. Scientific Reports 2025-12-05 10.1038/s41598-025-27232-1
单位: Department of Materials Engineering, The University of Tokyo, 7-3-1 Hongo, Bunkyo-ku, 113-8656, Tokyo, Japan. galiegue.lg@gmail.com
通讯: Louis Galiègue @ Department of Materials Engineering, The University of Tokyo, 7-3-1 Hongo, Bunkyo-ku, 113-8656, Tokyo, Japan. galiegue.l
相关性: 该论文研究LPBF过程中声发射监测，虽使用Ti-6Al-4V材料，但未涉及力学或疲劳性能，与L1方向核心内容无关。
边界: No mechanical/fatigue characterization or modeling; the paper focuses on process monitoring (keyhole detection) rather than mechanical performance of Ti-6Al-4V.
动机· 利用声发射监测激光粉末床熔融过程中的匙孔形成与演化。
方法· 系统研究扫描速度、功率、材料及光斑尺寸对声发射信号的影响。
结果· 发现匙孔深度与声发射频率成反比，高频成分重现对应匙孔致孔隙率。
验证· 通过单道和多道实验验证声发射信号的可靠性。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To monitor keyhole formation and evolution during Laser Powder Bed Fusion using airborne acoustic emission signals.
Method· Systematically investigated the effects of laser scanning speed, power, material (including Ti-6Al-4V), and spot size on acoustic emission signals.
Result· A distinct acoustic signature inversely proportional to melt pool depth was identified, with high-frequency resurgence correlating with keyhole-induced porosity.
Validation· Validated through single-track and multi-track LPBF experiments with varying parameters.

Role of Build Orientation and Surfaces on Passive Film Kinetics and Degradation of LB-DED Ti6Al4V in Fluoride Media

Lorenzo D’Ambrosi, Katya Brunelli, Saeed Khademzadeh, Christophe Lyphout, Arshad Yazdanpanah Metals 2025-12-05 10.3390/met15121340
单位: Department of Industrial Engineering, University of Padova, Via Marzolo 9, 35131 Padova, Italy
通讯: Lorenzo D’Ambrosi @ Department of Industrial Engineering, University of Padova, Via Marzolo 9, 35131 Padova, Italy
相关性: 该论文研究LB-DED Ti6Al4V的腐蚀行为而非力学或疲劳性能，与用户关注的力学与疲劳方向相关性低。
边界: No mechanical or fatigue testing; focus is on passive film kinetics and corrosion degradation, not tensile, hardness, or fatigue properties.
动机· 研究LB-DED Ti6Al4V在氟化物环境中钝化膜动力学和腐蚀行为。
方法· 通过电化学测试和微观结构分析，比较XY和XZ取向及上下表面对腐蚀性能的影响。
结果· XY取向表面形成最致密氧化膜，耐氟化物腐蚀；XZ下表面出现不稳定钝化和微点蚀。
验证· 电化学测试验证了不同取向和表面的腐蚀行为差异。
English summary & terms
Motivation· This work investigates how build orientation and surface position affect passive film kinetics and fluoride-induced degradation in laser-based directed energy deposited Ti-6Al-4V.
Method· Electrochemical testing in borate buffer and fluoride saliva environments, combined with microstructural characterization, was used to evaluate corrosion behavior for XY and XZ orientations with upskin and downskin surfaces.
Result· XY orientation formed the most compact oxide films and maintained stable passivity, while XZ downskin exhibited unstable passivation and extensive micro-pitting in fluoride media.
Validation· Experimental electrochemical measurements and microstructural analysis validated the orientation and surface-dependent corrosion performance.

Technological Assurance of Surface Roughness of Ti-6Al-4V Parts Made Using Additive and Conventional Manufacturing Methods

Artis Kromanis, Arturs Vevers, Gatis Muižnieks, Jyothi Prasad Gandreddi, Arturs Korenkovs Journal of Manufacturing and Materials Processing 2025-12-05 10.3390/jmmp9120402
单位: Material Processing Laboratory, Riga Technical University, Paula Valdena 1-107, LV-1048 Riga, Latvia
通讯: Artis Kromanis @ Material Processing Laboratory, Riga Technical University, Paula Valdena 1-107, LV-1048 Riga, Latvia
相关性: 该论文研究Ti-6Al-4V增材制造零件的后加工表面粗糙度，但未涉及力学性能，与用户关注的疲劳性能无直接关系。
边界: It only examines surface roughness after machining, without any fatigue, tensile, or other mechanical performance data.
动机· 探究增材制造Ti-6Al-4V零件机加工后表面粗糙度的工艺保证。
方法· 对比SLM与传统加工Ti-6Al-4V在不同切削参数下的表面粗糙度，并提出多维测量方案。
结果· 低进给和中等切削深度下，3D打印样品表面粗糙度优于传统加工。
验证· 通过实验测量表面粗糙度进行验证。
English summary & terms
Motivation· To investigate how machining parameters affect the surface roughness of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V parts compared to conventionally manufactured ones.
Method· Experimental comparison of surface roughness from SLM and conventionally produced Ti-6Al-4V samples under varying cutting speed, depth of cut, and feed, with a proposed multi-aspect measurement scheme.
Result· At low feed and low to average depth of cut, SLM samples achieved better surface roughness than conventional ones.
Validation· Experimental surface roughness measurements.

English summary & terms
Motivation· To improve surface quality of Ti-6Al-4V via cryogenic machining as a post-processing step.
Method· Bayesian neural networks are employed for multi-objective optimization of surface roughness.
Result· Optimal cryogenic machining parameters are identified to minimize surface roughness.
Validation· The model is trained on experimental data but no mechanical performance validation is provided.
